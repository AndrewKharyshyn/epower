Status: Rev 1, written before any code. Origin: Director review (Opus, "revise") of the external cell-spread / cadence recheck of 2026-10-22, items P1-1, P1-2 and the counts of P1-3 ("adopt with changes: a sidecar keyed by raw sha256, not new master columns, diagnostics and flags only, known-answer tests for jitter, outages and phase offset").
No change to `drive_master.csv` (MD5 `5556a0c6c8bcde0f6c64b8b9655208ed`), `summary_arrays.json`, the dashboard, any estimator, interval or published value. ML16 not touched. No filter, threshold or outcome definition is changed by this milestone: nothing here feeds an estimate.

## Facts read from disk (2026-10-22)
- `_i_channel_sampling_density` (`compute_drive_summary_v6.py:1908`) returns two numbers per drive: the median of positive current-channel intervals below 20 s and the fraction of logger rows carrying a current sample. It cannot tell uniform updates from jittering updates or from long outages (the recheck's synthetic counterexample).
- Cell spread (`compute_drive_summary_v6.py:~943-964`): `vmax`/`vmin` are paired with `merge_asof(direction='nearest', tolerance=SPREAD_TOL=300ms)` and `dropna`; spread = (vmax-vmin)*1000 mV; the loaded subset is the pairs whose current, matched by `_asof(..., V_ALIGN_TOL=1500ms)`, has |I| > 50 A; `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv` is `quantile(0.95)` of that subset with no minimum count; `n_loaded_spread_samples` is its size.
- The pair offset between the max-cell and min-cell reports and the age of the matched current sample are discarded by that code.
- Helpers `_prep_bytes`, `_series`, `_asof` are module-level; `tools/ah_throughput.py` (M402) is the pattern for a raw-sha256-keyed, incremental sidecar with `--check`.

## Design
1. `tools/timing_support.py` (pure library + CLI). For each master drive it parses the raw file with `_prep_bytes`, builds the PID series with `_series` exactly as production does (same column map, same value bands, same |I| < 900 A artifact filter, same SoC spike mask), and writes one row per master drive to the sidecar `timing_support.csv` plus `timing_support_summary.json`.
2. Per-PID timing block, for each of I, V, SoC, Vcell_max, Vcell_min, T1 and speed (prefix `i_`, `v_`, `soc_`, `vmax_`, `vmin_`, `t1_`, `spd_`): `n` (samples), `row_cov` (non-null share of logger rows; an acquisition-context variable, kept apart from elapsed-time rates), `rate_hz` (distinct sample times / elapsed span of that PID), `dt_q50`, `dt_q05`, `dt_q95` (seconds, over all positive intervals, NOT truncated at 20 s), `dt_iqr_ratio` ((q75-q25)/q50), `frac_dt_gt3s`, `n_dup` (zero intervals), `n_gap20` and `gap20_s` (count and total duration of intervals >= 20 s, the intervals the production density function discards), `max_gap_s`.
3. Regime stability, for I and for Vcell_max: the median interval is computed in fixed 60 s windows of PID time (windows with at least 5 intervals); `win_dt_min`, `win_dt_max`, `n_win` and the flag `regime_cross` = the window medians lie on both sides of the declared fast/slow gap (1.05 s, `tools/cadence_regime.py`). Descriptive; it does not replace the drive-level regime.
4. Pair block (max/min cells), replicating the production pairing: `n_pairs`; signed offset `off_med_ms`, `off_p05_ms`, `off_p95_ms` and `off_abs_p95_ms` of (time of the paired min-cell report) - (time of the max-cell report); `frac_off_gt50ms`; `n_neg` and `n_neg_loaded` (pairs with logged max below logged min, all and loaded) and `frac_neg`, `frac_neg_loaded`. Negative pairs are counted, never clamped, dropped or used to filter anything.
5. Loaded block: replicates the production loaded subset (|I| > 50 A after `_asof` to the current series with V_ALIGN_TOL): `n_loaded` (must equal `n_loaded_spread_samples` of the master), `n_loaded_dis`, `n_loaded_chg`, `n_episodes` (maximal runs of consecutive loaded pairs; a run breaks when the gap to the next loaded pair exceeds 10 s; the 10 s is a descriptive grouping, stated here before the run), `episode_max_share` (largest episode's share of the loaded pairs), `cur_age_p50_ms`, `cur_age_p95_ms` (absolute time between the pair and its matched current sample), `i_abs_med`, `i_abs_p95` (A, loaded subset), `soc_med` (loaded subset, SoC matched with V_ALIGN_TOL).
6. Flags (booleans, report-only, thresholds fixed here and labelled exploratory, not validated production thresholds): `flag_support_lt20` (n_loaded < 20), `flag_support_lt30` (n_loaded < 30), `flag_one_episode` (n_episodes < 3), `flag_skew_gt50ms` (off_abs_p95_ms > 50), `flag_neg_loaded` (n_neg_loaded > 0), `flag_regime_cross_i`, `flag_regime_cross_vmax`, `flag_gap20_i` (n_gap20 of the I channel > 0).
7. Incremental by content: a row is reused when the raw sha256 (`raw_manifest.json`, else hashed bytes) and `METHOD_VERSION` are unchanged. Columns: `file, date, raw_sha256, method_version, reason` plus the blocks above. Values are unrounded in memory; the sidecar rounds at export only (6 decimals). Missing raw file: `reason = raw_file_missing`, nulls.
8. `--check` is read-only (needs no raw files): one row per master drive; `n_loaded` equals the master `n_loaded_spread_samples` (null where the master is null); sha256 column present; flag columns consistent with their numbers. Run by `release_check.py`; stage `timing_support` in `tools/ingest_stages.json` after `ah_throughput`.
9. `timing_support_summary.json`: corpus counts of each flag, quantile tables of the main columns split by the drive-level regime (fast/slow) and the count of drives per method. A descriptive table; no test, no interval, no payload block, no dashboard text in this milestone.

## Pre-registered checks (each can fail)
- **Matched support**: for every master drive, `n_loaded == n_loaded_spread_samples` (including the null pattern). A mismatch is a failure of this tool, not a data finding.
- **Known-answer synthetic drives** (`tests/synthetic/test_timing_support.py`), written before the tool: (a) uniform 0.8 s current updates, intervals cycling 0.1/0.8/0.8/0.8/4.0 s, and two 30 s outages give identical production density summaries (median, coverage) but different sidecar rows (`dt_iqr_ratio`, `frac_dt_gt3s`, `n_gap20`, `gap20_s`, `rate_hz`) with the expected values; (b) a constant 20 mV simultaneous spread on a shared smooth voltage component with the min-cell report phase shifted 0.01 s versus 0.25 s: the sidecar reports `off_med_ms` of 10 and 250 (within 1 ms) and `flag_skew_gt50ms` only for the second, and the production loaded p95 of the second exceeds the first (the recheck's mechanism, shown on this synthetic only, not a size in real data); (c) a drive with logged max below logged min on k pairs: `n_neg == k`, nothing clamped or removed; (d) loaded counts: charge versus discharge split, episode count and `episode_max_share` on a hand-built trace; (e) identical bytes give a byte-identical row and an incremental rerun reuses the stored row; (f) a window-median regime change inside one drive sets `regime_cross` while the drive median stays on one side.
- **Production agreement**: on the synthetic drives of (b) and (d), `n_loaded` equals the `n_loaded_spread_samples` that `_v5_analyze_bytes` computes from the same bytes.
- **Corpus run**: all 606 drives processed; matched-support check passes for every drive; counts of the flags and the offset/age distributions are written to the summary and read once by the author. A flag that is raised on more than 90 % of drives is reported as non-informative, not tuned.
