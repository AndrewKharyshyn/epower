# M679 spec (Rev 1): N-04 follow-up, a valid interval for the matched early-versus-late contrast (payload only)

Status: pre-registered before any result for the new intervals exists. Follow-up F of the Director decision on M678 (`analyses/M677_spec.md` section 11): the Path A day-clustered bootstrap with fixed cells drops 29 to 32 % of draws (a used cell loses one side), so it is selected on cell survival and is "unstable, not for display". This spec pre-registers replacement interval constructions, a coverage gate that decides between them, and the reporting of a slow-only stratum. It changes no estimate: the point estimates, cells, windows and Path B of M678 are unchanged.

## 1. Question and non-claims
Which interval for the late-drive-weighted matched difference has an empirical coverage close to its nominal level at the real structure of the data (the real days, cells, cadence mix and drive counts of the corpus)? Non-claims are those of M677 section 1: a difference between early and late drives in matched state bins, not an ageing estimate; season, route mix, load, logger configuration and the software update move together. No display here.

## 2. Candidate constructions (declared before any result)
Both use the observed cells and the observed late-count weights, with calendar day as the cluster.
C1 Cell-by-side stratified day bootstrap: within every used cell and each side (early, late), resample the days that contribute to that cell-side with replacement (all drives of a drawn day in that cell-side are taken), recompute the cell means and the weighted difference (seed 42, 4000 draws). By construction no used cell loses a side, so no draw is dropped. Known limit: independence across cells ignores a day effect shared by the cells of the same day (anti-conservative when such an effect exists); the coverage study includes a planted day effect for this reason.
C2 Delete-one-day jackknife: remove one calendar day at a time (the union of early and late days, G days), recompute the estimate with the cells and weights fixed from the full data (a cell that would lose its only day on a side cannot occur: every used cell has at least 2 days on each side), jackknife variance (G - 1)/G x sum (theta_(-g) - mean theta_(-g))^2, interval = estimate +- t(0.975, G - 1) x SE (and the 98.33 % level with t(1 - 0.0083, G - 1)).
C0 (reference only, never primary): the M678 fixed-cell day bootstrap with dropped draws.

## 3. Selection gate (coverage study, offline, pre-registered)
Planted-effect study at the real structure of the Y2 outcome (loaded p95 cell spread): the real early and late drives with their days, cells (ladder and floors as in M678), cadence regime and covariates are kept; the outcome is replaced by simulated values y = cell mean + planted late shift delta + day effect + drive noise, with the cell means, the day-effect standard deviation and the drive-noise standard deviation estimated from the real data by a variance-components fit on the early window only (so the late data do not enter). Scenarios: (S1) delta = 0, no day effect; (S2) delta = 0, day effect at the fitted size; (S3) delta = 1.5 x the drive-noise SD, day effect at the fitted size; (S4) delta = 0, day effect at 2 x the fitted size. 400 replicates per scenario; each construction is applied to every replicate (C1 with 1000 draws in the study).
Gate: a construction is admissible if its empirical coverage of the planted delta is at least 0.90 in every scenario (the same threshold as the M676 follow-up F1) and its coverage does not exceed 0.99 in S1 (not wastefully wide). Primary = C1 if admissible, otherwise C2 if admissible, otherwise none (the contrast stays "interval not valid, not for display"). The choice is reported with all coverage numbers; nothing is tuned (no change of draws, floors or levels after the study).

## 4. Slow-only stratum
Because every early drive is slow-polled, the contrast is also reported on slow-polled drives only (cadence removed from the cell key; early and late drives restricted to the slow regime), with the same ladder and floors, the primary construction of section 3 and Path B, as a pre-registered second primary (not a sensitivity) for the winter ingestions. No contrast between strata is drawn and the fast-polled late drives remain unmatched and counted.

## 5. Outputs and validity
Additive keys inside `earlyLateContrast` (the block is rewritten by the same tool; point estimates unchanged and asserted equal to the M678 values): per outcome `intervalV2` (construction, 95 % and 98.33 % intervals, nDraws, dropped count, coverageStudyRef), `slowOnly`. A coverage file `analyses/M679_coverage.json` is written by `tools/early_late_contrast.py --coverage OUT.json`; the block reads it. Validity: V1 point estimates equal the M678 values; V2 C1 drops no draw; V3 the jackknife is deterministic; V4 the coverage file names the scenario seeds and counts. Known-answer tests: planted shift, jackknife equals the textbook formula on a simple mean difference, C1 never returns an empty cell, determinism. Language: the intervals are "day-clustered interval for the matched difference (construction C1/C2, empirical coverage x in the planted-effect study)"; never "95 % CI of an effect"; the M677 wording rules apply.

## 6. Out of scope and risks
Out of scope: display; changing the cells, windows or the show rule; Y1 (not shown). Risks: R1 the planted-effect study depends on the fitted variance components (reported); R2 C1 ignores cross-cell dependence from shared days (scenarios S2 to S4 test it); R3 the jackknife is a linearisation (poor for very few days; G is about 77 days); R4 a construction passing the gate on Y2 is not shown to pass on Y3 (the study is repeated for Y3 and both are reported, the gate applies per outcome).

## 7. Director review (2026-11-15, verdict revise; no change of question) and how each is applied (Rev 1.1)
These override the matching text above.
1. Gate order: if both C1 and C2 pass, take the one with the higher minimum coverage over S2 to S4; on a tie, C2. Optional C3 (declared now): day-level Dirichlet weights within each side (each drawn day carries a weight, no cell empties, cross-cell day dependence is kept); C3 enters the same gate.
2. C2: G_e and G_l count only days with at least one drive in a used cell. The stratified jackknife is used: Var = sum over h in {early, late} of (G_h - 1)/G_h x sum over g of (theta_(-g) - mean_h)^2, df = G_e + G_l - 2, interval = estimate +- t(0.975, df) x SE (and 98.33 %). Cells keep their weights and membership even if a deletion takes a cell below 3 drives.
3. Variance components: REML (random day intercept) on the residuals after removing the cell-by-side means, in both windows; the early-window-only fit is a sensitivity. If the fitted day SD is 0 or its interval includes 0, its upper 95 % limit is used in S2 to S4. The ICC is reported.
4. S3 uses a delta that differs by cell (the target is the late-weighted mean, delta_cell with the late weights equal to the mean delta); add S5: non-Gaussian errors from day-blocked resampling of the residuals.
5. The gate applies to the 95 % interval. Also reported: the 98.33 % coverage, the low and high misses separately, and the Monte Carlo SE; seeds per scenario are 42 + k; the gate runs separately for Y2 and Y3.
6. Slow-only: the contrast restricted to slow-polled drives is near-identical to the main contrast (all early drives are slow and cadence is in the cell key, so every used cell is already slow); it changes only the edges and the matched-share denominator. It is a descriptive variant, not a second primary, and never enters `persists`. Edges and the ladder are recomputed on slow drives from covariates only; windows stay frozen. Its interval needs its own coverage run or is not shown.
7. `persistsV2` = the `intervalV2` 98.33 % interval and the Path B 98.33 % interval both exclude zero (and the same sign), shown beside `persists` and `persistsRev11`.
8. V1 (point estimates equal to M678) is checked at the M678 corpus basis only.
9. A blind audit of C1, C2, C3, the coverage study and the C0 drop rates is required before any display (M677 section 11).
10. Restated: exploratory, payload only, no article figure; descriptive, not an M622 look, no alpha spent; no display without the Director. Language: "interval with simulated coverage", never "valid interval"; never "effect", "persists" (for a contrast) or "ageing"; Y3 is "median pack voltage".

## 8. Implementation record and blind audit (M680, 2026-11-15; Rev 1.2)
Implemented in `tools/early_late_contrast.py` (constructions C1, C2, C3, exact REML variance components, `--coverage OUT.json`, `intervalV2` and `persistsV2` inside `earlyLateContrast`), test `tests/synthetic/test_m680_el_interval.py`, coverage file `analyses/M679_coverage.json` (400 replicates per scenario, 1000 draws, seeds 42 + k), language rule `M679-interval-wording`.
Recorded defect and rerun: the first coverage run was discarded. The `statsmodels` mixed-model optimiser stopped on the boundary and the code fell back to a zero day effect, so scenarios S2 to S4 ran without a day effect (found because the fitted day SD was exactly zero while an ANOVA moment estimate showed a clear day effect). The fit was replaced by the exact one-way REML profile over the variance ratio (closed forms), with a known-answer test; the study was rerun in full. Only the rerun is kept.
Result of the gate (95 % coverage; per scenario S1 to S5): C2 (jackknife) 0.96 to 0.97 for Y2 and 0.93 to 0.97 for Y3, C1 0.73 to 0.92 and 0.69 to 0.90, C3 0.82 to 0.89 and 0.81 to 0.85; C2 is selected for both outcomes. Y2 day SD 1.97 (ICC 0.23); Y3 day SD 0.44 (its bootstrap interval includes zero, the upper limit 1.32 is used in S2 to S4).
Blind audit (`analytical-auditor`; spec and claim only): reproduced. Jackknife SE, df, G_e and G_l, the 95 % and 98.33 % intervals and the REML components agree to 1e-10 (REML 1e-6; Y3 day SD 0.4403 against 0.4406); an independent S3 coverage check of C2 (200 and 400 replicates) gave 0.96 to 0.98 against the claimed 0.97 and 0.94 (within Monte Carlo noise); the author's REML solver is robust where the `statsmodels` solver is not (Y3 day SD 0.024 there).
Spec gaps closed: the 98.33 % level uses alpha/2 = 0.05/6; the S3 delta ordering uses the sorted cell-key order; the mean structure of the simulated data is the pooled cell mean (Rev 1.1 rule 3: both windows); low and high misses are stored in the coverage file. Audit concerns kept: the jackknife treats the late-count weights as fixed (weight uncertainty ignored) and covers conservatively (0.93 to 0.97), the gate has an upper bound only in S1, coverage holds only under the fitted components (Gaussian day effect, no correlation across days or weeks), and the study is not re-run at each ingestion. Y2: 2.8 x SE is above the estimate.
Director decision on the results (M680, 2026-11-15; Rev 1.3): revise, applied. R1 the day counts are named in both places (Ge and Gl count the calendar days with at least one drive in a used cell; nEarlyDays and nLateDays count all days of the window frame) and the coverage file carries `designFingerprint` (used-drive-set hash, Ge, Gl, cells), added by `--fingerprint` from the same `setup()` with an assertion that the study's cells and frame day counts equal it (no number of the study changed). R2 C1 and C3 are marked "not admissible (coverage gate), reference only" in `intervalsAllConstructions`. R3 `coverageStudyRef.usedDriveSetHash` is compared with the current one; if they differ, `intervalV2.status` is "coverage study stale" and the interval is not for display; the study is rerun when the hash changes. R4 no change of the gate, the levels or the scenarios after the study. Display (later milestone, its own Director check, bound to `S.*` keys, Y2 only): "Late minus early, matched state bins: +2.0 mV; day-clustered interval with simulated coverage (C2 jackknife, 0.96-0.97 at nominal 0.95) 95 % [0.2, 3.8]; 98.33 % [-0.2, 4.2] includes zero"; the 95 % interval is shown only beside the 98.33 % one; caveats: exploratory, 37.5 % of late drives matched, season / route / load / logger / software update confounded, weights fixed, coverage holds only under the fitted variance components, not ageing, no effect, no article use; Y3 is not displayed. Over-coverage (0.97 at nominal 0.95) is conservative and is kept, not retuned. Follow-ups: C2w (weights recomputed per deletion) as a reported-only sensitivity before any display; a slow-only coverage run or keep it hidden; Y1 excluded.

## 9. C2w sensitivity (M681, 2026-11-15; Rev 1.4)
The reported-only sensitivity named by the Director (section 8, results decision) is implemented as `intervalV2.sensitivityC2w`: the stratified delete-one-day jackknife of C2 with the late-count weights recomputed after every deletion (an early-day deletion leaves the weights unchanged; a late-day deletion recomputes them). It is not a construction of the gate, is never selected and changes no flag. Result at the M678 basis: cell spread 95 % [0.28, 3.77] (SE 0.87 against 0.90 for C2), 98.33 % [-0.13, 4.18], so `persistsV2` stays false; median pack voltage 95 % [-0.38, 1.59]. The weights-fixed jackknife is therefore not materially narrower than the weights-recomputed one; the remaining display conditions are the Director's check of the rendered text and the language gate. Known-answer test: C2w equals C2 for one cell, differs (same estimate and df) when late counts vary across cells and days.
