## 0. What the existing block provides (read from `summary_arrays.json`, not recomputed)
- `bmsPowerMap.eras.{hp,kW}.{input,output}`: input = BMS-reported charge limit, output = BMS-reported discharge limit (possible power, kW, logged / app-calculated); per era and direction: drives, days, samples, seconds, dropped share (missing pack temperature or SoC), supported pack-temperature bins, the SoC 55 to 66 % ladder per 5 C pack-temperature bin (`ladderSoc55to66`: median, p5, p95, share at the logged maximum, below-maximum median and p5, 95 % day-clustered intervals, `medianAtLoggedMaximum`, `degenerateIntervals`, status supported / exploratory / not shown with the reason), the full cells (`"<tempBin>|<socBand>"`) with the same fields and floors (10 drives, 5 days, 600 s), a `monotone` description, `winter` support, `saturation` (era maximum, share at or above 63.70 kW, exceedance check of logged I x V power against the reported limit), `cadenceRegimeSensitivity` (fast and slow polling).
- Rules and caveats already pre-registered and audited there: eras are never pooled or contrasted (the hp era is May to September, the kW era starts 30 September; era and season are confounded); 63.70 kW is the "at the logged maximum" flag (possible one-byte saturation, not identified); the input channel covers generator charging and regeneration; below-maximum statistics are selected on the outcome; dropped samples are not random; "no capability, degradation or causal-temperature claim"; output direction: "logged-maximum marker, not a ceiling" (owner decision O1); no article use without the Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off.

## 1. Constraints
- Display only: no new payload key, no change to `bmsPowerMap`, `headroomUtilization`, `drive_master.csv` (MD5 `d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98`) or any other figure. Every displayed number is read from `S.bmsPowerMap`; none is typed.
- Wording (rules `M658` and `M627` already in `language_gate.py`; extended here by ledger id M779 with 'derating', 'protective limit', 'the BMS reduces power because', 'BMS limit depends on temperature'): "BMS-reported limit", "logged / app-calculated", "at the highest logged value (possible saturation)"; no capability, no cause, no identified threshold, no contrast between eras, no ordered relation unless the payload's own `monotone` text states it (quoted, labelled descriptive).
- Not an article figure.

## 2. Display (frozen before any display exists)
New section on the Thermal tab: "BMS-reported charge and discharge limits by pack temperature (logged, exploratory)". Era control: two buttons, "kW era (since 30 Sep 2026)" (default, it holds the cold bins) and "hp era (to 30 Sep 2026)"; one era is drawn at a time, never both together, no difference is shown; the date range, drives, days and dropped share of the era are printed under the control.
- Panel A, charge limit (input), and Panel B, discharge limit (output), aligned on a pack-temperature axis in the payload's supported 5 C bins (unsupported bins drawn empty with their n, never as zero): per bin a dot at the SoC 55 to 66 % ladder median with its 95 % day-clustered interval, the value printed above the dot (kW), the p5 to p95 range as a thin band, n drives and days under the bin; when the median is at the logged maximum the dot is drawn as a distinct marker labelled "highest logged value" and a degenerate interval is not drawn as precise. Below each panel one thin strip: the share of time at the logged maximum per bin (`maxShare` with its interval).
- Panel C (detail, inside "How we calculated it"): a table of the cells median by pack-temperature bin and SoC band for the selected era and direction, with status, n and the below-maximum median.
- One bound lead sentence per era: states the supported bins, the SoC band, the era dates and drives, and that values are the BMS-reported limit; it quotes the payload's `monotone` text for the logged-maximum share verbatim as "descriptive, not a temperature effect"; it states no comparison between bins other than that. Method note: rules, floors, dropped share, saturation and exceedance check, polling-regime sensitivity, the caveats of section 0.
- Cold bins below 15 C carry the label "exploratory" when their payload status is exploratory; a bin that is "not shown" says "not observed at this support".

## 3. Decision rules
- D1 (fidelity): every number on the chart and the table equals the payload field it is read from (JS test, same pattern as `test_fuel_stopped_redesign.js`); statuses, floors and the degenerate-interval flag are respected exactly.
- D2 (language): the display text passes `language_gate.py` including the M779 additions; the era contrast, "capability", "derating", "protective" and causal readings are absent; the lead contains no bin-to-bin comparison.
- D3 (era): the two eras are never drawn or compared together; no pooled value is shown.
- D4 (article): not an article figure; the CHANGELOG records this.
- Falsification of the display: if the display text implies an ordering or a cause the payload does not state, the Director rules it out.
