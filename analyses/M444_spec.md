## 1. Estimand

**SOC-standardized apparent KE recovery (assumed mass), standardized to the all-cohort reference SOC mix over the common-support bands S\*:** `R_std[g] = sum over s in S* of w~[s] * R[g,s]` with `R[g,s] = 100 * sum(charge) / sum(KE)` over the intervals of the v2 layer (M441: ratio of sums, assumed class masses, plateau and trimming rules of M441 Rev 2.2) whose pack temperature (or speed during braking) is in group g and whose SOC at the interval start is in band s. It adjusts for the SOC composition of the groups only. It is not adjusted for speed, demand, day, season or the cadence regime (the pooled native layer is cadence-mixed: the share of the KE from fast-polled drives runs from about 46 % to 3 % across the temperature bins), it is not a causal temperature effect, and it does not identify motor/inverter efficiency, friction-brake energy, a BMS limit or battery health. Language: "SOC-standardized apparent KE recovery (assumed mass), standardized to the all-cohort reference SOC mix over S\* (coverage x)"; pooled bars are "cadence-mixed"; never: effect of temperature, efficiency, measured, corrected, true, controls for, independent of SOC, resolved, confirmed by holdout.

## 2. Reference mix (one, fixed) (B3)

ONE reference for both views: cohort `all`, intervals with a valid pack temperature AND a valid SOC (the temperature-view population), assumed-mass KE weights over the SOC strata: `w[s] = KE_ref[s] / sum KE_ref`. The speed view uses the SAME `w` (there is no separate speed-view reference). `w` is computed by the script from the cache at every refresh, stored with its coverage and held FIXED under the assumed-mass range (the mass scenarios change the cell values, not the weights). It changes only if the reference cohort is deliberately changed by a spec change - never per bar, per cohort or per sensitivity.

## 3. Common support and comparability (declared before any result)

- **Joint cell support** = the M441 floors: >= 200 strict-drop intervals, >= 10 drives, >= 5 days in the cell (group x SOC band). **Week support (B8):** for the week-block interval a cell of S\* must also have data in >= 5 distinct ISO weeks (key = ISO year, ISO week of the master local start date); otherwise the week-block interval of the bars using that cell is unavailable.
- **Candidate groups** = the groups supported in the all-SOC view. For the speed view the candidates exclude the 0-10 km/h bin and the overflow bin >= 130 km/h (B5). **Common SOC support** `S*(T_c)` = the SOC bands whose joint cell is supported for EVERY group of the set `T_c`. **Coverage** `cov(T_c) = sum over s in S* of w[s]`.
- **Rule:** standardization is AVAILABLE for a view if `cov >= 0.80` and `|T_c| >= 2`. Otherwise drop from `T_c` one group at a time: the group with the fewest supported SOC bands first, ties by the lower total KE, a remaining tie by the LOWEST bin (B5); until `cov >= 0.80` or fewer than 2 groups remain. A dropped group is shown as "unsupported for the SOC-standardized comparison", never imputed, never extrapolated. No available set: "SOC-standardized comparison unavailable" with the reason, the observed view stands. The availability at coverage thresholds 0.70 and 0.90 is reported beside the decision and decides nothing (B5).
- **Weights:** `w~[s] = w[s] / sum over S* of w[s]` - ONE renormalization over S\*, identical for every bar. Per bar the payload reports the OBSERVED all-SOC value, the OBSERVED value on S\* (charge and KE of the S\* bands only, pooled) and the standardized value (B4); the excluded reference KE share `1 - cov`, the excluded drives and the fast-polled KE share of the bar (B1) are reported.
- A single selected SOC band is a conditional observed estimate, not a standardization. The below-40 row and bands without support in all groups are never in S\* by construction.

## 4. Uncertainty

Day-clustered percentile bootstrap (calendar day, B = 4000, seed 42, one resample matrix per cohort shared by all cells, bars and sensitivities); numerator and denominator of every cell are resampled together and the ratio and the standardized sum are recomputed inside every draw with the weights `w~` FIXED (conditional inference on `w` and S\*; stated). A draw with a zero denominator in any cell of S\* for a bar is counted as failed and dropped from that bar's percentile; more than 2.5 % failed draws = the bar's interval is unavailable (`failed`). A contrast between two bars: a draw fails if either bar's draw fails (B9). Support is never changed inside a draw. **Week-block sensitivity:** the same statistic with ISO weeks as the resampling cluster (week draws expanded to day counts). The statistical interval, the assumed-mass range (closed form as in M441 applied to the standardized sum) and the cadence/timing sensitivities are reported separately and never combined.

## 5. Braking-demand check (declared definitions) (B6)

Demand of a deceleration EPISODE: the mean deceleration of the episode `(v_start - v_end) / 3.6 / duration` (m/s^2) over its kept intervals (M441 episodes: maximal runs of valid engine-off intervals; trimmed plateaus excluded), assigned to every interval of the episode; deceleration classes = KE-weighted terciles of this episode deceleration in the reference population (strict-drop intervals with start speed >= 10 km/h), cut points computed by the script on cohort `all`, stored and applied to every cohort. Speed class by the interval START speed: 10-40, 40-70, >= 70 km/h. The check uses the population with start speed >= 10 km/h. **Ladder (first available level is used; a lower level is labelled "partial"):** L1 SOC (8 bands) x speed (3) x deceleration (3); L2 SOC (8) x speed (3); L3 SOC in {40-55, 55-66, 66-100} x the 9 speed-by-deceleration strata; each with its own reference weights from cohort `all` and the section 3 rule. If no level is available the result reads "not checked for braking demand" and the overlap table (KE share of each demand stratum per temperature bin and the unsupported strata) stands alone. The qualitative check compares the SIGN of each adjacent standardized difference between the SOC-only and the demand-level standardization on the COMMON groups and the same population (start speed >= 10 km/h); no monotonic pattern is required.

## 6. Chronological holdouts (B7)

The sorted calendar days with data are cut by `numpy.array_split` into four chronological blocks. The weights stay the FULL fixed `w` (no leakage question arises: they are exposure shares, and the primary holdout does not refit them). For block k: `S*_k` = the bands of the full-sample S\* whose joint cell is supported (block floors) for every comparable group supported in block k; the block is available if at least 2 groups remain and `cov_k = sum over S*_k of w >= 0.80`; the block bars use `w~` renormalized over S\*_k; the REFERENCE signs are those of the full-sample bars restricted to the same S\*_k. Reported: per block the groups, S\*_k, cov_k, and "same sign in k of n supported adjacent pairs"; no decision rests on it. A leave-block-out recomputation of `w` is a sensitivity. Chronological blocks are partly seasonal; a held-out difference is not physical ground truth.

## 7. Sensitivities (study block) (B1)

Full day-clustered tables (the same `w`, the same rule, the same bootstrap) beside the pooled primary for: native v2 per cadence regime (fast-polled and slow-polled drives separately; NO fast-versus-slow contrast is computed); the harmonized interp3 layer with the M442 C3 out-of-support mask; strict-drop-only intervals; all in-episode intervals including the trimmed plateaus; the ens-clean drives; the offset-corrected charge. PCL-1 holding rule (B2): drives on or after the PCL-1 START DATE are excluded from the cadence strata until the PCL-1 analysis has run. Each is a range over a declared convention, never combined with the statistical interval.

## 8. Outputs, cohorts and gates

`regenSocStandardized` (refreshed at every ingestion by the stage `regen_v2`): per cohort (all, warm, shoulder, cold - cold is thin by construction) the reference weights and coverage, the availability decision with its reasons, the observed / observed-on-S\* / standardized bars of the temperature view and of the speed view (>= 10 km/h; 0-10 km/h in the payload only) with day and week-block intervals, mass ranges, support statuses, the fast-polled KE share, the adjacent differences. `regenSocStandardizedStudy` (on demand, carried forward): the demand check, the holdouts, the sensitivities. Gates: (G1) reconciliation - the weights sum to 1; the observed bar equals the M441 all-SOC cell; a group whose SOC mix is proportional to the reference over S\* has a standardized value equal to its observed-on-S\* value (1e-12) (B4); (G2) the known-answer tests of section 9; (G3) the deep diff of `summary_arrays.json` shows only the two new keys and their stamps; (G4) `release_check` green; the spec SHA-256 is recorded in the CHANGELOG BEFORE the first real run and the first output is kept (B5); a blind analytical-auditor reproduces the reference weights, the common-support decision and two standardized bars with their day-clustered intervals from the raw files and sections 1-4; Director ruling before anything is displayed. No display, no effect claims.

## 9. Known-answer tests (criteria declared here, `tests/synthetic/test_regen_std.py`)

(1) Simpson fixture: identical within-SOC recovery in the temperature bins but different SOC mixes -> different observed bars, EQUAL standardized bars. (2) One weight vector for every bar, summing to 1; a changed reference cohort changes it, a changed bar does not. (3) A missing joint cell removes its SOC band from S\* for ALL bars (no per-bar renormalization, no imputation); below the coverage threshold groups are dropped in the declared order; with no available set the result is `unavailable` with the reason. (4) A bar whose SOC mix is proportional to the reference over S\* has a standardized value equal to its observed-on-S\* value (1e-12). (5) Failed draws are counted, dropped, and more than 2.5 % make the interval unavailable; a contrast draw fails if either bar fails. (6) A day pair in one ISO week is resampled together. (7) The episode deceleration cut points are KE-weighted terciles, stored, and applied (not recomputed) to another cohort; the ladder falls to the first available level and labels a lower level "partial". (8) The holdout uses the full fixed weights and the full-sample reference signs restricted to S\*_k. (9) Reconciliation: the KE over S\* plus the excluded KE equals the group KE.

## 10. Limits (disclosed in advance)

The standardization adjusts only for SOC composition; temperature remains confounded with speed, demand, season, day and the cadence regime (cadence is unresolved: M442, PCL-1); the intervals are conditional on `w` and S\*; the dropped groups are usually the cold or hot extremes, so the comparison is restricted to the retained groups; one vehicle, one driver, assumed masses, the 300 rpm engine-off proxy; SOC is the BMS SoC at the interval start; the 0.80 coverage threshold is a declared operational rule, not a scientific guarantee; the joint demand strata are expected to be partly or wholly unavailable; chronological blocks are partly seasonal; the cold cohort has 4 days; bars are unadjusted for multiplicity.
