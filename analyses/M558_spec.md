## 0. What exists
- `drive_master.csv` holds `odo_start` and `odo_end` for every drive (606 drives, none missing), read by the logger from the car odometer (0.1 km resolution). The odometer therefore localises unlogged travel to a SPECIFIC gap between two consecutive logged drives, not only to an ingestion interval.
- Pre-study check (exploratory, this session, ordering by `date` + `time_start`): of 605 consecutive pairs, 597 have |odo_start(b) - odo_end(a)| <= 0.5 km; 6 are forward jumps (one 6,564 km = the recorded scale restart of 2026-08-16; five of 1.9 to 39.2 km, about 78 km in all, under 1 % of the 10,461 logged km); 2 are backward steps (known repeat anomaly, `odometer_anomalies.json`). These figures are NOT quoted anywhere; the tool recomputes them.
- Cross-drive consumers that pair consecutive drives with `gap_h = ts_full(b) - te_full(a)`: `heatSoakCarryover` (`_heat_soak_carryover`), `interDriveCarryover` (`_linked_adjacent_drive_table`), `regimeTransition` chain with `max_gap_h`, `carOffStandby`, `thermalConvergence`. They treat every gap as parking. Unlogged travel inside a gap would contaminate a soak or standby reading.
- `odometer_anomalies.json` (M401, rules R1-R4) already lists the restart and repeat drives.

## 1. Question and non-question
For each pair of consecutive logged drives, is the gap consistent with the car standing still (parked), does it contain unlogged travel, or is the odometer evidence unusable (scale anomaly)? How much of the cross-drive soak time and how many consumer pairs are affected? Non-question: no change of any estimate, no exclusion in any existing analysis, no localisation inside a gap, no estimate of the unlogged route or energy.

## 2. Tool and definitions
Tool `tools/gap_audit.py`, block `unloggedGapAudit` (additive). Pair (a, b) = consecutive drives ordered by `date` + `time_start`, the same pairing as `_heat_soak_carryover` (verbatim reuse of `_full_datetimes`). Per pair: `odoGapKm = odo_start(b) - odo_end(a)` (rounded to 0.1), `gapH`.
- Class `parked_confirmed`: |odoGapKm| <= TOL (TOL = 0.5 km, five odometer steps; sensitivities 0.2 and 1.0 km reported, not gating).
- Class `unlogged_travel`: odoGapKm > TOL and the pair is not in `scale_anomaly`. Unlogged km = odoGapKm (a lower bound on the distance driven: an out-and-back trip adds zero, and a stop at the same odometer value is invisible).
- Class `scale_anomaly`: either drive is listed in `odometer_anomalies.json`, or odoGapKm < -TOL, or odoGapKm > 1000 km. Evidence unusable; never counted as parked and never as unlogged.
- Class `unknown`: an odometer value missing (expected empty).
- Reported with day-clustered counts: n pairs, n days, per-class counts, unlogged km (sum, median, max, with the share of logged km), share of total gap hours and of gaps >= 6 h and >= 12 h in each class, and for each consumer above the number of its pairs in each class (read from the same pair rule, not retyped).
- Boundary (left/right censoring): the first and last drive of the corpus, and the study ingestion boundaries, have no neighbouring gap; reported as counts of censored soak intervals per consumer. A drive that starts with a nonzero engine temperature offset after a `parked_confirmed` gap is not reclassified.
- Sensitivity: for each consumer whose pair table is exposed (`_linked_adjacent_drive_table`, `_heat_soak_carryover`), the worst-case bound of the flagged share (n flagged / n pairs), and the headline estimate recomputed with `unlogged_travel` and `scale_anomaly` pairs dropped, with the day-clustered CI of the full set, reported whether or not it moves (flag-only; the displayed estimate is not replaced).
- Ingestion stage `gap_audit`: recomputes at every ingestion; a new drive pair with `unlogged_travel` is listed with its dates; the stage never edits the master.

## 3. Known answers (pre-registered)

- KA3 (real, hard gate) every pair is in exactly one class; the class counts sum to n pairs; the 2026-08-16 restart pair is `scale_anomaly`; the pair before 20260801_180617.csv is `unlogged_travel`; the sum of `parked_confirmed` and the others equals the number of consecutive pairs of the master.
- KA4 TOL sensitivity: counts are monotone in TOL (a larger TOL never turns a parked pair into unlogged).
- KA5 the consumers' pair counts equal the counts from their own code for the same pairing (tested, not retyped).
- Expectation (not a gate, reported either way): fewer than 10 `unlogged_travel` pairs; no consumer headline moves outside its CI.

## 9. Rev 4 (2026-11-01): interval boundaries (owner correction)
Owner: readings were always given after the last drive of each ingestion. The date-based windows of section 8 (drives with date in (d0, d1]) mix drives across the boundary and are withdrawn (the same-day boundary shifts logged km by tens of km, even changing the sign of the excess). The interval is defined by the ingestion's last drive file: the registry stores, per reading, `lastDriveFile` (taken from the ingestion's corpus count in the CHANGELOG entry, then verified by the owner), and the interval is the drives strictly after the previous reading's last drive up to and including this one. A reading without a verified `lastDriveFile` is not used. Preliminary (not a result): with corpus counts, 45202 -> 45576 gives dash 374 vs logged 361.1 km, and 45576 -> 46930 gives dash 1354 vs logged 1122.9 km.
