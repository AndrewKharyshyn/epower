# M588 spec Rev 1 (2026-11-06): within-drive simultaneity of the cell-voltage reads behind the loaded p95 cell spread (sensitivity of the M573 / M577 step and slow terms to the pairing of Vmax and Vmin)

Status: Rev 1, frozen before code and before any result of this spec (Director review of Rev 0: "revise"; R1-R8 applied below). Base: `main` at M585 (`1ac2554`) plus the open PR 272 (M586/M587, no change to this analysis), `drive_master.csv` MD5 `1574a8156a36c6ea4ea1dfdaafc7cc72` (632 drives). Origin: follow-up named in the M577 Director ruling and the M582 spec ("within-drive cell-scan simultaneity check: non-simultaneous reads can inflate the loaded spread"). Owner instruction (Andrii, chat 2026-11-06): "you can write spec now, while CI is still running". Analysis only: no payload block, no dashboard change, no ingestion stage.

## Question
The production outcome `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv` is built from two separately polled PID series, `[BMS] Max Cell Voltage (V)` and `[BMS] Min Cell Voltage (V)`: each Vmax update is paired with the nearest Vmin update within `SPREAD_TOL = 300 ms` (`compute_drive_summary_v6.py`, `pd.merge_asof(... direction='nearest')`), then `spread_mv = (vmax - vmin) * 1000`, restricted to samples with `|I| > 50 A` (current paired within `V_ALIGN_TOL`), and the drive value is the 95th percentile. If the two reads are not simultaneous and the load changes quickly between them, the pair mixes two states and the apparent spread can be larger than the instantaneous one. The logger polling regime and the PID set changed over the study window (fast 0.7-1.0 s, slow 1.1-1.6 s; the header class B from 2026-11-03), so a pairing artefact could move with the regime. The question is descriptive and a sensitivity question: how large and how variable is the read offset, does the loaded p95 depend on it, and do the M577 `step` and `slow` terms change when the outcome is rebuilt from tighter pairs?

## Quantities (frozen)
For each canonical drive with both series (raw files via `ah_throughput.raw_loader`, 1:1 with the manifest, STOP otherwise; the production helpers `_series` and `_asof` are called, not re-implemented):
- `dt_pair_ms` of a pair = |t(Vmax update) - t(Vmin update)| in ms: the offset between the LOGGER RESPONSE times of the two PIDs, not the BMS cell-scan offset (which is not observable). The merge keeps the Vmax time; the matched Vmin time is carried explicitly (right-hand timestamp column) so that no information is lost.
- `dI_A` = I(t_Vmin) - I(t_Vmax), SIGNED (discharge-positive sign convention of the master; inflation requires the Vmin read at the higher discharge current, i.e. dI_A > 0), with I paired to each read time by the production `_asof` (tolerance `V_ALIGN_TOL`); `|dI_A|` is its absolute value. A pair without both currents has no `dI_A`. Because both reads often map to the same current sample under `V_ALIGN_TOL` = 1500 ms, `same_I_share` (pairs for which both reads map to the same I sample, dI_A = 0 by construction) is reported per drive and overall; dI_A = 0 in those pairs is not information (R1).
- the raw timestamp quantum per drive (the smallest positive difference between consecutive timestamps of each series, and its mode) and the share of Vmin reads reused by more than one Vmax (R2).
- the production sample set (loaded pairs, `|I| > 50 A` at the Vmax read) is the REFERENCE; its p95, computed with the production quantile defaults and `round(x, 1)`, must EQUAL the master column `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv` on every drive and `n_loaded_spread_samples` must match exactly (same missingness; hard stop; no tolerance, the comparison is made after the same rounding, R6).

## Designs (frozen; all report-only)
D0 (production): reference, as above.
D1 / D2 / D3 (tighter pairing): the same loaded pairs restricted to `dt_pair_ms <= 150`, `<= 75`, `<= 30` ms; a design whose threshold is below TWO raw timestamp quanta (per regime, as measured by E1) is `not estimable` and is reported as such (R2). A drive enters a design only if it keeps >= 200 loaded pairs under it (else it is dropped from that design and counted). D1-D3 are nested: they are read as a trend across thresholds, not as independent tests.
D4 (current-matched pairing): the loaded pairs with `|dI_A| <= 10 A` AND distinct current samples for the two reads (so that dI_A = 0 is not spurious); same >= 200 pairs rule; D4 is labelled `not interpretable` when `same_I_share` exceeds 0.5 over the pairs of the design.
For each design `y_k` = the p95 of `spread_mv` over the kept pairs of the drive.

## Estimands (frozen)
E1 Offset profile: per drive the median and 95th percentile of `dt_pair_ms` and of `|dI_A|`, the raw timestamp quantum and its mode, `same_I_share`, the share of Vmin reads reused by more than one Vmax, and the share of loaded pairs kept by D1-D4; summarised by cadence regime (`cadence_regime.csv`) and by header PID-set class (`pid_epoch.csv`) as n, median and quartiles of the drive medians. Descriptive, no test.
E2 Within-drive dependence of the spread on the read offset (R1): the primary offset variable is `dt_pair_ms`; per drive the OLS of `spread_mv` on the signed `dI_A` AND `|I(t_Vmax)|` (spread ~ dI_A + |I|, partialling the load level) over the loaded pairs with a distinct current sample for the two reads (same-I pairs excluded from this regression and counted), reporting the dI_A coefficient and the Spearman correlation between `spread_mv` and `dt_pair_ms`; summarised across drives (median and day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval, seed 42, 4000 draws, calendar day as the cluster). Descriptive.
E3 Outcome sensitivity: the M577 Rev 2 model A (frame, covariates, common temperature support, CR2 with t(G-1), Satterthwaite sensitivity, label ladder: `analyses/M577_spec.md` Rev 1 and `analyses/M577_spec_rev2.md`, unchanged) is refitted with `y_k` in place of the production outcome for k in {D1, D2, D3, D4}; the terms `step` and `slow` are reported per design with n drives, days, estimate, CR2 t(G-1) interval, Satterthwaite interval and the Rev 1 ladder label; the model changes only in the outcome and in the drives that satisfy the pairs rule (the population change is reported as n and as the drives dropped, with the production-outcome estimate on the SAME drives beside it, "D0 on the same set").
E4 Regime / class on the offset: the contrast of `median dt_pair_ms` between regimes is reported descriptively together with the change of the E3 `slow` term between D0 and D1-D3 on the same drives. No inference is drawn about the cause.

## Population and model (frozen; R3, R4)
Model: M577 Rev 1 model A without the class-B term (y ~ months + step + slow + T + I + soc + socr + lrest + first; calendar-day cluster; CR2 with t(G-1); Satterthwaite sensitivity as in Rev 2) on the M577 Rev 2 eligible frame of the 632-drive master (600 eligible drives before the support rule). The common temperature support of the two step arms is RE-DERIVED on the set of each design (the reduced sets differ) and the D0-on-the-same-set fit uses that same support. Per design the script reports, for the kept versus all loaded pairs, the |I| distribution (median, quartiles), and the drives and days dropped by cadence regime, header PID-set class, step arm and calendar day (a shift of the |I| load mix is reported as a possible mix effect, never hidden). Arm floor: >= 10 drives on >= 5 calendar days in EACH step arm of the design (else the term is `not estimable`).

## Decision rules and wording (frozen before any result)
Per term (`step`, `slow`) and design k the PAIRED difference (D_k minus D0 on the same drives and the same support) is estimated with a day-clustered pairs bootstrap in which the SAME day draws refit both outcomes (seed 42, 4000 draws; draws without both arms are counted as degenerate, > 10 % = not evaluable), with its percentile interval. The frozen relevance margin is the D0-on-the-same-set MDE80 of the term (mV) from the same fit. Readings: "no change beyond X mV detected" if the paired interval lies inside +/- the margin; "changes by about Y mV (interval ...)" if it excludes zero; else "not resolved at this precision". The includes-zero reading of each term under D_k and D0-on-the-same-set is reported beside it (a Rev 1 ladder label is attached to each fit, but the paired difference governs). A term whose D0-on-the-same-set reading already differs from the full-frame D0 reading is flagged "population-dependent". No multiplicity adjustment (up to 4 designs x 2 terms = 8 paired intervals, reported as such). Required: "logger response offset" (the offset of the logger response times, not the BMS scan offset). Allowed language: "apparent spread", "estimated", "the loaded p95 built from tighter pairs", "association with the read offset", "no change beyond X mV detected"; forbidden: "simultaneous", "inflation removed", "inflation proven", "not affected by non-simultaneity", "true spread", "corrected", "artefact proven", "the shift is explained by", "degradation", "imbalance growth", "cadence effect", "robust". The outcome of this analysis is a sensitivity statement about a method choice; it does not replace the production outcome and does not change the payload.
