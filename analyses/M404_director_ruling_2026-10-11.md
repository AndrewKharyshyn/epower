## Rulings
1. **(c)** Option C is reopened only as a pre-registered sensitivity (SoC-energy bookkeeping and closure residual; no extrapolation beyond 44-83 %); the basis does not change. Adjustment absorbed a substantial part of the slope, over- or under-adjustment are both possible; only the Ready-mode sweep decouples SoC level from current, engine state and cadence.
2. **(b)** "dual report only, global offset basis unchanged" confirmed. The VIF > 10 rule stays for this milestone (no retuning); for future specs prefer a direct precision rule on the bootstrap intervals and two steady loads to break the collinearity by design. (ii) is harmonised to the 90 % interval (outcome unchanged).
3. Offset values of different models and scales (legacy, the attenuated drive-level scale, the shadow scale) are never displayed side by side.
4. **Eligibility unchanged** is acceptable when stated as "0 flips of N drives under both thresholds; the shadow reproduces the master at 2.1 kWh". Condition: the shadow is rerun on every ingestion while the dual report stands; any flip escalates (implemented as a `semantic_gate` check).
5. **(h) and the zero-current proxy** support the existing limit "SoC is not a pure charge counter"; they are not scale estimates.
6. **Commit 2: conditional GO, limited to throughput normalisation** (FCE, standard EFC, GTC, C-rate) on "rated capacity 5 Ah (Nissan Article-10)". Preconditions: an M404 blind-audit verdict (done, see CHANGELOG M404), the control build (e) passing, the A2 language rules including the per-cell fix (done), Andrii's acceptance recorded in the CHANGELOG, the (f) method-change list.
7. **No-go** for switching SoC-energy bookkeeping, the residual or eligibility to the 1.795 kWh scale until the Option C sensitivity is done.
8. **Beside every affected headline in commit 2:** the legacy 2.1 kWh value; the f band (f_min to 1, both reference capacities, labelled a scenario, not a CI); raw Ah throughput as the primary figure; "scales inversely with the reference; physical throughput unchanged"; n drives / days; the weakest-cell caveat; GTC 20,000 `verified:false`. Counts on the 1.795 kWh energy reference are never headlined.
9. **Over-reads fixed in M404:** "level dependence present" reworded; "powered: false" is annotated as concerning the equivalence conclusion only; `windowFractionBandLow` renamed `windowFractionBand_fMinEnd`. Not to be written: that the data reject the rated 5.0 Ah (it is inside the method range; the direction-only window fits at 5.18 / 5.22 sit above the pooled estimate, hinting that turnarounds compress the pooled estimate).
