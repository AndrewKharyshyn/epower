# M380: written definitions of the regime-stratified view and covariate re-run (for a blind re-derivation; values-free, code-free)

Purpose: an independent derivation of (1) the cohort x logger-cadence-regime table, (2) the regime-stratified energy intensity of the warm cohort, (3) the sign, side of zero and support of the composition-adjusted warm-versus-shoulder contrast with and without the regime as a covariate, (4) one slow-equivalent energy intensity, (5) the flip classification of the contrast variants. It contains no study result. The regime and the emulated ratio are inputs. Do not read the repository's analysis code (`tools/regime_stratified.py`, `cohort_arrays.py`, `seasonal_adjust.py`, `refresh_seasonal_kpis.py`, `tools/cadence_regime.py`), `analyses/M380_regime_stratified.json`, `cohort_arrays.json`, `summary_arrays.json` or `cadence_regime.csv`.

## 1. Data and regime
- `drive_master.csv`: one row per drive (columns `file`, `date`, `gross_throughput_kwh`, `distance_km`, `speed_mean_moving`, `stationary_pct`, `pct_highway`, `drive_type`, `ens_invalid`, `ens_outlier_v2`, `I_sample_period_s`). `seasonal_drive_master.csv`: one row per drive with `file`, `thermal_regime` (values warm, shoulder, cold_insufficient). Join by `file`.
- Logger-cadence regime of a drive: "fast" if `I_sample_period_s < 1.05`, "slow" if `>= 1.05`, none if missing (this is the master's version of the median HV-current update interval; it was checked against the raw timestamps in an earlier audit).
- Canonical-clean: not (`ens_invalid` is True or `ens_outlier_v2` is True) (only an explicit True excludes a drive; missing stays in). A drive has an ENERGY BLOCK when `gross_throughput_kwh` is present and `distance_km > 0`.

## 2. Table (P1)
For the cohorts warm, shoulder and cold_insufficient and the regimes fast, slow: the number of drives with an energy block (no canonical filter), the number of distinct calendar days (`date`), the share of the cohort's gross throughput and of the cohort's km that the regime carries. Mark a cell "non-identifiable" if it has fewer than 5 days.

## 3. Energy intensity (P2, P4)
Energy intensity of a set of drives = `100 * sum(gross_throughput_kwh) / sum(distance_km)` (kWh/100 km) over canonical-clean drives with an energy block. Report it for the warm cohort within each regime (value, number of drives, number of days, and a 95 % percentile bootstrap CI that resamples calendar days with replacement; any seed, at least 2000 draws; compare at the level of Monte-Carlo noise). Slow-equivalent variant: multiply `gross_throughput_kwh` of the FAST-regime drives by r = 0.97412 (an input from an earlier emulation study; not a correction) and recompute the warm cohort's energy intensity over all warm canonical-clean drives (both regimes, value only) and the shift against the unscaled value.

## 4. Composition-adjusted contrast (P3, P7)
Outcome per drive: `y = 100 * gross_throughput_kwh / distance_km` for canonical-clean drives with an energy block. Cohort contrast: shoulder minus warm, adjusting for composition with the covariates `speed_mean_moving`, `stationary_pct`, `pct_highway`, `distance_km` and the categorical `drive_type`. Use any defensible estimator of the adjusted difference (for example an ordinary least squares regression of `y` on a shoulder indicator, the covariates and drive-type dummies, with a calendar-day-clustered bootstrap CI); the repository uses a standardised common-support estimator, so compare sign, side of zero and the support counts, not the exact value. Support: the number of warm and of shoulder drives with a finite outcome and all finite covariates. Variants: (a) as above; (b) plus a binary regime indicator (fast = 1); (c) plus two indicators for the fast runs (fast run 1 = fast drives with `date` up to 2026-09-06, fast run 2 = fast drives after that date). Report for each variant the sign of the adjusted difference, whether its 95 % CI includes zero, and the support counts. Flip classification: a variant flips against (a) if the sign changes or if the CI side of zero changes (includes zero versus excludes zero); report any flip.

## 5. What to deliver
(1) the table of section 2 with days per cell; (2) the warm-cohort energy intensity within each regime; (3) for the variants (a), (b), (c): sign, CI includes zero (yes / no), support counts; (4) the slow-equivalent warm energy intensity and its shift; (5) the flip classification. Report the choices this document leaves open and their effect where you can state it.
