Origin: `epower_audit_2026-10-29.html` F05 (P1) "Power-temperature view pairs maxima from different moments" and N07 "Timing age as part of every event metric". Audit figures are not targets and are never typed into the repo.

## 1. Defect (read from the code at M549)
| Item | Site | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| per-drive peak charge power | `compute_drive_summary_v6` `e['P'].min()` -> `peak_charge_kw` | `e` = current samples (native timestamps) with the voltage asof-aligned (`V_ALIGN_TOL` 1.5 s); P = Id * V / 1000, charge negative; the timestamp of the minimum is not stored |
| per-drive peak charge current temperature | V6 `T1_at_peak_Crate` (+ `_isFallback`) | T1 asof-aligned (`ENG_ALIGN_TOL` 3 s) to the sample of the drive's peak charge C-rate (the channel with the largest magnitude of eng_charge / dual / regen); fallback = drive-maximum T1 (`T1_peak`), flagged |
| risk map | `_crate_axes` -> `cRatePointsAK` rows `[T1_at_peak_Crate, C, band, A, kW, file, vmed]`; `CRateRiskMap` (jsx) | the x of EVERY unit view (A, kW, C) is `T1_at_peak_Crate` (or `T1_peak` when absent), including the kW view, whose y is the drive peak charge POWER; the source text itself says kW is "not co-timed with the peak current" and that implied V = kW*1000/A is outside [0.9, 1.1] x V_pack_median on a share of drives (`cRateAxes.diagnostics.impliedVoltageOverVmed`, `followUpTrigger`) |
So the kW point pairs a power maximum with the temperature at the (different) current-maximum moment. Disclosure does not make a joint exposure. The audit's correction: a temperature and freshness field at the native peak-POWER timestamp; peak-current temperature stays for the A and C views; if unavailable omit the point or label it a trip-summary pairing.

## 2. Design
- New read-only extractor `tools/power_peak_temp.py` over the raw bytes, using the SAME helpers as the master (`D._prep_bytes`, `D._series`, `D._asof`, `D.V_ALIGN_TOL`, `D.ENG_ALIGN_TOL`; the I filter and voltage band exactly as V6 / `tools/ah_throughput.py`), per drive: the timestamp `tPeakKw` of the minimum of P (the first sample when several are exactly equal; `nTiesAtMinP`), `peakKwRebuilt`, the T1 value at `tPeakKw` (nearest native T1 sample within `ENG_ALIGN_TOL`, not interpolated), `ageS` = |t(T1 sample) - tPeakKw|, the same fields for the existing current-peak pair (`tPeakI`, `T1AtPeakI`, `ageI_S`), and the timing gap `gapPeakKwToPeakI_S`. Per-drive sufficient statistics cached by the raw sha256 and the extractor source hash (`powerpeak_cache/`, git-ignored); aggregation never rereads raw.
- Evidence class (N07), one per pair: `cotimed` (age <= 1 s), `nearby` (1 s < age <= 3 s), `none` (no T1 sample within 3 s; the pre-existing master fallback applies to the current pair only). The class and age are stored with every reported peak pair, with the timestamps of both sources.
- Payload (additive): `powerPeakTemperature` = `{rule, perDrive [file, tPeakKw, T1AtPeakKw, ageKwS, classKw, tPeakI, T1AtPeakI, ageIS, classI, gapS], summary {counts by class for both pairs, age quantiles, median and 90th percentile of |T1AtPeakKw - T1AtPeakI|, n drives where the peak-power and peak-current moments differ by more than 3 s}, knownAnswer, limits, provenance}` and `cRatePointsKwT` = a column aligned by index with `cRatePointsAK` (same order, asserted) holding the kW-view x (`T1AtPeakKw` or null). `cRatePointsAK` and every existing key stay byte-identical.
- Display: the kW view of `CRateRiskMap` plots x = `cRatePointsKwT[i]`; a point with a null x is OMITTED from the kW view (primary decision) and counted in the caption ("n of N drives have no T1 sample within 3 s of the peak-power moment; omitted"); the A and C views are unchanged (x = `T1_at_peak_Crate`, fallback to `T1_peak` as today, which the existing flag labels). The kW view caption states "temperature at the peak-power sample (T1, within 3 s)". The current "kW is not co-timed" disclosure in the kW title is replaced by the generated count, not removed silently.
- N07 scope in this milestone: the two peak pairs above only (peak charge current and peak charge power, each with T1 timestamp, age, class). The other event/peak pairings found in the dashboard (e.g. the M294 cold-charge peak temperatures, the 1 / 5 / 10 / 30 s sustained peaks, the M239 peak-current thermal points) are INVENTORIED in the spec evidence with their pairing convention and left as named follow-ups (no change here).
- No master column is added (the master is never rewritten); no estimator, eligibility or A/C number changes.

## 3. Known answers and expectations (pre-registered)
- KA1 (audit acceptance, synthetic): current peak at 15 C and power peak at 35 C at different moments: the kW point plots at 35 C, the A point stays at 15 C; both ages and classes are stored.
- KA2 (rebuild identity): `peakKwRebuilt` rounded to 0.1 equals the master `peak_charge_kw` for every drive with a non-null master value (exact), and the rebuilt min of Id equals `peak_I_charge` (exact). Any mismatch STOPS (the replication of the P frame is wrong).
- KA3 (current-pair reproduction): the T1 at the global minimum-Id sample reproduces the master `T1_at_peak_Crate` on every drive whose pair is not a fallback and whose peak-C-rate channel is the global minimum of Id; the number of drives where it does not (the master picks the largest C-rate across gated channels) is reported, not tuned away. Expectation, not a gate: agreement on at least 95 % of non-fallback drives; below that the current-pair age field is reported as "channel-gated, not reproduced" for the others.
- KA4 (ties): with several exactly equal minima the earliest sample is used; `nTiesAtMinP` is reported; a synthetic frame with two equal minima at different T1 chooses the earlier and reports the tie.
- KA5 (age rule): a synthetic T1 sample 2.5 s from the peak sample is `nearby` with age 2.5; 3.5 s is `none`; no interpolation.
- KA6 (view): a jsdom test on a synthetic payload: the kW view uses `cRatePointsKwT`, the A / C views use `T1_at_peak_Crate`, null kW-x points are omitted and counted.
- Expectation (not a gate): the share of drives whose peak-power and peak-current moments differ by more than 3 s is reported; a large share confirms the defect, a near-zero share means the change is cosmetic and is reported as such.

## 4. Statistics
Descriptive per-drive values, no new inferential claim. Quantiles of ages and of the temperature difference are pooled over drives with n and the day count; no interval is attached to the per-drive point positions. Any statement about how much the kW view moves is a count (n of N) and a median shift with a day-clustered bootstrap interval (seed 42, 4000 draws) over canonical-clean drives.

## 5. Language
Old wording retired by `language_gate.py` rules with ledger id AUD29-F05 (dom, jsx, payload where it appears): the kW view title / caption that presents power against the peak-current temperature as a joint operating point ("Drive peak charge power (not co-timed with peak current)" stays only as the A/C-view footnote of what kW means there, if kept); allowed: "temperature at the peak-power sample", "peak-current temperature". Temperature channels are app-reported BMS probes (logged, not measured).
