## Facts the new wording must carry (read from the payload at render time, never typed)
- `offsetUncertainty.pointEstimateA` (-0.3834 A) is the constant current that balances the SoC ledger against the integrated current under the assumed capacity (`CAP_KWH=2.1`, `verified:false`) and a model without a resistive-loss term; its day-cluster interval is the day-to-day variability of that fit, not an uncertainty of a sensor zero.
- Capacity dependence: `offsetUncertainty.capacitySensitivity.rows` (1.7 kWh -0.4457 A ... 2.5 kWh -0.3212 A) - computed by the same code path; it was in the payload but not shown.
- Model dependence (M404, Director ruling `analyses/M404_director_ruling_2026-10-11.md`): `capacityBasis.offsetModelTest.full.offsetA` -0.0753 A (95 % CI -0.193 to +0.026) with an I2R term, `offsetAWithoutI2R` -0.4774 A; acceptance false: "dual report only: the global offset basis is unchanged"; `capacityBasis.currentOffsetFitA` 0.0388 A "not a calibrated sensor zero".
- The READY-idle hold (-0.6 +/- 0.34 A) is not a zero reference: the zero-current proxy of M404 (73 drives, mean of drive medians -2.99 A) shows stationary samples carry auxiliary and Ready-mode load.
- Gross throughput is not offset-corrected; the effect is shown as scenario rows D1-D3 of `integrationScenarios` (M420) and in `offsetGrossSensitivity`.
