## 1. Defects confirmed in the current code (file:line at the time of writing)

1. Captions under the temperature and speed views read GLOBAL payload values while the bars/curve are drawn from the active cohort: `xtrail_summary.jsx` ~3043 (`S.regenCaptureMeta.tempEffMin/Max/NMin/NMax`, `S.regenByTempMeasured`), ~3050-3055 (thin bins), ~3297-3330 (`S.regenCaptureMeasured`, plateau, thin points, totals). `regenCaptureMeta` is built from the global arrays only (`compute_summary_arrays.py` ~17872-17899). Under Warm / Shoulder the caption describes a different population than the chart.
2. Hard-coded axes: `TempChart` `maxEff = 70`, ticks `[0,20,40,60]`; `EfficChart` `maxEff = 80`, `maxSpd = 130`. New or cohort results above those values would be clipped.
3. Residual "measured" wording in display text for a quantity that is an apparent, assumed-mass kinetic-energy recovery ratio ("Measured Capture vs Speed" tab, "One series, all measured", "Measured capture is a much flatter", "measured plateau", "Every measured-efficiency figure"), plus causal statements that the metric does not identify ("the remainder lost to aero drag, rolling resistance, driveline and friction-brake blending", "motor speed is too low for meaningful back-EMF, so the friction brakes do the work", "roughly two-thirds"), plus a literal fallback `"~1.6"` for the mass-band percentage and the legacy-flat-mass fallback `1950 kg`. The language rule W4-measuredcapture (`language_gate.py`) catches only "measured capture efficiency / by pack".
4. The temperature view must always show the full range of pack temperatures the payload has bins for (empty winter and hot-end bins are explicit "no samples yet" bars) in every cohort, and must show new data in any bin automatically when it is collected (owner decision (e), 2026-10-14).

## 2. Changes (display only)

- **C1 Cohort-bound captions.** A pure JS function `regenMetaOf(tempRows, speedRows)` reproduces the payload's `regenCaptureMeta` fields from the rows the chart draws (`cohortOr(...)`): `tempEffMin/Max`, `tempLabelLo/Hi`, `tempNMin/NMax`, `tempThinBins` (a bin without samples is not a thin bin), `speedEffMin/Max`, `plateauLo/Hi` (valid points with 50 <= speed <= 110 as in the producer), `crawlEff`. Every caption number in the temperature and speed views is derived from `regenMetaOf` of the ACTIVE rows; under a cohort the caption names it ("Warm cohort: ..., a population filter, not a substitute for the battery temperature"). For the cohort "all" the derived values equal `S.regenCaptureMeta` (gate G1). The payload block `regenCaptureMeta` and all payload keys are unchanged.
- **C2 Data-driven axes.** y-extent = the smallest multiple of a tick step (10 below 60 %, otherwise 20) that is >= the largest displayed value, where displayed values are the point estimates (valid and thin) and the upper ends of the mass-uncertainty ranges; ticks from 0 to that extent; the same function for both charts; the speed axis extent from the largest speed bin + one bin width rounded to 10 km/h. Chart types, colours and layout are unchanged. `data-ymax` and `data-xmax` attributes expose the extents for the jsdom test.
- **C3 Full temperature range.** The temperature view draws the union of the global bin labels and the cohort's labels in the payload order (every bin of `S.regenByTempMeasured`), a bin missing from a cohort array is drawn as "no samples yet"; nothing is hidden when a bin is empty and nothing is clipped when a bin fills.
- **C4 Wording.** Display strings: "Measured Capture vs Speed" -> "Apparent KE Recovery vs Speed"; "One series, all measured." -> "One series of apparent-recovery estimates."; "Measured capture is a much flatter ..." -> "The apparent KE recovery is a much flatter ..."; "measured plateau" -> "plateau of the estimates"; "Every measured-efficiency figure below" -> "Every apparent-recovery figure below"; "M22 - real data, not a model." -> "Pack-temperature view (M22), computed from the logged current, voltage and speed with an assumed mass."; "the energy actually returned to the battery" -> "the charge-direction energy at the battery terminals (estimated from the logged current and voltage)"; the unidentified causal sentences become non-attributing ("the remainder is not attributed: aero drag, rolling resistance, driveline losses, friction-brake blending and grade are not separated by this ratio"; "the ratio is low at a crawl; this study does not identify why (low motor speed and friction-brake blending are candidate explanations)"); the "roughly two-thirds" literal is removed; the `"~1.6"` and `1950 kg` literal fallbacks are removed (unavailable instead). The old wordings become language-gate rules with their ledger id (M439-*), so they cannot return.

## 3. Gates and tests

## 6. Rev 2 (2026-10-15): Director review of Rev 1 (revise) adopted

- **B1 `regenMetaOf` follows the producer exactly.** Temperature side: the bins with `valid` true (min / max of `eff`, first / last label, min / max `n`); `tempThinBins` = invalid bins with a non-null `eff`. Speed side: `speedEffMin/Max` over all non-null rows and the producer's `plateauLo/Hi` over all non-null rows with 50 <= speed <= 110 (NO n gate); `crawlEff` = the first row with speed < 30, 0 decimals. Rounding is half-even (`pyRound`, Python semantics; known answers 62.5 -> 62 and 63.5 -> 64) because JavaScript `Math.round(62.5)` is 63. Zero valid bins renders "unavailable".
- **B2 One plateau definition in the caption and the band.** The chart's plateau band and the speed caption use bins with n >= 200 and speed >= 25 on the ACTIVE rows (what the chart draws) and say so; the producer's 50-110 plateau stays unchanged in the Conclusions cards (they read the payload and are not part of this milestone). The sentence "every caption number from `regenMetaOf`" is restricted to the temperature caption and the thin-bin note; the speed caption numbers come from the same active rows with the rule stated.
- **B3 G1 scope.** `summary_arrays.json`, `cohort_arrays.json` and `summary_config.json` stay byte-identical; the rendered-text diff of the cohort "all" lists every expected delta (new axis ticks such as 80 % and 140 km/h, removed literals, replaced sentences).
- **B4 Axis rule.** step = 10 if the largest displayed value < 60 else 20; y-extent = max(step, ceil(max / step) * step); x-extent = ceil((largest speed bin + bin width) / 10) * 10; values above 100 % are allowed; a negative efficiency fails the test.
- **B5 Language rules** are phrase-exact or scoped to the rendered DOM and the JSX strings; payload provenance strings ("real engine-off decel", "real-data claim") are unchanged and must not trip them.
- **B6 G2** includes a case in which the cohort value differs from "all" (a synthetic row set when the live cohorts coincide).
- **Cold and Compare.** Cold stays `ColdNoData` in this milestone (owner decision (e), "build the cold view", is NOT met here and is declared as open for ME); G2 / G3 iterate the available cohort keys and assert the cold no-data marker; Compare mode (`CompareMulti`) is out of scope.
- **Final strings (Director):** speed caption "Between {loS} and {hiS} km/h the apparent ratio stays within {lo}-{hi}% (bins with n>=200)." (no "flat", no "Capture sits"); "...so it is neither an upper nor a lower bound on conversion efficiency; GPS altitude is a prerequisite for a grade-resolved balance."; crawl "At crawl speeds the ratio is low ({e}% at {s} km/h, {e0}% at {s0} km/h); this ratio does not identify why (lower motor speed and friction-brake blending are untested candidates)."; temperature "The apparent KE recovery spans ~{min}-{max}% across {range} (bins with n>=200); bins are not adjusted for speed, SOC or drive-type mix." (no "much flatter"); the sentence "shown as an error band on the chart" is removed (no band is drawn); the 1860-1920 kg range is bound to `urbanMixedSensitivityRangeKg` and the +- percentage comes from the active `RegenByZone` rows. Also corrected: "actually recovered" (~3354), the 1950 kg fallback (~3351), "real engine-off" and the unbound "almost no cold-start" in the thin-bin note (~3055), the friction-brake attribution and "measured pack charge" in the Conclusions cards (~12244 / 12373: "measured" -> "logged", attribution removed).
