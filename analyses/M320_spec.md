# M320 pre-registered spec (rev 2): M119-v2 pack-temperature ladder, support-gated and data-driven

Status: written BEFORE any M320 result exists (2026-10-01; master 489 drives, MD5 bd9d10726bb064d857cf9ff98d2b7338; main at 2084a43, tag M318). Incorporates the Director's design decision of 2026-10-01 (decision "revise": replace the fixed literals by a support-gated, data-quantile ladder selected by a pre-registered distinctiveness test; the fitted model is NOT changed). Revision 2 folds in the Director's review of rev 1 (decision "revise", 9 required edits; rulings 1-3 accepted: Part C deviation, P25/P50/P90 secondary demand levels, whole-pipeline-refit conditional bootstrap), still before any result.
Wording: surfaces, hazard ratios and contrasts are model-derived and associational (pack temperature is confounded with season, calendar date and drive age, and EV operation heats the pack), never measured. The M318 V2 fit is labelled "not M299-reproducible (F03)". Dashboard-only, not article-eligible; no Andrii sign-off required unless a figure is proposed for the article.

## Problem
`m119v2_model._surfaces` renders SoC x speed transition-probability surfaces at three FIXED pack-temperature literals (cool ~20, warm ~28, hot ~35 C; `tempLevels`, grids `T{ti}_D{di}_U{ui}`). 20 vs 28 C are visually near-identical, 35 C is about the corpus median (at-risk pack temperature P5/P50/P95: start 18.5/32.8/41.5 C, stop 19.8/35.5/42.0 C; range 11.0-45.8 C; no data below ~11 C), and the coming shoulder/cold season will extend the range to about -15...-20 C. `supportMask` is MARGINAL over SoC x speed (n >= 20 s per cell) and ignores temperature. The pack-temperature term is a natural (restricted) cubic spline with 4 knots at the 5/35/65/95 data percentiles (`_knots`), hence linear beyond the 5th and 95th percentile: extrapolation outside the observed range is formally defined but not interpretable.

## Scope
Part A (data): selection rule, conditional support, distinctiveness test, recomputed surfaces at the selected levels, hazard-vs-temperature profile, difference maps, all with day-clustered CIs, written to a new block `socHysteresisV2.tempLadder` and to `surfaces.{start,stop}.tempLevels/grids` (regenerated from the SAME final fit). Part B (display): dashboard changes (xtrail_summary.jsx) bound to S.* only: difference-from-reference log-HR map with a shared diverging scale, hatched where the CI includes 0, beside the reference surface; a 1-D hazard-vs-temperature profile strip (CI band, at-risk rug, unsupported range shaded, and the regions beyond the P5 / P95 boundary knots marked "linear tail (spline-constrained)" separately from the unsupported shading); selector built from `tempLevels`; no hand-typed temperatures or counts. Part C: ladder drift monitor and the refit rule. Out of scope: any change to the fitted model (spline knots, covariates), the cold-regime covariates (coolant / ambient), the article, the Ukrainian companion.

## Part A. Selection, support, distinctiveness
Population: the M318 R2 complete-case at-risk tables (all 489 drives, CORE5 complete, per side start/stop), the same final fit as M318 (`_final_fit`, cloglog GLM, five-variable duration-aware spec with the three interactions, day-clustered robust covariance). Acceptance (known-answer / isolation): refitting reproduces the stored M318 surfaces at 20/28/35 C to 1e-6 absolute on every cell; every other `socHysteresisV2` key (ladder, partialDependence, sensitivity, rollingOrigin, methodology) is byte-identical to HEAD.
1. Candidate positions: fixed config grid with 2.5 C steps over [-25, 50] C (config constant `TEMP_LADDER_STEP`, `TEMP_LADDER_RANGE`, not data).
2. Levels: at-risk-seconds-weighted P5, P50, P95 of pack temperature, separately per side, snapped to the nearest grid point; reference = P50. Day-weighted quantiles (each calendar day equal weight) are a reported sensitivity. At most 3 levels (a 4th, colder level is a method change, see Part C).
3. Support gate (per side, per level): the band level +/- 2.5 C must contain >= 100 events and events on >= 10 distinct calendar days. A level failing the gate is refused (not shown masked) and replaced by the UI note "no data at this pack temperature; corpus range [min, max]" with min/max from `tempLadder.{side}.tpackMinC/MaxC`. No surface is rendered for a level outside [min, max] of the observed pack temperature.
4. Conditional support mask per level: a SoC x speed cell is supported at a level when the cell contains >= 20 at-risk seconds with pack temperature inside the level's +/- 2.5 C band (replaces the marginal mask for the temperature-ladder surfaces; the old marginal mask is kept as `supportMaskMarginal`).
5. Distinctiveness of a level pair (a, b): D = at-risk-weighted RMS over cells supported at BOTH levels (weights = pooled at-risk seconds of the cell in the two bands) of eta_a - eta_b, where eta is the linear predictor (cloglog log-hazard) on the SoC x speed grid. ONLY the primary cell (median demand level, 15 s "settled" duration level) feeds the decision. Secondary cells (existing low P25 and high P90 demand levels, 2 s and 60 s duration levels; tpack x demand is an interaction term; the stored demand levels replace the Director's P10/P90 wording so displayed levels and test agree, Director ruling 2) are diagnostic and gate nothing; a secondary cell that disagrees with the primary is reported as method disagreement. CI: day-clustered percentile bootstrap, 4000 draws, seed 42 (per-draw seeds from `numpy.random.SeedSequence(42).spawn(4000)`), each draw resamples calendar days of the complete-case table with replacement and REFITS the whole pipeline (DesignSpec incl. knots and standardisation, `fit_glm` without clustering) with levels held fixed. Interval: 95% two-sided percentile, i.e. a one-sided 2.5% lower bound. A pair is DISTINCT only if BOTH the percentile lower bound and the basic-bootstrap lower bound (2 D minus the upper percentile) are >= delta = log(1.25); if only one is, it is BORDERLINE (reported, goes to the Director, not a selection). D is an RMS (>= 0, upward-biased under noise), so the bootstrap bias (mean D* - D) is reported for every pair. Rationale for delta, stated before any result: log(1.25) is a materiality convention (a 25% higher or lower hazard at the same SoC and speed, a contrast a user could act on), fixed in advance and not derived from the data; the legacy levels are NOT used to calibrate it. Per draw, the set of cells supported at both levels and the cell weights are recomputed from the resampled data; a draw with an empty cell set counts as a failed draw. Failure rule: more than 1% of draws failing or non-converged -> the pair is INCONCLUSIVE, not published as distinct. Level selection is treated as conditional (not re-selected inside draws): disclosed limitation.
6. Merge rule (fixed-sequence test, so no multiplicity adjustment; the P50 step is an intersection-union test): test {P5, P95} first. If not distinct, show ONE level (the P50 reference) with the statement "pack-temperature effect not established at this resolution" (never "no effect"). If distinct, add P50 only if distinct from BOTH; otherwise drop it and show two levels; with two levels the REFERENCE is the lower level (P5), fixed now (the difference map then shows the higher level minus the lower). Interior or edge levels failing the gate are dropped.
7. Diagnostics reported (not decisions): D (with CI) for the legacy pairs 20-28, 28-35, 20-35 C and for every candidate pair, to quantify the observed near-overlap; D restricted to the core support (cells with >= 100 s per band) to check that the contrast does not come from thin cells; stability of the selected levels between the 410-drive (M286 population) and 489-drive corpora; day-weighted-quantile levels; the un-hysteresised levels (hysteresis makes levels path-dependent); the number of calendar days with events in the P5 band and in the P95 band (flag when fewer than 20: seasonal cold support may rest on a few days and make the day bootstrap unstable); the failed-draw rate per pair before any CI is trusted.
8. Profile and maps from the same bootstrap draws: hazard (cloglog inverse link) vs pack temperature at median SoC / speed / demand and 15 s duration, 1 C steps within [min, max], pointwise 95% bands; log-HR maps (level - reference) per cell with cell-wise 95% CI; at-risk rug as counts of at-risk seconds and events per 2.5 C bin. All stored in `tempLadder` (S.* keys), nothing typed.
Provenance fields per level: quantile, quantile value, snapped value, nEvents, nDays, nAtRiskS, admissible flag, role (low / reference / high). The label text ("P5 ~ 18 C", etc.) is generated from the stored value.

## Part C. Refit rule and drift monitor
M119-v2 is refit only on explicit demand (standing policy), so the ladder is derived on, and carried with, the V2 fit basis: it is re-derived whenever V2 is refit and otherwise carried forward with `basisNDrives`. On every ingestion a REPORT-ONLY drift monitor (cheap, no refit) computes the current-corpus weighted quantiles and support per side and flags (release_check WARN, not FAIL) when a quantile has moved by >= 1 grid step (2.5 C) from the carried level, when a candidate level colder than the carried P5 passes the support gate, or when the corpus range extends beyond the carried [min, max]. Whenever the monitor raises a WARN the dashboard shows the stamp "ladder basis N drives" (N = basisNDrives of the carried ladder); a WARN for a cold level passing the gate or for an extended range starts a NEW spec and never triggers a silent refit. The deviation from "re-derive on every ingestion" is accepted by the Director and recorded in the CHANGELOG entry. Hysteresis at refit: a level keeps its previous value unless its quantile moved >= 1 grid step. (This deviates from "re-derive on every ingestion": the frozen fit cannot be evaluated at levels chosen from data it was not fitted on; the Director is asked to confirm.) Adding a cold level, re-placing spline knots or adding coolant / ambient covariates (cold start and cabin-heat regimes) are method changes needing a new spec and a blind audit; early cold levels are labelled "early support" until >= 20 calendar days.

## Accept / reject
Validity (failure = no splice, HEAD stays): (a) known-answer reproduction of the stored M318 surfaces at 20/28/35 C to 1e-6, other V2 keys byte-identical; (b) support counts, quantiles and level selection reproduced exactly by the blind auditor from the rule and the data path only, and D and its lower bounds reproduced within 3 Monte-Carlo standard errors of the auditor's own independent bootstrap (SE of the 2.5% quantile by batch means, 40 batches of 100 draws); a lower bound within that tolerance of delta makes the outcome BORDERLINE and goes to the Director; (b2) known-answer tests for D under tests/synthetic/: identical levels give 0, a synthetic pure pack-temperature main effect gives the analytic RMS contrast; (c) no rendered cell outside the observed range or outside its conditional mask; no temperature or count literals in the JSX (S.* binding, dashboard string checks); (d) coefficients unchanged; isolation diff: only `socHysteresisV2.surfaces`, `socHysteresisV2.tempLadder` and the stamp change; (e) `release_check.py` green, `validate_jsdom.js` green, master MD5 unchanged, LF line endings.
Scientific outcomes (reported, escalated to the Director, never tuned): {P5, P95} not distinct (one-level display); a distinct pair whose contrast comes mainly from thin or edge cells; level churn between the 410- and 489-drive corpora; methods disagreement between weighted and day-weighted quantiles.
Falsification: if the legacy pair 20-28 C turns out distinct (CI lower bound >= log 1.25) the premise that the legacy levels were indistinguishable is not supported and is reported as such.
