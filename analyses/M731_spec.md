## 2. P6: observed quantity
Frozen prediction: `C_at_0C_soc60 = 18.85` (limit kW x 1000 / 360 V / 5 Ah, rated basis, verified:false) and the rule "the peak charge C-rate below 0 C does not exceed the limit-map C-rate at the logged temperature and SoC". The frozen limit map: b0 = 33.9221 kW at 0 C and SoC 60, bT = 1.5529 kW per C, bS = -1.0097 kW per SoC point (extrapolated below the observed range, labelled).

2.1 Per-drive sidecar `analyses/M731/p6_events.csv` (new tool `tools/winter_p6_frame.py`, additive, never writes the exposure frame): on the production 1 Hz grid of `scenario_frame.extract_drive` (same forward-fill limits, same sentinel masks, native per-second Imax), over the CHARGE seconds (native Imax > 0) with the coldest pack probe `Tlo` below 0 C and a finite SoC: `p6_n_s` (count), `p6_margin_c` = the MAXIMUM over those seconds of (Imax / 5 Ah minus the limit-map C-rate at that second's `Tlo` and SoC), and at the second that attains it `p6_peak_c`, `p6_Tlo`, `p6_soc`, `p6_limit_c` = (b0 + bT Tlo + bS (soc - 60)) x 1000 / 360 / 5 (frozen parameters, not clipped; a non-positive limit is kept and flagged), (the margin is judged on every second, not only the peak-current second). Drives without such seconds have `p6_n_s = 0` and are not in the cell.
2.2 Cell and statistic: drives of the scoring set (clean, start after F + 1 h, guard G1-G5 as in M726) with `p6_n_s > 0`. Support: at least 10 drives on at least 5 days, else "not evaluable". Primary statistic (the literal frozen rule): the count of cell drives with `p6_margin_c > tol` and the maximum margin over the cell, where `tol` is fixed now as a formula on C-P6c: tol = max(0, the 95th percentile of `p6_margin_c` over the frozen-basis drives with `Tlo` in 0..10 C (the region where the register is observed)), computed once by the check and written to `analyses/M731_consistency_check.json` BEFORE any scoring read; it is part of the freeze. Secondary (a stated deviation from the literal text): the exceedance share with a zero-event-safe upper bound (Clopper-Pearson on drives AND the day-clustered bootstrap, the larger upper endpoint is used; the percentile bootstrap alone gives [0,0] at 0 events and is never used alone).
2.3 Reading rule: "within the map" if the primary count is 0; "outside the map" if the count is at least 1 on at least 2 days (a single-day exceedance is "inconclusive"); the share and its safe upper bound are reported beside it as the stated deviation. Results are also reported by `Tlo` region (below -5, -5..0 C). The statement is about the BMS-reported limit register extrapolated linearly, not about delivered power, cell limits or safety.

## 3. P6: checks (criteria written here, before the first run; frozen basis only: day <= 2026-11-09, 665 drives, 0.000 h below 0 C in the logged corpus)
C-P6a (fidelity of the grid and peak rule): recomputing the all-seconds peak charge C-rate with the same code on every frozen-basis drive reproduces the frame column `peak_c_chg` within 1e-6 for 100 % of drives, or every exception is listed with its cause (missing native Imax, sentinel).
C-P6b (no sub-zero seconds): the sum of `p6_n_s` over the frozen basis equals 0, consistent with `h_T_<0` summing to 0.
C-P6c (defines `tol`, never a pass/fail check): the same margin definition (max over charge seconds) at `Tlo` 0..10 C on the frozen basis; tol = its 95th percentile over drives, written to the check file with n drives and days before any scoring read.
Known-answer tests (CI): a planted drive with a known peak, temperature and SoC gives the exact margin; no sub-zero second gives `p6_n_s = 0`; the share and its interval on a planted frame; support rule; the exceed rule; the reading rule.

## 4. P2: the realised-ambient band
4.1 Engine freeze: the replay engine and its inputs are vendored byte-exact FROM THE GIT BLOBS of the M713/M717 commit (not from the working tree), `monthlyTempC` (the climate normals; `load_model` reads the live `summary_arrays.json`) is vendored as a hashed copy, and imports are isolated from `tools/` (a test fails if the band tool imports a live module). Live input: only the realised winter ambient; everything else is frozen. Vendored contents: byte-exact into `analyses/M731_frozen_engine/` (`scenario_engine.py`, `exposure_state.py`, `park_model.py`, `scenario_frame.py`, plus an index with sha256 and the frozen inputs of M717: `state_params`, `park_model.json`, `M712_ranges.json`, the frozen exposure frame slice of the 665-drive basis as a hashed copy). The band tool imports only from the vendored copy.
4.2 Band definition: ONE scenario, S2 (balanced pattern, odometer-tilted) with the month shift table replaced by the realised shifts (it replaces S3a/S3b, which differ from S2 only by the shift). For each calendar month m with at least 5 scoring days carrying a driver-read ambient: shift_m = mean over those days of (engine drive-time ambient Abar minus the monthly normal minus the frozen engine BIAS term), i.e. the frozen engine's own anomaly convention (a raw daily mean minus the normal would double-count the drive-time warm bias). A month with fewer than 5 such days takes the frozen S3a/S3b bracket for that month (lower and upper endpoint from the two runs) and is flagged, not shift 0. Shift uncertainty enters: the day bootstrap of the shifts (seed 42, 4000 draws, paired to the engine draws). The band is the 95 % E-real interval of the Ah share below 10 C. If no month has 5 days, the realised band is "not available" and P2 stays on the frozen band, which is shown beside it in every case.
4.5 Domain mismatch (pre-registered; implementation detail added as Rev 2.1 after the Director review, to be covered by the freeze review): the frozen engine has no per-month output and is not edited, so the window match is done in the runner `tools/winter_p2_band_run.py` (new code, hashed in the registry): after `draw_days` every synthetic day outside the months of the scoring window is pointed at a calendar day without drives (a "null day": it adds no Ah, no hours and no starts), so the engine's own `ahShare_T_lt10` is the share over the window months only. Check C-P2d: with all twelve months in the window and the frozen S3b shift table the runner reproduces the engine's annual S3b result exactly. Both domains (annual and window-matched) are reported; the decision uses the window-matched band. The shift of month m uses the driver-read ambient: A_day = mean over the day's drives of the mean of the drive start and end ambient; shift_m = mean over scoring days of A_day minus the normal of month m minus the frozen engine BIAS (computed by the vendored model); the engine's own reconstructed ambient is not rebuilt for post-freeze drives (stated simplification). Thin months (fewer than 5 days) inside the window: months Nov-Mar take the S3a value in one run and the S3b value in a second run and the band is the envelope of both runs; any other thin month takes shift 0 and is flagged.
4.3 Reading: unchanged from the freeze (the day-clustered 95 % interval of the realised winter Ah share below 10 C from `extract_p2` lies inside the band); both the frozen band [0.0316, 0.1476] and the realised band are reported; the realised band decides when available, and the frozen band is shown beside it.
4.4 Checks: C-P2a (engine fidelity): the vendored engine run on the frozen inputs reproduces the E-real intervals of S2, S3a and S3b in `analyses/M713/scenario_results_production.json` byte-exactly (the union band [0.0316, 0.1476] is recovered). C-P2b: the shift derivation on a planted ambient series recovers the planted shifts (known-answer). C-P2c (descriptive): run time of the band at 4000 draws.

## 9. Rev 2.2 note (recorded before any scoring read)
Run 1 of the frozen-basis check (`analyses/M731_consistency_check.json`, label "run 1") returned C-P6a 665 of 665 drives equal, C-P6b zero sub-zero charge seconds and C-P6c a reference 95th percentile of -3.30 C (15 drives on 10 days, all margins negative). A tolerance below zero would make the count rule stricter than the literal frozen text (a drive merely within 3.30 C of the limit would count) and would fire by chance about 5 % of the time; the formula in 2.2 is therefore floored at 0 (`tolEffC = max(0, p95)`, here 0). This floor was added after seeing the C-P6c value of the frozen basis and before any scoring-set value is read; it is a disclosed change of Rev 2.1 and is put to the Director in the freeze review. No other rule changed.
