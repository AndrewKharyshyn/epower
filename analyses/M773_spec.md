## 0. Why a battery-side channel can carry road load here, and what it cannot
In this series hybrid the generator supplies extra demand and the SoC controller holds the battery near balance, so the drive-level net battery draw is NOT a road-load measure (M771 Director ruling). The exception is electric driving with the engine off (engine speed at most 400 rpm, moving): there the battery supplies all traction and auxiliaries, so the net battery energy per electric kilometre (`ev_kwh_per100km` in `drive_master.csv`, written by `compute_drive_summary_v6.py` from the logged current and voltage over the engine-off moving samples, signed: regeneration during those runs is netted; raw current basis, no offset correction) responds to the road load. It still carries speed, stop-and-go, regeneration, HVAC and route structure; the engine-off rule (400 rpm) is the master's own. The channel exists for 645 of 717 drives, including the 1 Jul to 13 Aug gap in the fuel basis (the engine-off share is logged without fuel flow).
Counts (before this spec, counts only): drives with the channel within 14 / 28 / 42 days of 26 Jul: before 79 / 92 / 133 drives on 14 / 20 / 31 days, after 37 / 87 / 152 drives on 8 / 20 / 33 days (drives of 2 km or more with the channel: 65 / 77 / 114 before, 29 / 66 / 110 after).

## 1. Constraints
- Report-only. `drive_master.csv` MD5 d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98 asserted before and after (a different master stops, exit 3, nothing written); the M733 snapshot table pinned by sha256; no winter-frozen file (M726); no positions, tracks or absolute cloud odometer values. Outputs `analyses/M773_*`: aggregates only; no per-drive rows, dates or file names (step day and the window offsets are written as relative days; the window labels are "14, 28 and 42 days around the top-up").
- Language: pressure "estimated from the cloud tyre-pressure channel, scale accepted by the owner from manual gauge control (24 Nov 2026), not independently re-measured"; the energy "battery-side energy while electrically propelled (engine off), estimated, integrated from logged current and voltage, regeneration netted", never "road load measured" or "rolling resistance"; "before/after look, season, route, load and period confounded, not a pressure effect"; no equivalence test; "not informative at this support" is a legitimate result; the owner decision is recorded verbatim.
- Statistics: calendar-day cluster bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); every estimate with n (drives, days), an MDE (2.8 x bootstrap SD) and the rank-deficient resample count; ONE confirmatory estimand; `design_mde` written before any estimate; expectation and reading rule fixed before any result.

## 2. Population and definitions
- **Eligible drive:** canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` not true; missing treated as clean, as in M769 to M771, counted), `ev_kwh_per100km` present, `ev_valid` true, electric distance (`ev_dist_km`) at least 1.0 km (assumed floor; 0.5 and 2.0 km sensitivities), master distance at least 2 km; the 14 Jul 2026 puncture day (M733) excluded and 14 to 16 Jul as a sensitivity; the step day (26 Jul) excluded from both sides.
- **Windows:** primary 28 days before and 28 days after the step day (the step day excluded); sensitivities 14 and 42 days. A window side needs at least 5 days and 20 drives, else counts only.
- **Covariates:** drive class (4), ambient (time-mean, seasonal master), electric distance fraction (`ev_dist_pct`), mean moving speed during the drive (`speed_mean_moving`) and its square, log electric distance, the long-soak start flag (3 h, M772 definition over the full master; the first drive unknown and dropped), thermal class. No month fixed effects (the windows span at most two months; the before/after contrast is the question).

## 3. Estimands
- **A1 (confirmatory):** the covariate-adjusted difference of `ev_kwh_per100km` (after minus before), in kWh per 100 km and as a percentage of the before-window mean, primary window 28 days, day-clustered interval, MDE. **Pre-registered expectation (assumed, stated before any result):** the pressure rose about 10 to 16 %; the pressure exponent of the rolling coefficient is -0.2 to -0.5, so the rolling coefficient falls by about 2 % to 8 %; rolling resistance is 0.35 to 0.55 of the EV-mode traction energy at urban and mixed speeds; the battery supplies the whole traction, so the pass-through is about 1.0 (regeneration returns kinetic energy, not rolling work); the expected change is therefore about -0.7 % to -4.4 % of the electric-driving energy, i.e. about -0.10 to -0.62 kWh per 100 km at a mean of 14.2. The opposite pulls are stated (a warmer or cooler August, HVAC, a different route and speed mix, a heavier occupant load, tyre warm-up behaviour with the higher pressure). Reading rule: "informative" only if the MDE is at most half of the LOWER end (about 0.05 kWh per 100 km, taken as 0.35 % of the before mean). A positive difference with an interval excluding zero is read as "inconsistent with the expected sign, confounding suspected", never as a finding.
- **A2 (exploratory, dose-response within the windows):** the slope of `ev_kwh_per100km` on the cold-equivalent four-corner pressure of the drive (M770 definition, per +10 %), using the drives with a snapshot; same covariates; the observed pressure change across the step (cold-equivalent median before and after) is reported as the dose.
- **A3 (exploratory):** the same difference for the regen-poor subset (`ev_nr_kwh`, `ev_nr_km`), the electric share of the distance (`ev_dist_pct`), and the mean electric speed class, as counts or differences without verdicts, to show what else moved across the step.
- **P (placebo windows, guarded):** the same 28-day (and 14-day) before/after difference at displaced centres of -84, -56, -42, +42, +56 and +84 days; a placebo window must not contain 26 Jul, the puncture day, the 10 and 16 Oct single-corner steps or the 1 Jul to 13 Aug fuel gap (not relevant to this channel, kept out for comparability is NOT required here: only the pressure steps and the puncture are protected). The placebo differences and their intervals are shown as the scale of the confounding; no ratio rule.
- **B (before-window stability):** the slope of the outcome on the date within the before window (a drift check), the distribution of the pressure before and after, and the share of drives with a snapshot.
- **C (integrity):** counts by class, thermal class and month, the 645-drive channel coverage, the drives dropped by each filter, the number with a missing outlier flag.

## 4. Falsification and acceptance
Known-answer tests before the first run (one CI-list line): a planted step in synthetic drives is recovered inside its interval through a planted drift and an ambient confounder; the window construction excludes the step day, the puncture day and the protected dates; counts-only below 5 days or 20 drives per side; the placebo windows avoid every protected date; the long-soak flag over the full master with the first drive dropped; the reading rule and the wrong-sign wording; the design MDE written first; the rank-deficiency counter; a foreign master stops; no dates, file names or per-drive rows in a result (privacy scan). An informative result and "not informative at this support" are both legitimate.

## 5. Not in scope
No dashboard binding or article use (needs a separate spec, Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off). No fuel basis (it has no drives in the gap). No change of any reconstruction constant or of M733 and M770. No claim about rolling resistance as a measured quantity. No re-validation of the pressure scale.

## 6. Questions for the Director
(a) Is the engine-off net battery energy per electric kilometre (`ev_kwh_per100km`) a defensible road-load-sensitive outcome for A1, given regeneration is netted and the stop-and-go structure dominates? (b) Is the expectation (-0.7 % to -4.4 %, lower end 0.35 % as the half-MDE threshold) defensible, and is the pass-through of 1.0 right for the EV mode? (c) Is the window design (28 days primary, step day and puncture excluded, 14 and 42 sensitivities, guarded placebo centres) sound, and is it acceptable that season (July versus August) is only partly controlled by ambient? (d) Should the before/after contrast use the observed per-drive pressure as the exposure (A2) as a co-primary instead of an exploratory? (e) Are the floors (1.0 km electric distance, 5 days and 20 drives per side) right, and is dropping month fixed effects right?
