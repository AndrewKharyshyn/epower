- Per-trip totals, same endpoints and same integration (rate integrated on its own valid sample times, dt capped at 5 s): counter delta V_cnt, raw vs originals: aggregate +0.008% (56.106 vs 56.101 L), max trip difference 0.0048 L. - Reading: the `raw/` re-export precision loss on these 107 files moves the fuel counter totals negligibly and the rate-integral totals by about a quarter of a percent in aggregate; the logged counter and the rate integral themselves differ by about +0.45% (originals: 56.354 vs 56.101 L), an exploratory pointer for FUEL-12, not its result (FUEL-12 uses its own eligibility and CI).
- Consequence for M337: FUEL-12/01/11 are shown on the `raw/` basis with this check cited as the F03 sensitivity (counter totals insensitive; rate-integral totals shifted by about 0.2%); the hash-passing/failing side-by-side remains "not a paired test". The 226 hash-failing files without fuel columns are irrelevant to M337. Method note for the tool: integrate the rate on its own valid timestamps, not on the all-row clock (an all-row clock with NaN-as-zero undercounts by about 90%).
