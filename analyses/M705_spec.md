Conditions kept: this spec records the deviation (1); C1 reports both rpm thresholds with no primary-only headline (2); each cadence stratum and the cold cohort are reported separately and never pooled across the failed B6 check, with speed zones for C1 (3); the reserved keys `oemCrossCheckC1` and `oemCrossCheckC2` are kept and C1b is a labelled sub-object of C1, each output with its own provenance (4); windows, ramp exclusions and the boundary convention come from M703 Rev 2 and are not tuned (5); a known-answer test case per window plus a not-comparable case (6); the blind audit reproduces each output independently including its own engine-on mask and returns pass or fail per output (7); the Director decides per output and the CHANGELOG has one decision line per C, a failure in one output blocks only that output (8); each output has its own language line (9); the market and calibration are recorded, no dashboard display and no article use without a separate spec step (10). ## 1. Question and non-claims
What share of logged engine-on time lies in (C1) a window of 150 rpm either side of the quoted 2,000 rpm best-BSFC point, (C1b) below 1,500 rpm outside the start and stop ramps, and (C2) a window of 100 rpm either side of the quoted 4,400 rpm maximum-output point? Non-claims: occupancy is not efficiency and not best-BSFC operation; a share is a description of logged rpm, not a validation or a test of the manufacturer; the manufacturer's 80 % statement (WLTC plus Extra High test mode, denominator not stated) is NOT comparable to an all-driving share and is carried as a fixed "not comparable" record, never as a target; the quoted points are quoted for the X-TRAIL VC-TURBO e-POWER and the market calibration (the 106 kW figure is stated for the domestic market) may differ from this vehicle (the vehicle's market is recorded in the block); the rpm is a generator-state proxy; one vehicle, 12 months; C1 and C1b are not blind (the `rpmDistribution` peak near 1,900 to 2,100 rpm, the `engineStateMachine` load-point range 1,400 to 2,300 rpm and the "low" band 400 to 1,400 rpm, and the turbo block's below-1,500 band were seen before this spec).

## 2. Data, grid and definitions (fixed before any result)
- Population: canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2 == False`), calendar day as the resampling cluster.
- Grid: the `engineStateMachine` 1 Hz grid (`compute_summary_arrays._esm_grid`: 1 s mean, last observation carried at most 3 s), columns rpm and speed (VCM speed through the speed-priority rule); NaN rpm is never engine-on and a NaN breaks a run.
- Engine-on: rpm > threshold, strictly; BOTH thresholds are computed and reported side by side: 400 (the engine-on rule of record, M546 option A) and 300 (the RPM-onset threshold of M701 and the detector of record); no primary-only headline.
- Window convention: a window `[lo, hi)` is lower-inclusive, upper-exclusive, on the 1 s grid values; "below 1,500 rpm" is `rpm < 1500` and "at or above" is `rpm >= 1500`.
- Share (the estimand): ratio of sums, seconds in the window divided by engine-on seconds (outside the ramp exclusion for C1b), pooled over the drives of a cell, time-weighted on the 1 s grid; day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws, calendar day = cluster); every share carries n (drives, days) and the seconds; a cell below the support floor (10 drives on 5 days) is "not estimable" with its counts.
- Cells: cadence stratum (fast, slow; `cadence_regime.csv`) and thermal cohort (cold, shoulder, warm; `seasonal_drive_master.csv`); fast and slow are NEVER pooled (the B6 check is failed and stays shown); a pooled-all row is shown only as a labelled descriptive row beside the strata. Speed zones for C1: the existing engine-on-by-speed zone edges (read from the payload `engineOnBySpeedRule` at implementation, not retyped), plus the pooled row.
- C1 (occupancy of a window around the quoted best-BSFC point): windows `[1850, 2150)` (primary), `[1900, 2100)` and `[1750, 2250)` (sensitivities), both thresholds, by speed zone and pooled.
- C1b (below-1,500 rpm share, descriptive): the share of engine-on seconds with rpm < 1500 outside the ramps; a ramp is the first r seconds of an engine-on run (after the onset) and the last r seconds of a run (before the stop), a run being a maximal contiguous engine-on run; r = 5 primary, r = 3 and r = 10 sensitivities, r = 0 (no exclusion) reported as context; no margin, no "consistent" label (a mismatch cannot be read: warm-up, cold start, low-SOC quiet starts and transients are expected below 1,500 rpm).
- C2 (occupancy of a window around the quoted maximum-output point): `[4300, 4500)` (primary), `[4200, 4600)` (sensitivity), both thresholds, descriptive; reported also as the count of drives and of seconds with any occupancy (a small share has a wide relative interval).
- Further sensitivities (all outcomes): without the first 60 s of a drive; reverse-consistent seconds (M698) excluded; slow-polled and fast-polled separately (already a stratum); the cold cohort separate (already a cell).

## 3. Cache and tooling
A per-drive compact histogram cache `rpm_window_cache/` (git-ignored, keyed by the raw-file sha256, the grid code hash and the variant; `tools/rpm_window_cache.py`): for each threshold in {300, 400} and ramp r in {0, 3, 5, 10} and each speed zone (plus "no speed"), the engine-on seconds per 50 rpm bin (0 to 6,000 and an overflow bin), with a with-first-60-s and a without-first-60-s variant. Every pre-registered edge (1,500; 1,750; 1,850; 1,900; 2,100; 2,150; 2,250; 4,200; 4,300; 4,500; 4,600) is a multiple of 50 rpm, so the bins reproduce the windows exactly; a window edge that is not a multiple of 50 is a hard stop. Aggregation never rereads raw CSVs. Tool: `tools/oem_crosscheck.py` (additive splice with an isolation guard, stamp via `carry_declared`, no ingestion stage until the Director approves one).

## 4. Outputs (additive payload)
Keys `oemCrossCheckC1` (with the labelled sub-object `c1b`) and `oemCrossCheckC2`. Each output carries: its estimand and window, both thresholds, strata and cohorts, speed zones (C1), n (drives, days, seconds), the interval, the sensitivities, the label of its own status ("descriptive"), the quoted statement ids with their source (sha256 prefix, PDF page, paraphrase) and the market note ("quoted for the X-TRAIL VC-TURBO; the market calibration may differ"), the fixed "not comparable" record for the 80 % statement, and the not-blind statement. No comparison against a manufacturer value is computed and no "consistent" label exists anywhere in the three outputs.

## 5. Falsifiers, accept and reject
These outputs are descriptive: there is no support or refutation of the manufacturer. Pre-registered failure conditions of the outputs themselves: F1 a share depends on the engine-on threshold (300 versus 400) by more than its interval (reported as threshold sensitivity, no tuning); F2 the C1 share changes sign of ordering across speed zones in a way the pooled share hides (reported by zone); F3 a cell below the support floor is "not estimable", never imputed; F4 the without-first-60-s or reverse-excluded variants move a share outside its interval (reported). A failing output (for example an audit mismatch) blocks only that output.

## 6. Tests and audit
Known-answer test `tests/synthetic/test_m705_rpm_windows.py` (in the CI python list): synthetic rpm traces with a planted share in each window (C1 for both thresholds and the three widths; C1b with ramps r = 0, 3, 5, 10 and runs shorter than 2r; C2), exact edge handling (rpm exactly at a window edge, exactly 400, exactly 1,500), NaN breaking a run, a not-comparable case (the 80 % record present and no comparison field), a drive with no speed (counted in the pooled row only). Blind audit: an auditor rebuilds the grid and its own engine-on mask from the raw CSVs and the definitions above (not from `tools/oem_crosscheck.py`), reproduces every output and returns pass or fail per output. The Director's results decision is per output; the CHANGELOG records one decision line per C.

## 7. Language
Rule `M703-oem-crosscheck-wording` (added in this milestone): forbids validates / confirms Nissan, BSFC, sweet spot, optimal, efficiency inferred from occupancy, "rated" without "quoted", assist torque, "fixed-point operation shown", 217 g/kWh attached to our data, "operates at best efficiency", "below 1500 confirmed", "hides turbo lag shown". Required wording: "occupancy of a window around the quoted point", "descriptive", "occupancy is not efficiency", "quoted for the X-TRAIL VC-TURBO; the market calibration may differ".

## 8. Risks and questions for the Director
R1 not blind (rpm distribution seen); R2 the window width is our choice around a source POINT (sensitivities cover it); R3 grid effects (1 s mean, 3 s carry) at ramps; R4 slow-polled drives smear short ramps; R5 cold cohort has few days; R6 pooled rows can hide stratum differences. Questions: (1) ratio of sums versus mean of per-drive shares as the estimand (ratio of sums chosen: time-weighted, matching the engine-on blocks); (2) should a pooled-all row be shown at all given B6 (chosen: labelled descriptive row beside strata); (3) the first-60-s sensitivity only, not an exclusion, for C1 and C2 (chosen); (4) one new tool and cache (`oem_crosscheck.py`, `rpm_window_cache/`) or an additive view on the existing `engine_on_blocks` sidecar (chosen: new cache, because the sidecar has no 50 rpm histogram and its state edges do not align with the windows); (5) is a stage `oem_crosscheck` (`verified:false`) wanted or none until a later ingestion.

## 9. Rev 1 (2026-11-16, Director review "revise"; overrides conflicting text above; frozen before any implementation)
Facts checked in the repository before writing this revision (design facts, not results): `rpmDistribution` has a mode at 1,475 to 1,525 rpm holding 3.7 % of all logged time and a mode at 1,975 to 2,025 rpm holding 24.8 % of all logged time (about 58 % of engine-on time); the engine rpm channel is logged on a 1 rpm grid in the sampled files (`signal_representation.json`, read again and recorded at implementation); the engine-on speed zones are the kernel constants `Z_ENGINE` with the payload labels 0-20, 20-40, 40-60, 60-80, 80-100, 100-120, 120+; the state-machine block covers 664 drives and the engine-on rule block 665.
1. No cross-stratum pooled row (condition 3 of the bundling ruling): the pooled-all row of section 2 is withdrawn. Within each cadence stratum an all-cohort row is allowed, the cold cohort is separate (never pooled with warm), and no between-cell contrast is drawn anywhere. Rows appear in a fixed table order with no highlighting.
2. Market and calibration: the vehicle's market is "unknown (not stated by the owner; `summary_config.json` carries no market field)" until the owner supplies it; the 106 kW at 4,400 rpm is stated for the domestic market and the Article 10 sheet is the European-market sheet; every output carries "quoted for the X-TRAIL VC-TURBO; the market calibration may differ" and the market field as "unknown" or the owner's value.
3. Bins are 25 rpm (not 50): every pre-registered edge and the boundary sensitivities are multiples of 25. Because the engine speed has a mode at 1,475 to 1,525 rpm, the below-1,500 share is boundary-dominated: C1b adds the boundary sensitivities `rpm < 1475`, `rpm < 1525` and the share in `[1475, 1525)` beside `rpm < 1500`; C1 and C2 add edge shifts of plus or minus 25 and plus or minus 50 rpm of the window edges; the rpm resolution is recorded from `signal_representation.json`. Stated in the block: C1 is predictable from the already-seen rpm distribution (the mode at 1,975 to 2,025 rpm), it is not new evidence; C1b is not blind.
4. Ramps (C1b): runs are maximal contiguous engine-on runs on the grid; a run of length at most 2r seconds is fully excluded and counted; the first r and last r seconds of every longer run are excluded; a run edge that is censored by the drive start, the drive end or a NaN gap is not an observed onset or stop, but its r edge seconds are excluded as well (conservative) and the number of censored edges is counted and reported.
5. Grid: the state-machine grid (1 s mean, last observation carried at most 3 s) differs from the engine-on rule grid (carry 15 s) and from the M543 grid: stated. Per-drive engine-on seconds on this grid are compared with the engine-on rule sidecar (`rpm > 400`); the per-drive differences are reported (disclosure, not a gate); the 664 versus 665 drives are explained by the drive without a usable rpm grid (named in the block).
6. Zone edges are read from the kernel constant `Z_ENGINE` and asserted equal to the payload labels (a mismatch is a hard stop).
7. Gate: for every drive and every pre-registered edge the histogram sums equal the direct mask counts (computed from the same grid by an independent code path); any difference is a hard stop.
8. The `c1b` sub-object carries its own provenance and audit status and a withheld path: "withheld: audit failed" replaces the figures if its audit fails, without withholding C1 or C2.
9. Language per output: three lines (C1, C1b, C2), each with the required wording of section 7. "BSFC" and "217 g/kWh" are allowed only in the phrase "quoted best-BSFC point" and in the source-record fields (implemented as a context rule of the language gate, not a blanket allow).
10. Audit: the grid, the speed priority, the engine-on mask and the runs are rebuilt from the raw CSVs with pandas from the written definitions, with no import of `_esm_grid` or `drive_raw_cache` or the tool; pre-set tolerances: per-drive seconds exact, shares 1e-9, interval endpoints 0.5 percentage points; the audit returns pass or fail per output.
11. C2 zero cells read "no occupancy observed in n drives on d days", never "never".
12. Cache key: raw-file sha256, grid code hash, bin width, zone edges, threshold and ramp sets, reverse-mask version, and the variant; no cohort, date or stratum enters the cache; frames come through `drive_raw_cache`.
Answers to the questions: (1) ratio of sums accepted, the per-drive mean is optional context with no headline; (2) no cross-stratum pooled row; (3) the first-60-s variant is a sensitivity only; (4) the new git-ignored cache is justified (no existing block holds a fine histogram; the gated `engine_on_blocks` sidecar is not touched); (5) no ingestion stage in M705, decided after the per-output results decision.
