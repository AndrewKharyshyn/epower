## 1. What "engine-on" means today (the problem)
- 300 rpm family (1 Hz grid, duration weighted): rpmDistribution bins (>= 300), rpmOptBand, rpmModeCentres, rpmFinding (superseded), engineOnBySpeed, turbo blocks, the engine-start detector (> 300, run >= 2 s), and your regeneration engine-off proxy (<= 300, owner decision 2026-10-14 (g)).
- 400 rpm family: the pipeline's own EV state and master `engine_on_pct` (eng_rpm > 400, "the same threshold"), engineRuntime (M512, > 400 on RPM-supported intervals), rpmSyncPoint, GTR interval block, capacity_basis, fuel_analytics2, engineStateMachine (>= 400 boundary).
- Not rpm rules, kept separate by name: generator-active (reconstructed Pgen > 0; the `simultaneity` engine-on leaf), the engine-start trigger (> 800 after <= 100), event classifiers (> 1200, > 2000).
- The two families differ by 2.325 h of 99.87 h (B1 grid >= 300 versus the M512 numerator, interval > 400). Decomposition in both orders: threshold first, then integration: threshold 1.59 h and integration 0.74 h; integration first, then threshold: integration 0.49 h and threshold 1.83 h; residual 0. The pure grid threshold step (>= 300 to > 400) is 1.587 h = 1.589 % of engine-on seconds (day-clustered interval [1.458, 1.746] %); the integration step on > 400 is 0.751 % [0.642, 0.872] %.

## 2. What the data say about the 300-400 rpm band
The band [300, 400) holds 1.572 h = 1.57 % of engine-on time. 62 % of its seconds sit next to an engine-off second (spin-up / run-down of the logged rpm) and 99 % carry positive app-calculated engine load, so the band is real loaded rotation, not noise; it is slightly larger in slow-polled drives (1.68 % versus 1.23 % in fast-polled). The forward-fill hold limit (native rows / grid seconds) matters only at zero: no fill drops 1 Hz engine-on hours by 21 % of B1, almost all in slow-polled drives (29 % missing seconds versus 2.8 %), so zero is not a usable candidate; 5 versus 15 changes them by 0.4 %. A hysteresis rule (on >= 400, off < 300) gives 99.77 to 100.46 h: within 1 % of the >= 300 hours (99.87 h) and 1.5 to 2.2 % above plain > 400. Seconds at exactly 400 rpm in the corpus: 0.015 h (53 s).
