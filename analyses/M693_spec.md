# M693 spec (Rev 1): calibrated location sets for the change points (follow-up F1 of M676; payload only, no display)

Status: pre-registered before any result of this analysis exists. Follow-up F1 of `analyses/M675_spec.md` section 11: the M676 wild-bootstrap location interval covered a planted step in only 0.624 of 200 replicates (flag below 0.85), so every location is labelled uncalibrated and nothing is displayed. This milestone asks whether ANY candidate construction reaches the pre-registered coverage at the real structure; the answer may be no, and a negative result is recorded as such.

## 1. Question and non-claims
Is there a location set for a break of a drive-level series, at the real drives-per-day structure, whose planted-step coverage is at least 0.90 in the core scenarios (including three planted steps and serially dependent noise) and not vacuous? Non-claims: a calibrated set is a statistical statement about the date of a break in a logged series; it is not the date of a software update, not a cause, not a degradation or capacity statement; the reference date (2026-08-25) is not used anywhere in this milestone (it enters only `compare_reference` after the primary hash, as in M676). No display, no article figure.

## 2. Candidate constructions (all conditional on the BIC-selected segmentation unless stated)
C0 (reference only, not admissible): the M676 day-clustered wild Rademacher residual bootstrap at fixed K (reproduces the 0.624 coverage; this is the known-answer link to the M676 study).
C1 Unconditional wild bootstrap: the day-clustered Rademacher residual bootstrap in which K is REFITTED by BIC in every draw; the interval of break j is the 2.5 to 97.5 percentile range of the location of the estimated break nearest to the fitted break j in each draw (draws in which no break lies within the nearest-neighbour rule are counted as unmatched and reported). 200 draws per replicate in the study (4000 in the payload).
C2 Profile likelihood-ratio set with cluster calibration (Bai 1997 limit): for break j, with its neighbours fixed at their estimates, the set of feasible locations tau with (RSS(tau) - RSS(tau_hat)) / (sigma2_hat x lambda_hat) <= c, where c is the 95 % quantile of 2 x max(E1, E2) for two independent unit exponentials (c = 2 ln(1 / (1 - sqrt(0.95))) = 7.35), sigma2_hat is the pooled residual variance of the fitted segmentation and lambda_hat the day-cluster variance inflation (variance of the day sums of residuals divided by the sum over days of the number of drives times sigma2_hat, floored at 1). The set is an interval (min and max of the accepted feasible locations).
C3 not pursued (day-block subsampling): stated reason, a rate-of-convergence assumption that the 633-drive series cannot support; recorded so that the candidate list of M675 section 11 is closed explicitly.

## 3. Coverage study (offline; `python tools/changepoints.py --coverage3 OUT.json`)
Planted structures at the real drives-per-day layout (X2 clean drives, n = 633, 126 days, feasibility rule of M675). Core scenarios, each with 200 replicates (seed 42):
S1 one step of 1.5 sd at n/2, iid noise; S2 the same with AR(1) noise (phi = 0.3); S3 three steps of 1.0, -1.0 and 1.5 sd at n/4, n/2, 3n/4, iid; S4 the same with AR(1) noise (phi = 0.3).
Descriptive scenarios (100 replicates): D1 one step of 0.75 sd; D2 one step of 3 sd; D3 one step at n/4 and D4 at 3n/4 (1.5 sd); D5 a day random effect (intraclass correlation 0.3); D6 t(3) noise scaled to unit variance.
Coverage definition (two versions, both reported): (a) conditional: replicates in which BIC selects the planted K (rate reported), a planted break is covered if its location lies in the interval of the estimated break with the smallest distance to it; (b) unconditional: every replicate; a planted break is covered if some estimated break has an interval containing it, where estimated breaks are matched to planted ones by smallest distance (a planted break with no estimated break within the segmentation is uncovered). Per-break coverage is averaged over the planted breaks of the replicate and then over replicates. Median interval width in drives and in days is reported per construction and scenario.

## 4. Gate and selection (pre-registered; no tuning, no new construction after the result)
A construction is ADMISSIBLE if in every core scenario its unconditional coverage (b) is at least 0.90 and at most 0.99, and its conditional coverage (a) is at least 0.90. Among admissible constructions the selected one has the smallest median width in the core scenarios (ties: the construction listed first). If none is admissible: no calibrated set exists; the block keeps the uncalibrated labels, records the study and the closest construction as "not admissible, reference only", and the Director is asked whether to close F1 for this series length. A construction may be admissible in a scenario subset only for display purposes if the Director rules so after seeing the result (not pre-approved).

## 5. Outputs
If a construction is selected: additive keys in the `changePoints` block per series and per break: `locationSetCalibrated` (days and drives), `locationSetConstruction`, `locationSetCoverageRef` (the study file, scenarios, rates), `locationSetCalibrated: true` only for a series whose estimated K was studied (K = 1 and K = 3); other K carries "no coverage study". The M676 keys `nominal95IntervalUncalibrated` stay unchanged (isolation guard: only the new keys appear). If no construction is selected: a `locationSetStudy` record only. The study file is `analyses/M693_coverage.json` (offline, with the design fingerprint of the real structure so a stale study is detected, as in M680).

## 6. Validity and tests
V1 known-answer test `tests/synthetic/test_m693_location_sets.py`: C2 on a constructed series with a planted step of known size recovers the analytic profile set (hand-computable RSS differences), c = 7.35 reproduced, lambda_hat equals 1 for iid and exceeds 1 for a planted day effect, C1 refits K per draw (a case where K changes across draws), a series with no break returns no set; C0 reproduces the M676 interval for a fixed seed; the coverage routine on a tiny structure runs deterministically (a 3-replicate version in CI for correctness only); isolation deep-diff adds only the new keys; fingerprint staleness detected. V2 determinism (seed 42). V3 language rule extension: no "95 % interval" wording for an uncalibrated set; a calibrated set is "location set with simulated coverage (scenarios)", never "confidence interval for the update date".

## 7. Risks and out of scope
R1 time cost: C1 at 200 draws x 200 replicates x four core scenarios is about one hour of compute; the study runs in the background. R2 the planted noise models are not the real noise (the real series have drifts and heteroscedasticity); the scenarios are a necessary, not sufficient, check, and the display (a later milestone with its own spec) must say "simulated coverage in the stated scenarios". R3 K = 3 with the fitted X2 configuration (observed effect sizes) is follow-up F2 and is not claimed here; the S3 and S4 steps are planted, not fitted. R4 series other than X2 have other drives-per-day structures; a set is shown only for a series whose structure the study covered (X2 here; others carry "no coverage study" until each is run). Out of scope: any display, any reading of dates as events, the reference-date comparison.

## 8. Director review (2026-11-16, verdict revise; no change of question) and how each is applied (Rev 1.1)
These override the matching text above. The critical value c = 7.35 is confirmed (the limit of [RSS(tau) - RSS(tau_hat)] / sigma2 in the mean-shift model is 2 x max(E1, E2)).
1. C2 variance correction: lambda_hat = [sum_d S_d^2 + sum_d S_d S_{d+1}] / sum_i e_i^2, with S_d the UNCENTRED day sum of residuals and a Bartlett weight at lag 1 day, floored at 1 (a conservative departure from Bai); sigma2_hat and lambda_hat are computed on the two segments next to break j; their distribution is reported per scenario.
2. C2: the set is the convex hull of the accepted set; the share of non-contiguous accepted sets is reported.
3. Matching (a, b and C1): a ONE-TO-ONE, ORDER-PRESERVING assignment of estimated to planted breaks that minimises the total distance; the matching window is the cell between the midpoints of adjacent estimated breaks; one estimated break never covers two planted breaks; a planted break outside its window is uncovered. C1: if more than 5 % of draws are unmatched, the interval is 'undefined' and counts as not covering.
4. Vacuity bound: admissible only if the median width in S3 and S4 is <= n/8 drives (half the planted spacing) and the sets of adjacent breaks overlap in <= 10 % of replicates; the false-break rate is reported.
5. Conditional coverage needs at least 50 replicates with the correct K, otherwise it is not estimable and the construction is not admissible. Wilson 95 % bounds are reported for every coverage; admissibility also requires the lower Wilson bound >= 0.85.
6. Deleted: "A construction may be admissible in a scenario subset only for display purposes if the Director rules so after seeing the result". Any subset needs a new spec revision and is labelled post hoc.
7. Selection metric: the mean over the core scenarios of the median width in days.
8. Output keys: `locationSet` (days and drives) and `locationSetCoverageStatus` ('simulated coverage passed (S1 to S5, X2, K)' or 'no coverage study'); no boolean named `locationSetCalibrated`.
9. Core set: D5 (day random effect, intraclass correlation 0.3) moves into the core set as S5-effect, and a semi-synthetic core scenario S5 is added: the real X2 residuals from the fitted segmentation, circularly shifted by whole days, plus the planted steps of S3.
10. 'Outside the studied structure' (no set shown, status 'no coverage study'): the fitted step at break j is smaller than the smallest step for which the construction was admissible, or the break is closer to a series edge than the D3/D4 positions, or the breaks are spaced more tightly than in S3, or K is not in {1, 3}.
11. C1 payload B equals the study B (200 draws), or the difference is stated in the block.
Missing items added: a blind audit reproduces the C2 coverage table before any key is written; the feasibility rule is cited exactly (minimum segment 8 drives and 3 distinct calendar days, M675 section 2); the study fingerprint carries n, number of days, a hash of the drives-per-day vector, the feasibility rule, K and B. Language: 'location set with simulated coverage (stated scenarios, X2, K)'; never 'calibrated confidence interval', 'update date' or 'measured'. Limits: simulated coverage only (real drift and heteroscedasticity are covered only through S5); asymmetric regime variances break the symmetric Bai limit.
