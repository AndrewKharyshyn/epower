# M499 spec (Rev 2): temperature-specification sensitivity of the resistance-proxy trend (powerFade S1) and a tipping-point row for residual temperature misspecification

Status: Rev 2, written 2026-10-24 BEFORE any M499 computation. Rev 1 was reviewed by the research-director (ruling "revise", eight required revisions, all folded in below). Origin: independent audit 2026-10-23, finding PB-05 (the resistance-proxy time trend is aliased with season inside a 5.1-month warm-season window: one degree C of uncaptured temperature effect equals about 4.4 months of slope) and PB-04 (the locked equivalence bound is a scale transfer). The bound itself is NOT touched here: it is locked (M108, 2026-08-11) and its change is an owner decision. Files: `audit_package_2026-10-23/pub/findings.csv`.

## Question
Does the sign, size or interval of the released primary resistance-proxy slope (powerFade S1, `vreg_R_pack_mohm` against months, with temperature, peak current and the two density covariates) depend on how temperature enters the model, and how large an error in the temperature coefficient would be needed to move the slope to zero?

## Estimand and data (frozen)
Primary design and population are those of `powerfade_equivalence.s1_rows` (`have_d`: clean complete cases with density; frozen basis: `drive_master.csv` MD5 5556a0c6c8bcde0f6c64b8b9655208ed, `seasonal_drive_master.csv` MD5 0018476d259a6e17102708d0f80dbdc3, 274 drives, 98 days): y = `vreg_R_pack_mohm`; months = days / 30.44 from the minimum date of the clean complete cases; covariates peak discharge current (`peak_I_discharge`), effective density `_de` and coverage `_dc`, and the temperature term of each specification below. The estimand is the coefficient of months (mOhm per month), a conditional association within a 5-month warm-season window, not a degradation rate and not a state of health.
The locked equivalence bound (0.5439 mOhm/month) and the TOST verdict are not recomputed or changed.

## Specifications (frozen)
T0 released: linear `T_pack_mean_avg` (reproduces `powerFadeEquivalence.s1`: slope 0.6564, the check below).
T1 quadratic in `T_pack_mean_avg`, centred at the mean of the fitted sample.
T2 piecewise-linear in `T_pack_mean_avg`: a continuous hinge basis (T, max(T-k1,0), max(T-k2,0)) with knots at the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of the S1 population (drive level).
T3 `pack_temp_start_c` (pack temperature at key-on, `seasonal_drive_master.csv`) instead of the drive mean; complete-case handling as T4 (drives without it are dropped and T0 is refitted on the same subset, paired).
T4 `ambient_time_mean_c` (`seasonal_drive_master.csv`) instead of the pack temperature, on drives where it exists; T0 is refitted on the same subset (paired comparison); the subset size and its overlap with the S1 population are reported. A subset specification (T3, T4, T5) is decisive only if it has at least 150 drives on at least 50 days; otherwise it is reported as non-decisive. If the paired T0 on the subset already excludes zero, that is attributed to the subset and not to the temperature form.
T5 `T_pack_mean_avg` and `ambient_time_mean_c` together (same subset as T4).
T5 also reports the correlation between the pack and ambient temperature terms and the variance inflation factor of both. No other specification is run; none is added after the results are seen.

## Method (frozen)
1. Fidelity stop: T0 reproduces the published S1 slope (0.6564 to 4 decimals), n = 274 drives, 98 days, the temperature coefficient bT = -2.868 mOhm per degC (3 decimals) and the published S1 day-bootstrap 95 % interval of `powerFade`/`powerFadeEquivalence`; otherwise STOP.
2. For each specification: the months coefficient by OLS and two intervals: (a) the day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws, `tools/day_bootstrap.ols_boot_slopes`), which GOVERNS every decision below; (b) the ISO-week-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws; ISO week = `isocalendar()` year-week; the number of weeks is computed from the data, about 23, and with that few clusters and up to nine parameters it probably under-covers), reported as a labelled sensitivity only. Draws that cannot be fitted (`min_unique_t`, `cond_max`) are counted; more than 1 % failed draws makes that interval non-decisive. Report slope, both intervals, n drives, n days, n weeks and whether each interval includes zero.
3. Tipping row (deterministic, for T0 design): let tau be the coefficient of months in the regression of `T_pack_mean_avg` on months and the other covariates (the within-design temperature drift, degC per month) and bT the released temperature coefficient (mOhm per degC). The slope after replacing bT by bT + d is slope0 - d * tau. Report tau, the slope for d in {-1.0, -0.5, +0.5, +1.0} mOhm per degC (also expressed as a fraction of |bT| and of the bT day-bootstrap half-width), and the d at which the slope is zero (reported as undefined if |tau| is below 0.05 degC per month). Here tau is the months coefficient of `T_pack_mean_avg` regressed on months and ALL the other covariates (peak current, `_de`, `_dc`) over the same rows, so that slope(bT + d) = slope0 - d * tau holds exactly. This is an arithmetic sensitivity, not an estimate of d; a scalar d covers only a linear coefficient error, nonlinearity is left to T1 and T2.
4. Spec dispersion: min and max of the slopes over T0-T5 (T4 and T5 compared with the paired T0 on their subset).

## Decision rules and wording (frozen)
Decisions use the day-bootstrap interval of each decisive specification (12 intervals are computed in total, 6 day and 6 week; the rule "any specification excludes zero" leans conservative and no multiplicity adjustment is applied).
- Robust: every decisive specification has a day interval that includes zero. Statement: "no temperature specification tested gives an interval that excludes zero; the trend is not established".
- Dependent: at least one decisive specification has a day interval that excludes zero. Statement: "the conclusion depends on how temperature enters the model", listing the specifications; the resistance trend remains unresolved and no slope may be promoted.
- Tipping-sensitive (reported in addition to either outcome): the slope falls to zero for |d| within the stated grid, or d_zero lies within the day-bootstrap interval of bT.
- Every statement carries the window length and the planning reading "defer trend claims until a full year, including winter, is logged" (a planning reading, not a result). No slope of any specification is described as a degradation rate or as rising/falling. No new equivalence verdict is computed; the locked-bound verdict is unchanged.
- Falsification clause: a single 5-month warm window cannot separate months from every seasonal factor, so these tests cannot establish or exclude season aliasing. If every slope lies within 0.25 of the T0 day half-width of the T0 slope AND d in [-1, +1] shifts the slope by less than a quarter of the T0 day half-width, the result is reported as "temperature-specification sensitivity not detected". Either outcome is reported as is.
