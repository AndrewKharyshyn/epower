## 1. Question and non-claims

How does the power that the BMS reports as possible (input possible power and output possible power channels) vary with pack temperature and SoC, how much logged time and how many independent days support each cell, and how often does the reported value sit at the logged maximum (63.70 kW or above) that may be a saturated byte? The channel is logged by the Car Scanner ELM OBD2 app (logged / app-calculated). The thesis context is the buffer role of the battery: the map describes the BMS-reported limit the buffer operates under, in particular for winter charging (regeneration) as winter data arrive.

Non-claims: no battery capability, no true power limit, no "the BMS limited regeneration", no degradation or ageing statement, no causal temperature effect (season, route, load and period move together), no extrapolation outside the observed temperature and SoC range. Wording: "BMS-reported possible power" always. Values below the ceiling are reported limits, not proof that the limit bound the demand (braking or driving demand can be lower than the limit).

## 2. Data and unit handling

Input per drive from the raw CSV through the existing M627 reader `compute_summary_arrays._hdrm_pp_series` (header-based, either column name; hp converted with the metric factor 0.73549875; kW as logged; unit gate and both-pairs check of M626/M627 unchanged). Eras: hp (2026-05-15 to 2026-09-30) and kW (2026-09-30 onward); never pooled across eras without the era shown (different logger regime and grid: 0.1 kW in the hp era, 0.05 kW in the kW era). Pack temperature (Rev 1.2, stated exactly after the blind audit found the Rev 1.1 sentence ambiguous): for each possible-power sample, the mean over the available probes T1..T4 of the nearest valid reading (-40 to 90 C) of that probe within 5 s of the sample (probes without a reading in the window are left out of the mean; no probe in the window drops the sample). SoC: the nearest valid logged HV-battery SoC reading (0 to 100 %) within 5 s; the pipeline's SoC spike filter is NOT applied. The logged-power-above-limit check pairs the possible-power sample with the nearest valid I (|I| < 900 A) and the nearest valid V (200 to 450 V), each within 2 s, charge power = I x V / 1000 > 0.1 kW, over all channel samples of the analysed drives (not restricted by the temperature / SoC requirement). Drives: canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` / `ens_invalid` excluded, explicit-True reading as in M646) with the channel present (432 of 665 at M627; 429 analysed).

Sample rule: a possible-power sample is used at its own timestamp (no hold across gaps longer than 15 s; the time weight of a sample is min(gap to the next sample of the channel, 15 s); the last sample of a drive carries the median gap of that drive, at most 15 s); samples where the pack temperature or the SoC is missing at that time (nearest valid within 5 s) are dropped and counted.

## 3. Quantities (all per era x direction x cell)

Cells: pack temperature bins of 5 C with edges at multiples of 5 C anchored at 0 C (fixed, they never move between ingestions; an end bin below the support floor is listed as not shown, not merged); SoC bins `[40, 50, 55, 60, 63, 66, 69, 72, 100]` with an explicit "below 40 %" row (the display edges of owner decision (b) of 2026-10-14; display bands, not identified BMS thresholds).

Per cell: supported time (s, time-weighted samples), drives, independent days, effective days (as in M648: inverse of the sum of squared day shares), time-weighted median and 5th / 95th percentile of the reported power (kW), the **logged-maximum share** (time share of samples at or above 63.70 kW, the same byte-maximum flag for both directions; deviation from Rev 1, which assumed an output ceiling: the output limit is not constant, kW era 41.5 to 63.75 kW, so for output the flag is a logged-maximum marker, not a ceiling), and the **below-maximum** median and 5th percentile (conditional on the reported value being below the logged maximum; descriptive, selected on the outcome, so composition can drive a trend; no censoring correction). Where the median or the 95th percentile of a cell is at or above 63.70 kW it is printed as "at the logged maximum (>=63.70 kW)", not as a number, and a zero-width interval is flagged degenerate, not precise. Support ladder (ordered): **not shown** below 10 drives on 5 days and 600 s (reason printed); **exploratory** when those floors are met and the effective days are below 10 (point and interval only, no reading class); **supported** with effective days >= 10. Only supported cells feed the reading ladder, the monotone rule and the winter rule. Intervals: day-clustered percentile bootstrap on calendar day, seed 42, 4000 draws, resampling the cell's OWN days (a thin cell cannot be emptied by days that are not in it), for the median, the 5th and 95th percentile and the logged-maximum share of every shown cell; the median-of-medians sensitivity uses the same day resampling with the per-drive medians re-taken in each draw; time-weighted quantiles are computed on samples weighted by their duration; a draw that leaves a quantity undefined is dropped and the drop rate is reported (more than 1 % makes the cell "not estimable").

Pre-registered descriptive reading ladder per direction (no test, no significance wording): (a) the ceiling share by pack-temperature bin at SoC 55 to 66 % (the band with the most time), (b) the below-ceiling 5th percentile by temperature bin, (c) the temperature range in which every pack-temperature bin of a row has a supported cell. A monotone-in-temperature statement applies to the below-maximum median and to the logged-maximum share (named quantities), within ONE era only, and is allowed only if the supported bins are contiguous (no unsupported gap), the order holds in every pair of neighbouring bins (a tie counts as not ordered) and the day-clustered intervals of the end bins do not overlap; otherwise "no ordered relation is established". No cross-era temperature contrast is drawn: the hp era is May to September and the kW era September onward, so era and season are confounded and any cold bin exists only in the kW era.

## 4. Saturation check (D-04), descriptive only
Is 63.75 kW = 255 x 0.25 a saturated byte? A temperature-independent design cap at the same value would give the same pattern, so the pattern alone cannot identify saturation. Script-written descriptions: the share of input samples exactly on the era maximum, the number of distinct values within 1 kW below the maximum, the maximum run length of the constant maximum, and the classes "maximum reached in every supported bin" / "maximum absent in some supported bin" / "undetermined" (a zero share in a high-SoC bin is also expected under a saturated byte). The one check that can separate a censored value from a limit that is actually imposed: the time share of samples where the logged I x V charge power exceeds the reported input limit while that limit is at the logged maximum versus below it (the M627 utilisation maximum is 1.171, i.e. the logged power exceeded the reported limit). The check is reported as a descriptive pair of shares with day-clustered intervals; it never converts the value into a physical statement.

## 5. Winter evidence rule

The map is recomputed at every ingestion over all drives (no snapshot constants). The coldest supported bin and its support (drives, days, effective days) are printed; a winter statement about the logged input possible power (which covers generator charging as well as regeneration; the channel does not separate them) is allowed only for bins that reach "supported"; below that the block prints "not observed at this support". Cold bins with less support are listed with their time and days, no value.

## 6. Method disagreement and falsification

Two computations of the same cell value are reported side by side: the time-weighted sample estimate (primary) and the per-drive median-of-medians (sensitivity). A cell where they differ by more than the larger of their interval half-widths and one logged grid step (0.1 kW in the hp era, 0.05 kW in the kW era) is flagged "estimator-dependent". A cadence-regime sensitivity (tools/regime_reader.py strata) is reported beside the primary; samples dropped for a missing pack temperature or SoC are counted by era and logger regime (the missingness may not be random). The analysis is falsified (no map shown) if: the unit gate fails in either era; the both-pairs check fails; the supported cells cover less than 25 % of the channel time of the analysed drives (raised from 10 % at the Director's review); or the known-answer test (section 7) fails. A change of the era-specific ceiling between ingestions is reported, never silently merged.
