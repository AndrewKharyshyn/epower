## 1. Questions and what the data can support
- **Q-A (drive-level association):** is the drive's settled tyre pressure (mean of the four corners, kPa) associated with fuel L/100 km (logged / app-calculated) and with the model-derived gross traction energy per 100 km, after adjusting for ambient temperature, speed, class, distance, buffer state? Physics: rolling resistance falls with pressure; the pressure exponent of the rolling coefficient is bracketed -0.2 to -0.5 (assumed range from tyre literature: Crr proportional to p^(-0.2...-0.5)); rolling resistance is 0.25 to 0.45 of the traction energy at mixed speeds (assumed); a hybrid's fuel passes only 0.6 to 1.0 of the traction change (regeneration and buffer). The pre-registered expectation per +10 % pressure is therefore a fuel change of -(0.2 to 0.5) x 10 % x (0.25 to 0.45) x (0.6 to 1.0), i.e. between about -0.3 % and -2.3 %; the reading rule uses the LOWER magnitude (about -0.3 %): informative only if the MDE (2.8 x bootstrap SD of the slope) is at most half of it, else "not informative at this support".
- **Q-B (before/after, the 26 Jul top-up):** all four corners were raised on 26 Jul 2026 (+19 to +38 kPa, +10 to +16 %: FL 242.7 to 280.9, FR 242.7 to 272.6, RL 225.1 to 249.5, RR 225.1 to 246.5). Compare fuel and traction energy per 100 km on drives in a window before and after the step (windows of 14 and 28 days, drives of 5 km or more, covariate-adjusted, ambient-matched), with a placebo step date (displaced by 14 and 28 days where no pressure step occurred). Season, route and period move together with the step: descriptive, labelled "before/after look", no effect wording; the 10 and 16 Oct single-corner steps are counts only.
- **Q-C (coast-down feasibility gate, no estimate):** rolling resistance acts most directly on the deceleration of the coasting car (zero drive torque, no braking), but the logger has no gear, no brake and no road grade in all drives, and speed is logged at 1 s and rounded. The milestone only reports whether enough clean coasting events exist (speed 40 to 90 km/h, no brake signal if logged, torque near zero for 5 s or more, consecutive speed samples, |grade| below a bound from the FABDEM estimate only as a screen, never a covariate in winter estimators) and how many per pressure tertile; no deceleration-based estimate is produced unless the pre-registered gate passes (at least 150 events on 40 days in each outer pressure tertile); if it passes, a follow-up spec is written.

## 2. Constraints
- Report-only. `drive_master.csv` MD5 d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98 asserted before and after (a different master stops, exit 3, nothing written); `fuel_recon_master.csv`, `analyses/M733_tyre_snapshots.csv`, the store manifest sha256 pinned; no winter-frozen file (M726); no absolute cloud odometer values. Outputs `analyses/M770_*`: aggregates only, no per-drive rows, dates or coordinates (the committed M733 snapshot table is the only per-drive input; step dates are the owner's own top-up and are named as "26 Jul 2026 all-corner top-up" in the spec only; the result file carries them as relative day offsets).
- Statistics: calendar-day cluster bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); every estimate with n (drives, days) and an MDE; ONE confirmatory estimand (A1 below), everything else exploratory; a design-based sizing (`design_mde`: pressure SD, intraclass correlation by day, effective days) is written before any estimate; the physical expectation and the reading rule above are fixed before any result. No equivalence test: a null is "not informative", never "no pressure effect". Proxy errors (a settled snapshot is not the pressure during every drive; a drive's snapshot is taken at a median of a few minutes into the drive: the pressure rises with warm-up) attenuate the slope; the settled-ambient and minutes-into-drive covariates are included.
- Language: pressure "estimated from the cloud tyre-pressure channel, scale accepted by the owner from manual gauge control"; fuel "logged / app-calculated"; traction "model-derived"; never "measured rolling resistance", never "effect of pressure" for Q-A or Q-B; "associated with" and "before/after look".

## 3. Estimands
- **A1 (confirmatory):** slope of fuel L/100 km on the mean four-corner pressure, per +10 kPa and per +10 % of the median pressure, covariate-adjusted: ambient (settled ambient), speed (mean moving speed and its square), drive class (4), thermal class, log distance, net battery energy per 100 km (buffer), first-drive-of-day, minutes into the drive of the snapshot. Eligible: canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` false), fuel basis, snapshot present, distance at least 5 km (2 and 10 km sensitivities as point estimates). n drives, n days, MDE, `design_mde` before the estimate.
- **A2 (exploratory):** the same slope for gross traction, generator, battery-to-traction and regeneration-to-battery energy per 100 km (labels as in M769) and for the regeneration share; no verdict wording.
- **A3 (exploratory):** the axle difference: front minus rear pressure and left-right asymmetry (M733 E4) as covariates one at a time; counts only below 100 drives and 30 days per cell.
- **B1 (before/after look):** fuel and traction energy per 100 km before versus after the 26 Jul step, windows 14 and 28 days, adjusted difference with a day-clustered interval; a placebo date; the supporting n; a seasonal caveat; the difference in pressure actually observed on those drives (the snapshots) is reported so the "dose" is known.
- **C (integrity):** pressure coverage by month and class, the puncture-window exclusion, the 6 steps listed in M733 E3 as counts, snapshot timing (minutes into the drive) distribution, the share of drives with pressure at the 'settled' definition.
- **G (feasibility gate, Q-C):** number of coasting events by pressure tertile and by day; the gate result (pass or fail by the stated rule); no deceleration estimate.

## 4. Falsification and acceptance
Known-answer tests (before the first run, one CI-list line): a planted pressure slope in synthetic drives is recovered inside its interval with a planted ambient confounder removed; the design MDE and the reading rule apply the lower magnitude; the before/after step design recovers a planted step and a placebo step shows no signal; the coast-down gate counts planted coasting segments exactly and refuses grade-contaminated or braking segments; a foreign master stops; no per-drive rows, dates or coordinates in a result (privacy scan). Control: at zero planted effect the placebo contains zero in at least 95 % of simulated datasets (a coverage check in the test). An 'informative' reading is a legitimate outcome, as is 'not informative at this support'.
