## 1. Quantities (per step, same OBD window as M757: the step [t_k + 16 s, t_k+1 + 16 s], the frozen 300 to 900 m steps)
- **Q1 regeneration bus energy** E_regen (kJ): the integral of the negative part of the HV bus power (battery current times pack voltage, sign convention as the published regeneration block and `recon_engine` documentation; the tool states it) over the step, all steps (no fuel channel needed). Reported per km (kWh per 100 km) and as the share of the total regeneration bus energy by class (km-weighted, day-clustered).
- **Q2 step-based apparent recovery**: E_regen over the energy released by braking and descent, A = max(0, -(kinetic energy change) - m g dh) with the ASSUMED class mass (1950 kg, labelled), restricted to steps with A at least 20 kJ; reported by class with the M753 mass scenarios (S1c, S2c, S3) as sensitivities. It is NOT the published `regenDisplayV2` quantity (event-based, kinetic only, temperature by SOC cells); the result states that and reports the control below.
- **Q3 traction energy distribution** (reconstruction, fuel basis; model-derived, never measured): positive and negative traction energy per km by class, the share of the negative part, and the energy balance residual.
- **Q4 battery charge**: net HV bus energy per km and the SOC change per km by class, and the share of positive bus energy that comes from the generator (reconstruction, fuel basis).
- **Q5 fuel**: the logged (app-calculated) fuel consumed per 100 km by class, the engine-on time share and the fuel per kWh of generator output where the reconstruction allows, fuel basis; never "measured".
- Estimators: (i) three-class means per km with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); (ii) the continuous association: each quantity per km regressed on the estimated grade (pp) with speed-band and cohort fixed effects, day-clustered, slope per percentage point with its interval (the expected potential-energy scale is about 5.3 kWh per 100 km per pp for 1950 kg at full efficiency: stated as a reference, never a target); (iii) the pre-registered contrasts downhill minus flat and uphill minus flat with intervals and the minimum detectable difference.

## 2. Measurement-error handling (frozen)
The grade is an estimate (inter-product step-grade error 0.36 pp, about 15 % of steps change class between products): between-class contrasts are ATTENUATED toward zero by misclassification. Sensitivities, all reported, none selected: (a) the consensus class (steps where FABDEM, NASADEM and AW3D30 agree on the three-class label); (b) the same analysis with NASADEM and AW3D30 as the class source (the spread of each estimate across the sources is shown); (c) the continuous slope with each source; (d) stratification by speed band and by thermal cohort (cold, shoulder, warm; no cohort contrast is read as a grade effect). The M757 displaced-DEM placebo (a displaced grade still shows a nonzero energy coefficient for every source, cause unidentified) is carried as a limit: every placebo coefficient and the placebo class contrast are reported beside each result.

## 4. Decision rules (frozen)
- R1 (control): the step set and its stages reproduce the M762 result (tolerance 0); the per-step integrals reproduce the raw channel integrals of the drive (the sum over steps of E_regen equals the integral over the covered windows); the sum of the three class contributions equals the total.
- R2 (support): a cell needs 30 days, 100 km and 1,000 steps for an interval verdict (M762 floors); below it counts only. Q2 needs at least 20 braking-release steps per cell and 10 days.
- R3 (reading, per quantity): "differs from flat" only if the day-clustered interval of the contrast excludes zero AND the sign and the 'consensus class' sensitivity agree AND the placebo contrast does not reproduce the same sign with a comparable size; otherwise "not distinguishable" (with the minimum detectable difference; never read as equivalence). The slope is reported with the placebo slope.
- R4 (falsification): the claim "regeneration energy per km depends on the estimated grade class" is falsified if the downhill-minus-flat contrast of Q1 has an interval containing zero with a minimum detectable difference below 15 % of the flat value; a contrast that exists only for one source is "source-dependent".
- R5 (escalation): any contrast whose sign is the opposite of the physical expectation (more regeneration energy uphill, or more fuel downhill) goes to the Director before interpretation.
