# M321 pre-registered spec (rev 3): ingestion drift monitor for the M119-v2 pack-temperature ladder (M320 Part C)

Status: written BEFORE any M321 result exists (2026-10-01; master 489 drives, MD5 bd9d10726bb064d857cf9ff98d2b7338; main at 4b92dd5, tag M320). Implements Part C of `analyses/M320_spec.md` (Director-accepted: the ladder is tied to the V2 fit basis and carried between refits; a report-only monitor runs on every ingestion; a cold level or an extended range starts a NEW spec and never a silent refit; "must merge before the next ingestion"). Revision 2 folds in the Director's review of rev 1 (decision "revise", 12 required edits, rulings 1-4), still before any result. Revision 3 is POST-HOC and disclosed as such: the first run of the implemented monitor on the current corpus (equal to the ladder basis, zero drift by construction) raised W2 on both sides (the 17.5 C band passes the gate with 181 / 160 events on 56 / 52 event days, because the lowest carried final level, 20 C, is only the P5 of the at-risk pack temperature, so a level one grid step colder always has support); the pre-registered falsification clause names a warning on the current corpus a monitor defect. Director ruling (2026-10-01): W2 is redefined on non-overlapping bands; the fix is structural and corpus-independent (built from existing constants, nothing calibrated); the quiet result of the new definition on today's data was known when it was chosen. The literal rev-2 W2 outcome is reported side by side with rev 3.
Scope: tooling and a gate. NO change to the ladder levels, the fitted model, the surfaces or any figure. Model-derived monitoring statistics only; nothing is article-eligible.

## Deviations from M320 Part C (stated, Director-reviewed)
(i) W3 (range extension) is split: an information flag with ZERO tolerance and a new-spec trigger at >= 1 grid step (Part C said "extends beyond the carried [min, max]"); (ii) the W2 reference is the lowest carried FINAL level (Part C said the carried P5; the lowest final level is the P5-level here, but the rule must not depend on that); (iii) W4 (support loss) is new; (iv) "starts a NEW spec" is implemented as a gate acknowledgement requirement (Director ruling 3), not a hard block on ingestion; (v) W2 candidate levels are restricted to NON-OVERLAPPING bands (rev 3, post-hoc, see the header).

## Problem
M320 levels (seconds-weighted P5/P50/P95 of the at-risk pack temperature, support-gated) are derived once, on the V2 fit corpus (the ladder basis). Each ingestion adds drives that may be shoulder or cold, with pack temperatures outside the fitted range where the ladder is silent and the spline is linear. Nothing tells the owner when the carried ladder is stale, when a colder level has gathered support, or when the corpus range has left the fitted range.

## Design
1. Exact per-drive histogram cache `analyses/M321_tpack_cache.json` (git-tracked, generated, never hand-edited). Per master drive file and side: `{tpack rounded to 4 dp: [n at-risk seconds, n events]}` over the complete-case rows (all of soc, speed, tpack, demand, durationS non-null), i.e. exactly the population of the M320 selection and support statistics, plus the calendar day key (`_day_key`, the day attribution of `support_stats`) and the sha256 of the raw file used. The build records the number of distinct values and the file size; the design does not depend on them (if pack temperature ever has many distinct values the cache grows, nothing else changes).
2. Frozen basis. The cache has a `basis` section written only by an explicit `rebase` (after a V2 refit and `tools/m320_ladder.py`): the sorted list of basis files, their raw sha256 and `selectionCodeSha256` = sha256 of (m119v2_model.py, tools/m318_run.py, tools/m320_ladder.py). Basis entries are never recomputed by `update`. If a basis drive's current raw sha256 differs from its basis entry, the monitor raises a separate F03 PROVENANCE flag (listing files), does not recompute the entry and does not count it as drift (no mixing of provenance and new data). `update` computes entries only for master files outside the basis. If `selectionCodeSha256` differs from the current code, the monitor status is `cache-invalid` (full `rebase` required; the gate FAILs).
3. Monitor (`tools/m321_ladder_monitor.py`, report-only), per side, on the current master: pooled, basis-only and new-only seconds-weighted quantiles P5/P50/P95 using the IDENTICAL `weighted_quantile` (inverted CDF, same tie rule) and `snap` imported from `tools/m320_ladder.py`; day-weighted quantiles as a sensitivity (never a warning); support (events, event days, at-risk seconds in the closed +/-2.5 C band, `support_stats` semantics) at every carried level; the current observed min / max; new drives and days since the basis. All thresholds (grid step, band, gate events and days, delta) are IMPORTED from the M320 config, not duplicated.
4. Warnings (pre-registered, evaluated on the pooled current corpus): W1 distribution shift: |current - carried quantile value| >= 1 grid step for any of P5/P50/P95 (a shift of the corpus pack-temperature distribution, seasonal and confounded; never "model invalidity" or "degradation"); W2 cold candidate: any grid level L with L <= (lowest carried final level) - 2 x BAND (a band that does not overlap the neighbouring level's band; BAND and STEP imported from the M320 config, no literal; empty if that bound is below the grid floor) whose closed +/-BAND band passes the support gate (>= 100 events on >= 10 event days), shown as "early support" while its event days < 20. Bands are closed and the pack temperature has a quarter-degree-like resolution, so the candidate band and the neighbouring band still share their endpoint value: the count at that shared endpoint is reported, and W2 is labelled "boundary-dependent" if excluding the endpoint rows flips the gate; L = (lowest final level) - 2 x BAND + STEP is never a candidate. Cold-tail growth short of a separate level is W1's job (P5 shift >= 1 step); W3 range extension: current observed min < fit-basis min - 1 grid step or max > fit-basis max + 1 grid step (new-spec trigger); W3-info: ANY extension of the observed range (zero tolerance; the observed min / max are deterministic), reporting the number of events, at-risk seconds and days outside the fit-basis range, and the UI range note is relabelled "fit-basis range" with the current observed range beside it; W4 support loss: a carried final level no longer passes the gate. Information: the high band now has >= 20 event days. Status: `ok` | `warn-refit` (W1 or W4 active) | `warn-newspec` (W2 or W3 active; takes precedence) | `cache-invalid`.
5. Output block `socHysteresisV2.tempLadderMonitor` (generated, nothing typed): status, `masterMd5`, `basisNDrives`, `currentNDrives`, `newDrivesSinceBasis`, `cacheSha256`, `selectionCodeSha256`, `warningSetHash` (sha256 of the sorted warning codes and levels), per side the quantiles (pooled / basis / new / day-weighted), carried quantiles, support at carried levels, ranges (fit-basis, observed), `coldCandidate`, the F03 flag list, the generated warning strings and the sentence "W2/W3 start a new spec; no silent refit". Written only into that key by an LF-safe, isolation-checked splice.
6. Dashboard: the "ladder basis N drives" stamp stays; the range note reads "fit-basis range" with the current observed range; when status is not `ok` an amber banner lists the generated warnings and shows the current corpus size beside the basis. No number is typed in the JSX.
7. Gate (`release_check.py`). FAIL when: the monitor block is missing; `masterMd5` differs from the current master; `currentNDrives` differs from `meta.totalDrives`; the cache's file set differs from the master's file set; `cacheSha256` does not match the cache file; `selectionCodeSha256` does not match the current code (status `cache-invalid`); `tempLadder.{side}.basisNDrives` differs from `socHysteresisV2.corpusSizeAtRecompute` (a V2 refit without a re-derived ladder); status is `warn-newspec` and no acknowledgement record matches its `warningSetHash`. WARN (not FAIL) for each W1, W4, W3-info and F03 flag. An acknowledgement is a record in `analyses/M321_acknowledgements.json` `{warningSetHash, ref, date}` where `ref` names a spec id or CHANGELOG M### entry that handles the warning set; it is written by hand by the owner/agent after the decision, never by the monitor. Ingesting cold data is therefore never blocked by method staleness, but the new-spec obligation cannot be ignored.
8. Ingestion: new stage `m321_ladder_monitor` in `tools/ingest_stages.json` after `record_spread_fit` and before `fuel_recon` (needs the staged `raw_only/`), running `python3 tools/m321_ladder_monitor.py update` (extend the cache for non-basis files, recompute, splice).

## Known-answer and validation tests
(a) Unit tests `tests/synthetic/test_m321_monitor.py`: histogram aggregation reproduces `weighted_quantile` / `support_stats` of the raw rows exactly (same tie rule, same day attribution); each of W1-W4 fires on a constructed case and does not fire just inside its threshold; the exact gate boundary (100 events on 10 days passes, 99 events or 9 days fails); a 0.25 C range creep raises W3-info but not W3; the info flag; status precedence; `warningSetHash` stability and acknowledgement matching; gate FAIL conditions on a mutated copy of the arrays (missing block, wrong masterMd5, wrong counts, file-set mismatch, selection-code mismatch, basis != corpusSizeAtRecompute, unacknowledged warn-newspec).
(b) Path equivalence: the incremental path (`m119v2_model._corpus_tables` on raw_only for a file subset) reproduces the M318-tables cache entries EXACTLY (histograms, day keys, events) for the 79 drives after position 410 plus 30 seeded-random earlier drives (all 489 if time allows).
(c) Equivalence on the M320 corpus: the cache-based statistics reproduce `tempLadder.{side}.candidates` (quantileValue, snapped, nEvents, nDays, nAtRiskS) exactly, both sides.
(d) Scenarios (diagnostic, reported, never tuned; rev 2 and rev 3 W2 outcomes side by side): the forward replay takes its reference level from the 410 ladder; the W2 boundary case (100 events on 10 days passes, 99 events or 9 days fails) is built at L = (lowest final level) - 2 x BAND, and L + STEP is shown never to be a candidate; the endpoint flip case;  forward replay: the ladder selection derived from the first 410 drives (M320 point stage on the 410 tables, no bootstrap) monitored on the 489 corpus, listing which warnings the 79 later drives raise; injected cold drives (synthetic entries well below the fit range); injected 0.25 C creep; an F03 hash change on a basis file; a selection-code change. On the current corpus (equal to the ladder basis) the monitor reports `ok` with zero drift (near-tautological with (c); not a validation by itself).
(e) Isolation: only `socHysteresisV2.tempLadderMonitor` and the V2 stamp note change; master MD5 unchanged; LF endings; `release_check.py` and `validate_jsdom.js` green.

## Can each warning fire on a zero-drift corpus? (rev 3, defect-class check; each row has a unit test)
| Warning | Zero-drift corpus (current = basis) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | cannot fire | compares the current quantile value with the UNSNAPPED carried quantile value (not the snapped level: P5 18.75 snaps to 20); identical data give identical quantiles |
| W2 (rev 3) | cannot fire unless a non-overlapping colder band already had support at the basis, which the ladder derivation would have excluded as an inadmissible low level; on the current data it is empty | candidate bands are disjoint from the lowest final level's band |
| W3 | cannot fire | the fit-basis range is taken from the cache's own basis population (same complete-case rows), not from the rounded arrays values |
| W3-info | cannot fire | zero tolerance on the same population, so equal data give zero extension |
| W4 | cannot fire | the carried final levels passed the gate on the same rows |
No acknowledgement record exists for the defective rev-2 warning set. The falsification clause stays: any warning on the current corpus under rev 3 means no merge, and there is no second fix without a new Director review.

## Accept / reject
Validity (failure = not merged): (a), (b), (c), (e); the gate FAIL conditions demonstrated in (a). Blind audit: given the rule and the tables, reproduce the monitor statistics, the warning decisions on the current corpus and on two injected scenarios (cold drives; 0.25 C creep) and the gate's FAIL conditions; stop there. Director decision before merge.
Falsification: any warning on the current corpus (zero drift by construction) is a monitor defect; a non-exact (b) or (c) means the cache is not the M320 population and the monitor is not merged.

## Procedure and labelling
Refit procedure (CHANGELOG entry and docs/workflow-and-tools.md): after an explicit `recompute_m119v2` refit run `python tools/m318_run.py tables --n-first <N> --out TABLES.pkl`, `python tools/m320_ladder.py run --tables TABLES.pkl --out DIR` and `... apply --out DIR`, then `python tools/m321_ladder_monitor.py rebase --tables TABLES.pkl` (new basis, new selection-code hash) and `... update`; the gate enforces it. All monitor output is model-derived and carries the V2 block's F03 label. Risks accepted and mitigated: seconds-weighted quantiles dominated by a few long drives (the day-weighted sensitivity is shown), seasonal confounding read as drift (W1 wording), alarm fatigue (acknowledgement gate), silent cache staleness (selection-code and file-set hashes), provenance drift mistaken for new-data drift (frozen basis plus the F03 flag). Out of scope: automatic refits, new levels, any change to the spline or covariates, the article, the Ukrainian companion.
