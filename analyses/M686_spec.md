# M686 spec (Rev 1): N-03 part B, time at pack temperature including parked time (modelled, payload only)

Status: pre-registered before any result exists. Backlog item N-03 part B (`analyses/self_audit_2026-11-11/backlog.json`); part A is M673/M674 (exposure of the driving hours). Depends on the Q3 cool-down constant of M666/M667 (`seasonalFits`, owner decision D-05) and on the owner statement of 2026-11-16: no temperature logger exists, ambient is registered for actual drives only, parking is outdoors in nearly all cases (indoor parking is a rare exception).

## 1. Question and non-claims
How many hours of the window between the first key-on and the last key-off does the battery spend in each pack-temperature band, split into driving hours (logged) and parked hours (modelled), and how large is the cold and hot parked share? The logger records only while the car is on, so the parked temperature is never observed between drives.
Non-claims. Parked hours are MODELLED from the cool-down relaxation fitted in Q3, the driver-recorded ambient of the two bounding drives and an owner-stated assumption (outdoor parking); they are not measured and not validated in the interior of a gap, only at its two ends (held-out next-start temperature). The result is an hours-by-temperature budget of the logged window (one May-to-November fragment), not lifetime exposure, not annualised, not an ageing estimate and not a life. Season, parking spot, sun load and the time of day move the true parked temperature in ways the data cannot see; the uncertainty is the stated grid of assumptions, not an interval.

## 2. Data and eligibility
Master `drive_master.csv` (key-on `date` + `time_start`, key-off `time_end`, driver-recorded ambient at start and end of each drive), the per-drive start and end pack temperature of M667 (mean over the four pack probes, within 60 s of the first or last timestamp, at least 3 of 4 probes; cache `seasonal_fits_cache/`, read through the existing function, no new raw reading), and the Q3 primary tau and its sensitivities from `seasonalFits`. Driving hours per pack-temperature band: the clean-drive hours of the exposure vector (`exposureVector.Q1_Q2_Q4_clean`, sensor T1); the hours of drives excluded as non-clean are reported as their own row. Window: first key-on to last key-off of the master drives, in time order; gaps are the intervals between consecutive drives (key-off of one to key-on of the next).

## 3. Model of the parked pack temperature
For every gap of length g hours between drive i (pack end temperature T0, ambient end a0) and drive i+1 (ambient start a1): A = (a0 + a1) / 2 (the ambient reference of Q3), T(t) = A + (T0 - A) exp(-t / tau), t in [0, g], tau = Q3 primary. Outdoor parking is the default. Bin hours are computed exactly from the closed-form edge-crossing times (no time stepping): the time T(t) crosses an edge E is t = -tau ln((E - A) / (T0 - A)) when that argument is positive and t lies inside the gap. Bands (declared display bands, not identified thresholds), deg C: below 0, 0 to <5, 5 to <10, 10 to <15, 15 to <20, 20 to <25, 25 to <30, 30 to <35, 35 to <40, 40 or more; a value exactly on an edge goes to the upper band (as in M674).
Gaps are classed by length (as the Q3 strata): below 1 h, 1 to <4 h, 4 to <12 h, 12 to <36 h, and "long" (36 h or longer). For a long gap the pack is at A after about 5 tau, and A (the mean of the two bounding readings) stands for the whole gap; the share of parked hours in the long class is reported separately because the ambient assumption is weakest there. A gap with a missing pack end temperature or ambient reading is "unassigned" (hours and number reported, never imputed).

## 4. Quantities
B1 Hours by pack-temperature band: driving (clean), driving (excluded drives, unbanded), parked modelled (assigned gaps), unassigned gaps, total; the share of the window in each; n gaps and n days contributing to each parked band.
B2 Parked share of the window time overall, by gap class, and the long-gap share of the parked hours.
B3 Cold and hot parked hours: modelled parked hours below 10 deg C and below 15 deg C (the calendar counterpart of the cold-charging screens S1 and S2), and at 35 deg C or more and 40 deg C or more.
B4 Endpoint validation of the model by gap class: the predicted next-key-on pack temperature against the observed one (MAE and signed bias), against persistence (no decay) and full relaxation to A, using the day-blocked cross-validated tau of Q3; and a placebo control in which A is shuffled across gaps of the same class (the skill must drop; if it does not, the ambient information is not what drives the skill and that is stated).

## 5. Uncertainty: a grid, not an interval
Pre-registered grid, evaluated for every quantity B1 to B3 beside the primary (min and max over the grid are reported, with the cell that gives each): tau in {primary, the lowest and the highest Q3 sensitivity (project-definition variant excluded)}; ambient reference shift in {-2, 0, +2} deg C; parked offset (solar and shelter effects) in {-3, 0, +3} deg C added to A (stated, as in the Q4 grid of M667); and an indoor sensitivity in which a fraction f in {0, 0.05, 0.10} of the parked hours, chosen by a fixed rule (every 1/f-th gap in time order), is treated as indoor with A moved halfway toward 15 deg C (assumed, verified:false). No bootstrap and no confidence interval is computed for modelled hours: the grid is the bracket, and a statement such as "below 10 deg C for x hours" is allowed only with its bracket. Support: a parked band cell is "not shown" with fewer than 10 gaps or fewer than 5 days contributing, "exploratory" with an effective day count (ESS of the cell's own per-day parked hours) below 10, "supported" otherwise.

## 6. Validity and tests
V1 time accounting: driving hours of all drives + the hours of all gaps (assigned and unassigned) equal the elapsed window time within 1e-6 h. V2 every consecutive pair of drives is a gap (assigned or unassigned with a stated reason). V3 the exact bin hours of one gap equal a fine-step numerical integration within 1e-3 h. V4 determinism. V5 the bin hours of a gap sum to its length. Known-answer test `tests/synthetic/test_m686_parked_time.py`: a single gap with analytic crossings of two edges; a warming and a cooling gap; a gap shorter than the first crossing; ambient shift moves hours as expected; a long gap lands entirely in the band of A; an unassigned gap; the grid min and max; the placebo drop on a constructed set with a planted tau; accounting identity on a constructed window.

## 7. Outputs and next steps

No display here (a display needs the Director's approval of the rendered text and the language gate). Language rule with the implementation: "modelled parked pack temperature", "assumed outdoor parking (owner statement 2026-11-16)"; never "measured parked temperature", "calendar ageing", "damage" or an annualised figure. The owner fact is recorded in the block (`assumptions`). Blind audit before the Director's decision on the results; no article figure.

## 8. Out of scope and risks
Out of scope: the Arrhenius index Q4 (unchanged, M667), annualisation, state-of-charge during parking, calendar-ageing statements, a parking-type log. Risks: R1 sun load and shelter move the true parked temperature away from A (offset grid); R2 the true ambient between two readings swings with the time of day and a long gap is represented by one constant (long-gap share reported separately); R3 tau was fitted on pairs, most of which are short (338 of 628 below 1 h), while most parked hours sit in long gaps (the hours share by gap class is reported); R4 the driving bands use sensor T1 while the parked path uses the probe mean (a sensor-definition shift of +-1 deg C is added to the grid as a sensitivity on the driving bands); R5 the window is one May-to-November fragment.

## 9. Director review (2026-11-16, verdict revise; no change of question) and how each is applied (Rev 1.1)
These override the matching text above.
1. Timestamps: the M667 rule is used: t0 = `local_iso[:19]`, t1 = t0 + `duration_s`, drives sorted by t0. A gap below zero is "unassigned: overlap" (V1 counts its hours as signed). The elapsed window time is DST-aware (the window contains 2026-10-25): times are converted to UTC, or the gap is flagged.
2. Unlogged driving: a gap whose next drive has `unlogged_distance_est_km` above 1.0 is assigned in the primary but has its own row in B1 and B2; sensitivity: treat it as unassigned.
3. T0 after an excluded (non-clean) drive: the cache value is used if 3 of 4 probes are present and these hours are reported separately; no other imputation.
4. V1 uses `duration_s`; the difference between the banded exposure-vector hours and the `duration_s` of the clean drives is a row "driving, unbanded (sample gaps)".
5. D-05 fallback: if `seasonalFits.Q3.heldOut.gatePassed` is false there is no primary: the grid only, plus a flag `d05Support`; `definitionDependent` is always carried.
6. The tau axis is named: {primary, Q3 stratum 4 h or longer, L2, previous-drive maximum} (the project variant excluded). The ambient-shift axis s in {-2, 0, +2} is paired with the Q3 tau refitted at that shift, not crossed with the primary tau.
7. Order of operations: A_eff = A + s + o; indoor: A_in = A_eff + (15 - A_eff) / 2. s and o are additive, so the combined range is +-5 deg C.
8. Indoor rule: among gaps of 4 h or longer, every k-th with k = round(1/f); the realised hour fraction is reported. A bounding rule is added: take the coldest-A gaps first (for the cold quantities) and the hottest-A first (for the hot quantities) until f of the parked hours is reached; min and max are taken over both rules.
9. Long gaps: the same exponential applies ("A stands for the whole gap" is deleted). Sensitivities by numerical integration at 1-min steps (the V3 tolerance applies): A linear from a0 to a1, and a 4 deg C diurnal sinusoid (the M667 D6 assumption). B3 is reported with and without the long class.
10. Support basis: the cluster is the arrival day of the gap, the weight is the cell hours per gap; calendar days must not inflate the ESS of multi-day gaps; "contributing" means more than 0 h in the cell; the status is computed on the primary only.
11. B4: the pairs are all consecutive pairs with both endpoints present, in every gap class (gaps longer than 36 h are fully out of sample). Placebo: tau fixed, shuffle of A within the gap class, seed 42, 1000 permutations; "drop" means the primary MAE is below the 5th percentile of the placebo MAE.
12. R4: the T1-minus-probe-mean offset at drive ends is estimated from the cache (median, IQR) if per-probe values are stored, otherwise +-1 deg C stays a labelled assumption. Driving (T1) and parked (probe mean) are not summed into one band total unless labelled "mixed-sensor".
13. Wording: "same thresholds as S1 and S2; no charging occurs while parked" replaces "calendar counterpart of S1 and S2". Language rule `M686-parked-time-wording` forbids: measured parked, calendar ageing / aging, damage, per year, annual, lifetime exposure, validated parked temperature. The block does not feed or compare with Q4 or `monthlyTempC`.
Stated sign of a known bias: the ambient is read at drive times (daytime) and the dashboard sensor heat-soaks at key-on, so A is likely biased warm against the 24 h mean and the cold parked hours are likely understated.
