## 1. Question and estimands

The M435 Kaplan-Meier (product-limit) estimate E2 pools cold starts whose coolant starts anywhere below 60 C. How does it differ from the start-stratified estimate, and do the readings differ between start-temperature strata?

Strata (declared, final: no re-cut, no merging, no other number of strata, no other horizons): terciles of the coolant temperature at the first valid sample of the coolant cold-start population, cohort "all", cut points computed ONCE with the numpy linear percentile at 33.33333 and 66.66667, each rounded as floor(2x + 0.5) / 2 (to 0.5 C). Stratum 1: start < c1; stratum 2: c1 <= start < c2; stratum 3: start >= c2; every cold start is in exactly one stratum. A drive's stratum (defined from its coolant start) is the same for the coolant and the oil cell; oil start temperature is not used. The coolant start temperature is an integer in the logs (quartiles 25 / 41 / 51 C), so the stratum shares are NOT equal by construction. The cut points and the stratum shares are written into the payload.

Headline cells (as M435): cohort all, coolant 80 C and oil 70 C, h = 600 s; descriptive horizons {300, 600, 900} s. Per stratum and cell: n, days, reached, censored by reason, E1(h), E2(h), F_high(h), the tilt rows theta = 0.5 and 2, the two readings with the M435 classes, the Kaplan-Meier median (identified or not), the M435 support rule (at least 10 at risk on at least 5 days at h; otherwise "not supported", never imputed). No tipping points and no restricted mean time per stratum (B5). The M435 estimators and conventions are reused unchanged (events before censorings, strict c < h, day-clustered bootstrap B = 4000, seed 42, ONE set of day resamples per cohort shared by all strata and cells; cuts and stratum membership are held fixed inside the draws).

Start-standardized estimate (direct standardization, B1): E2_std(h) = sum_s w_s E2_s(h) with weights w_s = the share of the cell's OWN cold starts in stratum s (coolant n = 389 on 113 days, oil n = 388 on 112 days on the live corpus; the weights differ slightly between the two cells), fixed across horizons and draws (conditional inference on the displayed weights). If any stratum is unsupported at h, E2_std(h) is "not available" (never renormalized over the supported strata). Identities, not estimates (B1): with these weights E1_std = E1 and F_high_std = F_high (pooled values); they are tested and not displayed.

Difference from the pooled estimate (B2): D(h) = E2_pooled(h) - E2_std(h), with the interval from the same shared resamples (paired), headline at 600 s. It measures how much the pooled Kaplan-Meier estimate differs from the start-stratified one, i.e. the sensitivity of the pooled estimate to censoring that differs by start temperature; it is not an effect. Contrasts between strata (descriptive, no test, no p-value, no family-wise claim): E2_s(h) - E2_1(h) and E1_s(h) - E1_1(h) for s = 2, 3, with paired intervals.

Not an estimand: a causal effect of start temperature (the start temperature is entangled with the season, the hour, the trip length and the decision to stop the drive: the right-censored drives are short destination trips with colder starts); warm-up physics; any cohort contrast.

## 2. Within-stratum assumption (B7)

Within a stratum the Kaplan-Meier estimate still assumes independent censoring; stratifying does not remove that assumption and it cannot be tested. Worst-case bounds and the tilt rows are reported per stratum with the M435 classes, and the script writes one class statement per stratum and cell ("within the lowest start-temperature tercile, fewer than half reached: <class>; half or more reached: <class>"). Nothing is said to "survive" conditioning.

## 3. Decision rules (declared before any result) (B5)

Cut points, strata, horizons and weights are final. A draw in which a stratum is empty or a denominator is zero is "unidentified" for that quantity; if more than 2.5 % of draws are unidentified the interval is printed as "not available". A failure of G1, a known-answer test or the coverage gate means no leaf is written and the case goes to the Director. A stratum with unsupported horizons is displayed as unsupported; the standardized value is then "not available" (a declared outcome, not a failure). The result may support only: "within start-temperature terciles the readings are as follows" and "the pooled estimate differs from the start-stratified estimate by D (interval)"; it may not support an effect of the start temperature, a seasonal statement or an article claim.

## 4. Gates and tests

- G1: strata partition each cell (n, reached, censored by reason sum to the M435 cell counts; days are the union); with ONE stratum (cut points at the extremes) every quantity equals the M435 cell (E1, E2, F_high, classes, median); E1_std = E1 and F_high_std = F_high to 1e-12.
- G2: ONE additive key `thermalSupportLedger.timeToThreshold.startStrata`, produced by the ledger stage from the records already built (no extra frame pass); deep diff: nothing else changes.
- G3: second run byte-identical.
- Known-answer tests (criteria declared here): (i) tercile assignment, the rounding rule and ties (a start equal to c1 belongs to stratum 2, equal to c2 to stratum 3) on a hand-made vector with integer starts; (ii) partition: counts and days; (iii) one stratum = the pooled cell; (iv) identity: if the within-stratum Kaplan-Meier curves are identical, the pooled curve equals them and D = 0; (v) a fixture with DIFFERENT stratum curves and stratum-dependent censoring (colder strata stop earlier): the pooled estimate is biased relative to the truth sum_s w_s F_s by more than 2 Monte Carlo standard errors and E2_std is within 2 (200 simulations, large n); (vi) an unsupported stratum makes the standardized value "not available" and the weights are never renormalized; (vii) the contrasts and D use the shared resample set (the interval of the difference is the interval of the paired draws); (viii) coverage simulation in the M435 design (113 days, Poisson 3.4 drives per day, day frailty on events and on censoring times, censoring times resampled from the live spans, 1 Hz) with a start covariate tied to the day frailty and colder starts getting shorter spans, the cut points recomputed in every simulation, the truth = sum_s w_s F_s with the population stratum shares from a large reference sample: the 95 % intervals of E2_std(600) and of the stratum-3-minus-stratum-1 contrast cover in [0.92, 0.98] in each of two scenarios (Weibull shape 1.5 and exponential); the share of simulations with E2_std "not available" is reported; (ix) fixed seeds reproduce.
- Blind audit (analytical-auditor, claim + data path only): reproduce the cut points, stratum counts, E1 / E2 / F_high at 600 s per stratum, E2_std and D for coolant 80 C and oil 70 C from the raw files and the rules above; the Director rules on the wording before the dashboard text.

## 5. Wording and display

Under the M435 tables (cohort All only): per headline cell a table with the strata as rows (start-temperature range, n, days, observed share and Kaplan-Meier (product-limit) estimate with intervals, worst-case bounds, classes), the standardized value with the fixed weights, D and the contrasts. Required: the M435 assumption sentence (R1) for the stratum tables; "within start-temperature terciles"; "standardized to the observed start-temperature mix"; "contrasts descriptive, no test"; "start temperature is entangled with the season, the trip length and the decision to stop the drive: the strata describe, they do not identify an effect of the start temperature". Forbidden (language rule M438-start-strata): "explained by" / "accounts for" the start temperature, "survives", "adjusted for", "controls for", "removes the censoring assumption", "effect of start temperature", "robust". No article use.

## 6. Limits (disclosed in advance)

Terciles are a declared planning choice with unequal shares because the start temperature is an integer; three strata leave few days per stratum at long horizons (the support rule decides what is shown, and the coolant 80 C cell has 78 drives on 61 days at risk at 600 s overall, so a stratum may fail support and E2_std then is "not available"); 2 cells x 3 horizons x contrasts give no family-wise claim; strata are not matched on trip length, hour or season; cohort all only; single vehicle, one driver, May-October.

## 9. Changes from Rev 1

Weights per cell from its own stratum shares, E1_std and F_high_std as tested identities (B1); D with a paired interval and the wording "differs from the start-stratified estimate" (B2); KAT (iv) replaced by the identical-curves identity and a different-curves fixture with stratum-dependent censoring (B3); coverage simulation with a start covariate tied to the day frailty, recomputed cuts, truth = sum w_s F_s, gate per scenario, report of "not available" (B4); decision rules (B5); disclosure of the post hoc origin, integer start temperature and the percentile and rounding rules (B6); per-stratum class statement, no "survives" (B7).

## 10. Rev 2.1 (2026-10-14, Director confirmation of Rev 2: approve with recorded clarifications C1-C4, before any run)

- **C1 (B1).** Inside every bootstrap draw the weights are recomputed from that draw's stratum shares (cut points and membership fixed); the point estimate uses the observed shares. E1_std = E1 and F_high_std = F_high then hold in the point estimate and in every draw, and G1 tests both. A stratum with weight 0 in a draw is left out of that draw's sum (it is not "not available").
- **C2 (B4).** Truth for E2_std = the marginal F(600) of the large reference sample, which equals sum_s w_s F_s for any cut. Truth for the stratum-3-minus-stratum-1 contrast = F_3 - F_1 in the reference sample under that simulation's cut points. The spans depend on the start temperature only through the stratum at the reference cut points; the censoring frailty is independent of the event frailty. 400 simulations, B = 400. Coverage is computed over the simulations that produce an interval; fewer than 300 such simulations means "not evaluable" and goes to the Director.
- **C3 (B5).** A stratum draw is unidentified if its number of drives is 0 or if its risk set at h is empty while S_KM(h) > 0. E2_std, D or a contrast is unidentified in a draw if any of its parts (strata with positive weight) is. If at most 2.5 % of the draws are unidentified they are dropped and their count is reported; otherwise the interval is "not available". The median keeps the M435 +infinity rule. Support is judged on the point sample only.
- **C4 (KAT v).** Pass: |mean E2_std - truth| <= 3 Monte Carlo standard errors, and the pooled bias is >= 3 Monte Carlo standard errors and >= 0.03.

## 11. Rev 2.2 (2026-10-15, after the result and the blind audit; Director ruling "accept", wording requirements adopted)

Script-written additions in the payload and the dashboard text: per stratum the right-censored share; a post-hoc, descriptive, not pre-registered profile (median trip distance, share of trips under 5 km, share starting before 10:00; label "not a test"), whose values equal the blind audit's; per cell a pattern note (the contrasts are against the lowest tercile with whether each interval includes zero, the highest-versus-middle difference as a point only, and "no gradient across the terciles is claimed"); a D note ("a difference D near zero is not evidence that censoring can be ignored: independent censoring is still assumed within each tercile", with the upper worst-case bounds of the terciles); a thin-support note for any stratum with fewer than twice the support floor (20 drives) at risk at 600 s.
