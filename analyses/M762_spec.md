# M762 spec: independent-DEM cross-check of the FABDEM step grade (replaces the logged-altitude validation that M757 could not run)

Rev 1, 2026-11-24. Committed BEFORE any download, code or result. Author: main session (Sonnet). Owner: "Yes, draft the M762 spec. And you may use any auxiliary DEM channels as an independent source" (answer to the question whether the logged-altitude validation should be replaced). Parents: `analyses/M757_spec.md` Rev 2 (frozen step definition, rules R1 to R4, negative control), result `analyses/M757_dem_grade_feasibility.json` (sha256 87b550d6...), audit `analyses/M757_audit.json`, Director decision `analyses/M757_director_decision.json`. Number taken after checking the CHANGELOG head (M761) and open PRs.

## 0. Why, and what stays unchanged
- M757 reported R2, R3 and R4 as "not testable", and the frozen displaced-DEM negative control FAILED (placebo b 0.086 [0.053, 0.118] against a primary b of 0.843 [0.792, 0.876]). The two logged-altitude checks (F1 and the b / b_obd ratio) cannot be run on a useful sample: 9 days overall and 1 day with fuel data, and the logged altitude channel is itself sparse and noisy. The M757 verdicts stay as recorded; nothing in M757 is reinterpreted or relaxed.
- M762 is a NEW analysis with its own frozen rules. It is designed after M757 was seen: this is stated here and in the result (design informed by the M757 outcome; rules frozen before any M762 result).
- Question: how large is the error of the FABDEM step grade at cloud-step scale, measured against other elevation sources whose errors are independent of FABDEM, and is that error small enough for the grade-class stratification that M737 envisaged (classes 2 pp wide)?

## 1. Constraints (as M757, restated)
- Report-only; OBD stays primary; every DEM quantity is "estimated / model-derived / proxy". No change to `summary_arrays.json`, payload, dashboard, `drive_master.csv` (MD5 `d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98`, asserted before and after), `regenDisplayV2` or any winter-frozen file. Raw files and the cloud store are read-only.
- Public repository: no DEM tile is committed (location outside the repository, env `XT_DEM_DIR`; one sub-folder per source); the result carries source, version, licence and the tile sha256 list; aggregates only (no coordinates, tracks or per-drive geometry).
- Optional stage: absent tiles or cloud store give "skipped", exit 0. Statistics: calendar day is the cluster; day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); n (steps, drives, days, km) with every estimate; not-distinguishable is never read as equivalence.

## 2. Sources and independence (frozen)
- **FABDEM v1.2** (in hand, 17 tiles; primary, the object under test). It is Copernicus GLO-30 with buildings and forests removed, so GLO-30 shares its radar base and is NOT an independent source of FABDEM errors. GLO-30 is used only for the bare-earth correction check below, never as an independence corner.
- **Candidate independent sources** (each used only if it passes R1; any subset): NASADEM or SRTM 1 arc-second (C-band radar, different mission and processing), ALOS World 3D AW3D30 (JAXA, optical stereo, a different sensor type), and optionally Copernicus GLO-30 as the surface (not bare-earth) reference for the FABDEM correction. A composite product without per-tile provenance (for example web terrain tiles) is not used as an independent corner.
- **Acquisition.** The tiles are not in hand. Before any download I give Andrii the exact list (source URL, tile names, file sizes, licence) and wait for a clear yes per source. Sources that need a login (NASA Earthdata, JAXA, OpenTopography key) are downloaded by Andrii; I never enter or store credentials. If no independent source is obtained, this milestone stays a spec and nothing is run.
- **Heights.** Geoid conventions differ between products, so only height DIFFERENCES between two points of the same product are compared (as M757).

## 3. Stages (each with a stop gate)
- **C0 integrity and coverage.** Tile sha256 versus a manifest written at download time (mismatch: stop, exit 3); DEM value present at least at 90 % of screened fixes (km-weighted) for each source (below: that source is dropped and reported). The reader is checked against a planted GeoTIFF (known-answer test, reusing the M757 reader).
- **C1 steps (reused, frozen).** The M757 step set is rebuilt by the pinned M757 code (tool sha256 `69edc468af40c9b8...`; steps between consecutive cloud fixes, OBD window shifted +16 s, step length 300 to 900 m, gap at most 120 s, exclusions: first and last step of a drive, minimum speed below 8 km/h, |FABDEM grade| above 8 pp). The same steps are used for every source (no per-source selection). Expected size (M757): about 3,600 steps, 289 drives, 82 days; recomputed, not taken from this spec.
- **C2 pairwise agreement.** For every pair of sources and every step: difference of step grade (pp). Reported: bias (median), median absolute difference, P90, Pearson, Spearman, Lin concordance, each with a day-clustered interval, by step-length stratum (300 to 500, 500 to 900 m) and by grade class (below -3, -3 to -1, -1 to 1, 1 to 3, above 3 pp).
- **C3 error variance of each source (three-cornered hat).** With three sources whose errors are assumed uncorrelated (FABDEM, plus two independent ones), `var(d_ij) = s_i^2 + s_j^2` for each pair identifies every `s_i^2` (with day-clustered intervals; a negative variance estimate is reported as such, not clipped silently). Output: the FABDEM step-grade error standard deviation `s_F` (pp) overall and per step-length stratum, and the reliability of the FABDEM step grade `lambda = 1 - s_F^2 / var(grade_F)` per grade class. With only ONE independent source, `s_F` is not identified; only the combined `s_F^2 + s_k^2` is reported and the adequacy rule R3 is "not testable".
- **C4 attenuation-corrected grade coefficient (descriptive).** The M757 F2 energy-balance regression is refitted with each independent source as an instrument for the FABDEM `m g dh` term (two-stage least squares; instrument strength reported by the first-stage F statistic) and with each source on its own. Reported next to the M757 `b`: `b_IV` with a day-clustered interval, `b` per source, and the displaced-DEM placebo (2 km perpendicular) for every source. Descriptive: no decision rule binds on `b`, and `b` stays conditional on the traction reconstruction and the assumed mass (M757 9.3).
- **C5 bare-earth correction check.** FABDEM minus GLO-30 step grade, by land-cover proxy: steps whose position lies where the two products differ in height by more than 2 m at either fix versus the rest. Descriptive; it states how much of the step grade the building and forest removal touches.
- **C6 report.** Grade-class shares per source (as M757 F3) and the share of steps whose grade class differs between FABDEM and each independent source (misclassification rate).

## 4. Decision rules (frozen before any result)
- R1 (C0 gate): each source needs intact tiles and at least 90 % km-weighted DEM coverage; otherwise the source is dropped and reported. At least ONE independent source must survive or the run stops with coverage only.
- R2 (independence support): the three-cornered hat needs FABDEM plus at least two independent sources, at least 30 days, 100 unique km and 1,000 steps. Below this, `s_F` is "not identified" and R3 is "not testable".
- R3 (adequacy of the FABDEM step grade for 2 pp grade classes): judged on `s_F` overall and for 500 to 900 m steps. `s_F` at most 0.67 pp (one third of the class width) with the upper end of its day-clustered interval at most 1.0 pp: "adequate for grade-class stratification". `s_F` above 1.33 pp or its lower interval end above 1.0 pp: "not adequate". Otherwise "marginal". The thresholds are fixed here; the minimum detectable `s_F` is stated. The misclassification rate of C6 is reported next to the verdict and never used to tune it.
- R4 (what the result does NOT license): "adequate" licenses only the grade-class stratification discussion of M757 F3; the `m g dh` potential-energy term needs its own spec and Director ruling (M737 R3 design), and the M757 placebo failure stays on record. Any binding, dashboard or article use needs an Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off.
- Falsification: the claim "FABDEM step grade is adequate for 2 pp grade classes at cloud-step scale" is falsified by R3 "not adequate". Limits: the three-cornered hat assumes uncorrelated errors between products; common-mode errors (shared terrain smoothing, shared geoid handling, the same road embankments or forests) are not identified and make `s_F` an underestimate; independent sources differ in resolution and acquisition date; steps are 300 to 900 m means, not a road profile.

## 8. Rev 2: Director rulings on Rev 1 (override sections 1 to 7 where they differ; review record `analyses/M762_director_review_rev1.json`, Rev 1 sha256 recorded in that file; decision: revise, every ruling adopted)
8.1 **R1 independence by data lineage.** At most ONE SRTM-lineage product counts as a corner (NASADEM or SRTM, never both). GLO-30 is never a corner. Void-filled cells are excluded through each product's own masks (GLO-30 EDM and FLM, NASADEM NUM, AW3D30 MSK), because GLO-30 fills from SRTM and AW3D30, and AW3D30 fills from SRTM. One common-support step set is used for all sources and the share of steps lost is reported.
8.2 **R2 limits text and stratum.** The three-cornered hat stays. Limits: correlation between FABDEM and a corner biases `s_F` DOWN; correlation between the two corners (both are surface models that see canopy and buildings) biases it UP; common-mode error (GPS position error, embankments, bridges, shared smoothing) is not identified. So `s_F` is the inter-product DEM error only, not the error against the road grade, and the R3 wording says so. R3 is ALSO evaluated on the C5 untouched stratum (FABDEM minus GLO-30 at most 2 m at both fixes); if the two disagree the worse verdict holds. No antisymmetry check is added (it does not test DEM error). The 9 days of logged altitude are a descriptive common-mode reference (M757 F1), with no verdict.
8.3 **R3 thresholds stay frozen (0.67 and 1.33 pp); implied misclassification stated before any result.** The verdict is the worse of the overall and the 500 to 900 m results. "Adequate" requires all three variance estimates to be positive; a negative estimate gives "not identified". Expected misclassification (computed before any M762 result, model-based, not a result): the M757 F3 step shares (-3: 1.4 %, -3 to -1: 8.9 %, -1 to 1: 79.3 %, 1 to 3: 8.5 %, above 3: 2.0 %) are matched by a symmetric stretched-exponential grade distribution (fit to P(|g|>1) and P(|g|>3); scale 0.52 pp, shape 0.69), and a Gaussian error is added (Monte Carlo, 2,000,000 draws, seed 42): the share of steps whose 2 pp class changes is about 21 % at `s_F` = 0.67 pp, 36 % at 1.0 pp and 47 % at 1.33 pp (13.5 % at 0.5 pp). Most steps sit near zero grade, close to the +-1 pp class edges, so even an "adequate" `s_F` of 0.67 pp implies roughly one step in five changing class; "adequate" therefore means adequate for grade-class SHARES at corpus level, not for per-step class labels. The result states this beside the verdict.
8.4 **R4 lambda.** `lambda` is reported overall only; a per-class value conditions on FABDEM (truncation) and is labelled descriptive. A sensitivity applies the 8 pp exclusion on the cross-source median grade instead of FABDEM.
8.5 **R5 2SLS.** Descriptive and secondary: `b_IV` is reported only if the cluster-robust first-stage F is at least 10; both stages are bootstrapped by day; the Hansen J test is reported descriptively; the displaced-DEM placebo runs for every source. Never "validated" and never "corrects attenuation".
8.6 **R6 coverage.** A source below 90 % km-weighted coverage is dropped; no stratification by coverage.
8.7 **R7 audit.** Sonnet blind audit suffices for report-only use; it also checks lineage and void masks. Opus audit plus Andrii's sign-off before any binding, dashboard or article use.
8.8 **R8 R4 text tightened.** "Adequate" does not unlock the M757 F3 regeneration-energy-by-grade-class quantity, does not change the M757 R2, R3 or R4 verdicts and does not explain the M757 placebo failure.
8.9 **R9 acquisition.** Per-source yes from Andrii (the Director's `needs_andrii_signoff: true` is this approval, and the later binding use); Andrii downloads anything behind a login; tiles go to a fresh `XT_DEM_DIR` sub-folder, are read with `python -I`, and the tool writes a sha256 manifest when they arrive, committed before the run.
8.10 **Conditions.** Rev 2 is committed before any download or result. The synthetic tests add: correlation between corners (the documented overestimate), void-mask intersection, and the pixel-is-point versus pixel-is-area half-pixel offset. `n` (steps, drives, days, km) accompanies every estimate.

## 10. Rev 4: Director rulings on Rev 3 (override sections 1 to 9 where they differ; review record `analyses/M762_director_review_rev3.json`, Rev 3 sha256 20f3de86a2cacb98...; decision: revise, every ruling adopted; committed before any manifest, code or result)

The dominant codes the tool used as "unflagged" (EDM 1, FLM 2) are exactly "not edited" and "not edited / not filled": the mask rule of 13 is CONFIRMED by the handbook, with no change of any value. The handbook also names the fill sources: pixels filled from SRTM (FLM 4, 5) or from AW3D30 (FLM 9) are flagged and excluded by the rule, which is the lineage guard intended in 8.1; in the used tiles FLM 9 is about 0.006 % of pixels, FLM 5 about 0.35 %, FLM 3 about 0.015 %. The first condition for lifting the provisional label ("handbook coding read and the mask rule confirmed") is therefore met; the second (the floor basis of the untouched stratum) is a ruling of the Director, not decided here.
