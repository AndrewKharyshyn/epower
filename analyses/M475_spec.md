# M475 spec (pre-registered): exact piecewise integration in the regen v2 extractor (audit 2026-10-21, "Measured optimization opportunities", adoption order third)

Status: REGISTERED NUMERICAL METHOD CHANGE of the interval extractor `tools/regen_v2.py` (EXTRACTOR_VERSION regen-v2.2 -> regen-v2.3). The estimand is unchanged. Gates: this spec (written before any comparison is run), blind audit (analytical-auditor), Director, and the M446 O7 escalation flags. Owner decisions respected: `regenDisplayV2` stays the cadence-stratified native display (2026-10-19); the B6 cadence-invariance check stays FAILED and shown; the run-once studies stay carried at their own basis.

## What changes
Today each speed interval is cut into slices of <= 0.05 s (20 Hz). On each slice the charge is a trapezoid of max(I V / 1000, 0) with I and V interpolated piecewise-linearly, and the slice's whole KE loss and charge go to the 10 km/h bin of the slice's mid-point speed. Both the quadrature and the bin assignment are approximations of a quantity that is exactly computable:
- I(t) and V(t) are linear between consecutive native samples; the speed is linear inside an interval. Pieces are cut at every native I and V sample time inside the interval and at every instantaneous-speed crossing of a bin edge (10, 20, ..., 130 km/h; bin 13 = overflow).
- On a piece I V is a quadratic; V > 0 (range 200-450 V), so max(I V, 0) is I V where I > 0. The positive part is integrated in closed form: the piece is cut at the zero of I (linear), and Simpson's rule (exact for quadratics) is applied to the positive sub-piece. The offset-charge variant uses I + i_off the same way.
- The KE loss of a piece is exactly v_a^2 - v_b^2; the bin of a piece is the bin of its mid-point speed (a piece never straddles an edge), so the bin assignment becomes exact instead of correct to one slice.
- The `touch` flag (a speed bin is touched by an interval) keeps its meaning at the former resolution: a bin is touched when the exact overlap is at least half a slice (0.025 s). This rule is registered here, not tuned after the comparison.
Unchanged: interval definition, engine-off, episode and trimming rules, attributes at interval start, the boxcar sensitivity path (it keeps the slice code, as it is defined on the 20 Hz grid), variants (lag, ceil2, decimation, common grids), the aggregation, all support floors.

## Pre-registered checks (thresholds fixed here)
E1 exactness (synthetic, closed form): constant I and V; linear I with a zero crossing; linear V; speed crossing exactly one and several bin edges; plateau on an edge; mixed knots. Exact integral agrees with the analytic value to 1e-9 relative; sum of piece KE losses equals v0^2 - v1^2 to 1e-9.
E2 convergence: on a stratified sample of drives (all variants of cadence present), the former slice method at h = 0.05, 0.025, 0.0125, 0.00625 s converges to the exact value with error shrinking about 4x per halving (second order); the exact value lies within the h = 0.00625 slice value by less than the h = 0.0125 error.
E3 per-drive and per-bin parity on all 606 drives (base variant): report the relative difference of total charge, of the offset charge, of KE (must be 0 to 1e-9), the number of intervals whose bin assignment changes, the number of touch flips, per drive and corpus-wide. Reporting only; expected scale per the audit (synthetic) 0.019 % of total charge.
E4 decision parity on the aggregated blocks (regenCaptureV2, regenCaptureV2H, regenSocStandardized, regenDisplayV2): old versus new cell by cell. Pre-registered ESCALATION to the Director (M446 Rev 1.1 O7): (a) any displayed cell changes support status; (b) S* changes in a displayed view; (c) a displayed point leaves its previous day-clustered interval; (d) the B6 cadence-invariance result changes; (e) any cell above 100 % appears or disappears. No tuning to avoid an escalation.
E5 timing: extraction time per drive for the old and the new code on the same sample (measured, reported with the environment; no pipeline speed-up is claimed beyond what is measured).

## Disclosure
The run-once study blocks (regenCaptureV2Study, regenCaptureV2HStudy, regenSocStandardizedStudy) were produced with the slice quadrature (extractor regen-v2.1, numerically identical to regen-v2.2) at the 578-drive basis and stay carried unchanged; their stamps state their own basis (M468). The cells of the live blocks use regen-v2.3 from this milestone; differences between a carried study cell and a live cell therefore include the quadrature change (stated in the CHANGELOG and the block provenance).

## Language
'exact piecewise integration of the logged channels (linear interpolation between native samples)'; never 'more accurate measurement', 'corrected', 'error fixed'; the quantity stays 'apparent battery-side KE recovery (assumed mass)'.

## Addendum: results of the pre-registered checks (recorded before the Director review)
- E1 exactness: `tests/synthetic/test_regen_exact_integral.py` passes (closed-form cases, telescoping KE, edge/plateau/overflow bins, touch rule at 0.025 s, random channels against a 2,000,001-point reference to 1e-6 relative).
- E2 convergence (40 sampled drives, seed 475): sum over drives of |slice(h) - exact| = 2.39e-4, 5.77e-5, 2.48e-5, 5.29e-6 kWh at h = 0.05, 0.025, 0.0125, 0.00625 s; ratios per halving 4.2, 2.3, 4.7 (about second order, noisy because max(I V, 0) has kinks); the exact value is the limit of the slice method. `analyses/M475_parity_convergence.json`.
- E3 parity (all 606 drives, 593 with intervals, 266,536 intervals): corpus charge +1.31e-5 relative (new 239.0659 vs old 239.0627 kWh); per drive median +8.7e-6, 5th-95th percentile -4.2e-5 to +5.4e-5, extremes -0.62 % and +0.23 %; KE (dv2) identical to 0; no interval changes its set of KE-bearing bins; no touch flip; largest single-interval charge difference 8.8e-6 kWh. `analyses/M475_parity.json`.
- E4 decisions (`tools/m475_cells.py`, `analyses/M475_cells.json`; blocks regenCaptureV2 1,350 cells, regenCaptureV2H 1,296, regenSocStandardized 64, regenDisplayV2 2,389; same keys in old and new): support-status changes 0; displayed points leaving their previous day-clustered interval 0 (cells and the 38 standardized bars); the one fixed reference mix identical (sha256 d355c9e7...); B6 reference not re-evaluated (the run-once study is carried at regen-v2.1, so the stored reference is identical by construction); cells above 100 % appearing or disappearing 0; the standardization state file records no flags. Shifts: supported cells at most 0.09 pp in regenDisplayV2 (0.07 in regenCaptureV2, 0.03 in the harmonized block), median 0.01 pp; the larger shifts (up to 0.89 pp) occur only in failed/thin cells with 1-12 intervals and no interval. Five support counts change by +1 interval (n 9730 -> 9731, 10035 -> 10036) in the speed bin 10-20 of the harmonized block regenCaptureV2H: the half-slice touch rule meeting a piece of about 0.025 s; no status change.
- E5 timing (one run, `analyses/M475_parity.json`, base variant, all 593 drives with intervals, this machine, 6 processes): whole-corpus extract_drive time 13.49 s (exact) vs 14.44 s (slices at 0.05 s), ratio 1.07, excluding the frame load; earlier runs of the same study gave 11.6 / 12.8 s (run-to-run variation of this order); a full cold run of the stage regen_v2 took 8 min 12 s. The extraction kernel is not a bottleneck: no pipeline speed-up is claimed (the audit's 32.6x was a synthetic 30,000-segment kernel).
- Blind audit (analytical-auditor, data bundle of two real drives with the definition only): per-interval charge, offset charge, per-bin KE and per-bin charge reproduced by an independent method to 1e-16 kWh (max relative 1.7e-11 on intervals above 1e-9 kWh), 0 bin mismatches in 834 intervals, closed-form handling of the 67 intervals with a zero crossing confirmed. Caveats recorded: the attribution of pack charge to speed bins treats dv2 as the KE change under an assumed mass (apparent recovery, not wheel-to-battery efficiency); linear interpolation of 1 Hz or slower samples is an assumption; the offset acts inside max(., 0) (nonlinear).
- Extreme drives of E3 (Director condition): the relative extremes are small-charge drives: 2026-10-13_07-42-34.csv -0.62 % on 3e-5 kWh over 5 intervals, 20260808_130411.csv -0.094 % on 7.0e-4 kWh (72 intervals), 20260723_200033.csv +0.23 % on 2.8e-4 kWh (16 intervals); no drive with appreciable charge differs by more than 0.02 %.
- Variant parity (E3 on every variant that feeds an aggregated block, report only, `analyses/M475_parity_variants.json`): decimated native variants d2p0, d2p1, d3p0, d3p1, d3p2: corpus charge +4.5e-6 to +8.9e-6 relative, KE identical, 0 KE-bin-set differences, 0 touch flips. Interpolated common-grid variants l3p0-l3p3 (regenCaptureV2H): corpus charge -2.8e-5 relative, KE identical, 228-272 of about 87,500 intervals have a different set of KE-bearing bins (a bin overlap shorter than half a former slice now carries its exact KE instead of the neighbouring bin), and ONE touch flip (l3p2): this is the origin of the five +1 support counts of the E4 list (regenCaptureV2H bin 10-20 speed views: n 9730 -> 9731 (all cohort, slow regime, two views) and 10035 -> 10036 (warm cohort, three views)); no status changes.
