## 1. Question and non-question
What is the highest net mean battery power the logger observed over 5 / 10 / 30 / 60 / 120 s windows, per drive and pooled across drives, in both directions (discharge: positive; charge: reported as a magnitude)? These are OBSERVED, window-based extrema of a logged, reconstructed I x V power. They are not ratings, capabilities, limits, operating-condition percentiles or time-weighted percentiles; longer windows can include reversals, so they are net moving means. The legacy 1 / 5 / 10 / 30 s sustained-current records (current, unrestricted interpolation) are a different quantity and are not reused (`sustained_current_peaks.py` is deprecated, M548).

## 2. Substrate (shared with M551)
The master's own current / voltage pair stream (`energy_packets.pair_stream`: `D._prep_bytes`, `D._series`, `D._asof` with `V_ALIGN_TOL`, |I| < 900, V 200..450; P = Id x V / 1000, discharge positive), power piecewise linear between pair samples, support rule: a raw step > 5 s is a GAP that ends the segment (nothing is bridged or extrapolated; no window spans a gap). Segment = maximal chain of supported intervals (raw step 0..5 s; duplicate timestamps allowed, they carry no time). Drives with `energy_null_reason` or `sign_check == ANOMALY` are excluded and counted; canonical-clean drives are the headline population.

## 3. Estimands
- Cumulative energy `E(t)` of the piecewise-linear power inside a segment is piecewise quadratic (exact: `E_i + p_i tau + (p_{i+1} - p_i) tau^2 / (2 d)` on each interval). The window mean over [s, s + W] is `(E(s + W) - E(s)) / W`, a piecewise quadratic function of the start `s`, with breakpoints where s or s + W meets a knot.
- PRIMARY: the EXACT continuous-start maximum and minimum of the window mean over `s` in [segment start, segment end - W] (candidates: every breakpoint and the vertex of each quadratic piece), per segment and per drive, W in {5, 10, 30, 60, 120} s. Discharge extreme = the maximum of the net mean (kW); charge extreme = the magnitude of the minimum (kW). A drive without a segment of length >= W is absent for that W (support counted).
- LOWER BRACKET: the 1 s start grid anchored at each segment start (what the audit computes). SENSITIVITY: the same grid shifted by 0.5 s. Reported per stratum: the median and 95th percentile of (exact - grid) per drive, and the change of the pooled p95 under the shift.
- Pooled across drives: percentiles of the per-drive extremes (p50, p90, p95, p99, max); day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws, the drive is the unit inside a calendar day cluster, days resampled) for p50, p90, p95; p99 needs at least 20 days for an interval, otherwise the point value, labelled exploratory; fewer than 5 days: no interval.
- STRATA: logger cadence regime (`cadence_regime.csv`: fast, slow, unknown), no pooled headline, no contrast between strata (B6 cadence invariance still fails).
- Duration confound: a drive with more eligible time has more windows, so its maximum tends to be larger. Per stratum the distribution of eligible duration (sum of segment time >= W, per W) is reported, and a sensitivity restricts to drives with at least 600 s (and 1800 s) of eligible time for W.
- The uninterrupted-direction variant of the audit (no sign change inside the window) is NOT built here (optional in the audit); named follow-up.

## 4. Known answers (pre-registered)
- KA1 (constant power P0): every window value equals P0 for every W; exact = grid = P0.
- KA2 (triangular ramp from 0 to P over T s): the window mean is analytic; the exact maximum equals the analytic value at the window ending at the peak; a start grid that misses the optimum gives the analytic shortfall; the 0.5 s shift changes the grid maximum by the analytic amount.
- KA3 (gapped segment): windows spanning a 6 s gap are absent; a window ending exactly at the gap edge is counted; a segment shorter than W gives no window for that W; a drive with only short segments is absent for that W.
- KA4 (hard gate, real drives): per drive the exact 5 s discharge extreme is <= `peak_discharge_kw` + 0.05 kW and the 5 s charge magnitude <= |`peak_charge_kw`| + 0.05 kW (a window mean cannot exceed the sample extreme; the master peaks are rounded to 0.1 kW). It is implied mathematically, so a violation means a pairing or implementation error and STOPS; it is not a validation. Also per drive: exact >= both grids by construction (checked).
- KA5 (closure): per drive the sum over segments of the integral of the power (`E` at the segment ends) plus the capped gap energy (net) equals the master `net_draw_kwh` within 1e-4 kWh.
- KA6 (monotone in W, hard gate restricted to comparable segments): per drive, over the segments eligible for both W1 < W2, the exact discharge extreme is non-increasing in W (a longer window mean is bounded by the maximum of its sub-window means); a violation is a bug and STOPS. (Across all segments it need not hold, because a longer W can lose a short segment.)
- Expectations (not gates): per stratum the pooled p95 decreases with W in both directions; charge extremes exceed discharge extremes at 5 s. The audit shows the same ordering; disagreement is reported, not tuned.
