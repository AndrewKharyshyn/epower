# M460 spec (Rev 1, DRAFT for Director review) - zero-logged-fuel distance share as the companion to the RPM-defined engine-off share (follow-up of M432 / audit F04)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT, written before any result exists. Rev 1, 2026-10-19. Owner: Andrii (chat, 2026-10-19: "M432 next"). Origin: `analyses/M432_spec.md` section 1 ("Not an estimand: the zero-fuel (motored-engine) definition of electric ... needs the fuel PID subset and a different population") and the open item (a) of the `evTraction` block ("a zero-fuel variant (engine-off OR motored-unfuelled) would be the natural companion metric for a fuel-consumption argument and is not yet emitted"). Scope: ONE additive payload block; the released engine-off share (`evTraction.evPctDistance`, 35.4 %) and its M432 detector-sensitivity range stay as they are; no dashboard change in this spec.

## 1. Question and states

For a fuel-consumption argument "electric" can mean two different things: the engine is not turning (RPM-defined, the released metric) or no fuel is being used (zero logged fuel rate). They differ when the engine turns without fuel (motored, e.g. engine braking or a start-up) or when it is stopped but a fuel rate is still logged. How large is each difference in distance terms, on the drives that carry both channels?

Per aligned 1 Hz second, with the released RPM rule (`eng_rpm <= 400`, nearest alignment 3 s, the released debounce) and a fuel-rate state `ZF` = the logged fuel rate is known and `<= eps`:
- A = RPM-off and ZF ("engine off, no fuel");
- B = RPM-on and ZF ("engine turning, no fuel": motored-unfuelled);
- C = RPM-off and NOT ZF ("engine stopped, fuel rate logged": an inconsistency flag, not a state of interest);
- D = RPM-on and NOT ZF ("fuelled").
Seconds with an unknown RPM or an unknown fuel rate belong to none of A-D; their distance is reported separately and is in the denominator of no share.

## 2. Estimand and population (named before any result)

Distance in each state = the integrated speed distance of its seconds times the odometer scale `k` of the released code (so that A-D sum to the drive's `ev_dist_km`-compatible distance). Population `P_F` = canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2` False, the 7 NaN drives kept as in M432) that carry the logged fuel-rate channel `Витрати палива (L/h)` (script-listed; 353 raw files carry it, 352 of them canonical), with `ev_valid` and the coverage gates of the released code satisfied for the RPM and speed channels, and the fuel-rate channel covering >= 95 % of the grid seconds (a declared gate; drives failing it are listed). Quantities, each a ratio of sums over `P_F` (percent of the drive distances in A-D):
- `share_RPM = (A + C) / (A + B + C + D)` (the RPM-defined share on THIS population; not the released 35.4 %, which is on a different population);
- `share_ZF = (A + B) / (A + B + C + D)` (zero-logged-fuel share);
- `motored = B / (A + B + C + D)` and `inconsistent = C / (...)`;
- `diff = share_ZF - share_RPM = (B - C) / (...)`, the headline of this study.
Every estimate carries n (drives, days) and a day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval (calendar day as the cluster, `numpy.random.default_rng(42)`, `integers(0, D, (B, D))`, B = 4000, the days with data of `P_F`; ratios recomputed inside each draw).

## 3. Conventions and sensitivities (declared before any result)

Primary `eps = 0` (the logged rate is exactly 0). The channel is discrete (median over files of the smallest positive value 0.35 L/h, file medians of the number of distinct values 34; sampling interval median 1.30 s, file medians 0.32-3.79 s; script `analyses/M460_inventory/fuel_channel_health.py`), so sensitivities are `eps` in {0.05, 0.10, 0.35} L/h and a counter-based zero-fuel state (the logged fuel counter `Використане паливо (L)` has a zero increment over the sample interval; independent of the rate channel). Alignment of the fuel-rate channel: nearest with the released 3 s tolerance (primary); 1.5 s as a sensitivity. All sensitivities are ranges over declared conventions, never combined with the statistical interval, and are reported on common support (the drives valid under both settings), with support gained and lost listed as in M432. Mandatory strata (descriptive, same machinery): the polling stratum (fast / slow, master `ev_rpm_dt_med_s`) and the thermal regime (cohort `warm` / `shoulder` / `cold`), because the sampling interval of the channel varies by an order of magnitude.

## 4. Reading rules (no decision threshold; declared in advance)

(R1) The released share and its detector-sensitivity range are not changed by this block. (R2) `diff` is reported with its interval; if the interval lies within +-1 pp the wording is "the two definitions do not differ materially on these drives under the stated conventions" (not "equal", not "equivalent"); otherwise the size is stated as found, with `motored` and `inconsistent` decomposed. (R3) A large `inconsistent` share (> 10 % of the RPM-off distance) is a data-quality flag on the fuel-rate channel (alignment, app calculation), not a finding about the controller. (R4) Nothing here is a physical claim about injection: the channel is logged / app-calculated (CLAUDE.md), the state is "zero logged fuel rate", and no mechanism (fuel cut, engine braking, start-up) is attributed.

## 5. Gates, tests, chain

(G1) Reconciliation: the RPM-defined state recomputed here equals the released `ev_dist_km` of each drive of `P_F` (the same detector, sum within 1e-6 km) and A + B + C + D + unknown = the drive distance. (G2) Known-answer tests (`tests/synthetic/test_zero_fuel.py`): a synthetic drive with all four states and known distances reproduces A-D; unknown seconds are excluded from every share; `diff = (B - C)/total`; the counter-based state needs a zero increment; `eps` thresholds; whole-day resampling is reproducible. (G3) Deep diff: only the new key (`evTraction.zeroFuelVariant`) and its stamp change; every other leaf is identical (single-key producer identity as M458). (G4) `release_check.py` and the CI unit-test list green; spec SHA-256 in the CHANGELOG before the first run, first output kept (run-once lock as M444); a blind analytical-auditor reproduces the states, `share_RPM`, `share_ZF` and the interval from the raw files and sections 1-3; Director ruling. A dashboard disclosure, if wanted, is a separate step with its own sign-off.

## 6. Limits (declared in advance)

The fuel rate is app-calculated and discrete; `P_F` is the fuel-instrumented subset (352 canonical drives) and is not the released population, so `share_RPM` on `P_F` differs from 35.4 % and the two are never mixed; the alignment of two channels with different sampling intervals can mislabel short states (debounce and tolerance sensitivities cover this); no causal or controller claim; one vehicle, one driver.

## 7. Open questions for the Director

- **Q1** Primary `eps = 0` versus a resolution-based `eps` (0.05 or 0.35 L/h) given the discreteness of the channel.
- **Q2** Is the counter-based state a sound independent definition, or does it inherit the same app calculation?
- **Q3** Should the debounce of the released RPM rule also be applied to the ZF state (symmetry), or is the raw aligned ZF state right for a state that can legitimately be short (fuel cut during braking)?
- **Q4** The +-1 pp reading rule and the 10 % inconsistency flag: confirm or replace.
- **Q5** Is the study worth running (does it change any displayed statement)? A "no" is acceptable.

## 8. Rev 1.1 (2026-10-19): Director review (verdict "revise"; the study is worth running); sections 2-7 are superseded where they differ

**Facts the Director verified, adopted.** `_ev_metrics` returns aggregates only (no per-second states), so the study needs a NEW evaluator that rebuilds the released grid with the released helpers (`_prep_bytes`, `_series`, `ENG_ALIGN_TOL`, `_ev_debounce`). The released docstring says the zero-fuel variant "would be strictly larger"; this study can falsify that (`C > B` makes the zero-fuel share smaller). The fuel-rate channel has implausible values in at least one file (it logs only 0 and 154.75 L/h).

**Changes adopted (D1-D6, Q1-Q5).** (a) CONSTRUCT-VALIDITY GATE before the headline (D1): over the drives of `P_F` that carry boost and load, the share of seconds classified as motored-unfuelled by the M46 / M48 classifier (rpm > 2000, boost <= -0.7 bar, calculated load <= 8 %, thresholds verbatim from `_dissipation_census`) that also carry the debounced state `ZF`; if that share is below 50 % (declared now) the channel cannot see the motored state, the study reports "not identifiable" with the share and STOPS (the headline is not emitted). (b) Primary `eps = 0`; `eps` 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.35 L/h sensitivities; a declared plausibility range: logged rates above 40 L/h are treated as unknown (the sensitivity table lists the drives affected) (Q1). (c) The state `ZF` uses the SAME debounce as the RPM state (primary): `_ev_debounce(known_fuel & (fuel <= eps), 3) & known_fuel`, i.e. unknown counted as not-ZF inside the debounce, the first run exempt, `& known` applied afterwards; the raw aligned `ZF` is a sensitivity; an undebounced RPM x `ZF` cross-tab is a diagnostic (Q3). (d) The counter-based state is a CONSISTENCY ROW only ("same app calculation", not an independent definition), after a declared resolution check (the share of fuelled seconds with a zero counter increment is reported) (Q2). (e) Reading margin +-1 pp (the whole day-clustered interval inside it; not TOST, not equivalence); the 10 % flag is `C / (A + C)`; if it fires `diff` is not interpreted (Q4). (f) G1 corrected (D2): `ev_dist_km` of the master = `A + C + (RPM-off with unknown fuel)` within the stored rounding (5e-4 km per drive), checked by a parity known-answer test on 10 drives chosen by the declared rule "the first ten drives of `P_F` in file-name order". (g) Denominators named (D3): the PRIMARY shares are `sum(distance in the state) / sum(distance_km)` over `P_F` (the released denominator); the unknown-fuel distance share is reported beside them; the known-support shares (A-D only in the denominator) are secondary. (h) `P_F` versus the released population `P0` (483 drives) is described by the script (D5): drive and day counts, date span, cohort mix, logging generation, fuel-channel sampling-interval distribution and the support lost at the 1.5 s alignment. (i) Strata (D6): the polling stratum of the RPM channel (master `ev_rpm_dt_med_s`), the fuel-poll stratum (per-drive median sampling interval of the fuel-rate channel below / at least 1.0 s) and the thermal cohort; label "raw/ = sha256-verified originals (M366)". Forbidden wording added: fuel cut, injection off, burns no fuel, zero fuel consumption, measured fuel rate, "strictly larger" (unless found), equal / equivalent, "independent counter". Risks recorded: `ZF` may be driven by the same MAF-based app calculation as the RPM-independent load (circular), `C` may be inflated by debounce or alignment, `P_F` is seasonally skewed, `B` is expected to be small (about 1386 s corpus-wide per the Director's check).

## 9. Rev 1.2 (2026-10-19): result and blind audit (no rerun, no number changes)

R1 The first and only run (`analyses/M460_result.json`, lock `analyses/M460_first.lock`) produced status "computed": P_F = 307 drives on 60 days (2026-08-12 to 2026-10-13; 176 of the 483 released-population drives carry no usable fuel-rate channel or fail the coverage gate), G1 parity passed (maximum 5.0e-4 km), the construct-validity gate passed narrowly (52 of 91 classifier-motored seconds, 57.1 %; the 91-second basis is small and its binomial interval includes values below the 50 % minimum), the inconsistency flag did not fire (C/(A+C) = 0.8 %). The pre-registered reading rule gives: "the difference is stated as found with its decomposition" because the whole interval of `diff` (+0.99 pp [0.86, 1.14]) is not within +-1 pp; the sensitivities reach 1.49 pp. The wording "the two definitions do not differ materially" is NOT licensed. R2 The blind audit (`analyses/M460_audit.json`, accept) reproduced every share and interval limit at 3 decimals; details that the spec left implicit and that the evaluator implements, recovered by the auditor from master parity (575 of 575 comparable drives): the 1 s grid starts at the first valid timestamp (not on integer seconds), nearest alignment by `merge_asof`, speed from the VCM channel when present else the OBD speed, the odometer scale `k` = odometer range / integrated distance. R3 The motored share (B) depends on the convention (34.5 km with the debounce at eps 0, 41.0 km undebounced, 41.1 km at eps 0.35): it is a convention range, not a point. R4 The result bears on the released docstring claim "a zero-fuel variant would be strictly larger": on P_F it is larger by about 1 pp (B exceeds C by a factor of about 3.8 in distance); that is a finding about these drives and these conventions, not about the controller, and the released 35.4 % and its M432 range are unchanged.
