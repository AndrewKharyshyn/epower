## 1. What the inventory of the raw headers shows (coverage only; read before this spec, no outcome statistic was computed)

Script `analyses/M457_inventory/mf_inventory.py` (written with this spec; output `mf_inventory.csv`) reads the headers and the range of the candidate channels (the counts below are the script's output):
- `[BMS] Input possible power (hp)` and `[BMS] Output possible power (hp)`: 252 canonical drives, dates 2026-05-15 to 2026-09-30; a separate `(Short) (hp)` variant in 4 more drives has a different range (0 to about 31) and unknown semantics, so those drives are not used.
- `[BMS] Input possible power (kW)` and `[BMS] Output possible power (kW)`: 89 canonical drives, dates 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-13 (the unit changes with the logger regime change of 2026-10-01).
- Absent: 233 canonical drives (2026-05-11 to 2026-08-16); `G07 BMS Power Limits ()` in 341 raw files and `[VCM] Motor Regenerable Power Maximum (%)` in 6 raw files (semantics unknown, not used in this stage).
- Value ranges: the hp-labelled input channel has a maximum of 86.74 in every file that reaches its plateau; the kW-labelled channel has a maximum of 63.75. 86.68 x 0.73550 (metric horsepower, PS) = 63.75 kW, whereas 86.68 x 0.74570 (mechanical hp) = 64.64 kW. The agreement of the two eras' plateaus therefore points to metric horsepower, but this is an inference from one number per era, not a documented unit: `verified: false`.

## 2. Estimand and why the primary is unit-free

The channel is read as the **BMS-reported possible input power** (the charge acceptance the BMS reports at the time; positive = charge allowed). Its semantics, sign and unit are assumptions until verified (section 4). The primary quantities are therefore **normalized within the era**: `u(t) = P_in(t) / P_ref,era`, where `P_ref,era` is the 99.9th percentile of the samples of the era (hp era; kW era) over all drives of that era (script-computed, stored). `u` is unit-free, so the hp / kW / PS question does not enter the primary. A conversion to kW (PS factor, the inferred one) appears only in a secondary field and carries `verified: false`.

Per kept v2 interval (M441 Rev 2.2 kept population; assumed masses; polling stratum as in M446) the interval value is the time-weighted mean of the latest-sample-hold of `u` over the interval, with an age rule (a sample older than 5 s is not used; the interval then has no `u`).

## 3. Quantities reported (all descriptive, per cohort x polling stratum x era x cell; cells = pack-temperature bin x SOC band, M441 support floors)

(Q-a) Coverage: the KE share and the interval share of the kept intervals that carry `u`, per cell, per era (the absent-channel drives are excluded from this block only, never from the primary analysis). (Q-b) The KE-weighted distribution of `u` per cell (median and the 10th / 90th percentile) and the KE-weighted share with `u` below 0.9 and below 0.5. (Q-c) The apparent KE recovery (ratio of sums, v2 definition, day-clustered 95 % interval) of the intervals with `u >= 0.9` and of those with `u < 0.9`, per cell, with the cell counts; unavailable if a side is below the support floors. (Q-d) The one pre-registered contrast: the SOC- and temperature-standardized (M444 machinery, the same reference mix) difference `R(u >= 0.9) - R(u < 0.9)` over the cells that have both sides supported, with a day-clustered interval; reported as a descriptive association, unadjusted for speed and demand. (Q-e) The charge power actually drawn: the interval mean battery-terminal charge power relative to `P_in` is NOT used to infer a limit; it is reported as the distribution of `p_charge / P_in` per cell with the explicit note that low values can reflect low braking demand.

## 4. Verification of semantics, sign and unit (gate, not a result)

(V1) Sign: `P_in >= 0` on every sample; a negative or zero fraction is reported. (V2) Plateau: each era has a plateau (a mode at the maximum) and the fraction of samples on the plateau is reported; the two eras' plateau values are compared after the PS and the mechanical-hp conversion (the inferred unit is recorded as `verified: false`). (V3) Dependence on the state: the cell medians of `u` against SOC and pack temperature are tabulated (the BMS acceptance is expected to fall at high SOC and low temperature; a channel that does not vary with either is flagged "no state dependence observed", which weakens its use but is not an error). (V4) Time alignment: the cross-correlation lag between `u` and the interval charge power is reported; no lag is applied. (V5) The `Output possible power` channel is inventoried only. If V1-V3 fail the block is written with `status: "channel not verified"` and the quantities (Q-b)-(Q-e) are omitted.

## 5. Uncertainty, gates, language

Day-clustered percentile bootstrap (`numpy.random.default_rng(42)`, `integers(0, D, (B, D))`, B = 4000; the days with data of the population; M444 S14), ratio of sums inside every draw, failure policy and support floors as M441 / M444. Gates: (G1) the primary v2 payload (`regenCaptureV2`, `regenDisplayV2`) is byte-identical after the stage; (G2) known-answer tests: era normalization, latest-sample-hold with the age rule, an interval without a channel sample carries no `u`, the contrast uses a fixed reference mix, whole-day resampling; (G3) deep diff shows only the new key and its stamp; (G4) `release_check.py`; the spec SHA-256 is recorded in the CHANGELOG before the first run, the first output is kept (run-once lock), a blind analytical-auditor reproduces the era plateaus, the coverage of one cell and the contrast Q-d; Director ruling. Language: "BMS-reported possible input power (normalized within its logging era)"; never "limit imposed", "the BMS limits regeneration", "charge acceptance limit identified", "explains the lower recovery", "caused by", "unit verified" (the unit remains inferred); an observed `u` below the plateau is not proof that the battery imposed a limit, and a recovery fraction below 1 is not evidence of a limit.

## 6. What would stop or change the stage

(i) V1-V3 fail or the channel never leaves its plateau in the braking intervals: the block states the channel is uninformative here. (ii) Fewer than two supported cells with both sides of Q-d: the contrast is unavailable (reported, not imputed). (iii) Any change to a v2 estimate: the stage fails G1. (iv) Spec changes after the freeze are clarifications appended as `Rev x.y`.

## 8. Rev 1.1 (2026-10-19): Director review (verdict "revise"); sections 2-7 are superseded where they differ

**Inventory facts (script output `analyses/M457_inventory/mf_inventory.csv`, re-checked against the master; D1).** Canonical drives by channel: hp-labelled input possible power 252 (2026-08-14 to 2026-09-30), kW-labelled 89 (2026-09-30 to 2026-10-13), the `(Short) (hp)` variant 4 (2026-05-15 to 2026-08-13; range 0 to about 31, semantics unknown, not used), none 233; 252 + 89 + 4 + 233 = 578. The hp-labelled plateau is NOT a single value: 86.6759 in 205 files and 86.7439 in 34 files (from 2026-08-29); the kW-labelled plateau is 63.75. At the metric-horsepower factor these are 63.75 and 63.80 kW; the unit stays `verified: false`. The era is defined by the HEADER (hp or kW label), not by date (the kW label starts 2026-09-30 20:12). Samples per drive: 10 to 777 (hp), 19 to 958 (kW), i.e. the channel is sampled roughly every 20-40 s. The resolution differs between eras (about 0.05 kW-equivalent versus 0.25 kW); equal semantics across eras is not established.

**Changes adopted (D1-D8).** (a) `P_ref,era` is the MODAL plateau of the era (the plateau values above are recorded), not the 99.9th percentile (Q1). (b) The channel is read through a SIDECAR cache keyed by the raw-file SHA-256 and the sidecar extractor source hash; the audited v2 cache and every audited block stay byte-stable (Q2, D8). (c) The block is strictly TABULAR: Q-a, Q-b, Q-c (per cell) and Q-e; Q-d (the standardized contrast) is demoted to a secondary descriptive figure computed within era x stratum only and never pooled or contrasted across eras (Q3, Q5). (d) The primary split is ON-PLATEAU (within one quantization step of the era plateau) versus BELOW; the thresholds 0.9 and 0.5 of the plateau are declared sensitivities, not the primary (Q4). (e) The 5 s age rule is replaced by a justified 30 s age rule (the channel's own sampling is about 20-40 s) with sensitivities at 10 s and 60 s; the sample-interval distribution of the channel is reported before any outcome (D2). (f) V4 (cross-correlation lag) is dropped: a lag cannot be resolved at this cadence; the sample-interval check replaces it (D3). (g) Every statistic is reported within era x stratum (hp era / kW era x fast / slow polled); the era x stratum drive and day cross-tab is reported first, because the kW era coincides with the fast-polled regime (Q5). (h) FUTILITY GATE before any outcome: the block stops with `status: "channel uninformative here"` if the KE share of the below-plateau intervals is under a floor of 2 % of the era x stratum KE, or if those intervals come from fewer than 5 days per era x stratum (D5). (i) Q-c and the secondary Q-d are labelled association only: below-plateau states co-occur with cold, high SOC and preceding charge (reverse dependence), and are unadjusted for demand and speed (D6). (j) Deviation from the outside prompt, stated: the prompt asks for normalization after verifying the unit; the unit cannot be verified from the data, so the primary is unit-free within the era (D7). (k) The block is separate from `highSocRegen` and transfers no result into the v2 levels (D8). Forbidden wording added (language gate when implemented): BMS-limited, power-limited intervals, charge acceptance limit, battery refused, measured possible power, measured charge acceptance, unconstrained intervals, limit-free, unit confirmed, explains the gap.
