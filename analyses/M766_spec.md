# M766 spec: replace the X1 note (logged distance versus the cloud-reported odometer) with the revised jump rule P ("revised rule")

Rev 1, 2026-11-24. Committed BEFORE any code or run. Author: main session (Sonnet). Owner: "Yes" to the question whether to spec the X1 replacement straight after M765. Parents: `analyses/M765_spec.md` Rev 3 (rule P, replacement trigger 7.9), result `analyses/M765_jump_rule.json`, audit `analyses/M765_audit.json`, Director decision `analyses/M765_director_decision.json` (allowed wording, disclosure sentence, conditions C1 to C3), `analyses/M760_spec.md` (the X1 note, its payload and wording rules), `analyses/M752_spec.md` (design), tool `tools/drive_odometer.py`. Number taken after checking the CHANGELOG head (M765) and open PRs.

## 0. Why, and what is replaced
- M765 fired the replacement trigger: under the physical rule P the displayed X1 numbers change (n 418 to 422, available 584 to 588, relative difference +1.20 % [1.04, 1.41] to +1.13 % [0.92, 1.39], median interval, strata and sensitivities). The Director ruled (M765 decision, ruling 3): the X1 block is replaced in its own milestone (spec, blind audit, Director ruling, wording "revised rule"), EVERY displayed X1 number from ONE P run (primary, strata, E_br, +16 s with P on the shifted endpoint rows, coverage by month), never mixing B and P numbers; the P-minus-excursion sensitivity is pre-registered here (C2); no article use without an Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off (C3).
- M765 computed only the primary agreement, E_br, the +16 s sensitivity and availability by month under P; the strata, the coverage by stratum and by reason and the remaining M752 outputs are not in the M765 result. This milestone adds one tool that reruns the full M752 output set under P from the pinned store and master, writes a new registered result, and replaces the X1 block in `cloudCrossChecks` and its dashboard note from that result. The M752 and M765 results stay on record unchanged.

## 1. Constraints
- Registered master (MD5 `d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98`) and cloud store (manifest sha256 `7cbab168...`) pinned and read-only; the M752 tool (sha256 `741bc24b...`) is not edited (the M765 tool pins it); the new tool imports its stream, bracket and statistics helpers.
- No absolute cloud odometer value, no cloud km total and no drive names or dates in any public artifact (M749 rule): the new result carries the M752 anchor only as `differenceKm` and `n` (the M752 result's absolute anchor fields are not copied); a privacy scan blocks the write.
- Dashboard change rule (CLAUDE.md): rebuild, render with the `S.*` keys, commit the dashboard with its sources in one commit. No master column, no change to `distance_km`, the logged-share KPI or the ODO rule. Article use: not requested.

## 2. New tool and result
- `tools/drive_odometer_rev.py` (optional stage, skipped without the store): reproduces the M752 outputs with the jump rule as a parameter. **R0 control:** run with rule B (the M752 tool rule) it must reproduce the registered M752 result leaf by leaf (availability by reason for all five estimators, selection by stratum and month, the whole P1 agreement block with intervals, P2) or the run stops (exit 3) and nothing is interpreted. **R1 P run:** the same code with rule P per estimator (spec M765 7.5: E_int and E_br use the endpoint rows from the last row at or before t0 to the first row at or after t1; E_near and E_near60 use the endpoint rows of their nearest-row windows, stated in the result; the +16 s sensitivity uses the shifted endpoint rows).
- Result `analyses/M766_odometer_agreement_P.json`: provenance (master MD5, store manifest sha256, M752 tool sha256, M752 and M765 result sha256, this tool's sha256, seed 42, 4000 draws), the control, `P0_coverage` (five estimators, unavailable by reason: outside span, no row within cap, hole, odometer jump under P), `P0_selection_check` (by stratum and by month), `P1_agreement` (the full M752 schema: primary identity class of 2 km or more, all drives of 2 km or more, identity class, strata), the three-valued verdict words (M765 ruling C1), `P2_resolution`, `P3_anchor` (difference and n only), the P versus B side-by-side counts from M765 (17, 13, 7; 4 kept, 2 and 6 relative to B3), the excursion sensitivities, `deviations_from_spec`.
- **P-minus-excursion sensitivity (C2, pre-registered).** Two definitions, both frozen: S1 (strict, as in M765) drops drives with an endpoint time t0 or t1 inside a level excursion span (first to last row below the running maximum by more than 0.05 km; 22 drives expected from M765); S2 (wide) drops drives with an endpoint between the last row before an excursion and the first row after it (31 expected). Reported: n (drives, days), median difference and relative difference with intervals, the verdict words; never selected for its result; P stays the primary. The disclosure states that P can miss an excursion that starts before the first endpoint row.

## 3. Payload and dashboard
- `tools/cloud_payload.py` `build_x1` reads the new result (pinned sha256 in PINS) in place of the M752 result and takes the 17 versus 7 counts from the M765 result; the field names stay where they are used by the page (`nDrives`, `nDays`, `available`, `medianDifferenceKm`, `relativeDifferencePercent`, `verdict`, `strata`, `sensitivity`, `coverage`, `integrity`, `anchor`, `disclosures`); new fields: `ruleLabel` ("revised rule P"), `verdict.within1pct` and `within3pct` as the three-valued strings (script-written from the interval, never typed), `excursionSensitivity` (S1 and S2). The M752 audit counts (7 and 586) are kept ONLY inside the script-written disclosure sentence of the Director (17 versus 7 from the extra window rows; under P 4 drives of the tool rule are kept; relative to the audit-like rule 2 it excludes and 6 it keeps; the 6-drive availability difference is not resolved).
- Dashboard note (`CloudCrossCheckDistance`): the headline names the rule ("revised rule P") and prints the verdict strings from the payload: under P the interval includes 1 %, so the sentence is "the interval includes 1 %; consistent within +-3 %", never "above", "exceeds" or "reaches beyond 1 %", never "within 1 %", no TOST or equivalence reading (M765 allowed wording). The change of the registered numbers is stated once in the disclosures ("the earlier note used the M752 window rule; replaced by the revised rule P").
- Language gate: the M760 rule stays; a new rule (ledger M766) forbids, near the X1 note, "above 1 %", "exceeds 1 %", "reaches beyond 1 %" unless the payload interval lies above 1 % (checked by the payload test, not by a static regex on the page), and "correction" in any form. `release_check`: the X1 block carries `ruleLabel`, no key in the forbidden set (`cloudLastOdoKm`, `cloudPlusLogged`), and its numbers equal the registered P result leaf by leaf.

## 4. Decision rules (frozen)
- R1 (control): R0 must reproduce the M752 result leaf by leaf (tolerance 0); a mismatch stops the run.
- R2 (consistency with M765): the P primary n, days, relative difference and interval, the median and interval, E_br and +16 s agreements, available counts and availability by month must equal the M765 P results (tolerance 0); a mismatch stops the run and goes to the Director.
- R3 (payload): block values equal the registered P result leaf by leaf; the splice changes only `/cloudCrossChecks` (and its stamp); the master MD5 is unchanged; no absolute cloud value in the block.
- R4 (display): every displayed X1 number binds to `S.cloudCrossChecks.x1LoggedDistance.*`; no B number is displayed except inside the disclosure sentence; the page states "estimated, lower bound"-style limits of M760 unchanged (same wheel-speed scale, not distance accuracy, selection by month and length).
- R5 (falsification): if the rendered numbers differ from the registered P result at any leaf, or a forbidden word or a B number appears outside the disclosure, the milestone does not merge. If the S1 or S2 sensitivity moves the sign of the difference or the +-3 % word, the result goes to the Director before any display.
