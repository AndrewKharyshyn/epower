# M677 spec (Rev 1): N-04, early-versus-late contrast within matched state bins (exploratory, payload only)

Status: pre-registered before any result for this contrast exists. Backlog item N-04 (`analyses/self_audit_2026-11-11/backlog.json`; depends on N-01, done as M622/M634). Related, already published: the Sep-versus-late matched contrast of the cell spread (M573/M574, `cellSpreadStep.matchedContrast`); this spec generalises the design to three outcomes and pre-registers it as one rule. Nothing below was computed before this spec was written.

## 1. Question and non-claims
Is the level of the resistance proxy, the loaded cell spread and the apparent Ah per 100 % SOC different between the first and the last third of the logged window when drives are compared only inside cells of equal pack temperature, peak current, SOC class and logging cadence?
Non-claims. A same-season year-on-year comparison is NOT available in a 12-month study (at most one month of overlap), so this is a within-study early-versus-late contrast, not an ageing estimate: season, route mix, load, logger configuration and the HV-battery software update (owner-reported around 2026-08-25, which falls between the two windows) move together with time on one vehicle. No causal or ageing wording; the result is "difference between early and late drives in matched state bins". Not a capacity statement: the Ah outcome is the apparent Ah per 100 % SOC (`cap_ah_est`). Resistance proxy and cell spread are estimated / model-derived. Exploratory; no article figure; no display.

## 2. Windows, outcomes and eligibility
Population: canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2 == False`), 651 drives at this basis. Windows are fixed by the calendar, not by the result: the window is the span from the first to the last clean-drive date; early = first third of that span, late = last third (script computes the boundary dates; the middle third is excluded, it contains the software-update date). Outcomes (existing columns, none recomputed): Y1 resistance proxy `a1_R` (`resistance_joint_sidecar.csv`, drives with `a1_n >= 600`), Y2 `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv`, Y3 `cap_ah_est`.
Match variables per drive (all existing master columns, the controls of the released degradation trends and of M573): pack temperature `T_pack_mean_avg` (drive-mean pack temperature), peak current `peak_I_discharge`, SOC level `rf_socmean_wtd_pct` (the M573 SOC covariate; "SOC start" below means this column), and the cadence regime (fast / slow, `cadence_regime.csv`). Drives with a missing match variable or outcome are dropped per outcome and counted.

## 3. Primary estimator (Path A, coarsened exact matching)
Cells: pack temperature in five classes at the pooled quintile edges of the eligible drives, peak current in five classes at pooled quintile edges, SOC level in four classes at pooled quartile edges, cadence regime in two classes (edges and classes are computed once from the pooled eligible drives of the outcome and written to the block). A cell is used when it holds at least 3 early and 3 late drives and at least 2 calendar days on each side. Estimand: the late-minus-early difference of the outcome mean, averaged over used cells with weights equal to the number of late drives in the cell (the effect for the late drives), reported in the outcome units and as a percent of the early matched mean. Share of late drives matched is reported; a contrast is "not shown" if fewer than 10 late drives on fewer than 5 days are matched, and labelled "weakly overlapping" below 50 % matched late drives.
Interval: day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws; calendar day is the cluster, days resampled with replacement within the early and the late group; cell edges stay fixed; a draw recomputes the used cells and weights; draws with no used cell are dropped and counted). Reported with n drives, n days (early and late), n cells, and the minimum detectable difference at 80 % power (2.80 x the bootstrap SE).

## 4. Path B and disagreement
Path B: ordinary least squares of the outcome on a late indicator with the pack temperature (Arrhenius form 1000/(T+273.15) for Y1, linear for Y2 and Y3), peak current, SOC level and the cadence regime, calendar-day cluster-robust (CR1) standard errors, on the same early and late drives. The two paths are compared by sign and by interval overlap with zero; disagreement is reported and never tuned to agree. A statement that a difference "persists" needs the same sign and an interval excluding zero under both paths.

## 5. Pre-registered sensitivities (shown beside the primary)
(a) halves instead of thirds (early = first half, late = last half); (b) cadence strata analysed separately (fast, slow); (c) late window restricted to 2026-09-01 and later and early to before 2026-07-01 (a wider gap); (d) exclusion of drives within 7 days of the owner-reported software-update date 2026-08-25 (only relevant for the halves variant; the date enters as an exclusion, never as a detection input); (e) leave-one-week-out of the primary contrast (ISO-week), reporting the range. No sensitivity changes the primary.

## 6. Validity and tests
V1 re-running is byte-identical (seed 42). V2 n drives per outcome equals the finite count after eligibility and the early/late split, and early and late sets are disjoint. V3 cell counts sum to the matched drive counts. V4 weights sum to one. Known-answer test `tests/synthetic/test_m678_early_late.py`: planted early-late shift in a matched design recovered with the interval covering it; a pure covariate shift (late drives hotter, outcome depends on temperature only) gives a matched difference near zero while the unmatched difference is large; no overlap in a cell: cell not used; empty-draw handling; day clustering widens the interval relative to treating drives as independent when a day effect is planted; Path A and Path B agree on a planted linear design and disagree (reported) on a planted interaction.

## 7. Outputs and next steps
Payload block `earlyLateContrast` (additive, stamp `_artifactStamps.earlyLateContrast`) with, per outcome, the windows, edges, cells, estimates, intervals, matched shares, MDE, Path B, disagreement flag, sensitivities, caveats; tool `tools/early_late_contrast.py`; ingestion stage `early_late_contrast` (verified:false until a first full ingestion; non-blocking). No display here; a display needs the Director's approval of the rendered text. Language rule with the implementation: no "ageing", "degradation", "capacity fade / loss", "resistance increase" or "caused by" for a contrast; allowed: "difference between early and late drives in matched state bins". Planned: the winter ingestions extend the late window; the window fractions are re-derived by script each ingestion and the first frozen run records its boundary dates.

## 8. Out of scope and risks
Out of scope: same-season comparison; equivalence testing of the contrast; any use as a degradation rate; matching on route or GPS. Risks: R1 the late window contains cooler drives, so overlap is partial (matched share reported); R2 the software update and logger configuration are inseparable from time; R3 quintile-edge cells on 651 drives are thin (floors above); R4 the pack temperature variable is a drive-mean, not a thermal-state series; R5 the apparent Ah per 100 % SOC depends on the SOC range of the drive (SOC class is a match variable).

## 9. Director review (2026-11-15, verdict revise; no change of question) and how each is applied (Rev 1.1)
These override the matching text above.
1. Windows: the primary boundary dates are frozen at the first frozen run and recorded in the block; later ingestions report a re-derived-thirds variant as a sensitivity only. The block asserts that 2026-08-25 lies in the excluded middle third; if it does not, a flag is set and the result is not shown.
2. Edges are computed from the covariates only, on the early and late eligible drives of each outcome (the middle third excluded); bins are right-closed with the lowest bin closed; duplicate edges are merged. V5: the edge function never receives an outcome column.
3. Cell granularity: before any outcome is read, a covariate-only ladder is applied (5 x 5 x 4 x 2 classes, then 3 x 3 x 3 x 2) and the first level that matches at least 50 % of the late drives is chosen; the chosen level is recorded.
4. Bootstrap: the used-cell set and the late-count weights are fixed from the observed data; days are resampled within each group; a draw that leaves one side of a used cell empty is dropped and counted; if more than 5 % of draws are dropped, the interval is flagged unstable.
5. Show rule: not shown if fewer than 10 matched late drives OR fewer than 5 matched late days.
6. Estimand wording: "late-drive-weighted matched difference on the matched late subset" (never "effect"); the percent is relative to the late-weighted early cell means.
7. Path B is primary on the matched-support drives and secondary on all early and late drives, with the same day clusters and t(G - 1) intervals.
8. Y3: the SOC-swing eligibility used by the published `cap_ah_est` is added as a filter (the eligibility is read from the existing computation, not redefined); mean SOC does not control the swing.
9. "Persists" only if both paths' 98.33 % intervals (Bonferroni over three outcomes) exclude zero; sensitivities never upgrade a result; everything else is descriptive and unadjusted, and says so.
10. M622: the block states "descriptive, not a look, no equivalence verdict, no alpha spent"; it never feeds the month-9 or month-12 looks.
11. Sensitivity (c) widens the late window and narrows the early one (it is not "a wider gap"). The distance class used by M573 is dropped from the cells here; it is added as sensitivity (f). The M573 window wording (late versus September) is not the same design and is not reconciled by this block.
12. Language-gate rules with the implementation: forbid "ageing", "degradation", "capacity fade / loss", "resistance increase", "caused by", "effect", and "capacity" for Y3.

## 10. Implementation record and corrections (M678, 2026-11-15; Rev 1.2)
Implemented in `tools/early_late_contrast.py` (block `earlyLateContrast`, stage `early_late_contrast`, language rule `M677-early-late-wording`, test `tests/synthetic/test_m678_early_late.py`, frozen windows `analyses/M678_frozen_windows.json`).
Correction of a premise (found while implementing, before any N-04 result was read): the master column `cap_ah_est` is `CAP_KWH * 1000 / V_pack_median` (`compute_drive_summary_v6.py`, maximum difference to 2100 / V_pack_median 0.006 Ah, correlation with the median pack voltage -0.999). It is a voltage proxy scaled by the assumed 2.1 kWh, not an Ah-per-SOC measurement and not a capacity, and it contains no SOC-swing eligibility. Consequences: (1) section 2 / Y3 uses `V_pack_median` directly (the monotone transform without the assumed-energy constant), labelled "median pack voltage"; (2) Director change 8 (SOC-swing eligibility for Y3) has no object and is not applied; the SOC level `rf_socmean_wtd_pct` remains a matching variable; (3) the M676 series X3 label ("apparent Ah per 100 % SOC") is corrected in the same milestone (labels only; point estimates and the audit are unchanged): X3 is `cap_ah_est`, a voltage proxy, and its breaks are shifts in the median pack voltage; (4) the language rule forbids `cap_ah_est` as a capacity.
Other deviations: (a) "persists" additionally requires status computed (matched late share at least 50 %) and a stable interval (at most 5 % of draws dropped); added after the first run was seen, stricter than Rev 1.1, never looser; (b) the frozen windows file is written by the first run and is read by every later run; (c) the regime for cadence strata and matching comes from `cadence_regime.csv`, drives with a missing regime are dropped per outcome; (d) sensitivity (e) leave-one-week-out uses 200 draws per refit and reports only the range of the estimate; (e) Path B regime term is a fast indicator; the Y1 temperature term is 1000 / (T + 273.15).
Blind audit (`analytical-auditor`; spec and claim file only): first pass NOT reproduced for the matched-support quantities (Path A and matched-support Path B) while n, unadjusted differences and all-drives Path B agreed; the cause was a defect in the author implementation (the cadence regime was coded per frame, so early all-slow and late mixed drives used different cell keys); fixed (fixed coding fast = 1, slow = 0), a known-answer case added, claims regenerated. Second pass: reproduced for all outcomes (Y2: 13 cells, 108 matched late drives on 24 days, share 0.375, estimate 2.02398, 9.56 %, matched-support Path B 1.88863 on 204 drives; Y3: 13 cells, 110 matched late drives, share 0.3691, estimate 0.60548, matched-support Path B 0.36483; Y1 not shown with 0 cells), maximum difference about 1e-10; the bootstrap drop rate and intervals were not audited.
Spec gaps closed here: (i) when neither ladder level reaches 50 % matched late drives the coarsest level is used and the status is "weakly overlapping"; (ii) matched-support Path B uses the early and late drives in the used cells; (iii) cadence is part of the cell key and all early drives are slow-polled, so fast-polled late drives cannot match (the matched late share has a structural ceiling of about 58 %); (iv) the matched late share for the ladder is measured with the cell floors applied.
