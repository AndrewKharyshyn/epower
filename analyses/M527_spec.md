## 1. Estimands (definitions taken from the CHANGELOG entries M205 and M218; where they are ambiguous the ambiguity is stated and BOTH readings are pre-registered)

All on the pipeline 1 Hz rpm grid (`compute_summary_arrays._RawAccum.add` / rpmDistribution convention, as in `tools/rpm_opt_band.py`), duration-weighted (1 sample = 1 s), pooled over the drives of the master.
- **C1 centre of mass of the 2000 rpm mode:** mean rpm of the samples in the window [1975, 2025) (M205: 2000.30 at 307 drives).
- **C2 centre of mass of the 1500 rpm mode:** window [1475, 1525) (M218: 1499.69-1500.08, window-dependent) and the narrower [1485, 1515) (M205: 1500.08). **C3 centre of the 1589 rpm mode:** window [1564, 1614) (M218: 1588.62-1589.29) and the narrower [1574, 1604). Window dependence is reported, not tuned.
- **S1 mass concentration:** M205: "96 %+ of that window's mass sits within +/-25 rpm of 2000". Because the stated window 1975-2025 is itself +/-25 rpm, the sentence is ambiguous. Reading (a): share of the [1900, 2100) mass that lies in [1975, 2025); reading (b): the same share after subtracting the flanking background density (mean density of [1900, 1975) and [2025, 2100) per rpm times the window width). Both are reported. M218: ">90 % of each spike's own mass in a single 10 rpm sub-bin": share of the window mass in the best 10 rpm sub-bin (1 rpm resolution), for each of the three windows.
- **S2 the old 45/55 split:** mass of [1900, 2000) over mass of [1900, 2100) (M205: 45.3 %/54.7 % across the old edge at 2000).
- **S3 background densities (M218, descriptive):** s/rpm in [1400, 1475), [1525, 1564), [1614, 1975) and the in-window densities of the three spikes (M218: 10.1, 21.9, 29.7; spikes 274 and 183 s/rpm).
Observed occupancy of a logged signal (engine rpm, ECU-reported via the logging app, resampled to 1 s means); not an efficiency, BSFC or "governed setpoint" claim beyond the descriptive reading already in the dashboard.

## 2. Method

1. Per-drive sidecar `rpm_setpoints_sidecar.csv` (tracked, keyed by the raw-file SHA-256; only new or changed drives are parsed): counts of the 1 Hz rpm samples in 1 rpm bins [1400, 2100) (700 integer columns; every edge of the stored rpmDistribution in this range - 1400, 1475, 1525, 1564, 1614, 1975, 2025, 2100 - is an integer, so the stored bins are exactly reproducible) and the exact per-drive sum of rpm and count for the six pre-registered windows (so the centres of mass carry no binning error). The grid function is imported from `tools/rpm_opt_band.py` (`grid_rpm`), not reimplemented.
2. FAITHFULNESS GATE (STOP if it fails): the 1 rpm counts collapsed to the stored `rpmDistribution` bins in [1400, 2100) reproduce `pooledHours` (round(h/3600, 3)) for all seven bins, max abs difference <= 0.002 h; and the window counts equal the 1 rpm sums over the same windows.
3. Statistics: pooled ratio-of-sums estimates; day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, `integers(0, D, (B, D))`, B = 4000, calendar day = cluster; the centre of mass is a ratio of sums: sum rpm / count) with n reported as drives and days; the per-drive distribution of the centre (median and IQR over drives with >= 60 samples in the window) as a descriptive sensitivity; a split by logger regime (slow / fast from `cadence_regime.csv`) and the exclusion of the canonical outliers (`ens_outlier_v2`) as descriptive sensitivities.
4. Output: sibling payload key `rpmSetpoints` (+ stamp, ingestion stage `rpm_setpoints`, declared dependencies, tracked sidecar), written by `tools/rpm_setpoints.py`; first output kept in `analyses/M527_result.json` (run-once lock; spec hashed before the first run). Comparison with the historical values is reported beside them, as a difference with the interval, not as agreement testing: the corpus has grown from 307 / 320 drives to 606 and the logger regime changed on 2026-08-14 and 2026-10-01.

## 3. Acceptance, falsification, escalation

- A1 faithfulness gate passes; known-answer test on synthetic drives (a spike with a hand-computed centre; samples exactly on window edges; ffill 15 s; window sums equal bin sums).
- A2 the pooled seconds in [1400, 2100) equal the `rpmDistribution` total of the seven bins; the drive count with samples in the range is reported.
- A3 blind `analytical-auditor` reproduces C1, C2 and C3 (primary windows) from raw (claim + convention + raw path only).
- A4 escalate to the Director if any centre differs from its historical value by more than 1 rpm or lies outside its 95 % interval around the historical value, if any mass share (S1/S2) differs by more than 2 percentage points, if the two readings of S1 disagree by more than 2 points, or if the slow and fast regimes' centres differ by more than 1 rpm (a logger-regime effect on the setpoint itself would matter for every rpm statement). These are tripwires, not tests.
- Stop: faithfulness failure; any change to an existing payload value or key; the stage exceeding +120 s per ingestion (cached path expected about 10 s).
