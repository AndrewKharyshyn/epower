# M560 spec Rev 1 (2026-11-01, before any code): sensitivity of the cell-spread relaxation to Max/Min pairing, forward fill and gap handling (follow-up named in M555)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT for Director review. Nothing implemented. Tier C (flag-only sensitivity; no published value changes, no master column). Branch base: `main` at `ec8bfd9` (M559). Ledger id `AUD29-N12b` (second part of the M555 follow-up).

## 0. What exists
- Published block `cellSpreadRelaxation` (M194, `_cell_spread_relaxation`, exploratory): 593 drives, 830 rest windows on 108 days; relaxation magnitude median 1.0 mV (p25 -0.5, p75 3.0), day-clustered bootstrap interval [1.0, 1.5] mV; half-time median 1 s (n 441, flagged sub-resolvable); 53.1 % of windows above 1 mV; loaded spread median 11.12 mV, rest asymptote 10.0 mV. Rest window = |I| < 5 A for >= 5 s directly after a load of mean |I| >= 20 A over 3 s; onset = mean of the spread at the last two seconds before the rest, asymptote = median of the last up-to-5 s.
- Pipeline (`_csr_grid`): Max, Min and current each resampled to a 1 s MEAN and forward-filled for up to 3 s; seconds where any of the three is missing are DROPPED (`dropna`), so a window can span removed seconds (gap-collapsing); spread = (Max - Min) x 1000 on that grid.
- M555 (`cellObservability`, merged) showed: Max and Min are polled in a cycle, not simultaneously; the pairing is one-to-one by the polling cycle; Max < Min beyond the resolution occurs in about 0.69 % of polling-cycle pairs in the Max/Min era (fast and slow strata); the published resampling carries forward a measurable share of 1 s grid points; the strata are not pooled. The follow-up named there: "sensitivity of the cell-spread relaxation to the Max/Min offset and carry-forward (spec first; no change to published values)".

## 1. Question and non-question
Does the published relaxation result (a median magnitude of about 1 mV, a small and fast widening at load-off) depend on how the non-simultaneous Max and Min samples are paired, on the 3 s forward fill, or on the gap-collapsing, and how large is the quantisation floor relative to it? Non-question: no change of the published block, no cell diagnosis, no claim about which cell, no health, balance or ageing statement.

## 2. Variants (each recomputes the same windows and metrics; only the stated step changes)
- V0 published reproduction (must reproduce the stored block exactly: n windows, magnitude, half-time, interval).
- V1 fill0: forward-fill limit 0 (no carry-forward); V2 fill1: limit 1; (V0 is limit 3).
- V3 pairPoll: spread formed per sample pair by the M555 polling-cycle pairing (tau 1.5 s), then the 1 s mean of the pair spreads and the same fill limit 3; V4 pairNear0.5 and V5 pairNear1.5: nearest-within-tau pairing (reuse allowed).
- V6 freshOnly: published grid, but a window is kept only if every second used for the onset and the asymptote carries a real (non-filled) sample of BOTH channels.
- V7 strictContiguity: published grid, but a window is kept only if its seconds are consecutive in time (no removed seconds inside it, onset, rest or asymptote).
- Reported for every variant and for each cadence stratum (fast / slow / unknown, existing `cadence_regime.csv` rule) separately, never pooled as a new headline: number of windows and days, the share of the published windows kept, magnitude median with the day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval (seed 42, 4000 draws, only with >= 5 days), half-time median, share above 1 mV, loaded spread and asymptote medians.
- Resolution: the Max / Min grid step from `signal_representation.json` (expected 1 mV) is reported with the spread resolution; the statement "median 1.0 mV is at the resolution floor" is a computed field, not typed.
- Comparison to V0 per variant: difference of medians (mV), whether the variant median lies inside the V0 interval, the share of the V0 windows kept; no p-values.

## 3. Known answers (pre-registered)
- KA1 (hard gate) V0 reproduces the stored `cellSpreadRelaxation` numbers exactly (n windows 830, medians, interval); a miss stops.
- KA2 synthetic: a Max/Min series with a known step at load-off is recovered by every pairing; a constant series gives magnitude 0; a Max sampled 1 s after Min does not create a spread inversion beyond the pairing tau.
- KA3 monotonic: windows kept by fill0 are a subset of those kept by fill1, which are a subset of V0's (fewer fills never add a window); freshOnly is a subset of V0.
- KA4 strictContiguity keeps exactly the V0 windows whose seconds are consecutive (tested on a synthetic series with a removed second inside a window).
- KA5 the payload outside the additive block is byte-identical.
- Expectation (not a gate, reported either way): all variant medians within 1 mV of V0.

## 5. Payload, display, language
Additive block `cellSpreadRelaxationSensitivity` (+ stamp); ingestion stage `csr_sensitivity` (per-drive cache keyed by raw sha256 and extractor hash, git-ignored). Display (after the audit): a compact table in the cell-spread / data-quality area (variant, windows kept, median magnitude with interval, half-time) per stratum and one sentence. Language rule `M560-csr`: never "the cell spread is balanced / healthy / unchanged by pairing" as a finding; use "the median magnitude changes by X mV (percent) under variant Y"; the result is a sensitivity of an exploratory block, and a 1 mV median at a 1 mV resolution is not a measured widening.

## 7. Rev 2 (2026-11-01): Director review (verdict revise); incorporated. Rev 1 SHA-256 before this edit: 2e2205854d482c954c1a72cdf02fa543ce44db48ba4002a9b3e3ed0f727da08e
Where this section and sections 1-6 differ, this section governs.
- R1 (Q1; two separate families). (a) RE-MEASUREMENT on the FIXED V0 window set, keyed by (drive, load-off timestamp): the same windows, only the spread value changes (V3 polling-cycle pairing, V4 nearest 1.5 s pairing, V8 native pair level). (b) WINDOW-SET changes (the windows themselves change): V1 Max/Min fill 0, V2 Max/Min fill 1, V6 fresh-only, V7 strict contiguity, V9 current fill. Added and dropped windows are reported against V0 for every family-(b) variant (counts, shares); no subset gate. V1/V2 change ONLY the Max/Min fill and hold the current fill at 3; V9 changes only the current fill (0 and 1) holding Max/Min at 3. V5 (nearest 0.5) is dropped (M555 nearest sensitivities agree within 0.002 pp); V4 keeps nearest 1.5 s.
- R2 (onset). The published onset is the mean of spread[i-1:i+1] = the last LOADED row plus the first REST row, so it contains an ohmic load term. V0 keeps it. V8 uses the last pair before load-off (and a second onset definition, the first pair at or after load-off, reported as a field), and the asymptote is the median of the pairs in the last 5 s. The pair timestamp is the later sample of the pair; both members must lie on the same side of load-off (a pair straddling load-off is excluded and counted). The >= 5 s rest, the 3 s preload and the asymptote are counted in ROWS of the (collapsed) grid in V0; V7 requires consecutive seconds across the preload, the rest and the asymptote.
- R3 (half-time; Q2). No median and no interval for the half-time (the published hit[0] indexes rows, not seconds, under gap-collapsing, and index 0 is the first rest row, already inside the onset mean). Under V7 only, report category counts (0, 1, 2, >= 3 s, none).
- R4 (placebo null; Q3). A placebo estimand with the same geometry: pseudo load-offs placed inside long rest runs (>= 20 s rest with |I| < 5 A and NO load in the preceding 30 s), same onset/asymptote rule; its magnitude median and interval per stratum. Escalate if the placebo median >= the V0 median or V0 lies inside the placebo IQR.
- R5 (lattice). Magnitudes lie on a 0.5 mV lattice (spread steps of 1 mV, median of up to 5 values); the interval [1.0, 1.5] is lattice-bound. The tool reports the lattice step and the share of windows on each lattice value as computed fields; a median of 1.0 mV is stated as "at the resolution floor".
- R6 (strata; Q4 partly). Each variant is compared with the per-stratum V0 (new numbers, with intervals), not with the pooled published one; the pooled V0 is reproduced only for KA1. Population passed in: 593 drives with a raw file and the Max/Min channels (stated in the block).
- R7 (audit; Q4). 30 windows (10 fast, 10 slow, 10 edge cases chosen from V0 grid conditions: a removed second inside the window, a filled second at the onset, a negative magnitude), keyed by (drive, load-off timestamp), emitted by a small window-key emitter that reproduces the V0 windows and is run BEFORE the variant code; the sample is committed before code. The auditor returns V0, V1, V3, V6, V7 and V8 magnitudes per window with tolerance 1e-9 mV.
- R8 (known answers). KA3 (subset) is removed. Added: a gap inside a rest run collapses on the dropna grid and is rejected by V7; a pure ohmic step (spread = k times I) gives the V0 magnitude k times I_preload / 2 and V8 k times I; a placebo with constant spread plus 1 LSB noise has a magnitude median about 0.
- R9 (escalation). Placebo median >= V0 median; V0 inside the placebo IQR; fast and slow strata differ in sign; windows added by any variant > 5 % of V0; V8 differs from V0 by > 0.5 mV; fresh-only keeps < 60 % of V0 in a stratum; a variant median leaves the stratum V0 interval; audit disagreement.
- R10 (wording; Q5). The published interpretation sentence ("relaxes within 1-2 s", "well-balanced, shallow-cycled buffer that never deeply stresses the cells") conflicts with the M555 language whatever the result: a SEPARATE Director wording ticket is opened (recorded in the triage file); M560 stays additive and changes no published text.
