## Pre-registered design (rules copied from analyses/M320_spec.md and M321_spec.md; nothing re-tuned)
- Population: the M318 R2 complete-case at-risk tables (CORE5 complete), START side only, canonical exclusion (`ens_outlier_v2`), day key `_day_key`; resampling unit the calendar day; bootstrap 4000 draws, seed 42, percentile intervals; delta for distinctiveness log 1.25; support gate 100 events on >= 10 event days, band +/- 2.5 C, all imported from `tools/m320_ladder.py`, not retyped.
- Phase 0 (support audit, no model fit): at the 12.5 C band and the neighbouring cold bands: events, event days, drives, at-risk seconds, calendar span, the split between basis drives and drives added since the basis, the share of events on the five busiest days, the count at the shared endpoint, day-weighted versus seconds-weighted. Flag "concentrated" when fewer than 20 event days (already reported) or when the five busiest days hold more than half of the events. Script-written from the M321 cache.
- Phase K (knot sensitivity; a DIAGNOSTIC on its own copy, never shipped, no payload or dashboard change, the shipped V2 block untouched): fit the M318 final-fit specification (`_final_fit`: cloglog discrete-time GLM, five-variable duration-aware spec with the three interactions, day-clustered robust covariance) for the START model on the CURRENT 665-drive corpus under four pre-registered knot sets for the pack-temperature spline: K1 = the shipped knot VALUES of the 606-drive basis (primary); K0 = `_knots` recomputed on the 665-drive corpus; K2 = K1 plus one knot at 12.5 C; K3 = K1 plus knots at 12.5 C and 15 C. The quantity is the M320 distinctiveness statistic D(12.5 C, reference) on the primary cell (median demand level, 15 s duration) over cells supported at both levels (conditional support mask, >= 20 at-risk seconds per cell and band), with the M320 conditional whole-pipeline bootstrap unchanged; secondary cells (low and high demand, 2 s and 60 s) are diagnostics and gate nothing.
- Primary analysis K1; sensitivities K0, K2, K3; method disagreement reported (seconds- versus day-weighted levels, primary versus secondary cells), never tuned to agree.
- Knot sensitivity S = max over K0, K2, K3 of |D_variant - D_K1| divided by the half-width of the K1 interval. Pre-registered classes: KNOT-ROBUST iff S <= 0.5 and every variant gives the same side of the log 1.25 threshold for the interval lower bound as K1; otherwise KNOT-DEPENDENT. A variant whose fit fails or whose bootstrap has more than 2.5 % failed draws is reported and is not a conclusion.
- Outcomes (reported and escalated, never tuned): (a) KNOT-ROBUST and distinct: "distinct from the reference and not knot-dependent among the pre-registered knot sets"; the next step is a separate spec for a fourth level (phase L: ladder rule change, refit start date from Andrii, blind audit, Director); nothing ships from this spec. (b) KNOT-ROBUST and not distinct: "not distinct from the reference at this resolution", never "no effect". (c) KNOT-DEPENDENT: "not established: the contrast in the 10-15 C band depends on the spline form". (d) Phase 0 flags the candidate as concentrated: reported first, phase K is still run and read with that flag.
- Accept / reject. Validity (failure = no result is reported): known-answer test of D on synthetic data (identical levels give 0; a planted linear-tail effect is recovered under K1 and K2); the reproduction of the shipped ladder block (levels, support counts, D for the shipped pairs) from the shipped fit within the M320 tolerance before any variant is read; isolation (no tracked data file changes except the new analyses/M617_* outputs; master MD5 unchanged); blind audit reproduces phase 0 counts exactly and each variant's D and interval within three Monte-Carlo standard errors from the rule and the data path only; the compute plan (runtime of four fits plus four bootstraps, estimated from a timed single fit) is stated to Andrii before the first full run.
- Falsification, stated before any result: if the shipped-knot (K1) D(12.5 C, reference) lower bound is below log 1.25 AND all three variants agree, the premise that a colder level may be supportable is not supported by this corpus and is reported as such; if K1 and K2 disagree on the side of the threshold, adequacy of the linear tail in the 10-15 C band is not established.
