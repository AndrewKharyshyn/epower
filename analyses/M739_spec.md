# M738 spec: exposure of the unlogged driving seen only by the cloud export, with masked-drive validation (report-only)

Rev 2, after the Director review of Rev 1 (decision "revise", nine required changes, all adopted; record `analyses/M738_director_review_rev1.json`; Rev 1 is commit 9d5ee3b, sha256 5a68eb1b9785ec7a63ec963ed859cb1482370cd71c55ee597a0943affd2f28aa). Committed BEFORE any tool is written or run. Author: main session (Sonnet). Next step: tool and tests, blind audit, Director decision. Milestone number follows M737 (map-grade G0, merged) and the winter session's M736.

## 0. Why, and what is already known
- M729 (report-only side tables, read from `analyses/M729_cloud_brief.json`, not re-derived here): the cloud odometer gained 13,285 km over the export span against 11,062 km logged in the master (the master covers all 717 drives, the export span covers 713 of them: the tool aligns both sides to the drives inside the export span before any comparison); 333 strict cloud-only movement bursts total 1,921 km (crude definition 1,956 km; mean about 5.8 km per burst against about 15.4 km per logged drive, so the unlogged units are mostly short trips, a selection the design must carry); gap classes between consecutive logged drives: 508 stationary-consistent, 72 driving-observed (1,808 km), 26 odometer-increase-only, 105 uncertain, 4 no cloud data, 1 export break; 21 of 194 dates have no export rows.
- The published "logged share of the odometer" and every drive-level KPI describe the logged corpus only. The study has no description of what the unlogged driving looks like (length, speed mix, ambient). That is the unknown this milestone bounds. It is NOT a correction of any published figure and no logged quantity is replaced.
- The cloud series are coarse (about 30 s cadence, displayed speed capped at 130 km/h, ambient in 1 C steps, positions with the M730 +16 s clock shift unapplied), so the first question is how much of a drive's exposure the cloud alone can reconstruct. That is answered on logged drives, where the OBD record is the truth, by masked-drive validation.

## 1. Constraints
- Cloud-derived quantities are "estimated / reconstructed / model-derived / proxy", never "measured". OBD stays primary; nothing replaces a logged channel.
- No published output changes: no `summary_arrays.json`, payload, dashboard, regeneration key or `drive_master.csv` change (MD5 `d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98`, 717 rows, asserted before and after); no frozen winter file read for writing, no winter prediction scored (M726). Outputs: `analyses/M738_*.json/csv`, aggregates only (public repository: no coordinates, no per-drive tracks).
- Statistics: calendar day is the resampling cluster; day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); n (drives or bursts, days, km) with every estimate; no pooling of unlike strata without saying so.
- Cloud missingness is itself a selection (21 dates without rows, holes of 24 h or more, the 30 Jul to 1 Aug break): reported as its own stratum with dates, never modelled. M726 scope: dates in November are covered like all others, but no U1/U2 output is an input to, or is read by, the winter session; nothing here scores or extends a winter prediction.
- Unlogged driving that the export also misses (the 21 dates without rows, the 30 Jul to 1 Aug break) is unobservable here: it is listed with its dates, never modelled.

## 2. Data (read-only)
`drive_master.csv` windows (padded 60 s, as M729/M734), raw drive CSVs (`XT_RAW_DIR`) for the OBD truth of the validation quantities, M729 store streams (Odometer, Trip_Information speed, Vehicle_Information ambient, Geolocation), `analyses/M729_cloud_gaps.csv`, M730 clock-shift estimate (sensitivity only), M734 screened-ambient rules (imported, not redefined).

## 3. Definitions (frozen by this spec)
- Cloud movement unit: a run of cloud odometer steps (the M729 step rule, restored in full: successive samples at most 35 s apart, positive increment, implied speed at most 180 km/h, and displayed speed above zero at one end of the step) joined while the pause between consecutive accepted steps is at most 120 s (the M729 strict burst rule). Units are built ONCE on the full cloud stream with no knowledge of any drive window (no cutting at window edges); each unit is then assigned by overlap with the master windows: overlap below 10 % of its duration = unlogged (U); overlap of at least 90 % = matched to a logged drive (V); between = boundary case. Overlap uses the padded window (60 s); the unpadded window and the M730 +16 s shift are sensitivities, with the number of units changing class reported, and boundary km bracketed (lower bound excludes them, upper adds the part outside the logged window). Moving time = time between accepted steps whose displayed speed is above zero at an end; duration = first to last accepted step; the truncation of up to one sampling interval at each end (about 30 to 60 s) is part of the duration error and is why duration has an absolute criterion.
- Exposure vector of a unit: distance (cloud odometer, km), duration (first to last accepted step, s), mean moving speed (distance over moving time), time shares in three displayed-speed classes (below 30, 30 to 80, above 80 km/h, from cloud speed samples, time-weighted trapezoid), stop-and-go proxy (number of cloud speed samples at zero inside the unit per km), screened ambient (M734 rule, reported with its sample count; absent when the M734 minimum is not met), hour-of-day class (Kyiv local, night 22 to 6, day otherwise), day type (weekday or weekend).
- OBD reference vector (logged drives only; called a reference, not a truth, because it has its own errors): distance (OBD integral of vehicle speed, cross-checked against master `distance_km`), duration (master), mean moving speed, time shares in the same three classes from OBD speed, ambient = driver-recorded manual ambient (a driver-recorded reference; the M734 screening rules were tuned on the same drives, so the ambient error is in-sample and stated so).

## 4. Estimands (pre-registered)
- **V1 reconstruction error (masked-drive validation).** For each canonical-clean logged drive with a unit matched to it by the overlap rule (units built on the full stream, the OBD record not used), compare the unit vector with the OBD reference. If a drive matches several units they are summed to one drive vector (distance, durations, time-weighted shares). Reported per quantity: signed and absolute error (median, P90), relative error for distance and duration, total-variation distance of the three-class time shares, ambient screened minus manual (M734 reuse); with day-clustered bootstrap intervals, n (drives, days, km), and by speed-class mix and by drive length tercile.
- **V2 detection.** Recall over ALL logged drives inside the export span, including those with fewer than two cloud steps (denominator = all, not the drives that passed a cloud-step filter): share with a matched unit holding at least 90 % of the master distance; precision: share of in-window units (overlap at least 10 %) that match no drive; the share of units with overlap below 10 % is the unlogged definition of U. Boundary cases (10 % to 90 %) are reported, not assigned.
- **U1 unlogged exposure.** Over the unlogged units (a census of the export, so the intervals cover measurement error only, propagated by applying the V1 stratum error: a bootstrap of the V1 error within the length-tercile by day-type stratum, day-clustered), restricted to the strata where V1 acceptance (R1) holds: number of units, days, total km, hours; distribution of length, mean speed and time shares; screened ambient where available. All as estimates with intervals; units are the strict M729 bursts (crude definition as sensitivity).
- **U2 representativeness.** Signed per-class differences (unlogged minus logged) of the speed-class time shares, the length-tercile mix, the day-type mix and the hour-class mix, km-weighted on BOTH sides, with day-clustered bootstrap intervals and the minimum detectable difference; the comparator on the logged side is the cloud-reconstructed vector of the logged drives (not the OBD values), so instrument error is not mixed with selection. A total-variation summary is reported only against a day-level permutation null (it is non-negative and biased upward, so its raw bootstrap interval cannot contain zero). The ratio of unlogged to logged km by month is reported. Descriptive differences; no statement about battery or energy results is made from them.
- **U3 km decomposition only.** The cloud odometer gain over the export span decomposed into a disjoint and exhaustive partition (gate: the parts sum to the total): logged-matched units, unlogged units, boundary units, odometer-increase-only gaps, remainder; each as km with its interval. No ratio is named a share and no dash-ODO anchor reconciliation is made here; the owner KPI (total logged km over the dash ODO, no date alignment) stays the only share.

## 5. Decision rules (frozen before any result)
- R1 (a quantity is reconstructable from the cloud alone, for use in U1/U2): distance: median absolute relative error at most 10 % AND km-weighted aggregate signed bias at most 5 % in absolute value (U1 reports totals, which a median does not control); duration: median absolute error at most 90 s (an absolute criterion because sampling truncation dominates short units) and aggregate signed bias at most 5 %; time shares: median total-variation distance at most 0.15 together with the signed per-class errors reported; screened ambient: median absolute difference at most 1.0 C; mean speed and the stop-and-go proxy are descriptive only (no gate, not carried to U1/U2). A criterion is met only when its upper interval bound is at most the threshold; an upper bound above the threshold but at most 1.5 times it reads "marginal" and the quantity is carried only with that label; at least 100 validation drives on at least 30 days.
- R2 (selection and transport): the unlogged units differ from the logged drives by selection (why the logger was off is unknown; mean unit length is about 5.8 km against about 15.4 km per logged drive). The V1 error is applied to a U unit only stratum-matched (length tercile by day type), under the stated transport assumption that the reconstruction error depends only on these covariates; a stratum with fewer than 30 validation drives, or units outside the validation support in length (below the shortest decile of validated drives), are reported as count, km and days only ("outside validation support") with no exposure statement. Cloud missingness is reported as a selection on its own (section 1).
- R3 (language): U1/U2 results are "estimated unlogged exposure (cloud-reconstructed)", with the V1 error stated next to them; never "the unlogged drives were the same/different as logged" without the interval; U2 differences are not read as an effect or as a correction of any KPI.
- R4 (no published change): outcome-independent: no logged figure, share or KPI is changed in this milestone; adopting any of it in the dashboard is a separate spec, blind audit, Director step and Andrii's decision.
- Falsification: "the cloud alone reconstructs a drive's exposure well enough to describe unlogged driving" is falsified (per quantity) when R1 fails for that quantity with n above the floor; "the unlogged driving resembles the logged corpus" is not established when the U2 interval excludes zero, and "not distinguishable" (with the minimum detectable distance stated) otherwise; absence of a difference is not equivalence.

## 8. Questions for the Director
(a) Is masked-drive validation on logged drives a sound basis for statements about unlogged units given the selection (R2), or should U1/U2 be restricted to reporting counts and km? (b) Are the R1 thresholds defensible against 30 s sampling, speed capped at 130 km/h and 1 C ambient steps, and is total-variation distance the right summary for time shares? (c) Is the unlogged definition (overlap below 10 %) and the boundary-case treatment adequate, given the +16 s clock shift? (d) Should U3 be in this milestone or left to the odometer-reconciliation owner rule (KPI = total logged km / ODO, no date alignment)?
Rev 2 answers (Director): (a) sound only conditionally: stratum-matched, in-support units with the transport assumption stated, otherwise counts and km; (b) distance fine, duration needs an absolute criterion, the 130 km/h cap does not affect the classes, ambient 1.0 C is fine against the 0.5 C quantisation, upper bound at most 1.5 times the threshold is lenient so it reads marginal, TV is fine for V1 beside signed per-class errors but not for U2 nulls; (c) mostly adequate: state padded versus unpadded, run the +16 s shift as a sensitivity, bracket boundary km; (d) keep U3 as a disjoint, exhaustive km decomposition only.

## 9. Rev 2.1 clarifications (written AFTER the first tool run and the first blind audit; they record the rules the tool already used and change no threshold, estimand or decision rule)
Rev 2 file sha256 before this section: de4a827b7be3093f42fa0ef1410ccfe6043a0b081fd12d20f7edd666871fa2dc. The blind audit reproduced every claim (C1 to C4) and asked that rules the spec left open be pinned:
- Unit-to-drive assignment: a matched unit (overlap at least 90 %) is assigned to exactly ONE drive, the one with the largest overlap with its padded window; units assigned to the same drive are summed to one drive vector BEFORE any comparison (distance, recall). Reading a unit as matching several drives, or testing recall on a single unit, changes the recall by up to 0.04 (audit: 0.804 to 0.847 against 0.839 here); the pinned rule is the primary, the others are not reported as results.
- Eligibility of the validation set: canonical-clean means `ens_outlier_v2` is false; a missing value (12 of 717 rows) is NOT clean and is excluded (n = 641; treating it as clean gives n = 650 and the same conclusions: audit, median relative error 0.0218, bias -0.0615).
- Drives with a master distance of zero are excluded from the relative distance error; in the recall test they count as hits when any matched unit exists (threshold 0.9 times 0), a small bias towards recall, stated here.
- Precision is the share of in-window units (overlap at least 10 %) that are matched (at least 90 %); an in-window unit always overlaps a drive by construction, so the Rev 2 wording "in-window units matching no drive" would be vacuous.
- Speed class of a time-share interval is the class of the interval mean speed; the +-16 s shift moves the cloud units, not the windows; "unlogged" means no OBD record, not that the vehicle was not driven.
- Rev 2 R1 verdict wording, restated: a criterion is "met" when its upper interval bound is at most the threshold, "marginal" when above it but at most 1.5 times it, otherwise "not met"; the aggregate signed bias of distance (-0.063, interval -0.104 to -0.029) is therefore "not met" against 0.05, and distance is not carried to U1/U2 as a reconstructed quantity (the cloud-odometer kilometres are still listed, labelled a lower-biased cloud total).
