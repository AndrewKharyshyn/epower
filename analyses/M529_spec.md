Part C is the proxy-level EMULATION (`tools/cadence_emulate.py`, M379b method): every fast-regime clean drive is thinned to the slow-regime interval distribution (40 seeded slow pool drives, `default_rng(7)`), `analyze_bytes` re-run, proxy recomputed (mean over 3 seeds), per-drive paired difference (emulated - original) by era with a day-clustered CI; slope re-estimated with the emulated values ("slow-equivalent"). It is an emulated sampling sensitivity, NOT a causal cadence effect (PID order, I-V co-timing and jitter are not emulated).
