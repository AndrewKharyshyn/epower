## 1. Question and scope
1. How does the HV-pack probe temperature change between the end of one logged drive and the start of the next (restart change), by gap class; is any short-gap rise real, or whole-degree quantisation or a mis-paired leg?
2. Which parked-cooling model describes the restart temperature well enough to drive the exposure and cold-start numbers?
3. Which start-temperature definition does the project ledger carry (R4) and how is it corrected without silently moving published figures?
Out of scope: heat-redistribution mechanism claims (untestable with these PIDs), any health or end-of-life statement, the exposure scenario model (WP-S, separate spec), dashboard changes.

## 2. Population, endpoints, pairs
- Population: every adjacent pair of logged drives of `drive_master.csv` in chronological order (665 drives, 664 pairs at the 2026-11-09 basis). Order and dates from the file-name digits (two key formats; match by digits); `time_start` is time of day only.
- Probes: BMS HV Battery Temperature Sensor 1-4 (whole degrees). Start reading = earliest native reading at or before first movement (speed > 0.5 km/h) and within 60 s of log start; if none before movement, the first reading within 30 s (flag `start_after_move`). End reading = last native reading within 60 s of log end. No forward fill, no time averaging, no composite.
- Primary quantity `sameProbeMean` = mean over probes with a reading at BOTH endpoints of (next start - previous end); T1 alone is reported beside it. A pair with no fresh reading at an endpoint is excluded and counted.
- Gap = next first-row time - previous last-row time on a UTC clock (local Europe/Kyiv converted; the DST change is handled by the conversion).
- Pair exclusions, each counted: overlap (gap < 0), duplicate start, clock reset inside a log, app-counter step > 1 km, no fresh probe at an endpoint, gap > 168 h. Gap > 168 h stays excluded (2 pairs at this basis): the two pairs are listed by ID and counted at every ingestion; it becomes a class only when >= 10 pairs occur.
- Unlogged-use flags (rules fixed before looking at pack temperature; they FLAG): SoC at restart >= 3 points above SoC at key-off; gap >= 6 h and engine coolant >= 15 C above the recorded ambient at restart; gap >= 1 h and engine coolant >= 5 C above its key-off value. The master "odometer" is the logger-app counter and cannot see driving while the app is off (dash-odometer: 1,148.6 km not logged since 2026-07-24 against 81.5 km of counter steps), so the counter step is not evidence of "no use".
- **E1 primary keeps the flagged pairs (n 639 at this basis); the without-flags version (n 596) is the secondary table.** Model fits (E3) drop flagged pairs.
- Gap classes: < 10 min, 10-60 min, 1-4 h, 4-12 h, >= 12 h (first two also pooled as < 1 h).
- Logger regime: slow-polled = drive whose T2-T4 median polling period is strictly above 10 s (214 of 658 drives at the exploratory run, first on 2026-09-06). This per-drive definition is primary; the stage writes per drive the T1 and T2-T4 median periods and reading counts so the rule can be audited. Sensitivities: the calendar rule "date >= 2026-09-06" (330 drives in the M707 check) and drives with a period of 8-12 s.

## 3. Estimands, methods, intervals, known weaknesses of the design
Resampling unit: the calendar day of the NEXT drive (the restart day). Day-clustered percentile bootstrap, seed 42, 4000 draws, 95 %. Every estimate carries n pairs and n days. Sensitivity (C8): cluster on the previous-drive day, and 2-day blocks of restart days, because overnight pairs share their previous drive with the pairs of the prior day.
- E1: mean restart change of `sameProbeMean` per gap class and pooled < 1 h; companions: T1 alone, share with change >= 1 C and >= 2 C, share with any positive change. Whole-degree probes make rise COUNTS a resolution artefact, not an event rate: the primary statement is the mean change; every rise share is shown beside a latent-change simulation (uniform rounding phase, Gaussian reading scatter, 400,000 draws per cell).
- E2: stop-end predictors of a short-gap one-step T1 rise (pack minus ambient at key-off, previous duration, current RMS, end slope): descriptive, exploratory, no causal wording.
- E3 park model, exact form (C1). One state, linear ambient trend plus a diurnal sinusoid, with W = 2 pi / 24 per hour, tau (h) and offset (C) free:
  G = 1 / sqrt(1 + (W tau)^2), phi = arctan(W tau);
  part(x) = Abar0 + slope x + offset - slope tau + G amp cos(W (clock0 + x - hpk) - phi);
  T_start(g) = part(g) + (T_end - part(0)) exp(-g / tau),
  where g = gap in hours, clock0 = local clock hour of the previous log end, Abar0 = mean ambient level, slope = ambient trend per hour, amp and hpk = diurnal amplitude and peak hour. The ambient inputs (Abar0, slope, amp, hpk) come from the ambient reconstruction of the exploratory run (`analyses/exposure_scenarios_2026-11-16/results/drive_ambient_recon.csv`, untracked), which was built from `ambient_samples.json` (untracked; source `vehicle_sensor`: the vehicle's own ambient reading at drive start and end), model-derived. `summary_config.json` `ambientByDrive` (tracked, 665 entries, driver-recorded from the vehicle display) is the same vehicle sensor: it is uncalibrated, heat-soaked after parking, and read only while driving; no independent thermometer exists. Bounds: tau in [0.5, 60] h, offset in [-10, 15] C; least squares on non-flagged pairs, day-clustered bootstrap interval. The production milestone (M709) rebuilds this ambient reconstruction under `tools/` and states whether it matches the exploratory file; if the production reconstruction differs, the E3 absolute targets, P-R3a and the P-R4 tau band are NON-BINDING; the milestone records the per-drive differences in Abar, slope, amp and hpk and continues, and escalates to the Director only if the mismatch cannot be attributed to a documented input difference. The exploratory CSV may be read as comparison-only data, never imported as code. Baselines: M1 persistence (T_start = T_end); M1b exponential to the recorded ambient (reported); M3 two-state (interior and probe states; identifiability check).
- E4: predicted vs logged number of restarts below 15 C, below 10 C and at or above 35 C under E3, with the bias near the threshold stated.
- E5 (R4): per drive, difference between the ledger `seasonal_drive_master.pack_temp_start_c` and the native start of section 2; mean and share >= 1 C by regime (per-drive definition primary), day-clustered interval, n drives and days.
Validation: fit May-August, test September-November (327 pairs / 55 days at the exploratory run); MAE by gap class; paired MAE differences with day-clustered intervals; rolling-origin as a sensitivity. **The hold-out is not clean and is reported as such (C2):** (a) the ambient reconstruction amp and hpk were fitted on the whole corpus, test months included; (b) the model form and the gap classes were chosen after full-sample results; (c) the May-Aug / Sep-Nov split is confounded with the slow-polling regime (from 2026-09-06) and with the HV-battery software update (intake-air PID outage). Winter drives are the first clean test.
Known weaknesses stated in the output: the unlogged-use flags miss short unlogged legs; nothing was tested below 0 C; a winter tau may differ physically.

## 5. Gates (pre-registered; a failed gate is recorded as failed and never relaxed)
- P-R0 (data): production eligible pair and day counts equal the target within +-3 pairs on the same basis AND the eligible pairs are listed by ID; the exploratory pair-ID list is the reference; every exclusion category counted and printed.
- P-R1 (reproduction, Q3): on the INTERSECTION of production and exploratory pair IDs (the IDs only in production and only in exploratory are listed), the class means of E1 equal the targets within 0.02 C for each of the five classes and < 1 h (the sampling intervals are far too loose for a reproduction test). No < 1 h pair with a 4-probe-mean rise >= 2 C (any such pair stops the stage for a ruling). New data after M708 fall under the standing rule "an estimate outside its previous interval escalates".
- P-R2 (two-state not justified; as pre-registered in the exploratory report section 7, C3): the second state is adopted only if it lowers the held-out (month-blocked) MAE by >= 0.25 C in a gap class of 1 h or more, or by >= 0.5 C below 1 h, with the paired interval excluding zero, AND tau_ip and C_i / C_p are interior to their bounds with a profile interval narrower than one decade. Rev 0's 0.1 C is withdrawn (it was weaker than the earlier, already frozen rule).
- P-R3a (reproduction of the model, only if the production ambient reconstruction matches, section 3): per-class held-out MAE within 0.05 C of the target on the same inputs. P-R3b (skill, always): held-out MAE at least 30 % below persistence at 1-4 h and at least 70 % below persistence at >= 4 h (observed 76 / 86 / 85 %), and for < 1 h the model's point-estimate MAE not above persistence (0.28 vs 0.36 C), with the paired interval reported (about [0.002, 0.175] at the exploratory run, i.e. near 0): superiority below 1 h is NOT required, persistence being close to optimal for short gaps. The absolute MAE check (<= 0.6 / 1.4 / 2.1 C) is NOT a gate at this basis (0.01 C margin at >= 12 h); it is the winter gate below.
- P-R4 (park constants): the flagged-kept refit reproduces the contamination direction (lower tau, higher offset): binding. The band tau of the non-flagged fit within [8.0, 10.5] h is binding only under the same condition as P-R3a (production ambient reconstruction matches); both fits reported.
- P-R5 (R4): on the same drive IDs, the fast-polled mean ledger-minus-native difference is within 0.02 C of 0.04 C and its absolute value is <= 0.05 C (a mean test; the fast maximum is 2.0 C); the slow-polled mean is within 0.02 C of 0.625 C and its day-clustered interval excludes 0.
- Frozen now for the winter data (from the exploratory report section 7, C3): heat-soak gate: a restart rise is called supported only if the share of pairs with a 4-probe-mean rise >= 1 C has a lower interval bound above 5 % in a gap class with >= 50 pairs on >= 20 days AND post-key-off logs (if the logger runs after key-off) show a peak >= 1 C above the key-off reading in at least half of >= 10 logged stops; otherwise the wording stays "no rise of 1 C or more supported". If the census arm passes but no post-key-off logs exist, the wording is "census rise share above 5 %, not confirmed by post-key-off logs", never "supported". Park-model gate for the exposure scenarios: held-out bias within +-1 C at >= 12 h and within +-0.5 C around the 10 and 15 C thresholds on winter pairs; otherwise the scenario outputs carry a boundary-offset bracket of the size of the held-out bias. The model parameters (tau, offset) are FROZEN from the pre-winter fit, recorded by value and CHANGELOG hash, before the first winter drive is ingested; each evaluation cell needs >= 10 pairs on >= 5 days, below that it is reported "not evaluable".
A displayed cell or KPI that leaves its previous interval, or a gate failure that is not a plain environment error, escalates to the Director.

## 6. Falsifiers
- "Short-gap restart rises of 2 C or more are not supported" is falsified if the production census finds >= 1 pair below 1 h with a 4-probe-mean rise >= 2 C, a fresh reading at both ends and no unlogged-use flag, confirmed on the raw probe rows.
- "The two-state model is not needed" is falsified if P-R2 passes on winter data.
- "Parking tau is about 9 h" is falsified if the winter fit leaves [8.0, 10.5] h with the interval excluding it.
- "The ledger start column is T1-weighted in slow-polled drives" is falsified if P-R5 fails or the slow-polled mean difference interval includes 0.

## 8. Language rules
Logged BMS probe readings are "logged"; model quantities are "estimated / model-derived", never "measured"; MAE is against logged readings. No "heat soak", "thermal mass" or "redistribution" claims; a rise is never called a rise until the raw probe rows show it; rise shares are a resolution artefact, not an event rate. "Two-state not justified" is not "two states do not exist". Flags are "evidence of use in the gap", not proof. No end-of-life or health wording.

## 9. Winter protocol (R3 / R5 link)
- At every ingestion the census is re-run on the whole corpus; additionally a winter-only table and a 4-12 h-only table (largest flagged-pair fraction, 21 of 82) with n and days; no pooling across thermal classes to reach a support floor. **Thermal class (corrected from Rev 1, whose "driver-recorded, not heat-soaked" distinction was unsupported: `ambientByDrive` is the same vehicle sensor):** winter-only means the reconstructed day-mean ambient (Abar, diurnal term removed) of the restart day is below 10 C, or the ambient reading at the restart drive after a gap of >= 4 h is below 10 C; both are labelled uncalibrated vehicle-sensor ambient, model-derived, and the pack start class is reported beside them.
- Requested driver records (owner-optional, before the first frost): keep the logger running 5-10 min after key-off if the logger allows; one frozen polling profile with all four probes in one group at 5 s or faster; probe IDs logged; parking context (sun, shade, garage) per day; a few parked USB-logger tests (cabin, under-seat, ambient shade); dash odometer at start and end of each test day.
- The S6 winter predictions (handoff section 2) are re-baselined after M711, then frozen.
