# M379 spec (Rev 1): logger PID-cadence regime as a first-class covariate, and its effect on the pipeline keys

Status: work in progress, Director review pending. Nothing implemented. Facts were read from `drive_master.csv` (524 drives, MD5 7c3bc9883f223e8a488b84ad053e13b5) on 2026-10-05. Milestone number is a proposal. Origin: the 2026-10-01 PID cadence change found at M376 and the open follow-up "cadence regime flag in the master and a stratified sensitivity" (M376 CHANGELOG, M377 Rev 2 follow-ups).

## 1. Facts (script output this session)
- The master carries the median update interval of the HV-current PID per drive, `I_sample_period_s` (512 drives; 12 have none because they have no current samples). Its distribution is bimodal with an EMPTY gap: no drive between 1.0 and 1.1 s (bins of 0.05 s: 0.70-1.0 s: 131 drives; 1.0-1.1 s: 0 drives; 1.1-1.9 s: 381 drives).
- Regimes by time, threshold 1.05 s (fast < 1.05 s <= slow): fast runs are (a) 2026-08-14 10:10 to 2026-09-06 12:29, 100 drives, 22 days; (b) 2026-10-01 16:46 to 2026-10-04 14:52, 30 drives, 4 days (the M376 change); one isolated fast drive on 2026-05-13 18:30; slow runs: 2026-05-15 to 2026-08-13 (224 drives, 54 days) and 2026-09-06 13:08 to 2026-10-01 08:14 (157 drives, 26 days). So the Oct 1 change is the SECOND fast-cadence regime of the study, not the first; weekly median I period: about 1.2-1.5 s (May to early Aug), 0.77-0.90 s (Aug 10 to Sep 6), 1.42-1.45 s (Sep 7 to Sep 27), 0.875 s (Sep 28 to Oct 4); median rows per second about 12 in the fast regimes and 16-20 in the slow ones.
- Per-drive entry point of the pipeline: `compute_drive_summary_v6.analyze_bytes(csv_bytes, filename)` (one raw CSV in, one master row out). The ML/refit columns are frozen (ML16 restore) and are not touched by this milestone.
- The M376 decimation experiment (scratch script, 25 new drives, I/V thinned to about 1.45 s spacing): gross throughput +0.34 % in total (per-drive median 1.0 %), net energy +0.14 kWh on 1.10 kWh; it covered only I and V, not the other PIDs, and the real change is about 1.6x in per-PID rate, not 2x.
- Existing master-level consequence: `release_check`, gates on sample counts (>= 20 / 100 PID samples), the M119-v2 monitor and the loaded-spread sample counts are defined on logged PID samples, so the regime can move which drives pass a gate (3 drives at M376).

## 2. Plan
P1 Regime flag (descriptive covariate, no re-baseline of the corpus): `logger_cadence_regime` per drive = "fast" if `I_sample_period_s < 1.05`, "slow" if >= 1.05, null when the period is missing; derived deterministically from an existing master column, so it is written as a sidecar (`cadence_regime.csv`, file, regime, period) and as an additive payload block `loggerCadence` (rule, threshold, per-regime n drives / n days, the fast runs with dates), by a stage `cadence_regime` and NOT as a new `drive_master.csv` column (adding a master column changes the corpus MD5 anchor and needs the full re-baseline / restamp cycle of M285c; a Director ruling is requested). The empty gap is asserted at every run (a drive inside [1.0, 1.1) STOPs the stage, Director).
P2 Observational strata (descriptive, confounded by season, ambient and drive mix, labelled so): for the headline KPIs of `tools/kpi_paths.json` and the energy keys listed in P3, per-regime estimate with day-clustered percentile bootstrap CI (seed 42, 4000 draws) and n (drives, days) for fast and slow; no test of equality is presented as a cadence effect.
P3 Paired emulation (primary, isolates the cadence): for every fast-regime drive with an energy block (about 130), build the emulated SLOW version of its raw CSV by thinning EVERY PID column independently: keep a sample of a column only if at least T seconds passed since the last kept sample of that column, with T chosen so that the median I-PID period of the emulated drive matches the median of the slow-regime drives (T set once from the master, not per drive; reported). Run `analyze_bytes` on the original and on the emulated bytes (scratch only; nothing is written to the master) and compare per-drive keys. Key list (pre-registered): `gross_throughput_kwh`, `gross_discharge_kwh`, `gross_charge_kwh`, `net_draw_kwh_corr`, `energy_residual_kwh`, `offset_kwh_removed`, `distance_km`, `engine_on_pct`, `regen_share_of_charge`, `ev_dist_pct`, `n_I_samples`, `n_loaded_spread_samples`, `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv`, `peak_discharge_kw`, `peak_I_charge`. Per key: n drives, n days, median per-drive relative difference, corpus ratio of sums (emulated / original) with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap CI; gate-flip table (drives whose pass / fail outcome of each count gate differs).
P4 Decision rule (frozen before any run): a key is "cadence-insensitive" when the 95 % CI of the corpus ratio of sums lies inside [0.99, 1.01]; "cadence-sensitive" otherwise (disclosure and, for quantities used in a headline figure, stratified reporting; the Director decides on any headline consequence). Count keys (`n_I_samples`, `n_loaded_spread_samples`) are reported as scaling factors, not tested. Gate flips are listed, never repaired.
P5 Validation of the emulator (known answer): (a) with T = 0 the emulated bytes reproduce the original row exactly for every key (identity); (b) the emulated median I period lies within 5 % of the slow-regime median; (c) a synthetic CSV with a known constant signal gives the same time-weighted integral at both cadences (analytic).
P6 Outputs: `analyses/M379_cadence_sensitivity.json` (script-written, provenance: master MD5, spec sha256, script sha256), `cadence_regime.csv`, payload block `loggerCadence` (rule, counts, runs, P2 strata, P3 results), a disclosure text in the dashboard Methods / limits bound to the block (regime counts and the P3 outcome, "cadence-sensitive" keys named), `data_health` shift flags annotated with the regime. Blind audit (analytical-auditor, definitions only): regime counts and runs from raw timestamps, and the emulated-vs-original ratio of gross throughput for 10 fast drives.

# Rev 3 (M379b implementation findings; recorded BEFORE the study run; FROZEN together with Rev 2; changes one acceptance criterion and fixes details Rev 2 left open)
Findings from the emulator development (script output, 25 fast drives, pools from 40 seeded slow drives, no result key computed yet): the native fast I-PID grid is about 0.8 s (median 0.798 s, mean below 5 s 0.829 s); the slow-regime pooled I interval (intervals below the pipeline's 5 s integration cap) has median 1.264 s and mean 1.392 s. A thinning emulation can only keep native samples, so kept intervals are mixtures of multiples of the native spacing. Plain nearest-sample selection snaps the median to 1.48 s. Dithered selection (the two samples bracketing the target time are chosen with probability proportional to closeness) preserves the expected interval: emulated mean below 5 s 1.403 s (within 1 % of 1.392 s), emulated median 1.476 s (17 % above the slow median) and a wider spread (p10 0.79 s, p90 1.98 s against 1.11 s and 1.81 s).
Amendments. (1) P5(b) / Rev 2 change 1: the interval match is evaluated on the MEAN interval below 5 s (acceptance: within 5 % of the slow-regime pooled mean; computed on all emulated drives in the final run and reported), the median, the quartiles and the 10th / 90th percentiles are REPORTED, and the quantization / spread limit of a thinning emulation is stated; the median criterion of Rev 2 is replaced. (2) Emulator-robustness variant (new): the same study at the nearest-sample selection (no dithering) on 30 fast drives chosen at random (seed 42) with 5 seeds (1..5), reported next to the dithered result; a classification that differs between the two is flagged. (3) Clarification of the frozen margins (no change of any number): ratio rule [0.99, 1.01] applies to gross_throughput_kwh, gross_discharge_kwh, gross_charge_kwh, distance_km, rf_efc, fce; +-0.003 kWh to net_draw_kwh_corr, energy_residual_kwh, offset_kwh_removed, soc_delta_kwh; +-0.003 kWh/100 km to net_draw_per100km_corr and ev_kwh_per100km; +-0.5 pp to engine_on_pct, regen_share_of_charge, ev_dist_pct; standstill_draw_kw, vsag_R_pack_mohm, iv_alignment_gap_s, the peaks and the counts have no pre-registered margin and are REPORTED only (the peaks are negative controls: movement expected). The quarter-half-width rule is applied to net_draw_per100km_corr against EnergyIntensity.all (the headline KPI it feeds). The study covers the keys emitted by `analyze_bytes` (+ the three offset keys recomputed with the frozen formula, checked against the master: maximum difference 4.8e-05 kWh on 12 drives); the generator / traction reconstruction (f_gen, eta_bus; `recon_engine`) is a different path and is NOT covered (stated limit). Sample: the study drives are the fast-regime drives with an energy block (gross_throughput_kwh present, distance_km > 0, not canonically flagged); the slow -> slower control is 100 slow drives chosen at random (seed 42) with an energy block. Emulated results are per-drive means over the 20 seeds; the seed spread of every corpus statistic is reported.
Language. "emulated sampling sensitivity"; the interval match is stated on the mean, with its limit.
