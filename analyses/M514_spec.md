# M514 spec (Rev 1, FROZEN before any M514 computation): joint instrumentation sensitivity matrix for the resistance-proxy slope (audit 2026-10-25 section 6 / 13.5)

Status: Rev 1, written 2026-10-25 BEFORE any M514 computation. Design basis: an Opus research-director design (2026-10-25). Origin: independent audit 2026-10-25 section 6: separate cadence, grid and alignment inventories are not a joint robustness analysis, and the audit's own exploratory result (adding a logged current/voltage grid-change-period indicator to the primary design gave 1.155 mOhm/month, day-bootstrap [0.164, 2.466], ISO-week [-0.052, 2.389], against 0.656 [-0.426, 1.837] for the primary) was selected during an audit. DISCLOSURE: that result is known to the authors, so the grid-period level G1 is NOT blind; it is included as a pre-declared cell, never adopted, and never promoted. The audit's numbers are not targets. The spec sha256 is recorded in the CHANGELOG before the tool is run.

## Question
Does the reading of the primary resistance-proxy slope (S1 design, conditional association, warm window) change across a small, fixed, pre-registered grid of response-construction, logged-grid-period, cadence/support and temperature-specification choices evaluated on one paired drive set, and how do the day, ISO-week and moving-block uncertainty procedures disagree? The matrix reports disagreement; it replaces no estimate.

## Estimand and primary
Slope of `vreg_R_pack_mohm` on calendar months (days / 30.44 from the minimum date), mOhm per month, a conditional association within the declared design, not a degradation rate or SOH. The PRIMARY estimate stays the published S1 design (cell A0,G0,C1,T0: 274 drives, 98 days at the current corpus, day-cluster percentile bootstrap, seed 42, 4000 draws, read from `powerFadeEquivalence.s1` and fidelity-checked). Nothing in the matrix becomes a headline.

## Paired drive set F*
F* = master drives that carry a proxy under BOTH the production alignment (A0) and the synchronous-interpolation variant (A1, M505 Rev 2 R1), have all covariates present (`T_pack_mean_avg`, `vreg_I_p95_A`, `eff_dt_s`, `coverage_frac`, the fast-regime flag) and a defined grid class (signal_resolution classes `undefined` / `undefined_sparse` drop the drive). Canonical-clean eligibility as in S1. All cells are evaluated on F* except G2, which is evaluated on its own subset and is paired against G0 on that same subset. Stop rule: F* < 150 drives or < 50 calendar days stops the tool and goes to the Director (nothing is written).

## Factors and levels (fixed; 2 x 3 x 3 x 3 = 54 cell ids exactly)
- A (response construction): A0 production alignment (as in the master); A1 synchronous interpolation, exactly M505 Rev 2 R1 (tools/vreg_alignment.py, only `_asof` replaced; every other step of `_vsag_metrics` unchanged).
- G (logged grid period, a per-drive flag, not a date window): G0 no grid term; G1 add the indicator `gridChange` (the drive's (current class, voltage grid step) differs from the corpus-modal pair; `signal_resolution` rev3, classes from `signal_resolution.classify`); G2 exclude `gridChange` drives (paired comparison against G0 on the same retained subset). The modal pair is FROZEN as read from `analyses/M503_result.json` and recorded by hash, not recomputed by `most_common`. A drive whose class is undefined leaves F*.
- C (cadence and support): C0 no cadence covariate; C1 `eff_dt_s` and `coverage_frac` (the S1 design); C2 C1 plus the fast-regime indicator (powerFade cadence design S3, `compute_drive_summary_v6._powerfade_cadence_specs`).
- T (temperature specification, on `T_pack_mean_avg`): T0 linear; T1 quadratic centred at the F* mean; T2 piecewise-linear hinge with knots at the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of F* (M499 Rev 2 definitions; knots frozen on the basis). M499 T3-T5 stay in M499 because they change the population.
Cell grid: A(2) x G(3) x C(3) x T(3) = 54 cells. A cell id is `A{0,1}G{0,1,2}C{0,1,2}T{0,1,2}`. No level or cell is added or dropped after results.

## Uncertainty (frozen)
Day percentile bootstrap (calendar day = cluster, seed 42, 4000 draws, common random numbers across cells: the same day draws feed every cell on the same subset) governs. Sensitivities: ISO-week bootstrap (same draws rule), and circular moving blocks of 7 and 14 days (M494 Rev 2 rules: fewBlocks flag, endpoint Monte Carlo SD from 8 repeats). A draw in which the indicator column is degenerate counts as failed; more than 1 % failed draws makes that interval non-decisive. Per cell and per difference-from-primary: a PAIRED day interval of (cell slope - primary slope) on the common draws. No resampling unit is chosen per cell. Intervals with fewer than ~25 clusters under-cover; the week/block intervals (about 23 ISO weeks) are reported as sensitivities and flagged, not as the primary.

## Reported quantities (no cell is preferred)
Per cell: slope, day interval, ISO-week interval, M7 and M14 intervals, n drives, days, weeks, condition number / VIF of the design, failed-draw share, decisive flag. Summary: range of slopes, count of positive slopes, spread ratio = (max - min) / (2 x primary day half-width), counts "k of N decisive day / week / M7 / M14 intervals exclude zero", and for the support margin the 6 A x G cells at C1,T0 on drives with `vreg_samples >= 2 x VREG_MIN_N`. Descriptive level margins (mean slope per level of each factor) are stated as descriptive only.

## Pre-registered reading ladder (thresholds frozen)
L0 "reading unchanged across the pre-registered matrix": every decisive DAY interval includes zero AND the spread ratio <= 0.5.
L1 "procedure-dependent, unresolved": any decisive cell differs from the primary reading (an interval excludes zero, or the spread ratio > 0.5, or a day/week/block disagreement on zero inclusion).
L2 "conditional positive association across the matrix": only if ALL decisive day, week and block intervals exclude zero on one side; L2 needs a blind audit and a Director decision before it may be displayed. A change of the ladder level between ingestions escalates to the Director.
Disagreement is stated as counts (k of N intervals exclude zero by procedure), never as "robust", "insensitive", "borderline significant"; G1 is described as aliased with calendar time (one contiguous run, 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-30 in the M503 record), so G1 and G2 change what identifies the slope.

## Multiplicity and repeated looks
Up to 216 intervals, no adjustment (the any-difference rule leans conservative). The matrix is re-run at every ingestion without alpha spending ("repeated looks"); a positive interval first appearing in a later cumulative window stays descriptive. Bootstrap positive-slope fractions are not posterior probabilities or p-values.

## Scope: cell spread
Not included. A separate later matrix M515 (pairing tolerance 300 / 100 ms or interpolated min cell, >= 30 loaded pairs) gets its own frozen spec before it runs.

## Implementation
`tools/resistance_joint_matrix.py`: reuses the production design builders (`powerfade_equivalence.s1_rows`, `m499_temp_spec.fit` basis constructions, `vreg_alignment` A1 proxy, `signal_resolution` classes); incremental sidecars keyed by raw sha256 (A1 proxy, grid class) so about one raw pass the first time; bootstrap in minutes. Payload block `resistanceJointSensitivity` {label, spec, specSha256, scriptSha256, masterMd5, factors, gridDefinition (frozen modal pair + hash), pairedSet, primary, cells[], summary, ladder, statement, repeatedLooks, blindAuditRef}; stamp as computed. Ingestion stage after `powerfade_equivalence` with a `--check` mode (release_check). Dashboard: one sentence and a margins table in the Power fade note after the Director rules, bound to `S.resistanceJointSensitivity`; no change to the powerFade estimators, bound or TOST. Language rule M514 (never "corrected", "true / measured resistance", "degradation rate", "robust", "insensitive", "borderline significant"; the 1.155 figure is never quoted as a result).

## Tests and known-answer checks
Injected grid offset recovered by G1; null case gives L0; common random numbers give zero cell-minus-itself difference; a degenerate indicator draw is counted as failed; the frozen modal pair is read from the hash-recorded record; the A1 patch sentinel (tools/vreg_alignment.py) is present; exactly 54 cell ids; fidelity stop: the A0,G0,C1,T0 cell equals the payload S1 (0.6564, 274 drives, 98 days at the current corpus, read from the payload not typed); F* below the stop thresholds stops. Blind audit (analytical-auditor, claim + data path only): reproduce the cell slopes on F* from raw for a stated subset of cells. Director decision on the result and ladder.

## Must not
Adopt 1.155 or promote any cell; add or drop levels or cells after results; pick a resampling unit per cell; average cells into an estimate; filter production on `gridChange`; touch the master, `powerFade`, the bound or the TOST; run before this spec's sha256 is in the CHANGELOG; describe any slope as a degradation rate, SOH or true resistance.


## Rev 2 (research-director ruling "revise", R1-R9; this section replaces any conflicting Rev 1 text above; written before any M514 computation)
Meaning: the design stands; Rev 1 left choices that would have to be made after seeing results (primary/F* mismatch, pairing of draws, failed draws, ladder precedence). Each is settled here in text. The estimand does not change. Wording: A1 is "synchronous-interpolation variant", never "corrected alignment"; the proxy is an estimated quantity and the slope a conditional association (never a degradation rate, SOH or true resistance); rule M514 also forbids "significant", "confirmed", "better cell", "preferred cell"; the audit's 1.155 is never quoted as a result.
R1 (primary vs F*): the fidelity stop runs on the published S1 set (read from `powerFadeEquivalence.s1`: slope, nDrives, nDays; not typed). Because the A1-proxy and fast-flag requirements can make F* smaller than the S1 set, the MATRIX REFERENCE is the cell A0,G0,C1,T0 evaluated on F*; paired differences are taken against that reference; the F*-restriction delta (reference slope on F* minus the published S1 slope, with the drive/day counts of both) is reported beside the summary. Everywhere Rev 1 says "primary" inside the matrix it means this reference; the published S1 estimate remains the only primary estimate of the project.
R2 (support names): the column is `n_vreg_samples` (not `vreg_samples`); per M505 Rev 2 R2 `vreg_I_p95_A` and `n_vreg_samples` are recomputed per variant, so the support margin uses each variant's own `n_vreg_samples >= 2 x VREG_MIN_N` (VREG_MIN_N as in the production code; the margin is descriptive).
R3 (grid classes): the drop rule is the rev3 `undefined` set exactly (current class `undefined` or `undefined_sparse`, OR voltage `grid_step` is None); voltage steps are compared after `round(step, 6)`.
R4 (common random numbers): a draw-indexed wrapper returns NaN for a failed draw; pairs use the common valid draws only. G2 and the G0 cell on the G2 subset share subset-level draws (seed 42, `ols_boot_slopes`); there is NO G2-minus-reference paired interval (different subsets); G2 is compared only with G0 on its own subset.
R5 (failed draw): a draw is failed if any non-constant design column has zero variance among the drawn rows, or the condition number exceeds 1e9, evaluated before any `lstsq` fallback; the same rule for day, ISO-week and block resampling; the ISO-week bootstrap uses the M499 `cluster_boot`.
R6 (blocks): block resampling uses per-day block-coverage weights in X'WX; only the D < 10 L and < 10 weeks `fewBlocks` rules apply; the Monte Carlo SD seeds are named; an endpoint within Monte Carlo error of zero is non-decisive.
R7 (decisive): a cell interval is decisive iff failed-draw share <= 1 %, NOT fewBlocks, and the point-fit condition number <= 1e9. G1 and G2 additionally need at least 10 `gridChange` drives on at least 5 days in F*; the G2 subset has the stop thresholds 150 drives / 50 days (else its cells are listed non-decisive, not dropped). T2 knots come from F* for every cell (numpy linear-interpolation quantiles at 1/3 and 2/3).
R8 (ladder): precedence L2 > L1 > L0. "Includes zero" agreement is judged against the REFERENCE reading, not against zero alone (if the reference interval excludes zero the L0 requirement is that every decisive day interval has the reference reading). Week/block intervals count in L1. The spread ratio uses all 54 cells with half-width = (hi - lo) / 2 of the reference day interval. Paired-difference intervals and the support margin are descriptive only (not ladder inputs). A fourth state "not assessable" applies when more than 25 % of the cells are non-decisive.
R9 (infrastructure and tests): the sidecars are keyed by raw sha256 AND the `dep_hash` closure hash of the builder (M498). Additional tests: A1 differs from A0 on at least one drive; ladder edge cases (reference spans zero and one cell excludes it; reference excludes zero; >25 % non-decisive); a weighted-block known-answer; G2 excludes exactly `signalResolution.flagsByFile.gridChange`.
