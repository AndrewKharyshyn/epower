# M754 spec: engine-coolant snapshots of the cloud export: a key-off cross-check against OBD, the engine state at the end of unlogged trips, and engine cooling anchored at an unlogged trip (report-only)

Rev 1. Committed BEFORE any tool is written or run. Author: main session (Sonnet). Number taken after checking the CHANGELOG head (M752), open PRs (M753, PR 428) and the remote branch list. Origin: the owner's ideas 5 (engine coolant as a secondary cross-check of OBD) and 2 (unlogged trips for warm-up and cool-down analysis) of 2026-11-23, and the Director's ruling that the cloud has no pack-temperature channel, so unlogged warm-up / cool-down can only be studied for the ENGINE coolant. Next step: Director review, then tool and known-answer tests, blind audit, Director decision, CHANGELOG, PR. No master column, payload, dashboard or published figure changes.

## 0. Why, and what is already known
- The cloud `EngineCoolantTemp` (Vehicle_Information) has only 1,169 rows in the whole export (median gap between rows 49 min), so it is a snapshot channel, not a trace. Probe (not a registered result; aggregates): 506 of the 508 stationary gaps between consecutive logged drives have a coolant row within [end - 2 min, end + 10 min] of the previous logged drive, and 317 of the 331 strict unlogged units (M739) have one within [end - 2 min, end + 10 min] (median 69 C, 10th to 90th percentile 54 to 83 C), while only 85 have one within [start - 10 min, start + 2 min]: the snapshot is mostly a KEY-OFF reading. 72 of the 98 gaps with estimated unlogged movement (M729 classes driving_observed and odometer_increase_only) have a snapshot near the unlogged trip's end (60 of them with at least 0.25 h parked before the next logged start).
- The only registered comparison of cloud and OBD coolant (M734-era probe) had n = 57 drives (median difference -2 C, 90th percentile of the absolute difference 3 C) because it looked for rows INSIDE the drive window; the key-off snapshot lies at or just after the end of the window, so a much larger cross-check is possible.
- The battery pack has no cloud temperature channel: nothing in this study speaks about the pack. The engine coolant is not the pack temperature. Unlogged means no OBD record, not no driving; the driver of the unlogged trips is a different person (owner-reported), which is a confound for any comparison of logged and unlogged trips.

## 2. Data and definitions
- Cloud snapshots: Vehicle_Information rows with a numeric `EngineCoolantTemp`; time in UTC; no clock shift in the primary (the +16 s shift of M730 is a sensitivity).
- OBD coolant per drive: the first and last valid sample of the engine coolant channel inside the drive (`coolant_start`, `coolant_end` as in the corpus function `_battery_temp_traj`, read from the raw files exactly as M751 does); drives without the channel are listed.
- **Key-off snapshot** of a logged drive: the cloud row nearest to the drive end t1 (master start + duration) inside [t1 - 120 s, t1 + 120 s] (primary) and inside [t1 - 120 s, t1 + 600 s] (sensitivity); its age = row time - t1.
- **Unlogged trips:** the strict unlogged units of M739 (331 units; overlap with the padded drive windows below 10 %); the key-off snapshot of a unit uses the same windows around the unit's last accepted odometer step.
- **Cooling pairs.** For a consecutive pair (previous drive a, next logged drive b) from the heat-soak pairing: the anchor is (T_a, t_a) and the arrival is (T_b, t_b) with T_b = OBD `coolant_start` of b and t_b = start of b; ambient T_amb = the manual ambient of b (the mean of `ambientByDrive`, as in the heat-soak block). Anchor types: **S_obd** (class stationary_consistent gaps; anchor = OBD `coolant_end` of a at t1 of a), **S_cloud** (same gaps; anchor = the cloud key-off snapshot of a, primary window), **D_cloud** (gaps of class driving_observed or odometer_increase_only; anchor = the cloud snapshot within [t_u - 120 s, t_u + 120 s] of the last cloud movement t_u = t_b - `last_move_to_next_h`; only gaps with parked time of 0.25 h to 24 h). Parked time = (t_b - t_a) / 3600 h.
- Excess over ambient: e_a = T_a - T_amb and e_b = T_b - T_amb; a pair enters the cooling fit when e_a > 10 C and e_b > 1 C (the engine must be clearly warmer than ambient at the anchor and still measurably warm on arrival); decay model ln(e_b / e_a) = -k x parked hours through the origin (Newtonian), slope by Sen's estimator, k = -slope (per hour), the same estimator family as the published soak fit; parked time capped at 6 h for the primary fit (the engine coolant reaches ambient within hours, so longer gaps carry the ambient-reference artefact described in M751) and 24 h as a sensitivity.

## 3. Estimands
- **K1 key-off cross-check:** logged drives (clean: `ens_outlier_v2` False, missing is not clean) with an OBD `coolant_end` and a snapshot in the primary window: difference d = cloud snapshot - OBD `coolant_end`. Reported: n drives, n days, median d, median |d|, 90th percentile |d|, each with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval (cluster: calendar day of the drive start; seed 42, 4000 draws); by OBD end coolant (below 60 C, 60 C and above) and by snapshot age tier (0 to 60 s, 60 to 120 s after the end, and before the end); the dependence of d on the snapshot age (slope in C per minute, with interval) because the engine cools after key-off; sensitivities: window to +600 s, +16 s shift.
- **K2 end-of-trip engine state of unlogged trips:** n units with a snapshot, the median and quantiles of the cloud key-off coolant with a day-clustered interval for the median, by unit length tercile (cloud kilometres) and month, beside the OBD `coolant_end` of the logged drives in the same length terciles (median difference with a day-clustered interval); descriptive only ("estimated", confounded by the different driver and by trip type).
- **K3 cooling anchored at an unlogged trip:** k, its day-bootstrap interval (cluster: arrival day; seed 42, 4000 draws), n pairs and days for S_obd, S_cloud and D_cloud; differences k_D - k_S_cloud (the unlogged effect with the same anchor type) and k_S_cloud - k_S_obd (the anchor-type effect), each with a paired day-clustered interval; ratio of the decay rates.
- **K4 coverage and selection:** share of logged drives and of unlogged units with a snapshot by month and length tercile; the D gaps without a snapshot (26 of 98) versus those with one, by parked time and month.

## 4. Decision rules (fixed now; judgement thresholds, Director to rule)
- K1: "consistent at key-off" when the median |d| is at most 2 C and the 90th percentile of |d| is at most 5 C and the day interval of the median d lies within +-1 C; otherwise "difference of x C [interval]" with the age dependence stated. Support floor 20 drives and 10 days per stratum, else counts only.
- K3: the unlogged effect is "material" when the interval of k_D - k_S_cloud excludes zero AND the ratio k_S_cloud / k_D lies outside [0.80, 1.25]; "not distinguishable" when the interval includes zero (the minimum detectable difference, 2.8 times the bootstrap SD, is stated; absence of a difference is not equivalence); "small" otherwise. A set below 20 pairs or 10 days reports counts only ("not estimable at this support"). If the anchor-type effect is itself material, the D_cloud comparison is read only against S_cloud.
- Whatever the outcome this milestone shows nothing on the dashboard and does not extend the heat-soak pack results (the pack has no cloud channel).

## 5. Falsification and limits
- Falsified ("the cloud key-off coolant snapshot equals the OBD end coolant within the pre-registered consistency"): K1 not consistent.
- Limits: the snapshot cadence is not controlled and "key-off snapshot" is a hypothesis that the age distribution tests; the engine can still cool or warm in the first minutes after key-off (age dependence reported); engine coolant is not the pack temperature; the ambient is the manual driving-time value of the next drive (long-gap artefact); a single exponential for the engine coolant may be too simple for gaps beyond a few hours (hence the 6 h primary cap); cloud snapshots are 1 C quantised; unlogged trips have a different driver and trip type; the D gaps with a snapshot may not be representative of all D gaps (K4).
