## 1. Question and scope
How much battery exposure does one year of this vehicle's use produce, with an honest interval and a visible assumption spread? Exposure = charge throughput (Ah, GTC, FCE), where it happens (pack temperature, SoC, current), cold-charge exposure index, warm-up and cold-start counts, hot tail, and the calendar (parked) temperature dose. Replaces the "trip class x km" blocks (`cycleLife`, `seasonalLife`, `kLadderScenarios`, `dod`, `parkedTimeBudget`) as the scenario basis; those blocks are to be demoted in M715 with Andrii's sign-off, never deleted (section 10). Out of scope: end-of-life or health forecasts, plating or any mechanism, article figures, dashboard changes (separate milestone).

## 2. Why the current blocks are replaced (audit findings, to be reproduced by the production run, not typed)
Class x km cannot represent short trips (per-start Ah, per-km Ah, per-stopped-hour Ah); the x1.18 January multiplier is unsupported; M28 cold-cycle share (24.9 % below 10 C) vs the sequence model (6.2 % [4.7, 8.0] in the prototype); M28 calendar factor 0.399 vs 0.75 [0.70, 0.80]; parked time carries about 90 % of the Arrhenius time dose; the hot share of Ah is probe-dependent (T1, hottest probe, pack mean); unlogged driving hides inside "parked" hours (logged km = 83.6 % of dashboard-odometer km since 2026-07-24).

## 3. Estimands (all model-derived; "estimated", never "measured")
Two named quantities per metric, both reported (C1): (E-exp) the EXPECTED annual exposure of the scenario, whose interval is the day-pool bootstrap (estimation uncertainty of the mean over the pool); (E-real) the exposure of ONE realised year, whose predictive interval is the distribution of the replayed-year totals over the draws (adds one-year realisation variability). The interval of the prototype tables ("mean [2.5 %, 97.5 %] over day-block draws") is the E-real interval and is labelled so; the E-exp interval is narrower. Neither contains assumption uncertainty (section 6).
Per scenario, annual: distance (km, odometer-anchored), drives, engine starts, Ah throughput and GTC / FCE on BOTH capacity bases (5 Ah rated and 2.1 kWh legacy, both `verified:false`); the integration rule is PINNED to the project rule (the Ah and kWh integrals of `tools/ah_throughput.py` / the master columns): the prototype's own integration rule is 1.044 times the project rule, so every prototype target below is compared on the project rule (S2, 2.1 kWh basis: 2,045 [1,664, 2,476] GTC on the project rule against 2,135 on the prototype rule), and the integration-rule bracket (hold vs linear sign-aware, about 4 %) is reported as a sensitivity, Ah and Ah x C by pack-temperature bin (< -10, < 0, < 10, < 15, >= 35, >= 40 C), by SoC bin and by C-rate, cold-charge index below 10 / 0 C (Ah x C, derated by the extrapolated charge-limit map and not derated), starts with pack < 15 / < 0 C, Ah while pack < 15 C, hot-tail Ah by THREE definitions side by side (T1, hottest probe, pack mean) with unassigned support shown, cycle Arrhenius factor and 25 C-equivalent cycle dose (Ea 40 / 50 / 60 kJ/mol), calendar dose of driving and of parked time separately, parked share of the dose, parked hours below 0 C and at or above 30 C, mean parked pack temperature, coldest start of the year.

## 4. Population, pools, replay unit
- Source: `drive_master.csv` (665 drives, 126 clean days) with the per-slice (10 s) current, speed and temperature statistics rebuilt under `tools/` from the raw files; raw read-only. Whole calendar days are the replay unit (drive sequence, clock times, gaps, zero-drive days, weekday pattern, first departure, overnight soak and the order of urban and highway legs stay intact); complete weeks are a sensitivity.
- Pools: summer-pattern = May-Aug days, autumn-pattern = Sep-Nov days. Excluded days, each counted and listed by date: ambiguous logging (assumption, 2026-05-19..06-08), missing channels, unlogged driving by the dash odometer. Pools are NOT pooled across thermal classes to reach a support floor.
- Season map of the primary scenario (owner statement): Jun-Aug summer pattern, May and Sep half and half, Oct-Apr autumn pattern. Occasional long winter trips: stated explicitly (a day-pool draw never invents a trip the pool does not contain; a long-trip sensitivity adds the logged long days at a stated frequency).
- Zero-drive vs unlogged day (C7): a calendar day with no logged drive is a TRUE zero-drive day when the dash-odometer interval check (S4) shows no distance outside the logged drives and the engine-coolant / SoC rule of M708 shows no use in the surrounding gap; otherwise it is an UNLOGGED day, excluded from the pools and counted in f_a, never treated as parked time. Both counts are listed by date.
- Initial state of every drawn day: the pack temperature at the first departure is NOT taken from the source day; it is the output of the state model carrying the previous drawn day's end state across the overnight gap at the target-month ambient (a sequence of days is a chain, not independent blocks). The first day of the year starts from the state model's steady parked state at the target ambient.
- Pool influence (C8): a leave-one-day-out influence check of every headline metric on each pool (summer 85 days, autumn 55 days, one multi-day trip) is reported; a single day moving a headline by more than its E-exp interval half-width is named and listed.
- Annual distance is NOT identified by a half-year window (prototype: 26,165 [18,367, 35,864] km untilted). Primary scenario: day draw tilted (exponential tilt on day km) so that the expected annual distance equals the odometer lifetime mean, derived from the dash odometer at the latest ingestion, never typed. The odometer target counts LOGGED km plus unlogged km once: the tilt is solved on logged km per day with f_a applied after the draw, so unlogged km are not counted twice (a known-answer test); the tilt also changes the trip mix and the per-km intensity, so S2 and S2u are shown side by side (prototype: 0.1183 vs 0.1095 GTC per km).

## 5. Method
- Climate baseline for ambient (owner idea 2026-11-17, parked-time exposure from the usual Ukrainian climate): the repo's monthly normals (`seasonalLife.assumptions.monthlyTempC`, Kyiv, user-supplied external climatology, NOT measured by this study), interpolated to the day; each source day keeps its own anomaly; the diurnal swing (amplitude and peak hour) comes from the driver-recorded vehicle-sensor readings (uncalibrated; the same sensor, heat-soaked after parking). It enters as a MONTHLY DISTRIBUTION (daily mean + diurnal cycle), not a single mean: the Arrhenius dose is convex. Hourly reanalysis ambient (external download, owner decision needed) is a sensitivity for winter, not the baseline.
- Temperature transform of a source day placed in a target month: ambient shift = target daily mean - source daily mean applied to every ambient reading of that day; the driving itself (speeds, currents, SoC) is unchanged; engine-on share is not transformed (stated); throughput per km is NOT raised in the cold by default (kappa = 0, data; the M28 range is a sensitivity).
- Pack temperature (state model): PARKED = the M709 one-state diurnal park model (`analyses/M709/park_model.json`, sha256 3677301a...: tau 9.105 h [8.19, 10.16], one pack-above-ambient offset 0.469 C [-0.578, 1.407] on 596 non-flagged pairs; the exploratory 7.55 h and the season-specific offsets +1.16 / +3.41 C are SUPERSEDED). The offset bracket of the sensitivity analysis is [0, 2.7] C (owner prior: a parked car is at or above ambient; upper bound from the autumn estimate +1.8 [0.8, 2.7] of the restart-temperature handoff); the M709 interval's negative lower bound is not used, the end-of-drive pack temperature carried across the gap; DRIVING = I^2 R(T) heating plus relaxation toward a coolant-air boundary (c0 + c1 x ambient), R(T) with the fitted activation energy (17.6 kJ/mol, one Ea, extrapolation labelled). The two-state model is not used (M709 census: not justified; P-R2 pending).
- Parked time budget: gaps are classified parked versus unlogged driving using the dash-odometer interval check (S4); flagged unlogged-use gaps are not parked time. The unlogged-distance factor f_a applies to AUTUMN-pattern days (distance outside logged drives; ranged in `analyses/M712_ranges.json`); summer-pattern days use f_s, derived from the odometer of the summer window and held fixed (not ranged), both derived from the dash odometer, never typed. f_a is an ASSUMPTION with a stated range, derived from the odometer, and carries into throughput.
- Calendar dose: parked and driving separately, Arrhenius with Ea 40 / 50 / 60 kJ/mol (labelled sensitivity), parked SoC from the end-of-drive SoC (SoC-weighted dose with gamma = 0 / 1 as sensitivity). Calendar and cycle terms are not separable on one vehicle (time vs cumulative Ah correlation 0.99); only the sum and a labelled prior ladder are reported.
- Charge derating: the logged BMS charge-limit register map linearly extrapolated in temperature (labelled); charge above the cap kept at the capped rate (exposure-conservative).

## 6. Uncertainty
- Resampling: whole days, day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws): E-exp (expected annual exposure) is the day-pool bootstrap: for additive metrics (km, Ah, kWh, GTC, FCE, Ah by bin, doses) the exact tilted expectation of each bootstrap pool, for chained or threshold metrics (starts below a threshold, episode counts) an inner Monte Carlo with a fixed number of replayed years and a fixed seed per pool; E-real (one realised year) is the predictive distribution of the replayed-year totals; extremes (coldest start of the year, hot-tail maxima) are E-real only. Neither holds assumption uncertainty.
- Assumption uncertainty: a Sobol total-order analysis plus a one-at-a-time tornado over the pre-registered ranges of `analyses/M712_ranges.json` (sha256 770a8f00..., frozen with this spec; the scenario shift table S3a / S3b / S3c / S4 and the season map are in the same file) (winter ambient shift, parking offset, tau_p, f_a, kappa, in-drive coolant-air slope c1, Ea_age, R(T) Ea, gamma), reported beside every scenario. Winter shift and tau_p dominate the cold outputs (kappa and the winter offset follow) in the prototype; the unlogged-distance factor and kappa dominate annual GTC; c1 dominates the hot share. For the cold outputs the assumption spread is several times wider than the day-block interval: the uncertainty is structural, stated beside every figure. The Sobol ranking follows the chosen ranges (it is a statement about this table, not a finding); it is REPORTED, not gated.

These used the SUPERSEDED park constants and the prototype's own integration rule (1.044 x the project rule). The expected direction of the M709 park refit on the cold-charge index and the hot share is NOT stated here as a target (the earlier +12 % / -2 % figures came from the restart-temperature exploratory run, `analyses/restart_temperature_2026-11-17/results/exposure_impact.json`, S2 only, not re-run by the production model); the production run reports both park versions and the delta.
- G-S0 (identity): with no temperature transform, no tilt and every pool day drawn once, the replay conserves per-day and total km, Ah and energy exactly (synthetic known-answer test and the real corpus).
- G-S1 (anchoring): the tilted primary scenario hits the odometer-anchored expected distance within 0.5 % on logged-plus-unlogged km, without double counting f_a.
- G-S2 (consistency check, in-sample: tau_p, c0 / c1 and alpha are fitted on the same days, C3): on the observed eligible day sequence the simulated cycle Arrhenius factor (Ea 50) is within 5 % of the observed-temperature value and the simulated count of starts below 15 C within +-15 % of the logged count. It is NOT a validation.
- G-S2b (validation, month-blocked hold-out): the state model parameters (tau_p, c0 / c1, alpha, offset) are fitted on May-August days and scored on September-November days: two-sided |simulated - observed| <= 8 percentage points for the Ah share at or above 35 C (pack mean) and <= 15 % for starts below 15 C; the relative error is reported beside the absolute one (the Sep-Nov hot share is small, about 10 % in S1, so 8 points alone is weak); the hot share is reported as a bracket whose bias bound is taken from this hold-out bias, not from the in-sample 6 points (a direction-only rule would accept a false-optimistic bias). The hold-out is not clean (ambient amp / hpk fitted on the whole corpus; split confounded with slow polling and the battery software update), as for M708.
- G-S3 (reproduction, binding): the production run on the IDENTICAL inputs of the prototype (the superseded park constants and the prototype's integration rule; the prototype pool rule = the ambiguous-logging ASSUMPTION window 2026-05-19..06-08 excluded as in the prototype, NOT the Q5 data rule; the prototype's day exclusions (21 ambiguous, 8 missing channels, 14 unlogged); seed 42, 4000 draws) reproduces the S2 headline estimates inside the prototype day-block intervals above; THEN the M709 park version and the project integration rule are reported and the delta attributed per input. The prototype E-real intervals are about +-25 % wide, so the point delta per metric and the E-exp check are reported beside the interval test. A binding mismatch on identical inputs stops the milestone for the Director.
- G-S4 (assumption table): Sobol and tornado outputs are produced for every headline metric and reported with the ranking (prototype order for the cold-charge index: winter shift, tau_p, kappa, then the winter offset); not a gate.
- Escalation: a displayed cell or KPI leaving its previous interval, or a failed gate that is not a plain environment error, goes to the Director; replacing a displayed block needs Andrii's decision.

## 9. Falsifiers and winter protocol
- The model is falsified for cold outputs if on Dec-Feb drives the free-run start-temperature MAE exceeds 3 C or |bias| exceeds 2 C on >= 10 drives / 5 days; the throughput-per-km claim (kappa = 0) if Ah per km at matched trip length and speed is higher than in autumn by the M28 range (+6..16 % per 10 C); the single-Ea R(T) extrapolation if resistance at 0 C is outside 1.914 x R(25) [1.833, 1.992].
- Frozen winter predictions P1-P7 of the prototype stay the template (P1 start-temperature MAE <= 3 C; P2 Ah share below 10 C within the S2-S3b band; P3 R(0 C) / R(25 C) = 1.914; P4 charge limit 33.92 kW at 0 C and SoC 60 %, 18.4 kW at -10 C; P5 Ah per km not higher than in autumn; P6 peak charge C-rate below 0 C within the limit map; P7 engine-on share +0.8 points per C of cooling); their parameters are frozen at the M709 values (`analyses/M709/park_model.json`, sha256 3677301a...: tau_p 9.105 h, offset 0.469 C) and the other parameters of the production state-model fit, by value and CHANGELOG hash BEFORE the first winter drive; predictions are scored before any refit; refits are new versions with the old predictions kept; sub-zero pack data trigger an R(T) and limit-map curvature refit. Each evaluation cell needs >= 10 pairs / drives on >= 5 days, else "not evaluable". Operational tests (C9): P2 holds if the day-clustered 95 % interval of the realised Ah share below 10 C lies inside the S2-S3b band computed for the REALISED winter ambient; P5 is a one-sided test: Ah per km at matched trip-length and speed class is NOT higher than in autumn, rejected when the day-clustered lower 95 % bound of the winter-minus-autumn increase exceeds +6 % (the lower end of the M28 alternative +6..16 % per 10 C, scaled to the realised temperature difference). The parameters are frozen by value and CHANGELOG hash before 2026-12-01 (before the first expected winter drives).
- Winter driver records (optional): dash odometer at each ingestion, unlogged-segment notes, parking context (sun, shade, garage), long-soak gaps.
