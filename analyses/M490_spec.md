# M490 spec (Rev 1): subdivision-invariant interval/bin support (touch) rule in the regeneration extractor v2

Status: Rev 1, written 2026-10-23 BEFORE any M490 computation. Origin: external audit Update 7 (2026-10-22), finding A06 (P2), confirmed on disk by an Opus feasibility review. Needs: this spec, a Director ruling on the questions below, a blind audit, and (M446 Rev 1.1 O7) a Director decision if any displayed cell changes support status.

## Question
The registered rule (analyses/M441_spec.md, policy `touchMinS = 0.025`) says: a braking interval TOUCHES a speed bin iff the total overlap duration of that interval with that bin is at least 25 ms. The implementation (`tools/regen_v2.py`, `_exact_energy`, line `touchw: (h >= touchMinS)`, and line 296 `bincount(comb, weights=touchw) > 0`) thresholds EACH piece, where a piece is a sub-interval between consecutive knots of the union of the speed, current and voltage native timestamps, and then asks whether ANY piece passed. Inserting a redundant native knot (a current or voltage sample that adds no information) therefore splits an overlap into smaller pieces and can turn a touched cell into an untouched one without any change of the signal. Example (audit counterexample): a 100 ms overlap split into ten 10 ms pieces has the correct charge energy but touch = false.

## Estimand (the rule being implemented, unchanged in words)
For interval i and speed bin b: `overlap(i, b) = sum of the durations of the pieces of i whose mid-point speed lies in b`; `touch(i, b) = overlap(i, b) >= touchMinS` (with a 1e-9 s tolerance so that a total of exactly 25 ms touches). Property required: touch(i, b) is invariant to the insertion of redundant knots (subdivision invariance) and monotone in the signal.

## Change (frozen)
1. In `_exact_energy`, return the piece duration `h` (as `piece_h`) instead of the thresholded `touchw`; in `extract_drive` compute `touch = (bincount(comb, weights=piece_h) >= touchMinS - 1e-9)`.
2. Everything else is untouched: the exact positive-part energy kernel, the speed-edge cuts, the telescoping kinetic-energy differences, the energy bins (dv2, charge, chargeOff), the interval list, the strict/plateau/trimmed classes, the aggregation, the bootstrap, the support floors (>= 200 strict intervals, >= 10 drives on >= 5 days).
3. The boxcar/slice path (`_slice_energy`, sensitivity variant `box3`, used only by the locked M442/M444 study) has no touch threshold at all (any piece counts); it is NOT changed and is NOT re-run (the locked studies stay as recorded). The disclosure is added to the spec of record (here) and to the CHANGELOG entry; the Director is asked (question 2) whether the slice path should be aligned in a later milestone.
4. `EXTRACTOR_VERSION` is bumped; the per-drive caches rebuild automatically through the M489 dependency-closure key (`closureSrc`, `extractorSrc`).
5. Scope of the effect, established from the code before any run: the touch flag enters only the SUPPORT counts (`Ns`, `Np`, `Sec`, `Pd` in `regen_v2_agg`, the KE-share mix); the energy and charge bins that form the displayed ratios are NOT touch-weighted. So no displayed ratio changes by construction; only intervals-per-cell, drives-per-cell, seconds-per-cell and the support status derived from them can change. The first run verifies this claim (acceptance A below).

## Known-answer tests (written before the run, `tests/synthetic/test_regen_touch_subdivision.py`)
- Constant signals: a 100 ms overlap built as 1, 2, 4, 10 and 40 equal pieces (inserted redundant I/V knots): touch identical (true), energy identical to 1e-12 relative, bin assignment identical.
- Varying signals: the same overlap with a redundant knot inserted at 1, 3 and 7 random positions: touch and energies identical.
- At and around the threshold: total overlap 24 ms (false), 24.999 ms (false), 25 ms (true), 25.001 ms (true), each also split into 2, 5 and 12 pieces: results identical to the unsplit case.
- Two disjoint overlaps of one interval with the same bin cannot occur (the speed is monotone inside a braking interval); an interval crossing a bin edge touches both bins only for the portion in each.
- A known failure of the old rule is reproduced on the old code path (the 100 ms / ten 10 ms case) and passes on the new one.

## Reporting and acceptance (frozen; pre-registered)
Run on the current corpus (606 drives) with the new extractor for the base variant and every displayed variant (native fast-polled and slow-polled strata; the SOC-standardized views read the same cells):
- A. Energy invariance (must hold, else STOP): every displayed ratio, numerator (charge), denominator (dv2) and interval estimate in `regenDisplayV2` and `regenCaptureV2` is byte-identical before and after (the touch flag does not enter them). Any difference is a defect in the change, not a result.
- B. Flip accounting: the number of (interval, bin) touch flags that change false -> true, per cadence stratum (fast, slow) and in total, with the share of all touch flags; no flag may change true -> false (monotonicity; else STOP).
- C. Support accounting: for EVERY displayed cell (cohort x view x stratum x SOC selection x basis x speed bin) the support counts (intervals, drives, days, seconds) and the support status (supported / thin / outside) before and after, with the list of cells whose status changes.
- D. Cadence dependence: the flip share per stratum, because fast polling creates more knots and is expected to lose more touches under the old rule; reported as a descriptive sensitivity, never as a gate and not as a correction of the carried B6 cadence-invariance check (that study stays FAILED and carried at its study basis; this milestone does not re-run it).
- E. Decision: if C lists any displayed cell whose support status changes, or `S*` (the fixed SOC-mix reference) changes in a displayed view, or a point leaves its previous day interval (M446 Rev 1.1 O7), the milestone STOPS before the payload splice and goes to the Director with the compact list. If C is empty (counts may change, no status changes), the payload is refreshed through the normal `regen_v2` stage and the new counts are published.
- F. Language: 'support rule applied to the total overlap of an interval with a speed bin'; never 'corrected', 'fixed estimator' or 'more accurate'. The payload keys are unchanged; `regenDisplayV2` counts may differ at the next stage run.

## What would falsify the change
(i) A displayed ratio or interval differs (A). (ii) A flag changes true -> false (B). (iii) The new rule is not subdivision invariant on any known-answer case. (iv) The cache rebuild does not reproduce the energy bins of the old cache to 1e-12 relative (extraction regression).

## Validation chain (frozen)
py_compile, the known-answer tests, a cache rebuild of all drives for the base variant, the A-C comparison script `tools/m490_touch_effect.py` (reads the old and the new cache, writes `analyses/M490_results.json`), `node build_html.js`, `node validate_jsdom.js`, the isolation diff (only the touch-derived leaves change), `python release_check.py`, the CI unit list (run with new files staged), blind audit (`analytical-auditor`, Sonnet, claim + data path only: the subdivision invariance and the flip counts from the cache files), Director decision.

## Questions for the Director
1. Is the total-overlap rule (with the 1e-9 s tolerance) the right reading of the registered 25 ms rule, and is a monotone growth of touched sets acceptable without a re-registration of the M441 support floors (200 intervals, 10 drives, 5 days)?
2. The slice (boxcar) path has no threshold at all. Leave it (locked study, sensitivity only) and disclose, or align it in a later milestone?
3. If acceptance E finds no status change but counts rise, may the refreshed counts be published at the next ordinary `regen_v2` run without further escalation?
4. Stratum asymmetry (D): any wording constraint for the disclosure that the two cadence strata are affected unequally?

## Rev 2 (Director ruling 2026-10-23, decision 'revise'; written BEFORE any M490 computation)
Answers: (1) Yes: total overlap >= touchMinS (1e-9 s tolerance) is the faithful reading of the M441 rule (the `regen_v2.py` docstring says 'exact overlap >= touchMinS'); the per-piece threshold was an implementation deviation. The floors (200 intervals / 10 drives / 5 days) need no re-registration; the growth of support counts is disclosed as an alignment with the registered rule. (2) The slice / `box3` path stays untouched (locked, sensitivity only), disclosed; no alignment milestone unless `box3` is ever displayed. (3) Yes, only if the extended acceptance E is clean (amendment 2) and only as its own no-new-drive milestone; publication through the `regen_v2` stage plus `regen_refresh_report`. (4) Wording: 'support rule applied to the total overlap of an interval with a speed bin, as registered in M441'; never corrected / fixed / more accurate / cadence-corrected; the flip share per stratum is descriptive; never 'fast vs slow' and never a cadence effect on recovery; B6 stays FAILED.
Amendments (frozen; they replace the conflicting text above):
- A1. The section-5 claim 'no displayed ratio changes' holds for the OBSERVED basis and for the charge / dv2 sums only. On the SOC-standardized basis `regen_std.joint()` builds the joint-cell support from touch-weighted `Ns` / `Pd` / `present`, and `common_support` / `cand` select the fixed reference mix S* and drop groups from it: a status flip can change S* or group membership and therefore a standardized ratio. Acceptance A is restricted to observed cells and to the charge, dv2 and chargeOff sums; a standardized value may change only through S* or group membership, which is an E trigger, not a defect.
- A2. Acceptance C and E also cover: the joint (group x SOC band) cells, the S* strata and S* itself, dropped groups, the `b_nat` bracket set (`regen_display`), and the cold view. Any change of S*, of a dropped group, of a displayed cell's support status or of a bracket-set membership stops the milestone before the payload splice and goes to the Director.
- A3. Touch consumers (verified at implementation by grep and listed in the CHANGELOG): `Ns`, `Np`, `Pd`, `Sec`, `nZeroCharge` (`Zs`), `days` (from `Ns` and `Np`) and `mix.intervalShare`; `keShare` is NOT touch-weighted. Only the speed and zones views read `bin_touch`; the temperature, class, speed-start, speed-end and all views use one-hot touch and must be byte-identical (additional control).
- A4. Every cache variant the stage reads is rebuilt and compared: `base`, `d2p0`, `d2p1`, `d3p0`, `d3p1`, `d3p2` and the offset files; `box3` must be byte-identical (control). The old caches and the old payload are snapshotted before the run.
- A5. State whether the stage recomputes `regenCaptureV2Study` / the B6 cadence-invariance block; if it does, report its supported-cell set before and after. It stays FAILED and carried at its study basis; the locked M442 / M444 studies are not re-run.
- A6. Disclosure: `Sec` counts the full interval `dt` in every touched bin, not the overlap; unchanged by this milestone and disclosed.
Risks recorded by the Director: an S* shift moves a displayed standardized bar; fast-stratum status flips; mixing the change with an ingestion confounds attribution (hence no new drives in the same milestone).
