## Facts on disk (step-0 inventory, `analyses/M413_cap_inventory.md`, static trace, anchors spot-checked)
- One constant (`CAP_KWH = 2.1`, `compute_drive_summary_v6.py:245`, `compute_summary_arrays.py:57`) serves BOTH exposure normalisation (`gtc = thr/CAP_KWH`, `fce = thr/(2 CAP_KWH)`, `cap_ah_est = CAP_KWH*1000/v_nom`, the `*_Crate` columns) and SoC bookkeeping / eligibility (`soc_delta_kwh`, `energy_residual_kwh`, `RESIDUAL_MAX_KWH = 0.35 = 2.1/6`). Only the first role is in scope.
- Rated-basis exposure is Ah-based: GTC_rated = (Ah_d + Ah_c) / 5.0, FCE_rated = GTC_rated / 2, C-rate = peak current / 5.0 (a constant divisor). The Ah sidecar `ah_throughput.csv` (M402, blind-audited, same I/V pairs as the kWh integral, offset-uncorrected like `gtc`) provides the per-drive Ah. The ratio to the legacy basis is NOT a uniform constant (per-drive factor 2100 / (5 V_eff), V_eff = kWh/Ah, median 360.4 V); the aggregate ratio is 1.1587 (`capacityBasis.exposure`, n = 538).
- `rf_efc` (SoC rainflow) does not depend on CAP_KWH and is unchanged; the ratio rf_efc/fce flips from about 1.06 to about 0.91 (matched drives), so the ledger cross-check interpretation and one hard-coded dashboard sentence change.
- 19 top-level payload keys plus `seasonalCharts` leaves, per-drive-derived chart points, 74 + 82 hand-typed ledger notes in `summary_config.json`, hard-coded dashboard text, gates and tests that pin the "2.1 kWh" wording depend on the exposure normalisation (list in the inventory).

## Design (to be reviewed by the Director BEFORE any code)
1. **Split the constant by role.** `CAP_KWH` keeps its SoC-bookkeeping and eligibility role unchanged (no behaviour change anywhere in bookkeeping, offset, residual, eligibility, `ens_*`, `net_draw_kwh_corr`, the energy Monte Carlo draws, GTR). A new exposure reference `CAP_AH_RATED = 5.0` is used ONLY for the exposure quantities. Master columns are NOT rewritten (the master MD5 and the corpus anchor stay; the legacy columns `gtc`, `fce`, `efc_per100km` (ML feature), `*_Crate`, `cap_ah_est` keep their meaning and are documented as legacy-basis columns).
2. **Mechanism: one in-memory substitution point in the arrays step.** Directly after the master is loaded in `compute_summary_arrays.py`, an exposure frame replaces `gtc`, `fce`, the `*_Crate` columns and `cap_ah_est` by their rated-basis values (from the Ah sidecar and the existing `*_peak_A` columns) when `EXPOSURE_BASIS == "rated5Ah"`; downstream producers are untouched except the few that use `CAP_KWH` directly for exposure (`cycleBySpeed` rate accumulator, `metricConvention`, the C-rate strings). No master column is written; ML columns, `efc_per100km` and `ens_*` stay as published.
3. **Control build, defined as in M403 gate (e):** two FRESH arrays builds on the same master and raw set, build A = `legacy`, build B = `rated5Ah` (same code, flag only); the diff B vs A (never a fresh build vs the published file) must lie inside the expected-changed set derived from the inventory; every changed leaf is attributed (exposure normalisation / C-rate / text); the leaf-level diff B-A is then spliced into the published payload (allow-listed paths), so no unrelated leaf moves. `drive_master.csv` MD5 before and after must be identical; the 16 ML columns are reported for information only.
4. **Dual report.** Every switched headline keeps its legacy value in a disclosure line bound to the same key: a top-level block `exposureBasisLegacy` (scalar headlines and chart-level summaries computed in build A: gtcSum, fceSum, class intensities, C-rate medians/maxima) and the existing `capacityBasis.exposure` table. The dashboard shows the rated-basis value as the headline and "legacy 2.1 kWh basis: x" next to it; the basis label ("rated 5.0 Ah (Nissan Article-10, documentary); throughput integrated from logged current, estimated") is bound to the registry, never typed.
5. **Method-change list (gate (f)).** The shifts (+15.9 % aggregate for GTC/FCE; C-rate by the factor 5.0/cap_ah_est per drive) are declared a method change, not drift; the headline-gate exceedances they cause are listed in the CHANGELOG entry and are not counted as gate failures; `masterRefitAudit`/determinism gates are unaffected because the master is unchanged.
6. **Language.** Old wording (kWh/2.1 basis statements) becomes a language rule with its ledger id; required: "rated capacity 5 Ah (Nissan Article-10)", "documentary, not a measurement, not a confirmation of the fitted SoC scale"; forbidden: "measured/usable/true/actual capacity", any derived "rated kWh", GTC life-fraction claims.

### Adopted design after the ruling
1. **Q1-Q6 as ruled above.** The mixture is therefore explicit and per block: headline exposure sums, class intensities and C-rate switch; the six threshold-comparison blocks, the energy Monte Carlo, the legacy ML feature and the e4orce side of the cross-vehicle comparison stay legacy; every block carries a script-written `exposureBasis` tag (`rated5Ah` or `legacy2p1kWh`), and the semantic gate allows one basis per rendered panel unless both are tagged, and no ratio across bases.
2. **dailyFingerprints k-means feature `gtcMean`: stays legacy** (it is a feature of a clustering fitted on the legacy master column; switching it would change cluster membership for no reporting benefit). Declared; if the cluster profile text quotes GTC it carries the legacy tag.
3. **Item 4 completed (M404 ruling 8):** the dual report keeps raw Ah/kWh throughput as the primary figure, the window-fraction band as a scenario parameter (not a CI), n drives / days, and the weakest-cell caveat; the legacy value sits in the disclosure line bound to the same key.
4. **One shared exposure function** (`tools/exposure_frame.py`, `exposure_frame(dm, basis)`) is the only reader of `gtc`, `fce`, `*_Crate`, `cap_ah_est` for exposure in every master consumer (compute_summary_arrays, cohort_arrays, compute_seasonal, speed_split, cadence tools, regen_seasonal_raw, extend_session_ledgers, release_check audits). A grep gate (`tests/synthetic/test_exposure_readers.py`) fails if a new direct reader appears. The default basis is `legacy2p1kWh` until S2.
5. **Sidecar as an ingest stage with a coverage gate** (`ah_throughput` is already a stage, M402): the exposure frame requires sidecar rows for every master drive that has a finite legacy `gtc` (per-drive NaN otherwise, never a legacy fallback); the 538 vs 540 populations are reconciled in a printed table (master finite gtc, sidecar rows, ens_outlier_v2-clean, intersection) before S2.
6. **Control build hardening:** build A is run twice (null diff must be empty); a leaf is spliced only where build A equals the published payload at that leaf, otherwise it escalates; arrays whose order changes are replaced whole; every output file is covered (summary_arrays.json, cohort_arrays.json, seasonal files, summary_config.json ledgers); the sidecar MD5 is recorded; the expected-changed set has a must-change half (leaves that MUST differ: the exposure headline sums, intensities, C-rate leaves) and a may-not-change half (everything else).

## Rev 2 (S1 = M414 implementation notes, 2026-10-12; no change to the Rev 1 decisions)
- **One substitution point, allow-listed splice.** Instead of splitting every producer by block (which would touch a 17.8k-line file in dozens of places), `build_summary_arrays` calls `exposure_frame(dm)` once; at the flip (M415) build B (`XT_EXPOSURE_BASIS=rated5Ah`) is compared with build A (legacy), and only the allow-listed leaf paths (Q1-Q6: the headline exposure sums, class intensities, C-rate leaves, ledger numbers) are spliced from B into the published payload. The threshold-comparison blocks, the energy Monte Carlo, the legacy ML feature, dailyFingerprints and the e4orce leg therefore stay legacy by construction (not in the allow-list), and each switched or legacy block gets its `exposureBasis` tag in the splice.
- **Routing (S1).** DataFrame readers call `exposure_frame()`; dict-row readers (`compute_seasonal.py`, `cohort_arrays.py`) call `row_overrides()`; `tests/synthetic/test_exposure_readers.py` lists every direct reader as ROUTED or LEGACY_BY_DESIGN (with the reason) and fails on a new one. Default basis `legacy2p1kWh` returns the same object / `None`, so S1 is byte-identical by construction and by the null control diff (build at HEAD vs build with S1).
- **Coverage.** The sidecar covers exactly the drives with a finite legacy `gtc`: master rows 554, finite gtc 540, sidecar finite Ah 540, intersection 540, canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` False) 538 (`analyses/M414_exposure_reconciliation.json`, stage `exposure_coverage`, `release_check` step). - **Deferred to S2 (found while routing):** producers that use `CAP_KWH` directly for exposure instead of a master column: the `cycleBySpeed` rate accumulator (raw pass accumulates |I V| kWh; an Ah accumulator is needed), `metricConvention` (`capKwh`, EFC standard = discharge/Cref) and the C-rate strings built from `cap_ah_est`.
