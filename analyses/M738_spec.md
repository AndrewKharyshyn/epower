# M738 spec: exposure of the unlogged driving seen only by the cloud export, with masked-drive validation (report-only)

Rev 1. Committed BEFORE any tool is written or run. Author: main session (Sonnet). Next step: Director review, then tool and tests, blind audit, Director decision. Milestone number follows M737 (map-grade G0, merged) and the winter session's M736.

## 0. Why, and what is already known
- M729 (report-only side tables, read from `analyses/M729_cloud_brief.json`, not re-derived here): the cloud odometer gained 13,285 km over the export span against 11,062 km logged in the master; 333 strict cloud-only movement bursts total 1,921 km (crude definition 1,956 km); gap classes between consecutive logged drives: 508 stationary-consistent, 72 driving-observed (1,808 km), 26 odometer-increase-only, 105 uncertain, 4 no cloud data, 1 export break; 21 of 194 dates have no export rows.
- The published "logged share of the odometer" and every drive-level KPI describe the logged corpus only. The study has no description of what the unlogged driving looks like (length, speed mix, ambient). That is the unknown this milestone bounds. It is NOT a correction of any published figure and no logged quantity is replaced.
- The cloud series are coarse (about 30 s cadence, displayed speed capped at 130 km/h, ambient in 1 C steps, positions with the M730 +16 s clock shift unapplied), so the first question is how much of a drive's exposure the cloud alone can reconstruct. That is answered on logged drives, where the OBD record is the truth, by masked-drive validation.

## 1. Constraints
- Cloud-derived quantities are "estimated / reconstructed / model-derived / proxy", never "measured". OBD stays primary; nothing replaces a logged channel.
- No published output changes: no `summary_arrays.json`, payload, dashboard, regeneration key or `drive_master.csv` change (MD5 `d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98`, 717 rows, asserted before and after); no frozen winter file read for writing, no winter prediction scored (M726). Outputs: `analyses/M738_*.json/csv`, aggregates only (public repository: no coordinates, no per-drive tracks).
- Statistics: calendar day is the resampling cluster; day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); n (drives or bursts, days, km) with every estimate; no pooling of unlike strata without saying so.
- Unlogged driving that the export also misses (the 21 dates without rows, the 30 Jul to 1 Aug break) is unobservable here: it is listed with its dates, never modelled.

## 2. Data (read-only)
`drive_master.csv` windows (padded 60 s, as M729/M734), raw drive CSVs (`XT_RAW_DIR`) for the OBD truth of the validation quantities, M729 store streams (Odometer, Trip_Information speed, Vehicle_Information ambient, Geolocation), `analyses/M729_cloud_gaps.csv`, M730 clock-shift estimate (sensitivity only), M734 screened-ambient rules (imported, not redefined).

## 3. Definitions (frozen by this spec)
- Cloud movement unit: a run of cloud odometer steps (M729 step rule: successive samples at most 35 s apart, positive increment, implied speed at most 180 km/h) joined while the pause between consecutive accepted steps is at most 120 s (the M729 strict burst rule). The same unit is built inside logged drives (masked, section 4 V) and outside all padded drive windows (unlogged, section 4 U).
- Exposure vector of a unit: distance (cloud odometer, km), duration (first to last accepted step, s), mean moving speed (distance over moving time), time shares in three displayed-speed classes (below 30, 30 to 80, above 80 km/h, from cloud speed samples, time-weighted trapezoid), stop-and-go proxy (number of cloud speed samples at zero inside the unit per km), screened ambient (M734 rule, reported with its sample count; absent when the M734 minimum is not met), hour-of-day class (Kyiv local, night 22 to 6, day otherwise), day type (weekday or weekend).
- Truth vector (logged drives only): distance (OBD integral of vehicle speed, cross-checked against master `distance_km`), duration (master), mean moving speed, time shares in the same three classes from OBD speed, ambient = driver-recorded manual ambient.

## 4. Estimands (pre-registered)
- **V1 reconstruction error (masked-drive validation).** For each canonical-clean logged drive that has at least two cloud steps in its padded window, build the cloud movement unit(s) inside the window (the OBD record is not used) and compare with the truth vector. If a drive yields several units they are summed to one drive vector (distance, durations, time-weighted shares). Reported per quantity: signed and absolute error (median, P90), relative error for distance and duration, total-variation distance of the three-class time shares, ambient screened minus manual (M734 reuse); with day-clustered bootstrap intervals, n (drives, days, km), and by speed-class mix and by drive length tercile.
- **V2 detection of logged drives.** Share of logged drives for which the cloud builds at least one unit with at least 90 % of the master distance (recall), and the share of cloud-only units that overlap a logged window by less than 10 % of their duration (this is the definition of "unlogged" in U). Cloud units near a logged window boundary (overlap between 10 % and 90 %) are reported as boundary cases, not assigned.
- **U1 unlogged exposure.** Over cloud units outside all padded windows, restricted to the stratum where V1 acceptance (R1) holds: number of units, days, total km, hours; distribution of length, mean speed and time shares; screened ambient where available. All as estimates with intervals; units are the strict M729 bursts (crude definition as sensitivity).
- **U2 representativeness.** Total-variation distance between the speed-class time-share vector (and the length-tercile mix, and the day-type and hour-class mixes) of the logged corpus (km-weighted) and of the estimated unlogged exposure, day-clustered bootstrap interval; and the ratio of unlogged to logged km by month. Reported as descriptive differences. No statement about battery or energy results is made from them.
- **U3 odometer bracket.** Logged km over cloud odometer gain, with the unlogged part split into: cloud-only units (U1), odometer-increase-only gaps, remainder unexplained (gap of the cloud odometer to the sum), each as km with interval from V1 distance error; the cloud odometer gain over the dash ODO anchors where cloud-only data exist is a reconciliation, not a new KPI.

## 5. Decision rules (frozen before any result)
- R1 (a quantity is reconstructable from the cloud alone, for use in U1/U2): median absolute relative error at most 10 % for distance and duration, total-variation distance of time shares at most 0.15 (median), screened ambient median absolute difference at most 1.0 C; each with the upper interval bound at most 1.5 times the threshold; at least 100 validation drives on at least 30 days. A quantity that fails is reported for the validation set and is NOT carried to U1/U2 ("not reconstructable at this precision"); distance is expected to pass (the cloud odometer is independent of speed capping), speed shares may not.
- R2 (selection): the unlogged units differ from the logged drives by selection (why the logger was off is unknown). The validation set therefore stratifies by length tercile and by day type; if cloud-only units fall outside the validation support in length (for example below the shortest decile of validated drives) those units are reported separately as "outside validation support" and carry no exposure statement beyond count and km.
- R3 (language): U1/U2 results are "estimated unlogged exposure (cloud-reconstructed)", with the V1 error stated next to them; never "the unlogged drives were the same/different as logged" without the interval; U2 differences are not read as an effect or as a correction of any KPI.
- R4 (no published change): outcome-independent: no logged figure, share or KPI is changed in this milestone; adopting any of it in the dashboard is a separate spec, blind audit, Director step and Andrii's decision.
- Falsification: "the cloud alone reconstructs a drive's exposure well enough to describe unlogged driving" is falsified (per quantity) when R1 fails for that quantity with n above the floor; "the unlogged driving resembles the logged corpus" is not established when the U2 interval excludes zero, and "not distinguishable" (with the minimum detectable distance stated) otherwise; absence of a difference is not equivalence.

## 7. Out of scope
Any dashboard, payload or KPI change; imputing unlogged drives into any energy, regeneration, thermal or winter analysis; ambient forcing for unlogged driving beyond reporting it; map matching or grade (M737 closed at G0); payload (belt) sensitivity; the ingestion-stage hook.

## 8. Questions for the Director
(a) Is masked-drive validation on logged drives a sound basis for statements about unlogged units given the selection (R2), or should U1/U2 be restricted to reporting counts and km? (b) Are the R1 thresholds defensible against 30 s sampling, speed capped at 130 km/h and 1 C ambient steps, and is total-variation distance the right summary for time shares? (c) Is the unlogged definition (overlap below 10 %) and the boundary-case treatment adequate, given the +16 s clock shift? (d) Should U3 be in this milestone or left to the odometer-reconciliation owner rule (KPI = total logged km / ODO, no date alignment)?
