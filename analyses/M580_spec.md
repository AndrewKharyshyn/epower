## Problem
The merged dashboard note `data-csstep-row="confirmatory"` (`xtrail_summary.jsx`, bound to `S.cellSpreadStep.confirmatory`) prints the label, the mean residual and the post-step slope with ONE count pair (`nDrivesAfterCutoff` drives, `nDaysAfterCutoff` days), which belong to the residual only; the slope uses n 133 / 22 days. It carries no temperature-range caveat. The M579 ruling forbids showing the label alone and requires separate counts. Until this milestone is merged, the dashboard displays the label against that ruling.

## Scope (no change of rule, floor, labels, step date, cutoff or models; the M574 outcome labels stay script-written)
1. Additive payload fields in `cellSpreadStep.confirmatory`, written by `tools/m573_cell_spread_step.py` (stage `cell_spread_step`, leaf-level splice, deep-diff: only this sub-block changes; the existing leaves stay byte-identical):
   - `preStepTRange`: {min, p5, max, nFitDrives} of the drive-mean pack temperature `T_pack_mean_avg` over the drives of the pre-step fit (audit: 12.7, 16.53, 44.0 C, 467 drives); p5 = numpy default linear-interpolation 5th percentile; the fit coefficients are stored as `preStepFitHash` (sha256 of n and the rounded coefficients) and the pre-step fit is FROZEN: on every refresh a change of n or of the hash is flagged and escalated to the Director (R7);
   - `nBelowPreStepTmin` (strict `<` min) and `nAbovePreStepTmax` (strict `>` max) among the post-cutoff drives (audit: 6 and 0 of 24) (R3);
   - `predictionMet`: boolean written by the script from the M573 label logic (`raised level, flat slope` iff residual interval above 0 and slope interval spans 0); the JSX never derives it (R1);
   - `inRangeSensitivity`: the SAME mean-residual estimator as the M574 block (intercept-only OLS on the residuals, calendar-day cluster covariance, t(G-1)) restricted to post-cutoff drives within [min, max]: `n`, `days`, `meanResidualMv`, `meanResidualCi95`, `meetsSupportFloor` (>= 10 drives on >= 5 days; meeting it never turns the line into a test) and the fixed `label` "post hoc; below the floor" while the floor is unmet, else "post hoc sensitivity" (R2) (audit: n 18, 4 days, 7.307 [-0.952, 15.566], floor unmet);

   - `evaluationNumber` and `terminalEvaluation` (below), derived from an append-only looks ledger (R6).

## Terminal evaluation point (Director decision: A with a fixed fallback)
The block refreshes at every ingestion, so each look is one of several. One evaluation is declared terminal in advance; every other look is an interim look with no inferential status.
- Terminal = the first calendar day D on which, after the cutoff 2026-10-17, there are >= 20 in-range drives (pre-step T range [12.7, 44.0] C, strict bounds as above) on >= 10 distinct in-range days. Data set = all post-cutoff drives dated <= D, whatever the batch size. If D has not occurred by 2027-01-31, the fallback is the post-cutoff drives dated <= 2027-01-31.
- The literal M574 rule on the full data set gives the label; the in-range result sits beside it. The outcome is frozen in `terminalEvaluation` {rule, D, dataThrough, n, days, values, outcome, specSha256, frozen: true}; later refreshes keep being displayed, labelled "after the terminal evaluation; descriptive". The stopping rule depends on temperature coverage and the calendar, not on the outcome.
- `evaluationNumber` (R6): derived idempotently from an append-only looks ledger (`analyses/M580_looks.json`, one record per ingestion keyed by master MD5, written only by the ingestion stage `cell_spread_step`); re-runs of the same master, arrays-only refreshes and M580 itself are not looks. The M578 ingestion (master `1574a815...`) is look 1 (the first evaluation at the minimum support).
- The pre-step fit is not refreshed (a refit would be a method change). No post hoc change of the 10 drives / 5 days floor, the cutoff, the step date or the models. The spec sha256, the floor, the cutoff, the step date and the models are recorded in the CHANGELOG before the next ingestion.
