# M504 spec (Rev 2): covariate balance and design-dependence of the Warm-vs-Shoulder urban energy-intensity contrast (audit ST-09)

Status: Rev 2, written 2026-10-24 BEFORE any M504 computation. Rev 1 was reviewed by the research-director (ruling "revise", nine required revisions R1-R9, all folded in below). Origin: independent audit 2026-10-23, finding ST-09: the released contrast (`comparisonCube.urbanContrast`, Shoulder minus Warm urban energy intensity, M491 joint calendar-day interval, M494 block sensitivity) is described as confounded in words, but the payload has no covariate-balance table, and an exploratory look in the audit changed the "includes zero" reading. The audit's numbers are NOT targets. Additive and reporting-only: the released contrast, its interval and the M494 verdict are read, never changed. The same-date restriction below is post hoc (prompted by that look); D1-D3 are therefore a sensitivity, not independent confirmation.

## Question
(A) How different are the Shoulder and Warm urban drives in calendar date, start hour, start SoC, pack temperature, moving speed, distance and weekday share? (B) Does the "interval includes zero" reading of the contrast depend on the design (all drives; same-date restriction; common-support restriction; both)?

## Estimand and population (unchanged, frozen)
Per band (all urban trips; < 3 km; 3-10 km; >= 10 km): difference = ratio of sums (Shoulder) minus ratio of sums (Warm), 100 x sum(gross_throughput_kwh) / sum(distance_km) per cohort, on the population of `tools/urban_week_block.population` (the comparison cube's: canonical-clean, city read as urban, gross energy integral, distance > 0, thermal_regime warm or shoulder). The tool HARD-STOPS unless its D0 point estimates, drive and day counts AND the D0 interval bounds equal `comparisonCube.urbanContrast.bands[*]` exactly (R7: D0 is computed with the cube's own joint-day function and its generator stream, seed 20260925 stream SEED+2, consumed band after band as in the cube; D1-D3 use separate fixed streams, project seed 42 + design index, 4,000 draws). Cold is not used. Bands overlap (all urban contains the three sub-bands), so the band verdicts are correlated; 12 non-D0 intervals are computed across the 4 bands (3 designs x 4 bands), with no multiplicity adjustment; the "any estimable design differs" rule leans conservative.

## Part A. Balance table (descriptive, frozen)
Per band and covariate: n non-missing and n missing per cohort, mean and SD per cohort, the standardised mean difference SMD = (mean_Shoulder - mean_Warm) / sqrt((SD_Shoulder^2 + SD_Warm^2) / 2), and the common-support shares in both directions: share of Shoulder drives inside the Warm 5th-95th percentile range and share of Warm drives inside the Shoulder 5th-95th percentile range (numpy linear percentiles). Covariates: day of year (`date` ordinal); start hour (hour of `time_start` plus minutes / 60, treated as linear); start SoC (`soc_start`); pack temperature at start (`pack_temp_start_c`, seasonal master); mean pack temperature over the drive (`T_pack_mean_avg`, labelled "measured during the drive": partly caused by the drive itself, shown for description only and never used in a restriction); moving speed (`speed_mean_moving`); distance (`distance_km`); weekend share (Saturday or Sunday; the SMD of a proportion = (p_S - p_W) / sqrt((p_S(1-p_S) + p_W(1-p_W)) / 2)). A drive with a missing covariate keeps its place in the table (counted as missing). No test or threshold is attached to an SMD; |SMD| > 0.25 as notable is a reading aid only. The ambient-threshold cohort definition makes the date SMD large by construction.

## Part B. Design-dependence of the contrast (sensitivity, frozen; no design is preferred)
D0 = the released unadjusted contrast (reproduced, see above). D1 = same-date restriction: only drives on calendar dates on which both cohorts have at least one drive in the band. D2 = common-support restriction: only drives whose start hour, start SoC and pack temperature at start each lie inside the intersection of the two cohorts' 5th-95th percentile ranges (percentiles per band on the full band population, numpy linear); a drive with a missing value of one of these covariates is excluded from D2 and D3 and counted (`nExcludedMissingCovariate`). D3 = D1 and D2 together.
Per design: both cohorts' ratio of sums, the difference, n drives and n days per cohort (the verdict line names the restricted n), and the joint calendar-day percentile interval of `joint_day_bootstrap.joint_difference` (dates of the design resampled together). A design is NOT estimable when either cohort has fewer than 10 drives or fewer than 5 days; a design with 5-9 days in a cohort is flagged `lowPrecision`. A design whose dropped-draw share exceeds 0.5 % is non-decisive and counts as NOT estimable for the verdict, never as a differing reading (R6). The width ratio of each design interval to D0's is recorded; a design is also `lowPrecision` when the ratio exceeds 1.5.
Verdict per band: "reading unchanged" if every estimable design has the same includes-zero reading as D0; "reading depends on the design" if any estimable design differs, recording for each differing design the direction of the flip (excludes zero -> includes zero or the reverse) and its width ratio, and labelled "reading depends on the design; restricted design has lower precision" when every differing design is `lowPrecision`; "not assessable" if no design other than D0 is estimable. The overall verdict is the most cautious band verdict.

## Wording (frozen)
The contrast is an unadjusted comparison of ambient-threshold classes that are confounded with calendar date and season; the balance table shows by how much. The dashboard shows the balance (the largest |SMD| and its covariate, in both directions the common-support share of the most imbalanced covariate) and the Part B verdict with the restricted n; D1-D3 point estimates live in the payload and the result file only and are never a headline (R9). The displayed interval stays the M491 joint-day interval. Forbidden: "adjusted for", "controls for", "after accounting for", "deconfounded", "bias-corrected", "matched estimate", "true effect", "thermal effect" (language rule ids ST-09/M504). No thermal effect, no causal statement, no preferred design.

## Escalation (R8)
The ingestion stage `urban_contrast_balance` (cube data only, no raw files) escalates to the Director if the overall verdict changes between ingestions: the stage prints the change and exits non-zero unless `--ack-verdict-change` is passed; the stamp note records the drive counts of the run.

## Outputs
`analyses/M504_result.json` (balance tables, design results, fidelity block, provenance: master and seasonal-master MD5, script and spec sha256, seeds). Script `tools/urban_balance.py`; known-answer test `tests/synthetic/test_urban_balance.py`. Payload: additive block `urbanContrastBalance` + stamp, regenerated at every ingestion by the stage placed after `urban_week_block`. Dashboard: one short paragraph with the balance and the verdict in the comparison section.

## Out of scope
Any adjusted (regression) estimator of a thermal effect; the Cold cohort; the cube's own interval; other metric families.
