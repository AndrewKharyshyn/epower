## Why (evidence; none of it is typed into the repo)
1. `CAP_KWH = 2.1` (`verified:false`) is one point constant used across the pipeline (inventory in step 0). Independent analyses (Opus study, blind audit, an external review) place the apparent capacity of the BMS SoC scale at ~4.9 Ah per 100 points over SoC ~44-83 % (energy ~1.75-1.80 kWh depending on the voltage used); 2.1 kWh would need ~5.9 Ah. Method spread ~+/-9 % exceeds the day-bootstrap CI (~+/-2 %).
2. The global offset (-0.382 A) is defined so the mean residual vs dSoC x 2.1 kWh is zero (`_v5_postprocess_master`): "residual = 0" is circular. Independent fits attribute most of that offset to resistive loss.
3. Nissan publishes both 5 Ah (Article-10 sheet; rated, defined as Wh / nominal voltage) and "a high-output 2.1kWh battery" (Nissan Australia press release for the X-Trail e-POWER with e-4ORCE, Ti/Ti-L; page checked 2026-10-11); neither defines gross/usable/net, and 2.1 kWh is not confirmed for the FWD pack. So 2.1 kWh is a qualified manufacturer reference, not an "unpublished" number.
4. The canonical-clean eligibility rule itself depends on the legacy capacity (`f_domain`, `f_domain_2p` flag |`energy_residual_kwh_corr`| > `RESIDUAL_MAX_KWH` = 0.35 = 2.1/6; they feed `ens_invalid` / `ens_outlier_v2`). Therefore the corpus on which the new basis is estimated is partly defined by the old one (auditor blocking #1).

## Definitions (fixed)
Four quantities are kept apart in every text and payload key: (1) reference full capacity (Ah/Wh between manufacturer-defined endpoints; NOT measured here); (2) BMS-accessible capacity (energy between controller limits; boundaries unverified); (3) observed operating buffer (energy over the SoC range actually used); (4) remaining capacity / SOH (against a comparable new-pack baseline; NOT established).
- `CAP_AH_RATED = 5.0` Ah: documentary (Article-10), the primary normaliser for throughput quantities. Source URL and retrieval date go to `constantProvenance`. Not a measurement; no "rated kWh" is derived.
- `Q_SOC_EST` (Ah per 100 BMS-SoC points): output of estimator B (below), written by script; interval = bootstrap CI and, separately, the method range (see Reporting). Valid over the observed SoC range only; "per 100" is an extrapolation (raw SoC extremes in the corpus 42.5-88.0 %; p0.1-p99.9 = 45-81.5 % on 540 drives; three clean drives reach 85.0, 85.5, 88.0 %; the dash gauge reads 85 % at BMS ~78 %).
- `E_PER_SOC100_EST` (kWh per 100 points) is the OUTPUT of the energy estimator itself (terminal energy per 100 points; the I2R-removed value is reported next to it), written by script. It is not `Q_SOC_EST` x voltage. `V_CONV` = E_PER_SOC100_EST / Q_SOC_EST is a diagnostic ratio reported with both numerators' definitions (terminal vs I2R-removed energy; Q always from estimator B); the median pack voltage while driving (358.9 V in the audit) is shown as the alternative. The two estimators are never mixed into one product (auditor: 4.89 Ah x 358.9 V = 1.755 kWh, not 1.80).
- `WINDOW_FRACTION f` (scenario parameter, not an estimate) = `Q_SOC_EST / Q_full`, a CHARGE fraction (Ah/Ah): the part of the cell's true capacity spanned by the BMS 0-100 % scale. Scenario band from `f_min = Q_SOC_EST / (2.1 kWh / V_CONV)` (2.1 kWh read as gross, converted to Ah with the same `V_CONV`; ~0.84-0.86 expected) to `f = 1`. The primary basis `CAP_AH_RATED` corresponds to `f = Q_SOC_EST / CAP_AH_RATED` (~0.98). Not testable from BMS-scale data (a gross 2.1 kWh with a buffer <= ~15 % cannot be excluded); the band is carried, not resolved.
- `CAP_KWH = 2.1` remains only as a labelled legacy scenario.

## What each quantity uses (decision from the Director ruling plus the window question)
| Quantity | Primary basis | Scenario band |
|---|---|---|
| Ah throughput | unchanged | none |
| FCE, standard EFC, GTC (throughput / reference capacity; the three EFC quantities stay separately named) | Ah throughput / (2 x `CAP_AH_RATED`) (GTC: / `CAP_AH_RATED`) | reference capacity = `Q_SOC_EST / f` for `f` in [`f_min`, 1] |
| C-rate | current / `CAP_AH_RATED` | same band (a larger true capacity lowers C-rate) |
| Rainflow-EFC and DoD-based damage | SoC-depth as logged (BMS scale) | true DoD = BMS DoD x `f`, band as above |
| SoC-based energy bookkeeping | `E_PER_SOC100_EST` | method range |
| kWh turnover | not used as a stress metric (depends on the voltage chosen); Ah is the basis | - |
Every series cell carries the same Ah. A pack-level FCE/EFC is a lower bound for the weakest cell ONLY if that cell's capacity is at or below the reference (new cells often exceed their rated capacity); say so wherever shown. The 20,000 GTC threshold stays `verified:false`; no life-fraction wording.

## Estimators (primary fixed; the rest are sensitivities)
Population: drives with `ens_outlier_v2 == False` and usable I-V pairs (540 drives / 108 days at the audit; the exact n is printed). The 12 NaN-flag drives are handled by test (d). HV current = `[BMS] HV Battery Current (A)` (never `(Low)`: -1600 fill), I and V paired by time interpolation, gap cap 10 s, trapezoid, negative current = discharge.
- **B (primary):** charge at each 0.5-point SoC transition regressed on level with segment fixed effects and a pooled drift term; day-cluster percentile bootstrap, seed 42, 4000 draws.
- **Method-range set (descriptive, fixed list):** A forward (start-to-end per segment), B reverse, 3 s gap cap, ZOH integration, +/-2 s boundary-timing shifts on a fixed window set, 5-minute windows (post-hoc in the blind audit: sensitivity only), fast-leg (|I| >= 30 A) and slow-leg strata. The method range is min-max over this list, written by script. It is NOT a confidence interval and NOT a union with the bootstrap CI: CI and method range are reported side by side; adding a sensitivity to the list requires an amendment, because the range grows with every entry.
- **Energy version:** terminal energy and I2R-removed energy; R estimated from data; the gap to the OEM 0.210 Ohm at 80 % SoC (different definition) is disclosed, not tuned away.

## Pre-registered tests (each reports its MDE; a test with MDE larger than its margin is labelled "not powered" and cannot gate)
MDE is computed by the known-answer harness (below) by simulation with n and noise fixed from the audit files (`analyses/capacity_blind_audit_2026-10-11/results.json`, `analyses/capacity_study_2026-10-11/`), before any new data are touched. An unpowered test defaults to the CONSERVATIVE outcome, never to pass: (a) escalates, (b) means no I2R switch (dual report), (c) is treated as 'not equivalent' (Director reopens Option C).
(a) **Heterogeneity check (not a correctness check; odd and even weeks interleave the seasons):** estimate B separately on odd and even calendar weeks; TOST equivalence of the two estimates within +/-0.25 Ah (~5 %). Failure => escalate. (The v1 "+/-3 % of the audit value" check is dropped: same estimator, same data, wider than the CI, could not fail.)
(b) **Offset vs I2R, 2x2 design with in-fold fitting:** models {global offset, I2R + offset} x scales {2.1 kWh fixed, estimated}. Folds = whole calendar days, >= 5, scale and R fitted inside each training fold, never on the full corpus. Metric: held-out per-drive terminal-energy vs dSoC residual RMS. I2R is accepted only if: (i) at the SAME scale it lowers held-out RMS vs the offset model with a day-clustered paired CI excluding 0 (not just a majority of folds); (ii) the offset left in the I2R model is TOST-equivalent to 0 with margin +/-0.10 A (v1 "CI contains 0" is absence of evidence); (iii) R is stable across folds (max/min <= 1.25) and its temperature dependence is FITTED as a term (R(T) physically depends on T; no fixed 25 % stratum rule); (iv) identifiability of [duration, integral of I^2 dt] is judged on STANDARDISED columns by the variance inflation factor; VIF > 10 => not separately identifiable, dual-report only. Disclosure: the offset result 0.02 A [-0.02, 0.07] was known when the +/-0.10 A margin (about twice the 0.05 A current resolution) was chosen, so (ii) is a consistency check, not a blind test. Offset-vs-I2R identification uses the CORPUS (currents up to > 100 A); the stationary experiment cannot identify R (see protocol). Failure of any => dual report only.
(c) **Level dependence, ONE pre-registered test:** a linear slope of apparent Ah/100 on segment-centre SoC, adjusted for mean |I|, I2R share, temperature, cadence regime and engine state; TOST equivalence to 0 with margin +/-0.10 Ah per 10 SoC points. Engine state and cadence regime are themselves driven by SoC, so adjusting for them pulls the slope toward 0 (false-equivalence risk): report unadjusted and adjusted slopes, the VIF of the SoC term and the MDE; equivalence may be concluded only if the adjusted slope is equivalent, VIF < 5 and MDE < margin, else 'unresolved' (Director reopens Option C). Terciles are descriptive only (the corpus values 4.47 / 4.84 / 5.10 Ah for <55 / 55-65 / >=65 % already have disjoint CIs, so "gain > 0" cannot fail and is dropped as a criterion). Not equivalent after adjustment => the Director reopens Option C. Not equivalent before adjustment but equivalent after => confounding is disclosed. A nonlinear scale cannot be told from residual confounding with this data; say so.
(d) **NaN-flag drives:** report how many of the 12 have usable SoC and I-V (2 have no SoC), the estimate with them added, and the delta. The row count change is printed; a delta of 0 with 0 rows added is reported as "no effect, k = 0", not as a pass. |delta| > 1 % of the primary with k > 0 escalates.
(e) **Control build, defined transitively:** (e1) from the step-0 inventory produce the expected-changed key set; (e2) two FRESH builds on the same raw set and the same no-new-drive state: build A = HEAD code, build B = M399 code; the diff is B vs A (never a fresh build vs the HEAD file); (e3) every differing leaf must be in the expected-changed set, else fail; changed keys are attributed to capacity, offset or eligibility; (e4) `drive_master.csv` MD5 before/after with the changed columns listed in advance; (e5) the 16 ML columns are reported for information only: the freeze/restore mechanism makes "0/16 diffs" true by construction, so it is NOT counted as evidence; the eligibility-flip count (change step 4) is the real check; (e6) the GTR closure pin (M398) is expected to drift because `I_offset_A_applied` feeds `recon_engine`; if the drift exceeds the M398 auto-re-pin conditions it is re-pinned by the manual path with a Director reason, not by loosening the gate.
(f) **Method change declared:** the shifts in FCE/EFC/GTC/C-rate (order +/-17-20 % between bases) are declared a method change, not drift; expected CI exceedances are listed in the CHANGELOG entry, not counted as headline-gate failures.
(g) **Chronological check (descriptive):** estimate Q_SOC_EST before 2026-08-01 and after; report the difference. It is confounded with season and with the fast/slow logging regime and says nothing about fade; it replaces the v1 comparison against the legacy 2.1 kWh basis, which loses by construction. External figures (e.g. prediction errors) are not typed into the repo.
(h) **Key-on reset consistency (new):** SoC jump at key-on (BMS reset toward a voltage-based value) vs net Ah since the previous anchor, as a function of rest time; the only check tied to cell voltage rather than to the same current sensor. Report the regression of the reset on net Ah with day-clustered CI and the dependence on rest time. Its slope is (unknown reset weight) x (scale mismatch), not 'Ah per point'; it is also confounded by relaxation/hysteresis, so stratify by the direction of the last current. A consistency check, not an estimate of the scale.
(i) **Experiment analysis (descriptive; the experiment does not feed test (b)) (when data exist, `analyses/M399_experiment_protocol.md`):** legs are classed by ENGINE STATE, never selected by SoC excursion; per-leg results are descriptive, compared with the method range without a pass/fail band (the v1 4.3-5.7 Ah band is dropped: stationary legs sit in the < 15 A regime where a selection artefact of ~4.1-4.3 Ah is known). Few distinct days => no day bootstrap; cluster by session, label "descriptive, not inferential".

## Known-answer harness (`tests/synthetic/test_capacity_basis.py`)
Synthetic drives with known capacity, SoC noise (0.5-point quantisation, flicker, key-on reset jitter), a known constant offset, known I2R loss, sampling gaps and a polling-time skew between channels. Must show: B recovers the capacity within its CI; the drive-level regression shows the documented attenuation; swapped regression and forward/reverse bracket behave as stated; the I2R model recovers R and drives the offset to ~0 when truth has no offset, and does NOT when truth has an offset (the test must be able to fail); an injected level dependence is detected by (c) and an absent one is not; folds never share a day; the MDE of each test is output. Also `semantic_gate` A2 rules for the new wording.

## Known limits (disclosed, not resolved here)
Shared current-sensor gain error cannot be cancelled or tested; the BMS SoC is not a pure charge counter (key-on reset toward a voltage-based value, flicker, ~1.7-point RMS vs integrated current) and may share the same sensor and an internal capacity model; no information outside SoC ~42-88 % (and little outside 44-83 %); a gross 2.1 kWh with a hidden buffer of up to ~15 % cannot be tested from BMS data; fade below ~3 % is not resolvable at ~8.9k km; one vehicle; OEM resistance (0.210 Ohm at 80 % SoC) vs data (~0.12 Ohm) not reconciled; cell imbalance makes pack-level FCE/EFC a lower bound for the weakest cell.
