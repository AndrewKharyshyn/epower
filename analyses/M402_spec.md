## Facts read from disk (2026-10-11)
- There is no Ah-throughput producer: `drive_master.csv` has `gross_throughput_kwh`, `cap_ah_est` and kWh-based `gtc`/`fce`; `compute_drive_summary_v6.py:692` computes `gtc = thr / CAP_KWH`.
- kWh are integrated in `compute_drive_summary_v6.py` (energy block after line 625): current samples `I = _series(df, t, 'I')` with `|v| < 900`, voltage `V = _series(df, t, 'V', lo=200, hi=450)`, pairs `e = _asof(I, V, 'V', V_ALIGN_TOL='1500ms').dropna(subset=['V'])`, `Id = -v` (BMS current is charge-positive), `dt_h = diff(t)` clipped to `[0, DT_CAP_S = 5 s]`, piecewise-linear power with a zero-crossing-aware split of each interval into discharge and charge (M229). Fewer than 2 pairs -> no energy (`energy_null_reason = no_integrable_iv_pairs`).
- `_prep_bytes`, `_series`, `_asof`, `DT_CAP_S`, `V_ALIGN_TOL` are module-level, so the same pairs can be built without refactoring the 1000-line per-drive function.
- Master `gross_discharge_kwh` / `gross_charge_kwh` are rounded to 4 decimals; raw files in `raw/` are the sha256-verified originals (M366).

## Design
1. `tools/ah_throughput.py` (pure library + CLI). For each drive it builds the SAME I/V pairs as the kWh energy block (same helper functions, same tolerances, same current artifact filter, same dt cap and clip) and integrates the discharge-positive current `Id` with the same piecewise-linear zero-crossing-aware rule, replacing power by current: for an interval of duration `d` (h) with endpoint currents `i0, i1`: non-crossing: `d*(i0+i1)/2` to discharge if positive, to charge (magnitude) if negative; crossing: `d*(i0^2 + i1^2)/(2(|i0|+|i1|))` split by endpoint sign (positive endpoint squared -> discharge, negative -> charge). Outputs per drive (unrounded floats; rounding only at CSV export, 6 decimals): `ah_discharge`, `ah_charge`, `ah_throughput`, `ah_net` (= discharge - charge), `n_pairs`, `n_crossings`, and, from the identical loop, the unrounded `kwh_discharge`, `kwh_charge` as the matched-support cross-check. Drives with fewer than 2 pairs get empty values and a reason (not zero), exactly like the kWh block.
2. Sidecar `ah_throughput.csv` (columns `file, date, ah_discharge, ah_charge, ah_throughput, ah_net, n_pairs, n_crossings, kwh_discharge, kwh_charge, kwh_per_ah_throughput, raw_sha256, reason`) and `ah_throughput_summary.json` (corpus totals over drives with pairs, n drives, matched-support check results, tool sha256, `status`). Both are written ONLY by the tool; the master is read-only.
3. Incremental by content: a drive is recomputed only when it is not in the sidecar or its raw sha256 differs (`raw_manifest.json` sha256 is used when present, else the file is hashed); the tool never reads all raw files for a routine ingestion. A full recomputation is `--rebuild`.
4. Stage `ah_throughput` in `tools/ingest_stages.json` after `stage_raw` (outputs: the two files). Release gate: `release_check.py` runs the tool's `--check` (sidecar covers every master drive with pairs; matched-support identities hold) in the clean copy.
5. No payload block and no dashboard text in this milestone: the capacity milestone consumes the sidecar through `capacityBasis`.

## Pre-registered checks (each can fail)
- **Matched support (the acceptance test of "same intervals")**: for every master drive with non-null `gross_discharge_kwh`, `|kwh_discharge - gross_discharge_kwh| <= 5.5e-5` and the same for charge (rounding of the master to 4 decimals); drives where the master has null energy have `reason = no_integrable_iv_pairs` in the sidecar; row sets identical. Any exception is listed by name and fails the check.
- **Known-answer synthetic drives** (`tests/synthetic/test_ah_throughput.py`): constant 10 A discharge for 3600 s on a 1 s grid -> 10 Ah discharge, 0 charge (BMS sign: current -10 A); constant charge; a +10 A -> -10 A single 1 s crossing interval -> 2.5/3600 Ah to each side with `n_crossings = 1` (the M229 identity: gross = `d*(i0^2+i1^2)/(2(|i0|+|i1|))`); a 60 s logging gap is clipped to the 5 s cap; an `|I| >= 900` artifact sample is dropped; an unpaired current sample (no voltage within 1.5 s) is dropped; fewer than 2 pairs -> empty with a reason, not 0; net identity `ah_net = ah_discharge - ah_charge`; throughput = discharge + charge; invariance to row order is NOT assumed (sorted by time like the kWh block).
- **Ah / kWh consistency**: for every drive `kwh_throughput / ah_throughput` lies inside the voltage filter band (200-450 V) and the corpus ratio is reported next to the median pack voltage while driving (the audit's reference ~358.9 V); a drive outside the band fails.
- **Independent blind reproduction** (analytical-auditor, claim + data path only, no code): given the definition above and `raw/`, reproduce `ah_discharge`/`ah_charge` for a stratified sample of at least 25 drives (both logging regimes, including the 5 odometer-anomaly drives, a drive with crossings, and a sparse drive) to a relative tolerance of 1e-6; the auditor also states whether the sidecar totals are plausible against the independently integrated Ah of the audit packages. Report as JSON brief < 2 KB.

## Out of scope
Any normalisation by 5.0 Ah or 2.1 kWh (capacity milestone); master columns for Ah (can be promoted later after a control build); offset-corrected Ah (the sidecar is the raw-current integral, like `gross_throughput_kwh` before the residual offset); payload/dashboard changes.

## Rev 1a (after the blind audit)
- The blind-reproduction tolerance is `abs <= 2e-6 Ah + 1e-6 x value`: the sidecar is exported with 6 decimals, so a strict relative 1e-6 cannot hold for drives under 1 Ah. Result: 20 of 20 valued drives agree (largest absolute difference 4.8e-07 Ah = export rounding), `n_pairs` and `n_crossings` equal, and all 5 no-value drives agree (analyses/M402_audit/compare_results.json; auditor script and results in the same folder).
- The auditor's sensitivity test shows the 5 s dt cap is material (31 logging gaps > 5 s in the sample; removing the cap changes values by up to 86.5 %); tie-breaking, tolerance inclusivity, duplicate timestamps and the 900 A boundary are not exercised by the sample. The cap is therefore part of the definition and a stated sensitivity for the capacity milestone (the self-audit's 3 s vs 10 s gap-cap sensitivity of the kWh integral applies equally to Ah).
