# M553 spec Rev 1 (2026-11-01, before any code): direct thesis test, how much of the assumed battery window the drives use (audit 2026-10-29 N13, triage gap)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT for Director review. Nothing implemented. Tier C (new analysis). Branch base: `main` at M552 (`1b9bda7`). Ledger id `AUD29-N13`. Thesis under test: the HV battery is a power buffer, not an energy reservoir. `CAP_KWH = 2.1` is `verified:false` and is NOT changed; every quantity that needs it is reported beside a reference-free twin.

## 1. Question and non-question
Per drive, how large is the excursion of the battery's cumulative net charge (Ah) and energy (kWh), and of the reported SoC (points), and how does the energy excursion compare with the assumed window? Non-question: no capacity estimate, no state-of-health statement, no claim that the window is or is not 2.1 kWh. The reported SoC is a BMS display value of unknown resolution (0.5 point steps in the master) and is not an energy meter.

## 2. Substrate
- Pair stream: the M551/M552 master pair stream (`energy_packets.pair_stream`; |I| < 900, V 200..450, `V_ALIGN_TOL`), signed power P = Id x V / 1000, discharge positive; raw step > 5 s is a GAP (no bridging; segments as in M552). Linear interpolation inside a segment, closure by the trapezoid rule.
- Per segment cumulative net charge Q(t) = integral of Id dt / 3600 (Ah, discharge positive) and net energy E(t) = integral of P dt / 3600 (kWh). Running excursion per drive: the sum over segments of (max - min) of the segment trace (no bridging; segment excursions are added, the gap energy is not used) AND the single-segment maximum (so bridging is never needed to read the result); the drive value is reported as `excursionAh` / `excursionKwh` (additive over segments) with the largest single-segment value beside it.
- Reported SoC points: `soc_max - soc_min` from the master (`soc_band`), `soc_start`, `soc_end`; drives without SoC (2 rows) are excluded and counted.
- Eligibility: canonical clean (`ens_outlier_v2` false), no `energy_null_reason`, `sign_check != ANOMALY`. Strata: logger cadence (`cadence_regime.csv`: fast, slow, unknown), no pooled headline, no contrast between strata (B6 still failed).

## 3. Estimands (per stratum, per-drive-weighted, day-clustered)
- E1 reference-free: percentiles (p50, p90, p95, p99, max) of per-drive `excursionAh`, of the reported SoC excursion in points, and of Ah per SoC point (`excursionAh / excursion points`, only for drives with >= 4 points; resolution flag: the point value is stated with the 0.5-point resolution bracket, i.e. recomputed with the points +-0.5).
- E2 assumed-window twin: `excursionKwh / CAP_KWH` (share of the assumed window), percentiles as E1, and the share of drives whose share is >= 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 ("window used up" = share >= 1.0 on the assumed 2.1 kWh, label always "at the assumed 2.1 kWh (verified:false)"). Sensitivity grid CAP_KWH in {1.5, 2.1, 3.0, 5.0}; the 5.0 value stands for the OEM Article-10 rated 5 Ah lead (a lead only, not an adopted value).
- E3 throughput twin: gross throughput (`gross_throughput_kwh`) divided by the same assumed window (cycles of the assumed window); the three EFC quantities (FCE, standard EFC, Rainflow-EFC) are not reused or relabelled.
- E4 drive-level agreement: Spearman correlation of reported SoC excursion with `excursionAh` per stratum (descriptive; a low value would say the display does not track the integrated charge).
- Intervals: day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws), interval only if n * (1 - q) >= 5 and days >= 5, p99 only with >= 20 days, never for the maximum. Every estimate carries n drives and days.
- Duration confound: excursion grows with drive length; per stratum a sensitivity restricted to drives >= 600 s and >= 1800 s, and the Spearman correlation with duration.

## 4. Known answers (pre-registered)
- KA1 constant discharge current I0 over T s: excursionAh = I0 T / 3600, single segment, trace monotone; excursionKwh = P0 T / 3600.
- KA2 sine-wave current with zero mean: excursion = peak-to-trough of the analytic integral (amplitude A and period T: A T / (pi x 3600) Ah, up to the interpolation error bound stated in the test).
- KA3 a 6 s gap splits the segment: excursion is the sum of the two segment excursions, the gap contributes nothing, and the single-segment maximum is the larger one.
- KA4 (hard gate, real drives): per drive the net charge at the segment ends sums with the capped gap term to the master `net_draw_kwh` within 1e-4 kWh (reuse of the M552 KA5 closure) and `excursionKwh >= |net energy of the drive|`, `excursionKwh <= gross_throughput_kwh + 1e-9` (a peak-to-trough range cannot exceed the throughput); violation stops.
- KA5 shares: the share is monotone in CAP_KWH (decreasing); the threshold shares are monotone (>= 0.25 >= >= 0.5 >= >= 1.0).
- Expectations (not gates, reported either way): the thesis predicts that the share of the assumed window used up is small in every stratum and that Ah per SoC point is stable across drives; disagreement is reported, not tuned.

Where this section and sections 1-7 differ, this section governs.
- R1 (Q1). Both bounds kept. The additive per-segment sum is the UPPER bracket (conservative against the thesis) and is the primary; the largest single segment is the LOWER bracket. Per drive the gap time share is reported; escalate if the gap share exceeds 10 % of eligible time in more than 10 % of drives, or if the p95 of upper / lower bracket exceeds 2 in a stratum.
- R2 (units; Q2). 5 Ah is not 5.0 kWh. The kWh grid is CAP_KWH in {1.5, 2.1, 3.0}; the OEM Article-10 rated 5 Ah enters only as a separate Ah twin (`excursionAh / 5 Ah`), labelled "lead, not adopted". Section 3 E2 "5.0" is withdrawn.
- R3 (Q3). Reference-free co-row added: reported SoC excursion divided by the corpus-observed reported-SoC range (p0.5 of soc_min to p99.5 of soc_max over canonical drives), labelled "observed range", never a BMS limit. The assumed-window twin keeps "share >= 1.0 at the assumed 2.1 kWh (verified:false)".
- R4 (Q4). Ah per SoC point is withdrawn from this milestone (it is a capacity-type estimate and Q and SoC extremes are not time-aligned); it moves to M554 / N12. Section 3 E1 loses that item.
- R5 (Q5, A3). The 5 % rule is applied to the upper bracket and also at 1.5 kWh. The 0.5 correlation rule uses the partial Spearman correlation controlling for duration (both scale with T), with the 0.5-point SoC resolution floor caveat.
- R6 (Q6; falsifiable rule, fixed before data). R = gross_throughput_kwh / excursionKwh per drive (CAP-free; a reservoir-type drive gives R near 1, buffer use gives R much larger than 1). Rule per stratum: "consistent with buffer use" iff the lower 95 % day-clustered bootstrap bound of median R > 2 AND the share of drives with share >= 1.0 at 2.1 kWh is <= 5 %; otherwise "not supported" (never "refuted" or "proved"). Escalate if the median-R interval contains 2, if the verdict differs between 1.5 and 2.1 kWh, or if the offset sensitivity (R7) flips a share >= 1 classification or moves a p95 outside its interval.
- R7 (offset sensitivity). Repeat excursion with the current offset removed (Id - I_offset_A_applied, and with I_offset_2p_A_applied); known answer: a constant offset i_off over T adds exactly |i_off| T / 3600 to a flat-current drive's Ah excursion in the monotone case (KA1b).
- R8 (E3 and tests). E3 is dropped as a separate throughput-in-window-cycles quantity (no fourth EFC-like quantity); R replaces it and `gross_throughput_kwh` is used with its master basis stated. KA4 changes to: |sum of segment net energies| (no gap term) <= excursionKwh <= gross_throughput_kwh + 1e-9, and the M552 KA5 closure kept; an Ah closure is added only if the master has a net-charge Ah column (none found: not added, stated). KA2 pins inputs (A, T, sample period 1 s, tolerance stated in the test). KA7 (M552 R8): a duplicate timestamp with a power jump leaves the trace continuous and the excursion independent of row order.
- R9 (language and limits). Ah is primary; kWh is terminal energy (includes I^2R), not stored energy. Labels: "net charge excursion (Ah, integrated, estimated)", "terminal energy"; never capacity, depth of discharge, cycles, state of health, proves, measured, pooled (language_gate rules for AUD29-N13). Limits: reported SoC confinement is a controller policy, not proof of capacity; 0.5-point SoC resolution; no time alignment between SoC and Q; CAP_KWH unverified.
