## 1. The legacy estimator (verified in `compute_summary_arrays.py::_RawAccum.add`, "regen capture" block)

On the 1 Hz grid `hz` (per-channel `ffill(limit=15)` on the raw rows, then `resample('1s').mean().ffill(limit=15)`), rows lacking speed, current or voltage are dropped (`rq`); engine-off = `eng_rpm.fillna(0) <= 300`; a step is a decel step when `speed < previous kept row's speed`; charge energy per step = `max(-P, 0)/3600` with `P = (-I) V / 1000` (BMS current is charge-positive) at a FIXED dt of 1 s; KE loss = `0.5 m (v_prev^2 - v^2)/3.6e6` with the class-conditional assumed mass; a qualifying step needs engine-off, decel, speed > 0 and KE loss > 0 (zero-charge steps are kept); speed bins use the END speed of the step (10 km/h mid-bins), temperature bins the mean of T1-T4 skipping missing probes (`REGEN_TEMP_BINS`); the published ratio per bin is `100 sum(cap) / sum(ke)`; a temperature bin is `valid` with n >= 200 qualifying steps. The offset of the logged current is not applied.

## 2. Estimand and variants (declared before any result)

For each variant v and each published bin b (8 temperature bins, the speed mid-bins with n >= 10 as published): `eff_v(b) = 100 sum_{steps in b retained by v} cap / sum ke` and the paired difference `Delta_v(b) = eff_v(b) - eff_0(b)` against the legacy reproduction `eff_0` on the same drives. Because the variants remove, re-bin or recompute steps, the output also reports: the retained share of steps, of KE and of drives and days; and the ratio of the REMOVED steps (`eff_removed`), which shows the direction of the selection (a removed set with a higher ratio than the retained set lowers the bar).

Variants (defects of the legacy extractor; each applied alone, plus the combination):
- **V1 rpmKnown** - exclude steps whose RPM grid value is missing (legacy: missing = engine-off).
- **V2 age(k)** - exclude steps where the age of any of speed, current, voltage or RPM exceeds k seconds, k in {2, 5, 10}; age = seconds since the last native (non-filled) sample of that channel at the grid second.
- **V3 contiguous** - exclude steps whose previous kept row is not exactly 1 s earlier (legacy joins non-consecutive times and treats the step as 1 s).
- **V4 startSpeedBin** - speed view only: bin by the step's START speed instead of the end speed (the axis reads "speed at start of braking"); temperature view unchanged.
- **V5 fullProbe** - temperature view only: require all four probes present at the step second (legacy: mean over the present probes).
- **V6 offset** - apply the declared current offset: `I_corr = I + i_off` with `i_off = offsetUncertainty.pointEstimateA` (-0.3888 A, discharge-positive frame; the BMS current is charge-positive, so the corrected logged current is `I - 0.3888`), sign verified by a known-answer test against the v6 `net_draw` convention.
- **V0 combined** - V1 and V2(5) and V3 and V5 together (speed view: V1, V2(5), V3, V4), the nearest to a defect-free extraction on the existing data; also with V6.
The 300 rpm engine-off proxy, the assumed masses and the n >= 200 validity gate are kept unchanged (owner decision (g)); no variant is chosen: the legacy bars stay published until the later stages (MC / ME) under the owner decision "replace".

## 3. Uncertainty and reproduction

Day-clustered percentile bootstrap (cluster = calendar day, B = 4000, seed 42): `Delta_v(b)` recomputed inside each draw from the per-day sums of the retained and the total steps (numerator and denominator resampled together; never a bootstrap of percentages); n drives, n days and Kish effective days are reported. The assumed-mass scenario range and the timing/integration variants are NOT combined with these intervals. The legacy reproduction anchor: the tool's `eff_0` and step counts equal the published `regenByTempMeasured` and `regenCaptureMeasured` for the cohort "all" (G1, to the published rounding), established before any variant is read.

## 4. Gates and tests

- G1: reproduction of the published bins (n exactly, eff to 0.1) on the population that feeds `category_B`; the per-cohort variants (warm, shoulder, cold) use the same extractor and the thermal-regime split of `seasonal_drive_master.csv`.
- G2: output = ONE additive key `regenLegacySensitivity` (deep diff: nothing else changes) plus the dashboard note beside the regeneration section; on demand (re-run when the extractor, the gates or the polling regime changes, or after about 14 calendar days of new drives), ingestion carries the block forward with a staleness label (as M432).
- G3: a second run is byte-identical.
- Known-answer tests (criteria declared here): (i) a synthetic drive with a known speed drop, mass and charge waveform reproduces the hand-calculated KE and charge energy (legacy and variants); (ii) a missing-RPM second is engine-off in the legacy and excluded by V1; (iii) a channel whose last native sample is 7 s old is excluded by V2(5) and kept by V2(10); (iv) a 3 s gap between kept rows is a step in the legacy and excluded by V3; (v) a step at 52 km/h start / 48 km/h end is in the 45 bin in the legacy and in the 55 bin under V4; (vi) a step with one missing probe is in a temperature bin in the legacy and excluded by V5; (vii) the offset sign: a known charge current of +10 A with i_off = -0.3888 gives 9.6112 A and a zero-charge step with +0.2 A becomes discharge; (viii) pooled recovery is a ratio of sums (unequal-KE steps), and bin sums of the variants reconcile to the legacy sums plus the removed sums.
- Blind audit (analytical-auditor, claim + data path only): reproduce `eff_0`, the retained shares and `Delta_v` for V1, V2(5), V3, V4, V5 on the cohort all from the raw files and the rules above; Director ruling before any dashboard text.

## 5. Reading and wording

The result may support: "the legacy bar for bin b changes by Delta_v under defect v" and the direction of the selection. It may not support a physical statement about regeneration efficiency, a temperature effect or a replacement value: the replacement estimator is stage MC. Wording: "apparent KE recovery", "sensitivity to the extraction rule", never "corrected", "error" or "bias" for the legacy values (the legacy rule is a declared convention with known weaknesses); a range over declared conventions, not a confidence interval.

## 6. Limits (disclosed in advance)

Single vehicle, one driver, assumed masses; the 1 Hz grid and the native-sample age depend on the logger cadence regime (reported); V2 ages use the native samples of the raw rows, which the slim frame cache preserves with the original time strings; steps are not independent (hence the day clustering); V4, V5 act on one view each; the variants do not repair the legacy estimator (stage MC does).

## 7. Rev 2 (2026-10-15): Director review of Rev 1 (revise) adopted

- **B1 Decision rules (declared before any variant result is read).** For a bin and a variant: `Delta` is *material* if |Delta| exceeds the half-width of the bin's published mass-uncertainty range (`effRangeMassUncertainty` for the temperature bins, `regenCaptureMeasuredMassUncertainty` for the speed bins) AND its 95 % day-clustered interval excludes zero; the variant is *not comparable* in a bin if it retains less than 90 % of the bin's KE or of its days; otherwise *not material*. Consequences: the material defects are adopted as exclusions or corrections by the new extractor in stage MC; the legacy shape (including the flat plateau and the thin-bin values) is NOT a target of MC; nothing published changes in MB; no physical statement follows from a nonzero Delta (no ground truth: it does not show which rule is closer to the truth).
- **B2 Population.** `category_B` iterates every row of `drive_master.csv` that has a frame (no ens_invalid or comparison filter); the regeneration keys were last recomputed by the ingestion build that wrote the current arrays (a restamp is not a recompute) and G1 (exact equality of n and eff in every published temperature and speed bin, established before any variant is read) is the evidence that this tool uses the same population and the same rules. If G1 fails the run stops and no variant is read.
- **B3 V2 exactly.** A grid second is the bin [s, s+1) with the single reference point s+1; a *native* sample of a channel is a raw row whose value is numeric AND inside the channel's range gate (speed 0-260, RPM >= 0, voltage 200-450, current unrestricted); the age at a step is (s+1) minus the time of the latest native sample with time < s+1; the speed age is checked at the step row AND at the previous kept row (KE uses both speeds); current, voltage and RPM at the step row; a step whose channel has no native sample at or before the second (or the channel is absent) is excluded by every V2(k) and counted as "unbounded". KAT (iii): a channel whose last native sample lies 7 s before the reference point is excluded by V2(5) and kept by V2(10).
- **B4 Diagnostics and rules.** For every exclusion variant the identity `Delta = w (eff_retained - eff_removed)` with `w` the removed KE share of the bin is reported and checked numerically to 1e-9; V4 reports the bin-migration share and the KE moved up and down; V6 reports `sum(capOff - cap) / sum ke` in percentage points per bin; the published bin set is fixed (bins that are empty in the legacy stay empty and are not created); in the bootstrap a draw with zero KE in a bin is unidentified for that bin, a bin with fewer than 5 contributing days is "not evaluable" (point values only), and if more than 2.5 % of the draws are unidentified the interval is "not available".
- **C1** `i_off` is in the discharge-positive frame of v6 (`net_draw_corr = net_draw - i_off V h / 1000`), so the logged (charge-positive) current becomes `I_log + i_off`; it is read from `offsetUncertainty.pointEstimateA` at run time. The offset is coupled to the SoC-anchored solve and to `CAP_KWH` (verified:false); V6 is a sensitivity, not a replacement basis.
- **C2** The combined variants are named VCa (V1 + V2(5) + V3, with V5 on the temperature view and V4 on the speed view) and VCb (VCa + V6); never "defect-free".
- **C3** Retained step and KE shares are also reported by cadence regime (the master column `ev_rpm_dt_med_s` below 1.0 s = fast, otherwise slow).
- **C4** `regenByZone` and the Charge Sources view are out of scope (same extractor, zone binning); the zone bars are expected to share the defects and are addressed in MC.
- **D** On-demand with a staleness label and carry-forward at ingestion, as M432. Wording: "apparent KE recovery (assumed mass)", "sensitivity to the extraction rule"; the range over variants carries no interval and is never merged with the bootstrap; never "corrected", "error", "bias", "robust", "insensitive" or "defect-free" for the legacy values or the combined variants.

## 8. Rev 2.1 (2026-10-15, Director confirmation of Rev 2: approve with recorded clarifications R1-R6, before any variant result was read)

- **R1 Labels (cohort all).** Order: `notEvaluable` (no legacy KE in the bin, fewer than 5 contributing days under the legacy or under the variant, or the interval is not available) > `notComparable` > `material` > `notMaterial`; the materiality threshold is the half-width of the LEGACY bin's published mass-uncertainty range.
- **R2** `notComparable` applies to exclusion variants only; V4 (reports bin migration) and V6 are exempt; VCa on the speed view uses its exclusion part.
- **R3** The identity check `Delta = w (eff_retained - eff_removed)` applies to V1, V2(k), V3, V5 and VCa on the temperature view only.
- **R4** A bootstrap draw is unidentified if the KE of the variant OR of the legacy bin is zero.
- **R5** A defect is *material* if it is material in at least one VALID legacy bin (temperature n >= 200; speed bins as published); invalid bins and the warm, shoulder and cold cohorts are descriptive. `notMaterial` is not evidence of harmlessness; stage MC may adopt any defect fix on design grounds.
- **R6** Section 2 wording "V0 ... defect-free" is superseded by C2 (VCa / VCb); -0.3888 A is the known-answer fixture value and the run-time value of `offsetUncertainty.pointEstimateA` governs; "at or before the second" in B3 means a native sample with time < s+1; a missing `ev_rpm_dt_med_s` means the cadence regime is "unknown".
