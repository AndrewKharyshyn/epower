## 2. Estimands (unchanged)
Headline estimators stay the day-clustered cluster-robust OLS slope per month (`degradationTrends.resistanceVreg`, `.cellSpread`, `tostEstimatorBasis = clusterRobustOLS`) and the primary equivalence design of `powerFadeEquivalence` (CR1 TOST plus day-clustered bootstrap, six hard stops, bound delta read from `releasedEquivalence.delta`; unchanged constants). This spec adds temperature-control specifications and a look schedule; it changes no headline definition.

## 3. Temperature-control specifications (pre-registered, evaluated at every ingestion from now on)
For each of the resistance proxy (S0 population) and the cell spread:
- T-lin: current specification (linear pack-temperature term).
- T-quad: linear plus squared term (centred).
- T-arr: Arrhenius form, log response on 1/T (K) with the same population and clustering.
- T-spl: natural cubic spline in pack temperature with a pre-set knot rule (three knots at the 25/50/75 % of the observed eligible temperature distribution at the time of the first run, then FROZEN; refit with the same knot values), reported only where the support flag holds (a temperature cell needs at least 10 drives on at least 5 days, the project cohort floor); outside supported cells the spline is not extrapolated.
Primary for the decision rules below: T-arr. T-lin, T-quad and T-spl are sensitivities. Selection rule (fixed now): the day-blocked cross-validation error (blocks = calendar day) decides only whether T-arr is retained as primary; T-arr stays primary unless another specification has a lower day-blocked CV error by more than the CV standard error on at least two consecutive ingestions; any switch is recorded as a deviation and not applied retroactively.
Disagreement between specifications is reported (sign, interval overlap with zero), never tuned to agree. A statement such as "not every interval spans zero" is allowed only when it holds under T-arr and T-quad.

## 4. Looks, equivalence and alpha spending
- Looks: month 9 (about February 2027) and month 12 (about May 2027) only. Interim ingestions refresh the descriptive trend and its interval but produce no equivalence decision and no stopping.
- Alpha: SUPERSEDED by section 0 (owner decision A or B; Rev 0 split 0.01/0.04 withdrawn).
- Attainability gate (reporting, never a stop): `powerFadeEquivalence.s1.seMaxForEquivalence` (delta / t(0.95, df), currently 0.3278 mOhm per month for the resistance proxy) is compared with the current day-clustered SE (currently 0.6162). If at a look the SE exceeds the ceiling, the verdict is "equivalence not attainable at this window", which is not "no degradation". The projected SE at months 9 and 12 (audit arithmetic: about 0.36 and 0.19) is recomputed by script from the observed day count; it is a forecast and carries no decision weight.
- The bound delta and the TOST design are not re-chosen after any winter result (delta lock date as recorded in the payload).
- No 18- or 24-month look exists: the study is 12 months.
