## Disclosure (post hoc)
The class below was defined AFTER the 2026-11-03 step had been seen in the data (the owner note and the header diff of the Nov 3-5 files). It is a post hoc, header-derived projection of the logger configuration, not an identified epoch. `pidSetHash` (14 distinct column sets across 78 drives since 2026-10-09, mostly singletons) is too fine to use as a covariate and is reported only; `nVcmColumns` and the column counts are descriptive only: the sampling period is not a function of the number of logged columns (slow regime, drives with 76-79 named columns: Spearman of period on column count -0.04, p 0.57, n 203).

## What the data show (script output on the 632 raw files; written before the design; descriptive, confounded with date, regime and drive mix)
- Classes by header (`[BMS] HV Battery Intake Air Temperature (℃)` present / `[VCM] Motor Power Consumption (%)` present): A (yes/no) 606 drives, 2026-05-11..2026-11-03, period median 1.283 s; B (no/yes) 14 drives, period median 0.970 s: 13 drives from `2026-11-03_15-07-48` to `2026-11-05_09-13-42` (3 days) and one early drive `20260513_182950` (0.771 s); C (no/no) 7 drives (2026-05-12..13 and 2026-08-16, no period); D (yes/yes) 5 drives (2026-05-15, 05-31, 08-13 x3; period 1.288-3.783 s).
- Header presence is a weak marker of polling: the non-null fraction of the Motor Power Consumption column is about 0.06 in the 13 post-step drives (0.0 in the early B drive) and the Intake Air Temperature column is non-null in a median 0.01 of its rows in class A (range 0.0-0.063): both are slow-rotation PIDs, so the header says that the PID was configured, not how often it was read.
- The within-day step of 2026-11-03: the two morning drives (class A) have periods 0.800 and 0.805 s, the afternoon and evening drives (class B) 0.919-0.984 s; the fast-regime median before the step is 0.817 s. One day with 2 vs 3 drives is a descriptive contrast with no interval and no inference.

## Scope of M582a (additive; no existing leaf changes)

Tests (fixtures pinned to master MD5 `1574a8156a36c6ea4ea1dfdaafc7cc72`, not live counts): synthetic-header known-answer test (classes A-D, 'Unnamed' columns, encoding errors, file-name styles, unmatched file STOP); tie-out of the real master to the numbers above (606 / 14 / 7 / 5, B median 0.970, A median 1.283, the 13 post-step drives and 3 days, non-null fractions); a semantic-gate check that the row prints counts and the statement from the payload; language-gate rule `M582-pidset-wording` (ledger id M582) forbidding `caused by`, `because of the VCM`, `PID effect`, `epoch identified`, `cadence-corrected` while passing the negated 'no cadence effect and no causal mechanism is claimed'.

## Decision rules and wording
No inference in this milestone: no p-value, no cadence effect, no mechanism. Allowed: "header-derived PID-set class (proxy)", "descriptive", "associated with". Forbidden: "caused", "because of the VCM PID", "PID effect", "epoch identified", "cadence-corrected". The regime rule (1.10 s, guard zone [1.06, 1.135)) is unchanged.
