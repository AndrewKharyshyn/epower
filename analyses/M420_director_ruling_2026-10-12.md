# M420 Director rulings (research-director, Opus), 2026-10-12: scope of the integration-scenario sensitivities and wording of the dashboard panel

## 1. Spec review (analyses/M420_spec.md Rev 0): decision "revise"
Implemented as Rev 1 before the tool was run on the corpus: net draw as a secondary estimand, shift in kWh only; the drive set pinned to the Monte Carlo set (gate: baseline total equals the Monte Carlo nominal); gross discharge and charge per row; `inMonteCarlo` pre-registered for every row (A0-A2 true, C2-C5 partial, A3 / B1 / B2 / C1 false, D1-D3 true); A0 (500 ms) and a 3 s cap added; a gap inventory; hold variants on the baseline alignment and cap; the midpoint rule cited, not duplicated; D1 must reproduce `offsetGrossSensitivity`; D2 / D3 deterministic rows without a variability interval and not called bounds; capacity rows in a separate subtable without a ratio interval; cadence and quantisation in a carried subtable with their own n; the day-cluster ratio interval allowed and labelled as variability across days; per-drive spread required (min, P10, median, P90, max, n undefined); the Monte Carlo as a separate reference row, never on one axis with the scenarios, never as whiskers, never unioned, with the statement that its centre is not the nominal; blind audit of the baseline, A3, B1, B2, C1, the 3 s and 10 s caps, D1, the per-drive spread and the bootstrap unit; audit numbers stay out of code and tests. Andrii sign-off not needed (reporting-only); the union-versus-side-by-side amendment of ruling 3(a) is recorded in `analyses/M403_spec.md`.

## 2. Panel wording (analyses/M420_audit/panel_text.txt): decision "revise"
Implemented: "a deterministic recomputation on this corpus" replaces "exact"; the Monte Carlo sentence says where the nominal lies in the interval instead of "around the nominal"; the midpoint rule is described as a different quadrature rule whose deviation reflects the rule difference, not an error of the released integration; "n drives with baseline < 0.1 kWh excluded from the spread"; the header "Day-to-day variability of the ratio, 95 % (calendar-day resampling)"; a kind label on every row ([estimator choice] for A3 / B1 / B2 / C1, [data process] for A0-A2, C2-C5, D1-D3); D2 / D3 without the negated "bound"; zero-ratio disclosure generated from the rows (a sparse-voltage drive integrates to zero at the tight tolerances, as in the Monte Carlo grid); the omitted list adds logger cadence and fuel-side quantities; net draw is a small difference of large integrals and not comparable with the gross shifts; left- and right-hold shift in the same direction, so the difference from the trapezoid is not a time-shift effect and its attribution is unresolved (generated from the signs); the 0.908 cadence emulation is labelled a stress emulation beyond the observed slow regime; the baseline reproduction (maximum deviation) and the excluded drives are printed; the sentence "must not be read as the accuracy of the throughput" became "the Monte Carlo interval alone understates the sensitivity of the throughput to the integration choices". Language rule `M420-integration-scenarios` in `language_gate.py` (the broad "bound", "scenario bias" and "worst case" patterns were left to the panel-level gate because the corpus uses them legitimately elsewhere). Open follow-ups named by the Director: a split of the shifts by month and cadence regime; any article use needs an Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off.
