## Definitions (fixed here, not tuned)
Population: `ens_outlier_v2` not True (the canonical rule, NaN treated as not excluded, as `degradation_trends._prep`), with non-null columns for each statistic.
- **Thesis KPIs** (all from the master): K1 corpus throughput / discharge = sum(gross_throughput_kwh) / sum(gross_discharge_kwh); K2 net share = |sum(net_draw_kwh_corr)| / sum(gross_throughput_kwh) (the published offset-corrected net) and K2raw with `net_draw_kwh`; K3 median over drives of `soc_band` (pp); K4 median over drives of `peak_discharge_kw`; K5 median over drives of |net_draw_kwh_corr| / gross_throughput_kwh. Each with (a) the day-cluster percentile bootstrap (resample calendar days) and (b) the week-block percentile bootstrap (resample ISO calendar weeks, non-overlapping blocks of the days present), seed 42, 4000 draws, plus the ratio of the two interval widths. Estimates are the full-sample values; the intervals are conditional on the specified resampling unit.
- **Trend slopes** (cellSpread, resistanceVreg; also the fast and slow logging regimes separately, the fast regime having fewer than 50 day clusters): the released design `y ~ months + T + I`. Reported next to the released CR1: (i) **CR2** (Bell-McCaffrey / Pustejovsky-Tipton bias-reduced linearisation): `A_g = (I - H_gg)^(-1/2)`, `V = M [sum_g X_g' A_g e_g e_g' A_g X_g] M`, `M = (X'X)^-1`, interval `b +/- t_df,0.975 SE` with the Satterthwaite df of the working model with independent homoskedastic errors: `df = (sum_g ||w_g||^2)^2 / ||W'W||_F^2`, `w_g = (I - H)[:, g] A_g X_g M c`, `c` the months selector; (ii) **wild cluster bootstrap-t** (Rademacher weights by cluster, seed 42, 4000 draws): the null-imposed p-value (restricted fit without months) and the unrestricted percentile-t interval (CR1 standard error per draw); (iii) the **day-cluster pairs bootstrap** and the **week-block pairs bootstrap** of the slope (resample whole days / ISO weeks, refit the full design); (iv) a **minimum effect with 80 % power** per standard error: `(t_df,0.975 + t_df,0.8) x SE` (two-sided 5 % test, power 0.8, given the observed standard error; not an observed-power statement; the payload text avoids the words the language gate reserves).
- Nothing is rescaled or tuned to make the procedures agree; disagreement is reported (CLAUDE.md: report method disagreement). The released estimator and its TOST decision are unchanged by this milestone.

## Known-answer harness (`tests/synthetic/test_dependence_sensitivity.py`, in CI)
CR2 with singleton clusters equals HC2 (statsmodels) exactly; the CR1 reimplementation reproduces the released slope/SE/CI on the real master (cellSpread and resistanceVreg); with few clusters and intra-cluster correlation, simulated coverage of the CR1 normal interval falls below nominal while the CR2 t interval is closer to 95 %; the wild-cluster null-imposed p-value is approximately uniform under the null (size near 5 %) and small under a real slope; the week-block interval is wider than the day-cluster interval on data with weekly autocorrelation and about equal on independent days; ratio and median KPIs equal hand-computed values; draws with a singular design are skipped and counted.

## Limits (disclosed)
Bootstrap and CR2 do not remove season, logging-regime or calendar-drift confounding (the trend slopes are conditional on the design); week blocks address serial dependence at the weekly scale only; with about 20 weeks the block bootstrap is itself coarse; CR2/wild are small-cluster corrections, not a model of dependence; one vehicle.
