## 1. What and why
- The published heat-soak block (Thermal tab, "Heat-Soak Carryover") shows a 6-12 h gap bin with a retained-heat median of 0.354. M751/M756 (cloud gap table M729, pinned) found that this is the only supported cell that moves materially when pairs with estimated unlogged driving are excluded: 0.354 published; the A-B and A-C shifts are 10.1 and 7.5 pp on the M751 basis and 8.1 and 7.2 pp on the M755 basis (M756 rerun), intervals above 0; the displayed medians come from the result file, not from this spec. The dashboard text today explains the 6-12 h and 12-24 h bins as a "floor artefact" and a selection effect; it does not say that unlogged driving between logged drives contributes. M751 deferred a dashboard display to its own spec and Director ruling: this is that spec.
- Scope: DISPLAY of an already-registered, audited result. No new estimate, no new method, no change to any published figure, to `heatSoakCarryover` or to the existing text claims; the sensitivity is added beside the 6-12 h bin, labelled.

## 2. Payload (additive, carried block)
- New key `heatSoakGapSensitivity` in `summary_arrays.json`, built by a one-off splice tool `tools/heatsoak_gap_payload.py` from the registered result JSON (script-written, never typed), isolation-diffed (only this key changes). Contents (aggregates only): `basis` (registered master MD5 d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98, 717 drives, M729 gap table sha256, M755 ambient basis, result sha256, tool sha256), `sets` (A, B, C with pair counts), per supported gap bin the published median (A), the B and C medians, the shift A-B and A-C in percentage points with the day-clustered 95 % interval, n pairs with ambient and the verdict word (material / small / not distinguishable / not estimable at this support), the tau and decay-rate rows for A, B, C with intervals, `unloggedShare` (share of pairs with estimated unlogged driving per bin), the registered overall wording, and `staleness`: `carried: true`, `corpusDrives: 717`, `refreshedAt: null`.

On any later ingestion the dashboard compares `basis.masterMd5` with the live master and states "computed on the registered corpus of 717 drives; later drives are not included" next to the numbers (script-written from `basis`). `release_check` gets a check that the block is present, that its numbers equal the registered result file (leaf by leaf) and that the labelled-carried fields exist; a stale basis never fails the gate (it is disclosed, like `generatorTractionRecon.staleBlocks`).

## 4. Decision rules (frozen)
- R1 payload: block values equal the registered result leaf by leaf (tolerance 0); the splice changes only the new key; `drive_master.csv` MD5 and `heatSoakCarryover` unchanged.
- R2 display: the only claim added is the M756 overall wording ("sensitive to unlogged driving (lower bound): a supported bin or tau is material under B and under C"), and only for the supported cells that the result marks material; cells marked "small", "not distinguishable" or "not estimable" are displayed with exactly those words and no interpretation. - Limits stated on the page: lower bound; gap table is estimated from the cloud odometer and geolocation (not an OBD record); ambient is the hand-entered driving-time value; the set sizes differ (A 706 pairs, C 613, B 504 at the registered basis; ambient subsets in the table); the carried block does not include drives ingested after the registered master.
