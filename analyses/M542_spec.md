## 1. What this wave does
Adds to the existing top-level key `engineOnRule` (declared leaf-only update, same producer `tools/engine_on_rule_block.py`, same per-drive sidecar, same stage) two additive sub-blocks, and changes nothing else:
- `byCohort` {warm, shoulder, cold}: for each thermal-regime cohort the same figures as `pooled` (valid seconds, rpm >= 300 seconds and hours, engine-on seconds and hours (> 400), closed-band seconds and hours, exact-400 seconds, engine-on share of valid time, band share of the rpm >= 300 time, each share with the day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval, seed 42, 4000 draws), plus n drives and n days.
- `aggregatorSensitivity`: the pooled and per-cohort engine-on hours and band hours recomputed with the "last value in the 1 s bin" aggregator instead of the mean (the blind audit of M541 found the band is aggregator-sensitive at drive level, e.g. 33 to 42 s and 75 to 82 s, the > 400 total much less so). Reported as a sensitivity, never as a replacement; the pooled figure of record stays the mean (the pipeline convention).
No display and no JSX change in this wave (`displayed` stays true for the pooled block already shown; the cohort leaves are not bound to any text here: that is M543).

## 2. Definitions and sources (all numbers script-written)
- Cohort of a drive: `thermal_regime` in `seasonal_drive_master.csv` (the cohort key of `seasonalCharts`), the same assignment the stored cohort `RpmDistribution` uses; counts must equal `seasonalCharts._meta.cohortCounts` (warm 423, shoulder 170, cold 13, unclassified 0).
- Per-drive counts come from the existing sidecar `engine_on_rule_sidecar.csv` (no raw reread for the mean aggregator). The last-value sensitivity needs one extra per-drive pass over the raw files: it is added as new sidecar columns (`gt400_last`, `ge300_last`, `n_valid_last`) under the same SHA-256 / row-function-hash key (`raw/` = sha256-verified originals); the sidecar meta hash covers the new function so a mismatch rebuilds.
- Grid convention (amended by the M541 audit; recorded here as the convention of record): the engine-speed column is aggregated by the MEAN within the clock-aligned 1 s bin, native forward fill (15 rows) before the resample, grid forward fill (15 s) after it, negatives dropped. The sensitivity changes only the within-bin aggregator (last non-NaN value in the bin).

## 3. Identity and faithfulness gates (STOP otherwise)
- G1 cohort partition: the three cohorts partition the 606 drives; engine-on, band, rpm >= 300 and valid seconds sum over cohorts to the pooled integers exactly.

If a cohort histogram turns out to be built on a different drive set, STOP and report; nothing is adjusted to agree.
- G3 the mean-aggregator figures of this wave equal the M540 pooled leaves exactly (no change to `pooled`).
