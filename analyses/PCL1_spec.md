# PCL-1 spec (Rev 2.1) - paired-cadence logging protocol and the pre-registered analysis that decides whether stage ME can proceed (milestone M443)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED, written before any drive of the protocol exists. Rev 2.1, 2026-10-15 (Rev 1 reviewed by the research-director: "revise", items B1-B9 adopted; Rev 2 confirmation: minor revise, adopted here: the randomized cadence effect is the PRIMARY estimand, the view-total tolerance excludes borrowed values and the 0-10 km/h bin, the extension rule and its 97.5 % level, and script-written sizing). Owner: Andrii (the protocol needs his consent and his action: section 2). Origin: M442 Rev 1.3 (analyses/M442_spec.md section 9): the cadence gate G2 (fast versus slow drives, paired by day, tolerance b + mass half-width) was NOT TESTABLE because only 3 even days contain both kinds of drive, and the existing corpus confounds the logger polling cadence with season, day and driving conditions (the unpaired fast-versus-slow difference at 50-60 km/h is about -12 pp). Stage ME stays blocked until G2, in its D4 form, passes on drives logged after Rev 1.3. This protocol creates those data by RANDOMIZING the polling setting within each day, which also gives a direct, unconfounded estimate of the cadence effect on the native v2 level.

## 1. Question and estimands

**Estimand (b) is PRIMARY; G2 is secondary.** (a) **G2 (the gate).** Does the harmonized estimator (M442: interp3 on the 3 s grid, four phases, assumed masses) give the same level for drives logged at the fast polling cadence and drives logged at the slow cadence on the same days? Per cell of the temperature view (no SOC condition) and the speed view (bins at or above 10 km/h; 0-10 km/h is excluded) with the M442 tolerance `b(cell) + mass half-width`, and at each view total. (b) **Cadence effect on native v2 (the primary estimand; it decides nothing by itself).** The paired-by-day difference `fast - slow` of the native v2 pooled ratio per cell with its day-clustered interval: the randomized estimate of how much the polling cadence moves the native level; it is the figure the ME display discloses as the resolution effect. (c) **Compliance** (share of drives whose observed regime equals the assigned arm) and the intention-to-treat sensitivity.

## 2. Intervention (what the owner does)

The logger is Car Scanner ELM OBD2; the polling regime is set by an app setting (the setting that moved the median HV-current interval between about 0.82-0.86 s (fast) and 1.3-1.5 s (slow) on 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-08 with the column set unchanged: M376, M396). Before each trip the owner sets the app to the arm given by `analyses/PCL1_schedule.csv` and starts the log. Nothing else about driving changes.

- **Design A (preferred): trips in permuted blocks of two.** `tools/pcl_schedule.py` (seed 42): for every calendar day from the START DATE the trips are numbered by start order; consecutive pairs (1,2), (3,4), ... form a block with one fast and one slow trip in seeded random order; an unpaired last trip takes the slot-1 arm of its block and does not count toward the paired-day rule.
- **Design B (fallback, weaker, B9: fixed BEFORE the START DATE and recorded with it):** half-day arms. Days are taken in consecutive pairs; within a pair a seeded draw decides which day has the fast arm in the morning; on every day the afternoon arm is the opposite of the morning arm; the boundary is 13:00 local start time. It identifies the effect only through the order randomized across days and has wider intervals. The design (A or B) cannot be changed after the START DATE.
- **Slot rule (B6).** The slot of a trip is its start order among ALL log files of that calendar day (including files that are later ineligible); a missed log or a split log is counted as non-compliance, not re-numbered.
- **START DATE (B5).** The owner chooses it; it must be at least the day after the commit that contains this spec and the schedule, and both SHA-256 values (`PCL1 spec sha256 <hash>`, `PCL1 schedule sha256 <hash>`) must be recorded in the CHANGELOG before the first protocol trip. Drives are in or out by the master local start DATE; the protocol is never applied retroactively.

## 3. Eligible drives and arms

Eligible: drives with master start date on or after the START DATE, canonical-clean, in support of the harmonized estimator (M442 C3). **Arm = the OBSERVED regime of the file** (master `ev_rpm_dt_med_s` < 1.0 s fast, otherwise slow; as-treated), because the measured cadence is what the estimator sees; the assigned arm is used for compliance and for the intention-to-treat sensitivity. A paired day is a calendar day with at least 2 eligible drives of each observed arm. Unpaired days are not used for G2 and are reported. **Holding rule (B4):** until the one-shot analysis has run, no cadence-stratified aggregate (the fast / slow / unknown views of the regen payload, the MD cadence stratum, any refresh of `regenCaptureV2HStudy`) may include protocol drives; the ingestion code excludes drives on or after the START DATE from them.

## 4. Analysis (run ONCE; pre-registered)

Run by `tools/pcl_gate.py` once, at the earlier of 100 paired days or the hard stop 2027-09-30, and not before 60 paired days (the lock file `analyses/PCL1_gate.lock`; the script refuses unless the CHANGELOG carries both SHA-256 values). (B3) If fewer than 60 paired days exist at the hard stop the result is INCONCLUSIVE without any cell and goes to the FAIL branch of section 6. Until then `tools/pcl_monitor.py` reports counts only (paired days, drives per arm, compliance, balance): no cell value, difference or interval of the protocol drives is computed or looked at. The analysis uses the first 100 paired days in calendar order. **Data:** the protocol drives; the harmonized tables (M442 variants l3p0..3) and the native v2 table from the per-drive cache. **Statistic:** per cell, `harmonized(fast) - harmonized(slow)` as a difference of pooled ratios over the paired days, one day-resample matrix (seed 42, 4000 draws, over the sorted paired days) shared by all cells and the totals, percentile interval. **Tolerance:** `b(cell)` is the M442 bracket frozen from `analyses/M442_result_rerun.json` (its SHA-256 is recorded in the result; a cell without an entry borrows the largest b of its view); the mass half-width is that of the cell with both arms POOLED on the paired days (B7). A view total uses the largest OWN b of the view's cells (values borrowed from other cells and the 0-10 km/h bin are excluded: stated deviation from M442 D3) plus the pooled mass half-width of the total; the borrowed b of cells without fast-drive support is replaced by that same largest own b. **Support (deviation from M442 C4, stated, B2):** `supported` in an arm = the M441 floors (>= 200 strict-drop intervals, >= 10 drives, >= 5 days) on the unrounded per-phase means in that arm on the paired days; the denominator of "at least half" is the set of cells supported on the paired days with the two arms pooled; speed 0-10 km/h is excluded; a view total is a cell. **Cell outcome:** FAIL if the interval excludes 0 and |difference| exceeds the tolerance; INDETERMINATE if the interval contains 0 and |difference| exceeds it; OK otherwise. **View-total outcome (B1; stricter than the cell rule):** OK only if the total is supported in both arms AND its whole 95 % interval lies inside +-tolerance; FAIL if the interval excludes 0 and |difference| exceeds the tolerance; INDETERMINATE otherwise (a wide interval is not a pass). **Decision:** PASS if, in each of the two views, at least half of the pooled-supported cells are supported in both arms, at least 3 temperature cells and 5 speed cells are tested, the view total is OK and no tested cell FAILS or is INDETERMINATE; FAIL if any tested cell or view total FAILS; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. **Director before any PASS (B8):** if compliance is below 90 % or the intention-to-treat decision differs from the as-treated decision, the result goes to the Director before it is acted on. **Extension (B3):** one extension is allowed after an INCONCLUSIVE result with at least 60 paired days: the analysis is repeated on the cumulative paired days (up to 160) and ends at the earliest of 160 paired days, 90 calendar days after the first analysis and the hard stop 2027-09-30; the EXTENSION result decides and the first result is reported beside it; two looks inflate the chance of a false PASS, so the extension uses 97.5 % intervals; INCONCLUSIVE at the extension end counts as FAIL. **Reported with the decision:** the native v2 cadence effect per cell (estimand b), the intention-to-treat sensitivity, the unpaired days, compliance, balance.

## 5. Size (script-written sizing)

`analyses/PCL1_sizing.py` -> `analyses/PCL1_sizing.json` (all numbers below are copied from that file; hypothetical arms drawn from the warm-season corpus, variance only, NO true cadence effect). (i) Support: with k calendar days of at least 4 drives assigned in permuted blocks of two, at least 3 temperature and 5 speed cells meet the floors in both arms in 0.835 of the trials at k = 20, 0.995 at k = 30 and 1.000 at k = 40. (ii) Precision (B1): the view-total tolerances are 2.247 pp (temperature) and 1.392 pp (speed), including the pooled mass half-width 0.570 pp. The 95 % half-width of the fast-minus-slow difference at the total has a median of 3.32 pp at k = 60, 2.92 at 80 and 2.59 at 100 (2.30 at 160 with the 97.5 % level of the extension). The upper bound of P(PASS) (both view totals inside their tolerance; the cell conditions are not simulated) is 0.000 at k = 60, 0.000 at 80, 0.000 at 100 and 0.000 at 160. **A G2 PASS is therefore not expected at any planned size**: trip-to-trip heterogeneity dominates the day-level difference. The realistic outcome of the protocol is the FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE branch of section 6, in which estimand (b), the randomized cadence effect with its interval, is the declared resolution bracket. The protocol is worth running for (b): a gap of the size seen unpaired in the existing corpus (about -12 pp at 50-60 km/h) is detectable at about 40 paired days, a 1-2 pp effect is not. Hence the Director-approved minimum of 60 and stop at 100 paired days are kept; the owner may stop earlier only by a recorded decision before the analysis. At about 5 drives a day and about two thirds of days with at least 4 trips, 100 paired days need roughly 5 months of logging. The winter temperature cells are not covered by the sizing.

## 6. Outcomes

PASS (after the Director's check of section 4): stage ME may go on to the bootstrap-CI audit, the Director ruling and Andrii's sign-off (M442 Rev 1.3 E4), with the harmonized level shown. FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE after the extension: the harmonized level is rejected as the displayed estimand; ME may show only cadence-stratified native v2 levels with the randomized cadence effect of estimand (b), whatever its precision, as the declared resolution bracket (a Director decision at that point). In every branch the legacy bars keep their cadence-dependence disclosure until replaced, and the randomized estimate (b) is reported.

## 7. Limits (disclosed in advance)

The owner knows the arm (no blinding; the outcome is an objective logged signal). Trip order and trip type within a day are not randomized beyond the permuted blocks, so trip type can still differ between arms (reported by distance and start-hour balance, not adjusted). The app setting may change more than the polling interval (checked by the column set and the observed regime per file). Fast drives are about a third of the corpus; b comes from the existing warm-season fast drives, so cold bins borrow it. The sizing is variance-only and warm-season. The protocol answers the cadence question for the vehicle, driver, app and adapter used; temperature, SOC, speed and demand remain confounded as before. The cost to the owner is the setting change before each trip for several months.

## 8. Implementation (declared)

`tools/pcl_schedule.py` (deterministic schedule from the START DATE, seed 42, design A or B), `tools/pcl_monitor.py` (report-only ingestion stage: compliance and counts, no cell values; state `analyses/pcl1_monitor.json`), `tools/pcl_gate.py` (the one-shot analysis, lock, freeze check and the B1-B8 rules), `tests/synthetic/test_pcl1.py`; the rules B1-B9 as tagged above; the holding rule of section 3 in `tools/regen_v2_agg.py`. No payload key and no display change; the result enters the CHANGELOG and, after the Director's ruling, the `regenCaptureV2HStudy` record.
