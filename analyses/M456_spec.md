# M456 spec (Rev 1, DRAFT for Director review) - new Path A (road-load) versus Path B (fuel/BSFC) cross-validation of the generator and traction energy on the current corpus

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT, written before any result exists. Rev 1, 2026-10-19. Owner: Andrii (approved the milestone in chat, 2026-10-19: "yes", after the question whether to commission a new cross-validation). Not yet reviewed by the research-director. Replaces nothing until ruled: the carried block `generatorTractionRecon.crossval` (pre-M366 basis, 82 drives, Path A coefficients unpreserved, `staleBlocks`) stays as it is, labelled historic.

## 1. Purpose and the honest limits

The historic cross-validation compared two independent routes to the same per-drive energies: Path B (logged fuel -> BSFC regime table -> generator electrical energy; the pipeline of `recon_engine.py` / `fuel_recon.py`) and Path A (road load: wheel power from assumed mass, rolling resistance, aerodynamic drag and inertia -> electrical-equivalent traction energy). Its per-drive Path A outputs survive but its coefficients (m, C_rr, C_dA, drivetrain efficiency) were never preserved (`crossval_gtr.py`), so Path A cannot be reproduced for other drives without declaring coefficients. Declaring coefficients now is allowed only as a pre-registered convention with its range; it is never "restoration", and the result is not a validation of the thesis.

Limits declared in advance: (L1) no grade information exists in the logs used here (no altitude channel in the sampled raw files; `Latitude` only), so Path A carries no grade term; on hilly drives it is biased in both directions and the pooled ratio is meaningful only as a ratio of sums over many drives. (L2) The logger fuel rate and counter are app-calculated (CLAUDE.md); Path B inherits that and the BSFC table, the generator/power-electronics efficiencies and the cold multiplier are assumed constants. (L3) Path A coefficients are assumptions with ranges (`verified: false`), never fitted to Path B for the primary result. (L4) One vehicle, one driver.

## 2. Estimands (per drive, then pooled)

- **Traction energy** `E_trac`: Path B = the pipeline's gross traction energy (`E_trac_gross` of `recon_engine.central_estimate_v2`, battery anchored to the master energies); Path A = positive wheel work from the road-load equation divided by the drivetrain efficiency (electrical-equivalent), integrated over all samples with a valid speed; negative wheel work is regenerative and enters only the secondary estimand below.
- **Generator energy to traction** `E_gen`: Path B = `E_gen_to_trac` of the pipeline; Path A = `E_trac_A` minus the pipeline's anchored battery discharge energy to traction (`E_batt_to_trac`), which both paths share. Because the battery term is shared, the PRIMARY comparison is the traction energy (Path A against Path B total), and the generator comparison is reported as secondary with the shared-term caveat stated.
- Pooled quantities: the ratio of sums `R = sum(E_A) / sum(E_B)` and the distance-weighted per-100 km values.

## 3. Population and eligibility

Fuel-instrumented drives of the canonical-clean set (`ens_outlier_v2` False), with distance > 0 and a recon result (the population of the `sensitivity` block, M377b: 348 of 350 fuel-instrumented drives at the time of writing; the script writes the live counts). Resampling unit = calendar day. Every estimate carries n (drives, days).

## 4. Path A road-load convention (pre-registered; the central decision for the Director)

`P_wheel = (C_rr * m * g + 0.5 * rho * C_dA * v^2 + m * a) * v`, speed `v` from the logged speed, `a` from the centred difference of the speed over the native samples (intervals with a time gap > 5 s are skipped), mass `m` by drive type from the existing assumed-mass convention (`MASS_BY_DRIVE_TYPE_KG` and its disclosed urban/mixed range), `rho` = air density at the logged ambient temperature or a declared constant when absent. The primary coefficient set is a **grid, not a point and not a fit**: C_rr in {0.009, 0.011, 0.013}, C_dA in {0.70, 0.78, 0.86} m^2, drivetrain efficiency (wheel to battery terminal, traction direction) in {0.85, 0.88, 0.91}; the central cell is (0.011, 0.78, 0.88). All values are `verified: false` assumptions; their sources must be stated in the Director brief and any change is a spec revision before the run. The reported Path A result is the central cell with the full range over the 27 cells as a convention range, kept apart from the statistical interval. Open choice (Director): whether a coast-down identification of C_rr and C_dA from the same logs (engine off, rpm <= 300, no braking inferred) can be offered as a secondary, noting that friction-brake use is unobserved; and whether a day-blocked calibration of the coefficients against Path B is offered ONLY as a labelled circular diagnostic of the equation's shape (never as a validation of the level).

## 5. Statistics

Primary: the pooled ratio of sums `R` of traction energy (Path A central / Path B) with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap (calendar day as cluster, `numpy.random.default_rng(42)`, `integers(0, D, (B, D))`, B = 4000), TOST equivalence against a pre-registered margin `delta` (proposed +-10 % of `R = 1`, for the Director to set; MDE reported) and the 90 % interval for the TOST; "equivalence not established" is not "disagreement" and "equivalence established" is not "validated". Secondary: Lin's concordance correlation coefficient and the Bland-Altman mean difference (kWh/100 km) of per-drive values with day-clustered intervals; Pearson r is reported but is never a decision statistic (it cannot detect a level bias). Stratified reports (descriptive, same machinery, no decision): drive type, speed class (urban / mixed / highway), cadence regime (fast / slow polled), temperature regime. The Path A convention range (27 cells) is reported as `R` min-max beside the central value, never combined with the interval. Method disagreement is reported as found: the spec never tunes a convention to bring the paths into agreement, and a grid cell is never chosen after seeing `R`.

## 6. Decision rules and falsification (declared before any result)

(D1) If the day-clustered 90 % interval of `R` lies inside `1 +- delta` for the central cell AND the central-cell `R` is inside the convention range of the other 26 cells' equivalence verdicts reported alongside: "traction-energy agreement within +-delta is established under the stated Path A convention (assumed coefficients, no grade term)". (D2) If the interval excludes `1 +- delta`: reported as a method disagreement with its size, cause analysis only descriptive (drive-type and speed strata), and escalated to the Director; the headline GTR figures are NOT changed by this block. (D3) Otherwise: "equivalence not established" (not "no disagreement"). The block cannot raise or lower a headline figure. What would falsify the claim of agreement: an interval beyond `delta` in the pooled ratio, or a stratum (drive type, speed class) whose interval excludes `1 +- 2 delta`.

## 7. Outputs, gates, tests and chain

Additive payload `generatorTractionRecon.crossvalV2` (stamp, spec SHA-256, master MD5, convention grid, Path A / Path B code hashes, counts, `R` with interval and convention range, secondary statistics, strata), produced on demand by a new `tools/crossval_v2.py` and carried forward at ingestion (re-run on the 14-day rule or when the pipeline changes); the historic `crossval` block stays and keeps its label. No dashboard change in this milestone; a Fuel-tab disclosure would be a separate step with its own sign-off. Gates: (G1) reconciliation: the Path B totals equal the pipeline's own totals (`fuel_recon_master.csv` / the `sensitivity` block basis) within 1e-9; (G2) known-answer tests (`tests/synthetic/test_crossval_v2.py`): constant speed on a flat road gives the analytic `P_wheel`, a constant acceleration gives `m a v`, negative wheel work is excluded from the traction integral, a time gap > 5 s adds no energy, the ratio of sums differs from the mean of ratios on unequal-energy drives, whole-day resampling is reproducible; (G3) deep diff of `summary_arrays.json` shows only the new key and its stamp; (G4) `release_check.py` green; the spec SHA-256 is recorded in the CHANGELOG BEFORE the first run and the first output is kept (run-once lock, as M444); a blind analytical-auditor reproduces `R` (central cell) with its interval from the raw files and sections 2-5; Director ruling before anything is shown.

## 8. Open questions for the Director

- **Q1** Is the grid of section 4 (with its stated sources) an acceptable pre-registered convention, or should the central cell be replaced by a coast-down identification? Are the proposed values defensible for this vehicle (X-Trail T33 e-POWER, FWD)?
- **Q2** Is traction energy (shared battery term removed from the question) the right PRIMARY estimand, with the generator comparison secondary?
- **Q3** Equivalence margin `delta` (+-10 % proposed) and the 90 % TOST interval: confirm or replace.
- **Q4** Is a circular day-blocked calibration diagnostic worth including at all, given it cannot validate the level?
- **Q5** How should the missing grade term (L1) be handled: accept and disclose, or restrict to drives whose GPS-derived altitude change is small (and is any altitude channel available)?
- **Q6** Anything that would make a "no" answer to the whole milestone the better decision?

## 9. Rev 1.1 (2026-10-19): Director review (verdict "revise") and the milestone's status: PARKED pending external road-load data
