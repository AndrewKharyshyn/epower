## 0. Why, and what is already known
- The corpus assumes a class mass per drive (`_mass_for_drive`): highway and mixed_highway 2000 kg, urban and mixed 1890 kg with the disclosed range 1860 to 1920 kg (M164, the retired hash split), the legacy flat 1950 kg only for unknown drive types; the constants are "curb + driver + kit", UNVERIFIED assumptions (`CONSTANT_PROVENANCE`). Two published families scale with it: the apparent kinetic-energy recovery of regeneration (KE loss = 0.5 m (v1^2 - v2^2); the displayed ratio scales as 1/m, the existing `_mass_uncertainty_bounds` multiplies the central value by reference mass / mass) and the descent energy budget and capture fraction (potential energy m g dh).
- Cloud seatbelt states (Trip_Information; code 2 = latched; the driver belt is latched on 100 % of moving rows, so the coding is validated) give a per-drive occupancy PATTERN for 586 of the 717 drives (modal state over the moving rows of the drive window). Owner-estimated seat weights (usual values, not measured): driver 82 to 85 kg on logged drives; front passenger 52 to 55 kg in 95 % or more of cases, otherwise 80 to 90 kg; rear left about 35 kg; rear centre 20 to 35 kg; rear right = child seat (8 kg) with a child of 18 to 19 kg and about 50 % real occupancy. A latched belt is not a weight (child seat or empty-seat buckling is not excluded).
- Probe (not a registered result): payload beyond the driver averages 71 kg on logged drives (bracket 60 to 105 kg, SD 39 kg = 2.0 % of 1900 kg), by drive class highway 110 kg (n = 20), mixed_highway 89, mixed 70, urban 68 (n = 504). The existing mass range (+-30 kg, +-1.6 %) is of the same size as the drive-to-drive spread of the payload.
- Which share of the class mass already holds a passenger allowance is unknown (no curb mass or mass in running order exists: the owner has no CoC / WLTP data), so the occupant-informed mass can only be defined relative to the class mass through an explicit allowance parameter.

## 2. Occupant-informed mass (definitions)
- Per drive i with a belt pattern: payload_i = sum over latched seats of the seat weight (passenger and rear seats; the driver is part of the class mass). Low / central / high from the owner ranges: passenger 52 / 55.1 / 90 kg (central = 0.95 x 53.5 + 0.05 x 85), rear left 35 / 35 / 35, rear centre 20 / 27.5 / 35, rear right 8 / 17.25 / 26.5 (seat plus child at 50 % occupancy, low = seat only, high = seat plus child).
- Allowance parameter A (kg of passenger load already contained in the class mass): grid A in {0, payload mean of the logged drives, the corpus 95th percentile} (S1: A = 0, the class mass holds the driver only; S2: A = the corpus mean payload, only the drive-to-drive deviation matters; S3: A = 95th percentile).
- Occupant-informed mass m_i(A) = m_class,i + payload_i - A. Drives without a belt pattern (no moving rows with all four belt readings) keep m_class,i and are listed (131 of 717).

## 3. Estimands
- **M0 per-drive table:** drive, class mass, payload low / central / high, pattern, availability reason; and the corpus summary of payload by drive class (n, mean, SD with a day-clustered interval).
- **M1 mass bias:** the aggregate relative mass difference sum(m_i(A) - m_class,i) / sum(m_class,i) for each A, with the low / central / high payload bracket and a day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval (calendar day of the drive start, seed 42, 4000 draws).
- **M2 effect on the mass-scaled estimates:** (a) the displayed apparent KE recovery bars (the stratified native v2 regeneration display) recomputed with per-drive mass multipliers k_i = m_i(A) / m_class,i applied to each drive's kinetic-energy sum before aggregation (route 1, preferred: the per-drive sufficient statistics of the regeneration cache, keyed by raw SHA-256, if they hold the per-bin kinetic-energy and electrical-energy sums; route 2 if they do not: the ratio-of-sums rescale with the per-class aggregate multipliers, stated as an approximation); (b) the descent capture fraction and descent energy budget (`_mountain_pattern`) rescaled the same way. Reported per displayed cell as the relative change versus the published value, beside the published mass range (the published value times reference mass / 1920 and / 1860 kg), for A = S1, S2, S3 and the payload bracket. A change is "outside the published mass range" when the recomputed value lies beyond that range.
- **M3 association check (exploratory, no verdict):** per-drive apparent KE recovery (ratio of sums over the drive's regeneration events) regressed on the central payload with the drive-class and temperature-band covariates the displayed bars already use; slope per 100 kg with a day-clustered interval, n drives and n days, and the minimum detectable slope (2.8 times the bootstrap SD). The prediction is a slope of about -1 / m per kg (about -5 % per 100 kg); if the minimum detectable slope is larger than that, the check is reported as "not informative at this support", never as agreement or disagreement.
- **M4 integrity:** counts of drives by pattern; belt-coverage by month; the 131 drives without a pattern compared with those with one by class and month (selection); control V0: every published mass-scaled figure the tool recomputes with k_i = 1 equals the published value (tool STOPS otherwise).

## 4. Decision rules (fixed now; judgement thresholds, Director to rule)
- A displayed mass-scaled cell is "sensitive" when its recomputed value under S2 (the pure drive-to-drive deviation) differs from the published value by more than the half-width of the published mass range (+-1.6 %), and "sensitive to the allowance" when the S1 value lies outside the published mass range while S2 does not. Otherwise "within the published mass range". Wording is per cell and per scenario; the central statement uses S2 and S1 is shown beside it.
- M3: slope reported with its interval and the minimum detectable slope; verdict only when the minimum detectable slope is below the predicted slope.
- Whatever the outcome, this milestone changes no displayed figure. If a cell is sensitive, the Director decides whether a labelled sensitivity (not a replacement) is added to the payload and dashboard in a later milestone.

## 5. Falsification and limits
- Falsified ("the published mass-scaled figures are insensitive to occupant load at the level of the published mass range"): a displayed cell whose S2 value lies outside the published mass range.
- Limits: seat weights are owner-estimated usual values, not measurements; a belt pattern is a proxy (empty buckled seats, child-seat occupancy at 50 %, luggage and fuel unobserved); the class mass allowance A is unknown, hence the grid; the pattern is the modal state of a drive, not the state at each regeneration event; 131 drives have no pattern; the unlogged drives (a different, lighter driver) are not part of the OBD-based figures; the regeneration display's own caveats (cadence strata, assumed mass) stay.

## 9. Rev 2: Director rulings on Rev 1 (override sections 1 to 8 where they differ)
9.1 **Allowance (class-aware).** The class masses already hold a load allowance (M124: highway 2000 kg versus urban / mixed 1890 kg because highway is assumed to carry more load; 1860 to 1920 kg were two load states), so one common A would double-count load on highway drives. Scenarios: **S2c (CENTRAL)**: A = the class-wise mean payload (highway, mixed_highway, mixed, urban / unknown each its own), m_i = m_class,i + payload_i - A_class, i.e. only the within-class deviation; **S1c**: a common base for every class, m_i = 1890 + payload_i, so highway's higher class mass is itself counted as load; **S2** pooled (A = the corpus mean payload) and **S3** (A = the corpus 95th percentile of the central payload) stay as brackets. A is recomputed inside each low / central / high payload bracket. S1 of Rev 1 (A = 0 on top of the class mass) is dropped as a scenario.
9.2 **Seat weights.** Rear-right high is 27 kg (8 + 19), central 17.25, low 8. Rear left is 30 / 35 / 40 kg (the owner's "about 35"). Front passenger 52 / 55.1 / 90 kg: the 90 kg is a worst-case bracket applied to every drive, not an interval for the mean, and is labelled so. Child seat: belt-fixed is assumed because the owner's 50 % occupancy statement only makes sense when the belt is latched with the seat installed; if the seat is ISOFIX, its 8 kg is present regardless of the belt (open question to the owner; the spec's reading is recorded, and the rear-right low bound then moves to 8 kg on all drives).
9.3 **Computation route.** Route 1 only: the regeneration cache stores mass-free per-interval dv2 with a drive index and `regen_v2_agg` applies KE_FACTOR x mass[drive] x dv2 at aggregation, so the per-drive mass array is swapped; route 2 is dropped. Charge is mass-free, so mass enters only the denominator. **V0** = every published regeneration cell recomputed with the class-mass array is byte-equal to the published `regenDisplayV2` cell, else STOP. The comparison threshold is each cell's OWN published mass range (it is weighted by the urban / mixed share per cell and is about zero for highway-heavy cells), not a flat +-1.6 %; the shift is also reported as a fraction of the cell's interval half-width. The SOC-standardized basis keeps the published KE-weighted fixed reference mix (owner decision c) with the re-weighted variant only as secondary; support counts and status are asserted unchanged (they are mass-free).
9.4 **Descent.** `captureFraction` is a row-wise median with a mass-dependent potential-energy mask (above 1 kW) and a clip, so 1/m is not exact: it is recomputed row-wise with the mask held at the published population, and the variant in which the mask moves is reported beside it. `netDropToSaturateM` is nearly mass-invariant (mass cancels in m x eta): reported, never flagged.
9.5 **M3 prediction (sign corrected).** The apparent ratio is charge / (m_class x dv2) and the true charge scales with the true mass, so the apparent ratio rises with the true load: predicted slope about +1/m, roughly +5 % relative per 100 kg of payload (pre-registered). Confounding is stated (occupancy goes with trip type, weekend and season), and the battery charge-power limit makes the expected slope smaller. The check stays exploratory with no verdict unless the minimum detectable slope is below the predicted one.
9.6 **Bias list.** Owner-estimated weights; the modal belt state when people board mid-drive; selection of the 131 drives without a pattern (by month and class, in M4); cloud-to-OBD drive matching (overlap reported); fuel mass (about 0 to 40 kg) unobserved and of the same order as the payload SD.
9.7 **Wording.** "occupant-informed assumed mass (owner-estimated seat weights, belt-state proxy)"; never measured mass, corrected, true recovery or validated mass; the class mass is not replaced. A later payload sensitivity needs a Director ruling; article use needs an Opus audit and the owner's sign-off. The driver's weight against the class-mass driver share is unknown and is stated as a limit.
