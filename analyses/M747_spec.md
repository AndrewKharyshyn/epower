## Pre-registered design
**Population.** Complete-case at-risk tables (CORE5 non-null, the M318/M320 selection) of ALL 717 canonical drives per side (start, stop), built by `tools/m318_run.py tables` from the raw files (read-only; F03 label), same final fit as M318 (`_final_fit`: cloglog GLM, five-variable duration-aware specification, the three interactions, day-clustered robust covariance, day key `_day_key`). Comparison population: the 606-drive ladder basis with the same code (separates "more data" from "knot placement").
**Knot sets for the pack-temperature term only** (all other terms, interactions and the duration handling unchanged; sets fixed here, none tuned):
- K0 default: 4 data-quantile knots at 5/35/65/95 % (reproduces the published fit on the 606 basis).
- K1: 5 data-quantile knots at 5/27.5/50/72.5/95 %.
- K2: 4 fixed knots at 12.5, 22.5, 32.5, 42.5 C (lower boundary knot at the candidate level).
- K3: 4 data-quantile knots at 2.5/34/66/97.5 % (boundary knots moved outward).
- K4: K0 plus a piecewise-linear cold hinge term max(0, 15 - tpack) (a slope change below 15 C allowed).
- K5: tpack linear, no spline (the non-flexible limit; reported, NOT part of the verdict because it cannot bend).
- K6: K0 plus a linear calendar-day term (days since the first drive) as a date-confounding sensitivity; reported, NOT part of the verdict, because pack temperature and date are collinear in the newest drives.
**Quantity.** D = the M320 Part A item 5 distinctiveness statistic (at-risk-weighted RMS over the SoC x speed cells supported at BOTH levels of eta_a - eta_b, eta the cloglog linear predictor, PRIMARY cell = median demand, 15 s duration, weights = pooled at-risk seconds of the cell in the two bands), computed by the unchanged functions of `tools/m320_ladder.py` (imported, not re-implemented). Pairs per side: primary (candidate 12.5 C, lowest carried final level of the side: start 32.5 C, stop 17.5 C); secondary (candidate vs the carried P50 reference of the side, and candidate vs 15.0 C). Secondary demand levels (P25, P90) and durations (2 s, 60 s) are diagnostics that gate nothing; a disagreeing secondary cell is reported as method disagreement.
**Interval.** Whole-pipeline-refit conditional bootstrap, calendar day = resampling unit; per-draw seeds from `numpy.random.SeedSequence(42).spawn(n)`; K0 4000 draws, K1-K6 2000 draws each (budget); data-quantile knots re-learned in every draw, fixed knots held; percentile interval and basic interval both reported; failed-draw rate per set and pair reported; a set with a failed-draw rate above 1 % has no trusted interval (reported "not estimable").
**Distinctness** exactly as M320: delta = log(1.25); a pair is DISTINCT when the percentile lower bound AND the basic lower bound are >= delta; BORDERLINE when only one is; otherwise INDISTINCT (the status strings of `tools/m320_ladder.py`).
**Verdict (per side, from K0-K4 only).** `robustly distinct` if the primary pair is distinct under K0-K4; `robustly indistinct` if it is indistinct under K0-K4; `knot-dependent` otherwise (including any BORDERLINE or non-estimable set). Added descriptors, never decisions: the spread of D across K0-K4 relative to the K0 interval width; the candidate's position relative to each set's boundary knots (inside the data-quantile range or in the linear tail); the hazard profile h(T) over 9-20 C per set with pointwise day-clustered bands; seconds- vs day-weighted candidate support; the number of event days of the candidate band (early-support flag below 20).
**What each verdict licenses.** robustly distinct: a Stage 2 spec may be drafted (an additional cold level, its display, its refit path), still behind the refit rule and the Director; robustly indistinct: the colder level adds no distinct surface and the W2 warning is closed as "no level warranted" for this corpus with an acknowledgement ruled by the Director; knot-dependent: no additional level, the model's behaviour in the tail below the boundary knot is not established, the monitor warning stays report-only with this spec's wording. In no case does Stage 1 change the carried ladder, the published surfaces, the payload or the dashboard.
**Reported with every output.** Counts (drives, days, events, at-risk seconds per band), F03 label, "associational, season/date confounded", the separation of the 606-basis and 717-corpus fits, and the model-derived label. No temperature or count literal typed anywhere.
