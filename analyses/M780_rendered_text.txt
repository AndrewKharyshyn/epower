Corpus Sensor-1 record — Jul20 D3, the single drive with the highest Sensor-1 sample in the corpus.

Panels share one °C scale (taken from the data, so values below 0 °C are drawn left of the dashed 0 °C line) so groups stay directly comparable. Ambient rows are 5 °C wide on the recorded ambient (the mean of the readings recorded for the drive) and every observed ambient range is shown; a drive recorded at exactly 5.0 °C is in the Cold cohort but in the 5 to <10 °C row. The regime tag on a row is the regime of the row's lower edge (thresholds of the thermal cohorts), not a classification of each drive. Rows with fewer than 5 drives draw no interquartile box. Extremes come from every drive with a recorded ambient, not from the canonical-clean set; these rows are not cohort counts.

Sensor 1 is the hottest of the four battery probes. Its range is 1-51°C across 717 drives and 11062.2 km (May 11 – Nov 20, 2026). The highest Sensor 1 reading is 51°C, on Jul20 D3. The highest pack mean on that drive is 46°C. Each is a maximum within the drive, and the two did not necessarily happen at the same moment. The mean of the four sensors settles at 35.7-37.4°C. The spread between sensors grows from 2.6°C at key-on to about 6.3°C once the pack is warm. Sensor 1 is always the hottest and Sensor 4 the coolest, so the ordering is stable and is not random scatter. Measured at the same moment, the gap between the hottest probe and the four-sensor mean is small: median about 1.2°C, up to about 6°C. This is a small, consistently ordered spread, not a large fixed difference across the pack. Two points affect how to read the ranges. (1) Ambient readings go back to the start of the study: 710 of 717 drives are in the ambient rows. The lowest value in the ambient rows is 1°C, which matches the lowest value in the whole data exactly, so the coldest packs are not hidden in the drive-class rows only. The remaining 7 drives are drives that do not enter the ambient rows, either because no ambient value could be read or because there is no Sensor 1 trajectory. The cause for each drive is not listed here. (2) No ordering of the median peak across the ambient rows is stated.
