# M559 spec Rev 1 (2026-11-01, before any code): orthogonal engine-context state, separate rotation / pack-flow / thermal / combustion-evidence fields (audit 2026-10-29 N03 and F09 follow-up)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT for Director review. Nothing implemented. Tier C (new analysis; additive; no existing classifier, payload key or status changes). Branch base: `main` at `1c7eb84` (M557 plus the N09 note). Ledger id `AUD29-N03`.

## 0. What exists
- Wording wave M547 already relabelled the fuelled-proxy / motoring wording (F09 wording part). What is missing is the DATA structure: the existing panels classify each engine sample into ONE state (`fuelled generation`, `motored-unfuelled` with `RPM_MIN 2000, BOOST_MOT -0.7 bar, LOAD_MAX 8 %`, M46/M48; `_dst_classify`, `_esm_classify`, `_isc_labels`, low-speed dissipation, `engineStartContext`), so rotation, pack flow, thermal context and combustion evidence are fused in one label, and a purpose (generation, dissipation, warm-up) is implied that no channel proves.
- Logged channels: engine RPM (proves rotation), calculated boost and calculated load and the app-calculated fuel rate / counter (proxies; no injector or fuel-cut signal), HV current and voltage, coolant and oil temperature, SoC, speed, ambient (driver-recorded), M556 `signalValidity` (which channels are valid), M557 `channelDependency` (the fuel channels are app-calculated).
- Audit text (N03): separate rotation state, net pack-flow state, coolant context and combustion-evidence class; allow warm-up plus charging and heating plus traction; acceptance: no high-SoC / no-charge shortcut to dissipation; threshold sensitivity and an unknown-purpose output retained. Audit F09: store the four as separate fields; an engine rotating at high SoC with neutral or discharging pack current stays purpose-unresolved or a thermal-context candidate unless independent signals justify a stronger label.

## 1. Question and non-question
How are engine-rotating 1 Hz samples distributed over four orthogonal fields, and how large is the purpose-unresolved share? Non-question: no purpose is assigned to any sample; no change of the existing dissipation, motoring or start-context results; no fuel-cut claim; no energy attribution.

## 2. Fields (per 1 Hz grid sample, engine-rotating = rpm > 400, the engine-on threshold of the engine-on registry)
- `rot`: `off` (rpm <= 400) / `rotating`; sub-class `low_rpm` (400-1000), `mid` (1000-2000), `high` (>= 2000).
- `flow` (net pack current, discharge positive, deadband `FLOW_DB` = 2 A pre-registered; sensitivity 1 and 5 A): `charging` (I < -FLOW_DB), `neutral` (|I| <= FLOW_DB), `discharging` (I > FLOW_DB).
- `thermal` (coolant): `cold` (<= 60 C, the existing `_ISC_COOL_COLD`), `warming` (60-80 C), `warm` (> 80 C); `unknown` where the coolant channel is stale (M556 validity) or not polled within 30 s.
- `comb` (combustion evidence, proxy only): `fuel_proxy_supported` (calculated fuel rate above its resolution floor AND calculated load above LOAD_MAX), `no_fuel_proxy` (calculated fuel rate at/below the floor AND boost <= BOOST_MOT AND load <= LOAD_MAX: the existing motored-unfuelled proxy), `ambiguous` (otherwise), `no_signal` (a needed channel invalid). Language: proxy; "fuel cut" is never stated.
- `purpose`: always `unresolved` unless one of the pre-registered, explicitly independent rules fires: `generation_candidate` (comb = fuel_proxy_supported AND flow = charging), `traction_support_candidate` (comb = fuel_proxy_supported AND flow = discharging AND speed > 5 km/h), `thermal_context_candidate` (thermal in {cold, warming} AND rot = rotating, never exclusive: a sample may carry several), `dissipation_candidate` (comb = no_fuel_proxy AND rot = rotating AND the existing downhill / high-SoC gating of the legacy classifier is NOT used here). A sample may carry more than one candidate (mixed functions allowed); none of these is "the purpose".
- Cadence: the fields are computed per cadence stratum (fast / slow / unknown); the strata are never pooled; coolant staleness from the slow multiplex group is explicit.

## 3. Estimands and statistics
- Per stratum: seconds and share of engine-rotating time in each (flow, thermal, comb) cell and in each candidate label; the unresolved share; day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws) on shares, n drives, n days. The 4 x 3 x 3 cross-table is stored with cell supports; cells below the common support floor (n >= 200 samples on >= 10 drives on >= 5 days) are shown as `unsupported`, not dropped.
- Threshold sensitivity (stored, non-gating): FLOW_DB 1 / 2 / 5 A; engine-on 400 / 600 rpm; coolant cut 55 / 60 / 65 C; fuel-rate floor at its observed quantisation. Report the largest change of any share.
- Mixed-function table: share of rotating time with 0, 1, 2, >= 3 candidate labels.
- Acceptance probe (the audit F09 sentence): among rotating samples with SoC >= 80 % and flow in {neutral, discharging}, the share labelled `dissipation_candidate`, and the share left `unresolved` (reported either way; no label stronger than a candidate).

## 4. Known answers (pre-registered)
- KA1 synthetic frames: each field value from hand-built samples (rpm, current, coolant, fuel, load, boost); the boundaries (400 rpm, +-2 A, 60 / 80 C) behave as defined; a sample with rpm 0 has `rot=off` and no candidate.
- KA2 orthogonality: for synthetic samples every combination of (flow, thermal, comb) is representable (a charging, cold, fuel_proxy_supported sample carries generation_candidate and thermal_context_candidate together); no field is a function of another.
- KA3 high-SoC probe: a synthetic rotating sample at SoC 90 %, I = 0, no fuel proxy gets `dissipation_candidate`, never a purpose; the same sample with fuel_proxy_supported gets `unresolved`.
- KA4 (real, hard gate) the sum of the cell seconds equals the engine-rotating seconds of the master-eligible drives per stratum; the `no_fuel_proxy` seconds equal the legacy motored-unfuelled seconds on the same drives within the declared tolerance (reconciliation to the existing M46/M48 census, tested not retyped).
- KA5 the existing payload blocks are byte-identical before and after (deep diff equals the additive block).
- KA6 a stale coolant sample is `unknown`, never `warm`.

Where this section and sections 1-7 differ, this section governs.
- R1 (Q1). `traction_support_candidate` is renamed: in e-POWER every fuelled rotating sample is generation, flow only says where the net power goes. Labels: `generation_net_charge` (fuel proxy supported AND flow charging), `generation_with_pack_discharge` (fuel proxy supported AND flow discharging AND speed > 5 km/h). `unresolved` is reported twice: `unresolvedNoCandidate` (no candidate at all) and `unresolvedExceptThermal` (no candidate other than thermal_context); thermal_context fires on every cold or warming rotating sample and lowers the first by construction.
- R2 (Q2). Thresholds stay pre-registered, not fitted. Fuel floor: per-second mean > 0 (sensitivity 0.05 and 0.10 L/h; 0.05 L/h is the logged grid step in `signal_representation.json`). FLOW_DB tied to the current-offset estimate (`i_off`), sensitivity 1 / 2 / 5 A. Coolant upper cut 80 C is a display band, not a thermostat value; sensitivity 75 / 80 / 85 C, lower cut 55 / 60 / 65 C.
- R3 (Q3, KA4). `comb` keeps the legacy boost classes (motored: boost < -0.70; fuelled: boost >= -0.55; in between ambiguous) and adds fuel rate as a SUB-FLAG (fuel and load are not independent evidence: the fuel rate is app-calculated from airflow, M557). The reference function is M46 `_dissipation_census` (operators `>` and `<`; grid, rpm > 800 base, rpm > 2000, boost < -0.70, load.fillna(0) < 8, SoC not missing); `mountain_pattern` uses `>=` / `<=` and is NOT the reference (samples at exactly -0.70 or 7.84 % change class; boost is on a 0.01 bar grid). KA4 becomes an exact decomposition: the legacy motored-unfuelled seconds equal the sum of the new motored cells over the same drives with 0 s residual, and the legacy seconds are broken down by the new `comb` levels. The Rev 1 statement "no_fuel_proxy is the existing motored-unfuelled proxy" is withdrawn.
- R4 (coolant). Coolant column `Температура охолодної рідини` (never an HV coolant column); no oil-temperature channel exists, oil is dropped. M556 has no coolant entry: staleness = time since the last NON-NULL raw sample, valid <= 30 s (29 s valid, 31 s unknown); the 1 s grid ffill(limit=3) is not used for this field. Escalate if the unknown share exceeds 50 % in a stratum.
- R5 (no_signal, strata, eligibility). `no_signal` for fuel channels constant at 0 over the whole file, drives missing boost or load (early logs), and files without the needed resolution data. Cadence strata exactly as the existing rule (`ev_rpm_dt_med_s` < 1.0 s fast, slow, unlabelled). Master-eligible = `ens_outlier_v2` clean plus the channel coverage above. Engine start and stop spin-up windows (within 3 s of an `_engine_start_scan` event) get a separate flag and are excluded from `dissipation_candidate`.
- R6 (Q4, A1). The blind audit uses at least one window per non-empty cell per stratum, plus windows next to each boundary (400 rpm, deadband, 60 / 80 C, -0.70 bar, 7.84 % load, 29 / 31 s coolant age), stale coolant, no_signal and a fuel-all-zero file: about 60 windows, seed 42, drawn and committed before code. The auditor is given the grid rule (1 s mean, ffill limit) in the definitions.
- R7 (extra KAs). Sign check (braking, rpm 0, falling speed gives I < 0, charging); boost exactly -0.70 and load exactly 7.84 % classed under the chosen operators; a fuel channel constant at 0 gives no_signal; coolant age 29 s valid, 31 s unknown; KA4 residual exactly 0 s.
- R8 (Q5). The legacy panels stay untouched (KA5); a pointer is a later wording follow-up. Extra escalation triggers: any non-zero KA4 residual, any audit disagreement, a failed current-sign check, coolant unknown share above 50 % in a stratum, a sensitivity run moving a share by more than 5 pp, a candidate requested as exclusive or as an article figure.
