# M442 spec (Rev 1) - regeneration programme, stage MC-H: apparent KE recovery at a common sampling resolution (harmonized extractor) and the gates H1-H3 that replace B6

Status: PRE-REGISTERED, written before any even-day or factor-3 output of this stage exists (see section 6, safeguards). Rev 1, 2026-10-15. Owner: Andrii. Origin: the failed cadence-invariance gate B6 of M441 (analyses/M441_spec.md section 11, Director ruling Rev 2.2: stage ME blocked until a Rev 3 rule is pre-registered and a re-run passes) and the Director's design ruling of 2026-10-15 on the exploratory evidence of analyses/M442_director_brief.json. `main` = M441 merged (tag M441). `drive_master.csv` MD5 as at the merge. Scope: the harmonized reduction, its cache variants, the aggregation as additive payload keys, the gates H1-H3, the sensitivities. NOT in scope: any dashboard binding (stage ME), the SOC-standardized view (stage MD), the BMS charge-limit channel (MF).

## 1. Why (evidence, exploratory)

M441 B6 failed (14 of 21 supported decision cells; full minus half-rate about +1 pp). Exploration (development days = odd day-of-month, 87 fast drives; scripts `analyses/M442_explore/`, labelled exploratory, not counted toward any gate): the interval/plateau rule is not the cause (strict-only invariant, kept +0.98 pp, kept plus trimmed +1.90 pp; jitter-tolerant episodes refuted, ratio inflates; zig-zag braking events leave +1.8 pp). Channel isolation: halving only current and voltage sampling lowers the integrated charge by 5 % (-3.4 pp on the ratio) over identical intervals, halving only speed raises the ratio by +1.6 pp, halving RPM does nothing; a signed (non-rectified) energy is also not invariant (-6 %, both phases equal). The dependence on cadence comes from how finely the I/V charge integral and the speed series are resolved; no extractor rule can remove it. Cadence in the corpus has two clusters (about 30 % of drives at 0.8-0.9 s, the rest at 1.2-1.7 s) and the fast share of KE per temperature bin runs from 46 % to 3 %: cadence is confounded with temperature. Bins can only be compared at a common effective resolution; the harmonized level depends on that resolution and is below the native fast-poll level; the continuous-time recovery is not identified.

## 2. Design (Director's ruling, verbatim in substance)

**Primary estimand: apparent KE recovery at common sampling resolution (3 s grid, assumed mass).** Every channel of every drive (speed, current, voltage, RPM, SoC, T1-T4) is reduced to a common grid of spacing Delta = 3.0 s: at `t_j = t0 + j * Delta` (seconds from the drive's first sample) keep the latest native sample in `(t_j - Delta, t_j]`, at its native time; an empty cell gives no sample. The M441 interval extractor (tools/regen_v2.py, policy unchanged) then runs unchanged on the reduced channels. Phases `t0 = 0, 0.75, 1.5, 2.25 s` (four grid phases); the estimate is the ratio of the phase-averaged sums (each phase weighs 1/4; counts reported as the per-phase average, rounded). Support counts (n, drives, days) use the per-phase average n. A drive is OUT OF SUPPORT of the harmonized estimator if the 90 % quantile of its native speed, I or V sampling interval exceeds Delta; these drives are excluded, counted, and their share of native KE is reported; more than 10 % of the KE out of support goes to the Director.

**Sensitivities (payload only, frozen before any even-day run):** native v2 (the P2 resolution bracket); P3 boxcar: the power `P(t) = I(t) V(t) / 1000` is replaced by its centred moving average over W = 3 s (computed on a uniform 20 Hz grid of the drive) before the positive part is integrated, applied to native-rate data; Delta = 2.0 s and Delta = 4.0 s (phases Delta/4 apart); the cadence regime (master `ev_rpm_dt_med_s` < 1.0 s fast, otherwise slow) as a stratum. Language: "apparent KE recovery at common sampling resolution (3 s grid, assumed mass)"; resolution-conditional; never measured, corrected, true or cadence-free.

## 3. Gates (replace B6 for the harmonized estimand)

- **H1 (the gate).** Fast drives (master `ev_rpm_dt_med_s` < 1.0), cohort all, EVEN calendar days of the month only. Pre-decimate the native samples per channel as in M441 R1 by factor 2 (two phases) and by factor 3 (three phases), then apply the harmonized reduction (four grid phases) to each. Test statistic per cell: the paired difference `harmonized(native) - harmonized(decimated)` (decimated = ratio of sums over its decimation phases x grid phases), with a day-clustered 95 % percentile interval (4000 draws, seed 42, the same resamples for both). A cell FAILS if the interval excludes 0 AND |difference| exceeds the half-width of the cell's assumed-mass range. Views: temperature all-SOC, speed all-SOC (instantaneous speed bins), SOC-band totals (the eight bands). Only cells that are `supported` in the even-day fast subset are tested. PASS needs: both factors clean (no failing cell) and every view total supported. If fewer than half of the cells of a view are supported the result is INCONCLUSIVE and blocks (inconclusive is not a pass).
- **H2.** The M441 B6 form (all fast-drive days, decimation factor 2 only) must pass for the harmonized estimand as well.
- **H3 (reported; a failure escalates to the Director).** Fast versus slow drives, same cell, paired by calendar day (days with both kinds of drive): the paired difference of the harmonized ratio with its day-clustered interval, per supported cell of the temperature and speed views.
- **On failure:** no display binds to v2, no re-tuning of Delta or any other constant, and any new design needs a new spec and drives logged after the freeze. After a pass: the Rev 2.2 bootstrap-CI audit (one bootstrap interval reproduced by the blind auditor) and a Director ruling; Andrii's sign-off is needed at ME (the displayed estimand departs from owner decision (a) and its level is lower than the native v2).

## 4. Implementation (declared)

- `tools/regen_v2.py`: parameters `harmonize=(Delta, phase)` (applied per channel after the optional `decimate`), `boxcar_s`; defaults leave every M441 number byte-identical (KATs). Per-channel native-interval quantiles (90 %) recorded in the cache header. Extractor version `regen-v2.2`.
- `tools/regen_v2_cache.py`: variant ids `h3p0..h3p3`, `h2p0..3`, `h4p0..3`, `box3`, and for the gate `d2p{0,1}h3p{0..3}`, `d3p{0,1,2}h3p{0..3}`.
- `tools/regen_v2_agg.py`: stacked phase tables with a row weight; payload keys `regenCaptureV2H` (cells like `regenCaptureV2`, refreshed at ingestion by the stage `regen_v2`) and `regenCaptureV2HStudy` (sensitivities, H1-H3; on demand, carried forward).
- `tools/m442_gate.py`: `--dev` (odd days only, development) and `--confirm` (even days and all fast days, run ONCE after the spec commit and the recorded hash; refuses to run if the CHANGELOG does not carry the spec hash).
- Tests (`tests/synthetic/test_regen_v2h.py`): the grid rule (latest native sample in `(t_j - Delta, t_j]`, empty cell, phases), unchanged defaults, a synthetic current ripple sampled at 0.8 s and 1.5 s whose native ratios differ more than the harmonized ones, the boxcar rule, support exclusion by the 90 % quantile.

## 5. Limits (disclosed in advance)

Fast drives only: H1 does not test drives that are natively slow (H3 compares fast with slow drives on the same days); Delta = 3 s smooths out sub-3 s charge flicker and lowers the level below the native fast-poll value; temperature and SOC remain confounded with speed, demand, day and cadence regime; no multiplicity control over cells; single vehicle, one driver, assumed masses, the 300 rpm proxy.

## 6. Safeguards (exploration then confirmation)

The spec is committed and its SHA-256 recorded in the CHANGELOG entry before any even-day output exists. Nobody, MD included, looks at even-day or factor-3 outputs before H1; odd-day results do not count toward the gate. Stage MD may run in parallel, payload only, on native-rate v2 with the cadence stratum and the strict-only and kept-plus-trimmed sensitivities; no harmonized or decimated even-day output is used before H1; no display, no effect claims.

## 8. Rev 1.2 (2026-10-15): Director pre-freeze ruling after the development-day evidence (odd days; `analyses/M442_dev.json`, `analyses/M442_explore/designs_dev.json`)

D1 Deviation: in the odd-day runs no tested reduction (last3, interp3, box3) was invariant to x2 / x3 decimation within the assumed-mass half-width (the 0-10 km/h bin failed in all of them; its half-width is 0.16 pp). H1 invariance and condition (2) of the M441 Rev 2.2 ruling no longer decide pass or fail; H1 and H2 are still computed in their Rev 1.1 form with the primary reduction and REPORTED, and do not block. D2 Primary reduction = interp3: speed, current and voltage are linearly interpolated to the grid nodes `t_j` (a node needs native samples on both sides with a bracketing gap of at most 5 s); every other channel takes the latest sample in `(t_j - 3 s, t_j]`; four phases; C2-C10 unchanged. last3 (the Rev 1 rule), box3 and the native v2 are payload sensitivities. D3 Resolution bracket `b(cell) = max over f in {2, 3} of |harm(native) - harm(decimated by f)|` (percentage points, ratio of the phase-averaged sums, point values) on ALL fast days; a cell that is not supported for the fast drives takes the largest `b` among the supported cells of its view. `b` is reported with every harmonized level and does not decide pass or fail. D4 `--confirm` (even days, run once) evaluates: G1 - at every view total (temperature, speed, SOC bands) and both factors, `|harmonized(native) - harmonized(decimated f)|` is smaller than `|native v2 - native v2 decimated f|` OR at most the assumed-mass half-width of that total; G2 - H3 (fast versus slow, paired by day, even days) with tolerance `b + mass half-width` per supported cell; G3 - C10 (out-of-support KE at most 10 %). A failure blocks stage ME; a G2 failure goes to the Director. D5 What stage ME may show (after the bootstrap-CI audit, the Director ruling and Andrii's sign-off): the all-day harmonized levels for the temperature bins, the SOC bands and the speed bins at or above 10 km/h, each with its day-clustered interval, its assumed-mass range and `b`; 0-10 km/h stays in the payload only. A contrast between ADJACENT supported bins A and B is labelled "resolved" only if (i) its even-day 95 % interval excludes 0, (ii) it exceeds `b(A) + b(B)` in absolute value, (iii) it has the same sign at both ends of the assumed-mass range, and (iv) it has the same sign on the even days, the odd days and all days (implementer's reading of "on all days"; flagged for the audit); otherwise it is labelled "not resolved at this resolution". Contrasts are unadjusted for multiplicity (stated). Language: apparent KE recovery at common sampling resolution (3 s grid, assumed mass); never invariant, cadence-free, measured, corrected or true. Limits recorded: the design was picked on odd days, so even days confirm only; `b` comes from fast drives (about 30 % of drives) and cold bins borrow `b` from their view; temperature and SOC remain confounded with speed, demand, day and cadence regime; on a synthetic rippled current no reduction reduced the cadence dependence. Follow-ups: the legacy bars disclose their cadence dependence until replaced; the blind audit reproduces one `b` and one contrast interval; the payload block `regenCaptureV2H` is produced only after `--confirm` (C9: no even-day harmonized aggregate exists before it). Andrii signs before ME binds (not needed for the freeze or for `--confirm`).
