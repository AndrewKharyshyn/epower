## 1. Estimand and nature of the block
Descriptive display of values already computed, drive by drive: for each eligible drive, the x value is the terminal-energy excursion of the net trace (kWh, upper bracket = sum of segment excursions; lower bracket = the largest single segment, so a gap-bounded drive is a horizontal RANGE [lower, upper]), the y value is the gross throughput of the same drive (kWh, `gross_throughput_kwh`). The 1:1 line marks gross = net excursion, the lower limit of the ratio. Nothing new is estimated: no new estimand, no new interval, no new verdict. The summary statement stays the existing R row (`throughputOverExcursion`), and the scatter is the picture of the same population. Words: "gross energy moved through the terminals" and "net swing of the integrated trace"; never capacity, usable window, depth of discharge, state of health or energy stored. The kWh is a reconstructed terminal I x V integral (estimated), not measured.

## 2. Population and eligibility
Exactly the `windowUse` population (canonical-clean `ens_outlier_v2` false, no `energy_null_reason`, sign check not ANOMALY, reported SoC and gross throughput present, pair stream usable), per logger-cadence stratum. No new exclusion, no tightening. Drives with zero upper excursion are listed in the count `excludedZeroExcursion` (R undefined) and are not drawn on the log axes (their number is printed).

## 3. Method
- Extend `tools/window_use.py` (the only writer of `S.windowUse`) with a per-drive table written into the same block as `strata.<stratum>.perDrive`: parallel arrays, rounded to 4 decimals, in master row order: `kwhUpper`, `kwhLower`, `gross`, `durationS`, `gapShare`, and a day index (dense integer per calendar day, with a `days` list of dates), so the page can draw the points and tell a day-clustered view from a drive count. No file names or timestamps beyond the date. Additive splice (allow-list `/windowUse`, stamp), nothing else in the payload changes (isolation diff).
- No new bootstrap. The display median of R and its day-clustered interval are the existing, unchanged values; the page binds them, never recomputes them.
- Reproduction check (known answer, STOP on a miss): from the published per-drive arrays, the median of gross / kwhUpper over drives with kwhUpper > 0 reproduces `throughputOverExcursion.median` to 3 decimals, the count of rows equals `nDrives`, the count of rows with kwhUpper = 0 equals `excludedZeroExcursion`, and the 1:1 condition holds (kwhLower <= kwhUpper <= gross + 1e-4, the existing tolerance) for every row.
- Reconciliation with the master: every row's gross equals `gross_throughput_kwh` of its drive; the row count by stratum equals the stratum `nDrives`.

## 4. Display (implemented only after the Director decision and the blind audit)
- In `WindowUseView` (Records tab), under the existing percentile charts and above "How we calculated it": a scatter (x = net swing of the integrated trace, kWh, y = gross energy moved, kWh; log axes, extent from the data), one dot per drive, a drawn 1:1 line labelled "gross equals net swing", the stratum switch already on the view applies; a drive with a gap-bounded range is a thin horizontal line from lower to upper bracket only when lower < 0.8 x upper (otherwise a dot, to keep it readable); the median R line (from the payload) as a second reference line y = R x x is drawn with its interval as a band.
- Group-or-hover rule: 701 dots in two panels are readable without clustering; a hover title per dot gives date, gross, swing and the bracket. The count of drives drawn and not drawn (zero swing) is printed under the chart (structural test).
- Copy: a bound lead sentence from the payload (R median with its interval and n drives / days); the strata are not comparable and are never pooled in one panel (two panels or the switch, never one point cloud); no word "typical", "efficiency", "capacity". The assumed-window shares stay under their toggle, untouched.
