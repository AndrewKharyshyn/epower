## 0. What is allowed and what is not
- Allowed (M762 Director wording): "FABDEM v1.2 step grade: adequate for corpus-level grade-class SHARES (2 pp classes) at cloud-step scale", with the limits: inter-product DEM error only, assumption-conditional, common-mode not identified, not per-step labels (the class of a step differs between FABDEM and NASADEM on about 16 % and AW3D30 on about 15 % of steps; the central-class share ranges across sources), the NASADEM mask removed water only, FABDEM's own removals are unmasked, AW3D30 has no mask and its licence is "not provided" (internal use only), the M757 placebo failure stands, never "validated", "measured" or "proven".
- NOT part of this milestone and still locked: regeneration, apparent KE recovery, fuel or traction energy BY grade class (M757 F3 regeneration share, M762 8.8 and R4). The grade coefficient b and 2SLS are not displayed. The shares are a description of where the covered driving takes place, never an energy statement.

## 1. Estimands (pre-registered)
Classes by FABDEM step grade (100 x height difference over the OBD path distance of the step), edges -3, -1, 1, 3 pp: below -3, -3 to -1, -1 to 1, 1 to 3, above 3.
- **E1** share of the COVERED driving by class: km-weighted (primary) and step-count shares, on the frozen M762 primary common-support step set (11,088 steps, 134 days, 6,373 km at the registered master; recomputed, not taken from this spec), with day-clustered percentile bootstrap intervals (seed 42, 4000 draws) and n (steps, drives, days, km) per class.
- **E2** the same shares per thermal cohort (cold, shoulder, warm; the thermal_regime labels of `seasonal_drive_master.csv`), each with its own support floor (30 days, 100 km, 1,000 steps as M762 8.1 R2); a cohort below the floor is shown as counts only.
- **E3** source spread: the class shares recomputed with NASADEM, AW3D30 and GLO-30 heights on the same steps; the displayed spread is the min and max across the four sources per class (a range, not an interval), shown beside the FABDEM shares.
- **E4** coverage and selection: the covered share of the logged km (covered km over the logged km of the same drives and over the whole corpus), by month and by drive-length stratum; the composition of the covered versus the uncovered drives by cohort (descriptive). The shares describe the covered driving, never all driving.

## 2. Tool, payload, dashboard
- `tools/dem_grade_shares.py` (optional stage, offline: needs the DEM folders, the cloud store, the raw files; pinned master MD5 and the M762 manifest): imports the M762 collection (`tools/dem_crosscheck.py` `collect_multi`, `build_surfaces`) so the step set is the frozen one; control R1: the FABDEM step shares equal the M762 C6 shares and the step stages equal the M762 `C1_step_stages` (tolerance 0) or the run stops (exit 3). Writes `analyses/M767_grade_class_shares.json` (aggregates only: no coordinates, tracks, per-drive geometry or dates).
- Payload: a CARRIED block `gradeClassShares` in `summary_arrays.json`, built by script from the registered result (pinned sha256), isolation-diffed, with `basis` (registered master, 717 drives, M762 manifest, "not refreshed by ingestion"), `staleness` (carried, corpusDrives 717), the shares with intervals and n, the cohort rows, the source spread, the coverage rows and the Director wording strings. A new ingest stage and a stamp declaration (as for `heatSoakGapSensitivity`); `release_check` requires the block, its label and leaf equality with the registered result.
- Dashboard: one chart "Grade-class shares of the covered driving (estimated, cloud-step scale)" as horizontal bars with day-clustered intervals (the existing interval chart component) and a table with the source spread, placed beside the existing mountain-pattern block (the Director rules the placement); caption with the limits above and the coverage line. Every displayed number binds to `S.gradeClassShares.*`; the wording comes from the payload. Language gate: a new rule forbids "terrain profile", "route is X % uphill", "measured grade", "true grade", any energy, regeneration, fuel or traction wording inside the chart block, and "validated".

## 3. Decision rules (frozen)
- R1 (control): the FABDEM step shares and the step stages reproduce the M762 result leaf by leaf; otherwise stop.
- R2 (support): a class or cohort cell with fewer than 20 steps or fewer than 5 days is shown as counts only (no interval); a cohort below the M762 floors is counts only.
- R3 (display): the only claim is the shares of the covered driving with their intervals and the source spread; the spread is stated as a bracket of four products, not an error bar; the misclassification (about 15 % of steps change class) is stated in the caption; no per-step or per-route statement; no energy statement.
- R4 (consistency): the payload and the page equal the registered result leaf by leaf (tolerance 0 before the frozen display rounding, percent two decimals half up on the decimal repr); the page states the carried basis. A later ingestion does not refresh the block; a refresh needs the cloud-refresh stage (M746, not built).
- R5 (falsification): if any class share differs between FABDEM and another source by more than the larger of 5 pp and 25 % of the FABDEM share, the cell is flagged "source-dependent" on the page and the Director is told before display; if the covered share of the logged km is below 50 % the chart carries a "partial coverage" label above the bars.
