# M495 spec (Rev 1): rolling-origin evaluation of the M119-v2 hazard pipeline on the current 606-drive corpus, and a frozen-fit protocol for its prospective evaluation

Status: Rev 1, written 2026-10-23 BEFORE any M495 computation. Origin: external audit Update 7, page 'Hazard models and simulation claims': the stored out-of-time evaluation (M428) is the 578-drive snapshot of a frozen earlier design, 'not a prospective test of a fit trained on all 606 drives'; the audit asks for strict chronological folds with saved transformations, event detector and model coefficients, scored for calibration, discrimination and baseline skill, and for rolling-origin evaluation on already available dates 'to test the pipeline design'; 'a future held-out period is needed to evaluate the current full-data fit unchanged'. Additive and reporting-only: no hazard model, surface, detector or displayed figure changes. Needs: this spec, a Director ruling on the questions below, a blind audit, a Director decision on the result. Related, not replaced: M428 (single-origin out-of-time evaluation of the frozen 489-drive design, 578-drive snapshot) and M222.1 (`socHysteresisV2.sensitivity.rollingOrigin`: forward-chaining CV of the selected spec, 12 rounds of 5 validation days from 40 training days, pooled log-loss / Brier / PR-AUC / calibration only, carried at its older basis, no baseline skill, no per-origin decision).

## Question
(A) Does the hazard pipeline (the declared fiveVarDur design, spline knots and scale learned on the training rows only, cloglog GLM with day-clustered inference, START and STOP separately) keep its skill over the constant-rate and socOnly baselines, and its calibration, at successive forecast origins on the dates already available, when it is trained strictly on the past?
(B) What is the exact, reproducible fit whose predictions a future held-out period can test, and when does that period exist?

## Part A. Rolling-origin evaluation (retrospective, pipeline design)
### Population and data (frozen)
`drive_master.csv` (606 rows, MD5 5556a0c6...), at-risk tables from the unchanged `m119v2_model._corpus_tables` on the staged `raw_only/` (raw = sha256-verified originals, M366; every raw file checked against `raw_manifest.json` with the M427 `replay_master.verify_raw_identity` contract before the pass); eligibility = complete case on CORE5 exactly as `_final_fit`; M231 detector and boundary rules unchanged (detector version recorded). Events are detector-derived, hazards model-derived.
### Folds (frozen)
Expanding window, cut on CALENDAR-DAY boundaries (a day never sits on both sides; M428 split mid-day by drive, this design does not): origin c_k = first date + 56 days + 28 k days (k = 0, 1, 2, ...); training = all rows with date <= c_k - 2 days (a one-day embargo, c_k - 1, is dropped from both sides); test = rows with c_k <= date < c_k + 28 days; the last window ends at the last date and is kept if it spans at least 14 calendar days, else merged into the previous test window. (Expected on the current corpus: four origins, test windows of about Jul 6 - Aug 2, Aug 3 - Aug 30, Aug 31 - Sep 27 and Sep 28 - Oct 17; the exact dates are written by the tool, never typed.) A fold is evaluable per side if its test window holds at least 100 events and at least 10 calendar days; otherwise it is reported with counts only.
### Method per fold and per side (no refit on later data)
1. Declared design fit on the training rows only (`fit_frozen` of `tools/m428_oot.py`: `DesignSpec(['soc','speed','tpack','demand','logdur'], INTERACTIONS).fit(train)` then `fit_glm(..., groups=day, link='cloglog')`); knots and scale learned on training rows only.
2. Baselines fitted on the training rows only: B0 constant (training event rate), B1 socOnly.
3. Predict every test at-risk second with the training-fitted model (`_predict`; restricted cubic splines linear beyond the boundary knots).
4. Metrics on the test rows (the `tools/m428_oot.Pieces` engine, unchanged): prevalence, log-loss, Brier, SKILL vs B0 and vs B1 (1 - LL_model / LL_baseline), O/E (observed events / sum of predicted probabilities), calibration slope and intercept, PR-AUC and lift, O/E by pack-temperature band (< 10, 10-20, 20-30, 30-40, >= 40 C), the share of at-risk seconds outside the training [P1, P99] per predictor and the excess over the training rows' own share, and the metrics on the in-support seconds (all five predictors inside the training [P1, P99]) versus the remainder (interpolation versus extrapolation).
5. Fidelity (hard stops): (a) the engine run with the M428 split (first 489 master rows against rows 490-578 of the 578-drive snapshot) reproduces `analyses/M428_result.json` primary point values (SKILL vs B0, O/E, prevalence) to 1e-6; (b) training at-risk seconds and events per fold reconcile with the tables.
### Uncertainty
Calendar day is the cluster. Per fold: day-clustered percentile bootstrap over the test days (seed 42, 4,000 draws, every statistic recomputed per draw) with the leave-one-day-out range and the effective number of day clusters (Kish). Pooled over folds: the test rows of all evaluable folds, day-clustered bootstrap over all test days. Intervals are conditional on each fold's fitted model (training uncertainty is not propagated). With 14-28 test days per fold the intervals are indicative.
### Decision rules (the M428 rules, planning thresholds, declared not validated; per fold and per side)
D1 skill: PASS if the lower bound of the 95 % interval of SKILL vs B0 is > 0. D2 calibration: 'within' if the O/E interval lies wholly inside [0.80, 1.25]; 'MATERIAL' if wholly outside; 'indeterminate' otherwise. D3 extrapolation: MATERIAL if, for any single predictor, the test out-of-support excess over the training share exceeds 10 percentage points.
Across origins (frozen): `stable across origins (planning thresholds; not equivalence)` iff every evaluable fold has D1 PASS, D2 not MATERIAL and D3 not MATERIAL; `degradation detected at origin(s) k...` if any fold has D1 FAIL or D2 MATERIAL; D3 MATERIAL folds are listed as `extrapolation`, not as degradation; `not assessable` for a side with no evaluable fold. Reported (no decision role): the trend of SKILL vs B0 and O/E across origins as a table, the pooled estimates with their intervals, and the comparison of the last fold with M428.
### Sensitivities (reported, none selected)
S1 embargo 0 and 3 days; S2 test windows of 14 and 42 days (same first cut); S3 per-drive skill (median over drives with at least 3 events, share with skill > 0); S4 SKILL vs the later-period constant (separates discrimination from a prevalence shift).

## Part B. Frozen-fit protocol for the prospective evaluation (freeze now, evaluate later)
1. Freeze: the 606-drive fit of the declared fiveVarDur design (START and STOP) is reproduced by the same code path as `_final_fit`, and the exact model is saved as `analyses/M495_frozen_fit.json`: the design definition, spline knots and scale, coefficient vector and covariance (day-clustered), link, the detector id and version, the at-risk table schema, the training master MD5, the n drives / days / events, the script and module sha256, and the SHA-256 of the artifact is recorded in the CHANGELOG entry. Fidelity: the reproduced fit must reproduce the stored `socHysteresisV2` event counts (`nStartAtRisk`, `nStartEvents`, `nStopAtRisk`, `nStopEvents`) within 1 % and its surfaces (`socHysteresisV2.surfaces`, `partialDependence`) within the declared tolerances of M428 section 3.3; otherwise the freeze STOPS.
2. Held-out period: drives whose date is AFTER the last training date (2026-10-17) and which were logged after the freeze. The evaluation runs only when the held-out period holds at least 100 drives on at least 40 calendar days (the PCL-1 / M481 convention; a planning choice), once for the primary reading; later looks are labelled repeated looks with no alpha spending. Nothing about the held-out period is inspected before the threshold is met; the ingestion stage `hazard_prospective_monitor` is flag-only and reports counts of held-out drives and days (no hazard value, no outcome).
3. Evaluation on the held-out period: the frozen model applied unchanged (no refit, no new knots, no re-selection), the Part A metrics, intervals and decision rules, plus the comparison with the Part A origins. The first look is its own milestone with its own Director decision; a refit is an explicit owner demand and a separate milestone.
4. Requires no other vehicle and no laboratory experiment.

## Block content, display and ingestion (reporting only)

Language (frozen, language-gate rules at implementation): 'rolling-origin evaluation of the declared pipeline design', 'stable across origins (planning thresholds; not equivalence)', 'out-of-time', 'frozen fit', 'held-out period'; events 'detector-derived (M231)', hazards 'model-derived'; never 'validated', 'proves', 'adequate', 'predicts the controller', 'measured'; the article restriction stands.

## Known-answer tests (written before the run, `tests/synthetic/test_m495_rolling_origin.py`)
KAT1 stationary synthetic hazard (known coefficients, enough data): every fold D1 PASS, O/E near 1, the verdict 'stable'. KAT2 a planted shift after the second origin (the coefficient on one predictor changes): the folds after the shift show a lower skill and a D1 FAIL or D2 MATERIAL, the verdict names those origins. KAT3 leakage: the day cut and the embargo make the training and test day sets disjoint (asserted on every fold); knots and scale are unchanged when the test rows are permuted or replaced (they use training rows only). KAT4 the engine run with the M428 split reproduces the stored M428 primary values (the hard stop). KAT5 an extrapolation case (test temperatures outside the training range) is flagged D3 and not counted as degradation. KAT6 the frozen artifact round-trips: applying the saved coefficients to the training table reproduces the fit's predictions to 1e-9. KAT7 the monitor reports counts only and no outcome.

## Acceptance (frozen)
A. Fidelity hard stops (5a, 5b, freeze fidelity). B. Isolation: only the registered payload leaves, the artifact and the registered dashboard paragraph change. C. Per-fold and pooled results complete for both sides with all flags (embargo, window, evaluable). D. Verdict per side as defined; `degradation detected` stops before the splice and goes to the Director. E. Blind audit (`analytical-auditor`, claim + data path only): the fold geometry, the training / test sets, and the SKILL vs B0, O/E and PR-AUC of two folds per side from the at-risk tables with the stated procedure; tolerance numeric, counts equal. F. Language as above.

## What would falsify 'stable across origins'
D1 FAIL or D2 MATERIAL at any evaluable origin, a monotone decline of SKILL across origins beyond the fold intervals, or a fidelity failure (the engine does not reproduce M428). The positive outcome is non-detection under declared planning thresholds and 14-28-day test windows, never a validation of the surfaces.

## Rev 2 (Director ruling 2026-10-23, decision 'revise'; written BEFORE any M495 computation)
Answers: (1) Keep the 28-day calendar-day expanding windows with the one-day embargo. Evaluability is counted in OBSERVED driving days (at least 10 days with at-risk rows, plus the reported effective number of day clusters), not calendar days. The M222.1 geometry rerun on the 606 corpus (observed days, 40 training days / 5-day steps, no embargo) is added as descriptive sensitivity S5. (2) The D1-D3 thresholds stand, but the across-origin verdict departs from M428 and is restated in amendment A2. 'Degradation detected' stops before the splice for Director review only and must not suppress publication. (3) Freeze now with a flag-only monitor: yes; the coefficient covariance is stored. 100 drives on 40 days is acceptable only with two more conditions: at least 300 events per side (checked by the monitor as a blind boolean) and an effective number of day clusters >= 15; one primary look, later looks descriptive only. (4) On demand is acceptable; a refresh appends origins and keeps earlier folds byte-identical. (5) The carried M222.1 block is NOT labelled 'superseded' but 'carried, 489-drive basis, pooled only' with a pointer to M495; the M428 block is labelled as evaluating the former 489-drive design, not the displayed fresh 606 fit.
Amendments (frozen; they replace conflicting text above):
- A1. Disclosure: the design (terms, knots, interactions, detector) was selected on data that include the test windows. Part A is out-of-time for ESTIMATION only; only Part B is prospective. Wording: 'rolling-origin evaluation of the declared pipeline design (design selected on the full data)'; never 'prospective' for Part A.
- A2. Verdict (restored M428 structure): per fold and side the jackknife t-interval (leave-one-day-out, G - 1 df) is computed beside the percentile bootstrap. `stable across origins (planning thresholds; not equivalence)` needs, at every evaluable fold: D1 PASS, D2 'within', D3 not MATERIAL, and the jackknife and the shared-day / embargo check (S1 embargo 0 and 3) give the same D-states; otherwise `partial: not established` naming the rule that is indeterminate or changes. 'Degradation detected at origin(s) k' is a FLAG (D1 FAIL or D2 MATERIAL), reported with a Bonferroni-adjusted sensitivity (99.4 % intervals over about eight fold-side tests); 'stable' is an intersection-union and needs no adjustment. D3 MATERIAL folds are 'extrapolation'. 'Partial' is the expected reading when the intervals are wide (in M428 the D2 'within' state was unreachable at 14 days; recorded as a risk).
- A3. Fidelity 5a: `m428_oot.main` rejects a master whose file list differs (606 vs 578); the 606 tables are therefore subset to the first 578 files for the reproduction, the `m119v2_model.py` sha256 is recorded and compared with the one in `M428_result.json` (hard stop on a mismatch of any value that depends on it). KAT4 needs the raw data and is skipped in CI (run in the acceptance run).
- A4. Freeze: event counts must match the stored `socHysteresisV2` counts EXACTLY (not within 1 %); the fit is run twice and the coefficients must be bit-identical; the convergence flag of `fit_glm` (it has a silent fallback) and the Python / numpy / scipy / statsmodels versions are recorded; the B0 rate, the B1 (socOnly) coefficients and the training P1 / P99 values are frozen in the artifact.
- A5. Held-out set: date > 2026-10-17 AND the master MD5 at the freeze recorded. Pre-registered expectation: cold pack-temperature D3 MATERIAL is expected in winter (extrapolation confounded with degradation); the in-support reading is reported beside the primary one.
- A6. Monitor: drive and day counts and the threshold boolean (including the 300-event and effective-day-cluster conditions as booleans only); NO event counts, no outcome values.
- A7. Additional KATs: day gaps and evaluability (a fold with fewer than 10 observed days is not evaluable); all-indeterminate D2 yields 'partial'; frozen baselines (B0 rate, B1 coefficients) round-trip; the planted-shift size of KAT2 is stated (coefficient change large enough that the post-shift SKILL falls below zero in the synthetic design).
- A8. Cost: with the sensitivities (embargo 0 and 3, windows 14 and 42, S5 geometry, the two M428-split reproductions, the freeze fitted twice) there are far more than eight fits; the tool prints a fit count and elapsed time and the CHANGELOG records them; the memory hygiene of M216 (gc.collect and del at fold boundaries) is kept.
Risks recorded by the Director: the held-out period falls in winter, so extrapolation is confounded with degradation; the pooled fold intervals ignore training uncertainty and mix models (no decision role).
