# M505 spec (Rev 2): sensitivity of the resistance-proxy trend to the current-voltage alignment rule (audit CD-05)

Status: Rev 2 (Rev 1 plus the section "Rev 2" at the end; the ruling "revise" of the research-director is folded in), written 2026-10-25 BEFORE any M505 computation. Origin: independent audit 2026-10-23, finding CD-05: the proxy `vreg_R_pack_mohm` (Huber regression of pack voltage on SoC and discharge current, `compute_drive_summary_v6._vsag_metrics`) pairs the asynchronously logged HV current, pack voltage and SoC samples by nearest-neighbour within 1,500 ms (`_asof`, `V_ALIGN_TOL`); the audit found that the choice of pairing rule moves the released trend and that this method disagreement is not reported. The audit's numbers are NOT targets. Additive and reporting-only: the released estimator, the proxy column of `drive_master.csv`, `powerFade` and `resistanceVreg` are read, never changed. Basis: raw/ = sha256-verified originals (M366).

## Question
Does the sign, size or interval of the released resistance-proxy trend depend on how the current samples are paired with the voltage and SoC samples?

## Estimand and designs (frozen)
The months coefficient (mOhm per month) of two released designs, each refitted with the proxy recomputed under each alignment variant: S0 = the `resistanceVreg` design (y = proxy; covariates pack temperature `T_pack_mean_avg` and `vreg_I_p95_A`; day-clustered, as `degradation_trends._cluster_ols(..., covars=('T','I'))`) and S1 = the primary `powerFade` design (S0 plus the two sampling-density covariates of `powerfade_density.csv`, effective interval and coverage; months scale days / 30.44 from the minimum date of the clean complete cases, as `powerfade_equivalence`). An estimated proxy and an association within one warm-season window, not a degradation rate or a state of health.

## Alignment variants (frozen; the estimator is unchanged, only the pairing differs)
The proxy is recomputed by calling the production `_vsag_metrics` on the prepared frame of each drive with only `_asof` replaced; every other step (series cleaning, SoC spike rule, rest/discharge selection, Huber regression, thresholds `VREG_MIN_N`, `VREG_MIN_I_P95`, `VSAG_R_LO/HI`) is the production code. Variants (applied to both the voltage and the SoC pairing): A0 = production (nearest within 1,500 ms); A1 = synchronous: the voltage (and SoC) linearly interpolated onto the current timestamps between the neighbouring samples on either side, only when both neighbours exist and are no more than 3,000 ms apart; A2 = nearest within 300 ms; A3 = backward only (the last sample not after the current sample, within 1,500 ms); A4 = forward only (the first sample not before it, within 1,500 ms). No other variant is run and none is added after the results are seen.

## Population and fidelity (frozen)
The 606 canonical drives; raw bytes by `ah_throughput.raw_loader` (14-digit key identity, 1:1 with the manifest, STOP otherwise). Hard stops: A0 must reproduce the master column `vreg_R_pack_mohm` for every drive (equal after the production rounding to 2 decimals, including which drives have a proxy: n = 274 in the S1 population), the S1 refit under A0 must reproduce the published `powerFade` slope (0.6564, 4 decimals), 274 drives and 98 days, and the S0 refit under A0 the released `resistanceVreg` slope. Otherwise STOP and report.

## Method (frozen)
1. Per variant: the proxy per drive; the paired per-drive difference to A0 (variant minus A0, mOhm) for drives with a proxy under both, summarised as n, median, and the 25th-75th percentile range, overall and by logger cadence regime (fast / slow, `cadence_regime.csv` rule `I_sample_period_s < 1.05 s`), with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval of the median difference (seed 42, 4,000 draws); and the number of drives that keep, lose or gain a proxy relative to A0.
2. Per variant and design (S0, S1): n drives, n days, the months coefficient and two intervals: (a) the day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4,000 draws, `tools/day_bootstrap.ols_boot_slopes`), which governs; (b) the ISO-week-clustered percentile bootstrap (same seed, 4,000 draws), a labelled sensitivity (about 23 weeks, probably under-covers). Draws that cannot be fitted are counted; more than 1 % makes the interval non-decisive and not estimable for the verdict.
3. Population of each refit: the drives that have the proxy under that variant and the covariates (a variant that changes the eligible set changes n; the n is reported beside each coefficient; no refit is restricted to the A0 set, and a second table restricted to the drives that have a proxy under A0 and the variant is reported as the paired comparison).

## Decision rules and wording (frozen)
Per design: outcome "reading unchanged" if every decisive variant has the same includes-zero reading of its day interval as A0; "reading depends on the alignment rule" if any decisive variant differs, listing the variant, the direction of the flip and the interval-width ratio to A0; the verdict "lower precision" label applies when a differing variant has fewer than 150 drives or a width ratio above 1.5. Also reported: the minimum and maximum slope over A0-A4 and the paired-set versions. No variant is preferred; production stays A0; no claim that a synchronous or nearest pairing is more correct; the paired shift of the proxy level is described as the alignment-dependence of the proxy, not as a bias correction. Forbidden wording: "corrected", "bias-corrected", "more accurate", "true resistance", "measured resistance", "degradation rate" (language rule ids CD-05/M505). Any outcome is reported as is; 10 intervals per design (5 variants x day) plus 5 week intervals, no multiplicity adjustment, the any-difference rule leans conservative.

## Outputs
`analyses/M505_result.json` (per-variant proxy summaries, per-drive proxies, refit tables, fidelity block, provenance: master and manifest hashes, script and spec sha256, seed). Script `tools/vreg_alignment.py`; tests `tests/synthetic/test_vreg_alignment.py` (each pairing rule on a synthetic asynchronous series with a known answer; the production `_asof` restored after use; variant refit on a synthetic corpus with a known slope; the verdict rule; a no-consumer grep). Payload: additive block `vregAlignmentSensitivity` (compact: per-variant slopes, intervals, n, paired shifts, verdicts, no per-drive table) + stamp, a dated snapshot of the corpus (no ingestion stage; restamped as carried at later ingestions). Dashboard: one sentence in the resistance-trend note bound to the block.

## Out of scope
Any change to the production alignment or to `drive_master.csv`; the cell-spread pairing (Vmax-Vmin, 300 ms); the cadence emulation (M393/M500); an ingestion stage.


## Rev 2 (the research-director's ruling "revise", seven required revisions R1-R7; this section replaces any conflicting Rev 1 text above; written before any M505 computation)
R1 (A1 defined unambiguously): for a current timestamp t_I the voltage (and SoC) is interpolated linearly between the sample at t_prev <= t_I and the sample at t_next >= t_I with t_next - t_prev <= 3,000 ms; when a sample sits exactly at t_I it is used directly; duplicate timestamps follow the `_series` output of the production code and the tie rule (the first of equal timestamps) is stated and the number of tied samples is counted in the result; a current row without both neighbours gets NaN and is dropped by production code (the current row set is the same in all variants, only the pairing differs). SoC is interpolated under the same rule. The result reports, for A0 versus A1, the share of paired rows whose SoC value changes.
R2: `vreg_I_p95_A` is recomputed under each variant (it is an output of `_vsag_metrics`). The pack temperature `T_pack_mean_avg`, the density covariates and the exclusions (`ens_outlier_v2` and the clean flags) stay FIXED from the master. Variant proxies get the production 2-decimal rounding.
R3: the replacement is in-process only (no multiprocessing: a spawned worker on Windows would silently run A0). The tool sets an active-patch sentinel on the replaced function, verifies it is active during each variant, and STOPs if any non-A0 variant matches A0 exactly on every drive.
R4: the fidelity targets (the published `powerFade` slope, 274 drives, 98 days and the released `resistanceVreg` slope) are read from the payload at run time, not typed in. Raw bytes by `ah_throughput.raw_loader`, production `_prep_bytes`.
R5 (verdict, supersedes the Rev 1 verdict rule): per design (S0, S1) and variant, two populations are reported: the PAIRED set (A0 restricted to the drives that have a proxy under both A0 and the variant, refitted twice, A0 and variant) and the OWN set (each variant on its own eligible drives). The paired set governs the attribution to the alignment rule; the own-set refit is reported beside it. A change of the includes-zero reading that appears only in the own set is labelled "population change under variant", not an alignment effect. Point-estimate sign flips (variant versus A0, paired set) are also reported. A variant with fewer than 150 drives in the paired set is "lower precision" (width ratio above 1.5 as before).
R6 (multiplicity): per design 4 non-A0 variants x 2 populations = 8 day intervals (+ the A0 reference intervals), plus 5 ISO-week sensitivity intervals labelled; no adjustment; the any-difference rule leans conservative toward reporting dependence.
R7: the block is a dated snapshot with no ingestion stage; it records the corpus drive count, master MD5 and the A0 S1 slope; `semantic_gate.py` flags (WARN) when the live `powerFade` slope no longer equals the block's A0 slope, so a carried snapshot cannot be mistaken for a current one.
Allowed wording: "reading unchanged across the five pre-registered alignment rules" (never "robust" or "insensitive to alignment" in general), "estimated proxy", basis "raw/ = sha256-verified originals (M366)". Never: corrected, bias-corrected, more accurate, true or measured resistance, degradation rate, "logger lag identified". A2 (300 ms) will mostly drop slow-polled drives, so its own-set refit mixes alignment with population; A3/A4 bracket a timing offset but do not identify a logger lag; in one warm-season window an includes-zero reading under any variant is not "no change".
