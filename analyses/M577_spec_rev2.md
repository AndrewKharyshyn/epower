## Change 1: Satterthwaite-df sensitivity (labelled, never replacing the t(G-1) result)
For every CR2 estimate of model A (step, slow) the script ALSO reports the interval with the Satterthwaite degrees of freedom returned by `dependence_sensitivity.cr2` (`est +/- t_{0.975, df} * se`), labelled `A-sat`. The ladder, labels and MDE of the step and slow terms stay on the t(G-1) intervals (Rev 1). Beside each label: the Satterthwaite interval, df, G, and the one-way flag `df-dependent` (the t(G-1) interval excludes zero and the Satterthwaite interval includes it). The flag is a disagreement to report, never reconciled and never described as "fragile" or "robust".

## Change 3: PID-set class test (Q3), frozen now, executed only when the floors are met
- Indicator `B` (R3): 1 for class-B drives (header-derived `pidSetClass`, `pid_epoch.csv`, M582a) dated on or after the first post-step class B drive `2026-11-03_15-07-48`; 0 for class A drives. Primary: the early class B drive `20260513_182950.csv` and the class C and D drives (12 drives, May and August) are DROPPED from the analysis frame; coding them 0 is a sensitivity. The indicator does not depend on whether the logger is later switched back (a "final run" definition would move).
- Floors (R4): the test runs only when BOTH (a) the payload `pidEpoch.q3Readiness` shows >= 10 class B drives on >= 5 calendar days AND (b) the eligible, in-support analysis frame of the primary design shows >= 10 class B drives on >= 5 days. If the payload and `pid_epoch.csv` plus the master disagree, STOP. Before the floors: `not identified (floors not met)`, nothing computed.
- Primary design (R7), fixed now: fast-regime drives with step = 1 dated 2026-10-01 or later, restricted to the common temperature support of B and non-B drives in that window; model y ~ months + B + T + I + soc + socr + lrest + first (CR2). The full model A + B on the whole frame is a sensitivity. The 2026-11-03 within-day contrast (class A vs class B on that day) is reported descriptively, with n and no interval.
- What B means (R5): with `months` in the model, B is the deviation of the class B drives from the within-window linear trend given the covariates, not a mean contrast against the other step = 1 drives. The script reports the VIF of B, months, step and slow, the number of non-B and B drives and days, and the correlation of B with months. VIF > 5 for B gives `not separable`.
- Interval for the label (R6): B rests on about 5 clusters. The label uses the WIDER of the t(G-1) and the Satterthwaite interval (the t(G-1) interval alone is anticonservative); both are reported.
- Resampling (R8): the day bootstrap and the week-block bootstrap count the draws without any B cluster as degenerate; if more than 10 % of draws are degenerate the result is `not evaluable`; leave-one-week-out is reported with the number of weeks that contain B (a week that holds all B drives drops B entirely and is listed, not averaged). The cold-subset model C needs a B-arm floor of 10 drives on 5 days, else `not evaluable`.
- Looks (R9): the class B result is a look. A ledger `analyses/M577_pid_looks.json`, append-only and keyed by the master MD5 (idempotent, written only by the run), numbers the looks. Interim looks carry NO ladder label (descriptive estimates, intervals and flags only: "descriptive look k; no ladder label"). The terminal point is pre-declared here: >= 20 class B drives on >= 10 calendar days in the primary design's analysis frame; fallback: the data through 2027-03-31. At the terminal point (frozen once written) the Rev 1 ladder with the R6 interval produces the label. Labelling at every look would be optional stopping.
- Confounding: every class B drive is step = 1, fast-regime, cold and latest-dated, and the M574/M580 look-1 hold-out drives overlap the class B period. The result, if any, is described as "associated with, conditional on the stated covariates; header-derived, post hoc class"; the header class is a proxy and does not show PID polling; it cannot be separated from season or a non-linear time trend.

## Decision rules and wording
Rev 1 verbatim for the step and slow terms. Allowed for class B: "associated with, conditional on the stated covariates; header-derived, post hoc class; no PID-set effect and no cadence effect is claimed". Forbidden: "PID effect", "PID-set effect", "caused", "because of the VCM PID", "cadence-corrected", "epoch identified", "fragile", "robust" (rule `M582-pidset-wording`, extended with `PID-set effect` and `effect of the PID set`).
