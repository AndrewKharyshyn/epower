# M703 spec (Rev 0, pre-registered, spec only): manufacturer cross-check protocol (backlog N-12)

Status: DRAFT for Director review; written 2026-11-16, spec only: no estimator, no payload key, no display. N-12 acceptance is "specs; sources archived". Road-load / CoC / mass items stay closed.

## 0. Source status (blocking rule)
The manufacturer-side quantities of N-12 come from two kinds of sources: (a) the Nissan Article 10 sheet (X-Trail e-POWER; downloaded, stored outside the repository, sha256 `edd2e471...b3c8`, M642, owner decision D-08); (b) Nissan Technical Review No. 89 and No. 90, the figures of which entered the self-audit only as "third-party or search-summary only, not independently verified". RULE: no manufacturer-side number enters any block, wording, figure or comparison until its source document is archived (file stored outside the repository, sha256 and URL recorded in the block provenance, the page and sentence of each quoted quantity recorded). No download without the owner's approval (file name, source and size stated when asking). Until then every comparison below is "not run: source not archived". The audit figures (63.4 % vs 62.3 % within 1,850-2,150 rpm; 0.14 % at 4,400-4,600 rpm; rated 5 Ah / 66 kW / 0.210 ohm / 34 % at 14 years or 190,000 km) are NOT typed anywhere in the repo from the audit; they are read from the archived sources or not used.

## 1. Question and non-claims
Which of the manufacturer's stated design properties of the e-POWER powertrain can be compared with logged quantities of this one vehicle under a matched procedure, and what would a supported, unsupported or not-comparable outcome mean? Non-claims: a comparison of this vehicle with a manufacturer statement is a consistency check on logged channels, not a validation of the design, not a test of the manufacturer, not a degradation estimate; occupancy of an rpm window is not best-BSFC operation; one vehicle in one 12-month window is not a fleet; a statement made for a test cycle (WLTC) is not transferred to in-use driving unless the study matches the procedure.

## 2. Comparisons (each is a separate, independently gated protocol)
- C1 fixed-point occupancy: the share of engine-on time within the rpm window the source names (1,850 to 2,150 rpm in the audit's quotation; the window is taken from the archived source), per cohort, with the engine-on rule of record (rpm > 400, M546 option A; NaN = off), time-weighted on the 1 s grid, day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws), n (drives, days). The manufacturer's 80 % occupancy statement applies to WLTC plus the Extra High phase only: it is compared only against a speed-matched subset of logged driving (the speed range of the Extra High phase) and stated as not comparable to the all-driving share; a mismatch of procedure gives "not comparable", never "consistent" or "inconsistent".
- C2 high-rpm occupancy: the same share within the 4,400 to 4,600 rpm window (read from the archived source), descriptive (a small share has a wide relative interval): reported as a count of drives and seconds with the interval; no equivalence claim.
- C3 start hazard against speed at matched SoC and demand (No. 89): a view of the M701 fitted hazard (speed term of the hazard model, other covariates at fixed values): NOT a new fit; it inherits the M701 status (the pedal contrast is not established, pedal is logged only from mid-August: epoch-confounded), so C3 carries the same caveat and no supported wording unless a later M701 revision supports the demand term. A speed-only start-frequency comparison (the source's quantity, read from the archive) is attempted only on a matched SoC band and a matched demand band; if demand is not available for the matched subset the output is "demand not matched".
- C4 pre-event matched assist event study (No. 90): around each RPM onset (the existing detector of record), the SoC and the pack temperature at a fixed pre-event window are compared with matched non-onset windows (same drive, same speed band, SoC within 2 points, pack temperature within 3 C, same cohort; matching by nearest neighbour without replacement, day cluster); the estimand is the difference in pre-event HV battery discharge power between the onset windows and the matched controls, day-clustered bootstrap; "assist" is defined from the logged battery power and the generator state, never as a measured torque. Pre-registered falsifiers: the difference disappears after matching on speed and SoC (a speed or SoC artefact); it appears only in one cadence stratum (a sampling artefact, the B6 cadence check stays failed and shown).
- C5 rated disclosure values (5 Ah, 66 kW, 0.210 ohm, 34 % fade after 14 years or 190,000 km): compared with logged quantities ONLY under a matched procedure. The procedure of the sheet (test temperature, SoC, pulse duration, measurement convention) is read from the archived sheet; the logged apparent quantities (M403 apparent capacity of the logged SoC scale; the BMS-reported possible power map M658; the vreg and R_sh apparent resistance proxies) are defined for different procedures: unless a logged quantity can be put on the same temperature, SoC and time basis, the outcome is "not comparable" and the sheet's value is shown only as a quoted fact (as M642 / M653 already do); no cell of this study is labelled as a rated or a verified value; `CAP_KWH = 2.1` stays `verified:false`.

## 3. Eligibility, populations, intervals
Canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2 == False`); the support ladder (10 drives on 5 days) per cell; calendar day is the resampling cluster everywhere; every estimate carries n (drives, days) and an interval; cohorts and cadence strata are never pooled across the B6 failure; seed 42, 4000 draws; sensitivities: rpm threshold 300 vs 400 vs window edge shifts by one grid step, with and without the first 60 s of a drive, reverse-mask seconds excluded (M698).

## 4. Outcome labels (pre-set; no tuning)
Each comparison ends in exactly one of: "not run: source not archived"; "not comparable: procedure mismatch (reason)"; "consistent with the manufacturer statement (interval overlaps the quoted value or range)" (only for C1, C2 and C4 where the matched procedure holds and the quoted quantity is a share or a difference); "not consistent"; "not established (interval too wide / support below the floor)". "Consistent" never means validated; "not consistent" is a finding about this vehicle's logged channels and the procedure, never a statement about the manufacturer.

## 5. Falsifiers and risks
F1 occupancy depends on the engine-on rule and the window edge by more than the interval (sensitivity to the rule); F2 the speed-matched subset has too few drives (support); F3 a quoted manufacturer figure cannot be located in the archived source (then the comparison is dropped, not paraphrased). Risks: R1 the audit's quotations are third-party summaries and may differ from the source; R2 e-POWER fixed-point operation is a control-strategy statement, occupancy of one window is one proxy; R3 cohort mix (urban, highway, winter) changes occupancy; R4 matching in C4 may leave few controls at high power; R5 wording drift to "validates" or "rated value".

## 6. Outputs (for a later implementation milestone, one per protocol, each with its own spec revision, Director review, blind audit)
Additive payload blocks (names reserved: `oemCrossCheckC1`, `oemCrossCheckC2`, `oemCrossCheckC4`; C3 and C5 are views of existing blocks), provenance with the archived-source sha256 and page; known-answer tests (synthetic rpm traces with a known window share; synthetic start events with a planted pre-event discharge; a not-comparable case); language rule `M703-oem-crosscheck-wording`: 'consistency check against a manufacturer statement under a stated procedure'; never 'validates the manufacturer', 'rated value measured', 'proves', 'BSFC'. No dashboard display without a separate spec step and a rendered-text check.

## 8. Rev 1 (2026-11-16, Director review "revise"; overrides conflicting text above)
1. Blocking rule (section 0) extended: (a) the primary Nissan document only, never a third-party page or a search summary; (b) the version, date and language of the document and the provenance of any translation are recorded; (c) the Article 10 sheet is an image-only PDF, so every quoted quantity is transcribed in two independent passes with the page number and a hash of the page image; (d) windows, quantities and procedures are committed in a spec revision BEFORE any data access for the comparison (the quotation fixes the window, the data do not); (e) N-12 stays open until Technical Review No. 89 and No. 90 are archived (owner approval is needed for any download: file name, source and size are stated when asking; this is an owner decision, not an agent decision).
2. Section 4 "interval overlaps the quoted value" is removed. Each comparison pre-sets a margin in percentage points (or in the quantity's unit) and tests it with TOST, or reports the difference and its interval only (no label). "Consistent" requires the interval inside the pre-set margin; otherwise "not established" or "not consistent" (interval outside the margin); the margins are written in the revision that fixes the quotation.
3. C2 (4,400 to 4,600 rpm occupancy) is descriptive only (counts of drives and seconds with an interval) and is not eligible for "consistent".
4. C1: the 80 % statement (WLTC plus Extra High) is pre-labelled "not comparable" (matching on speed is not matching the procedure). C1 is an additive view on the `engineStateMachine` 1 Hz grid (no new detector). Stated: the rpm distribution (`rpmDistribution`, M131, peak near 1,900 to 2,100 rpm) has already been seen, so C1 is NOT blind; the window comes from the archived source, not from the data. Each cadence stratum and the cold cohort are reported separately (never pooled across the failed B6 check).
5. C4 redesigned: the onset detector is named (rpm > 400, run >= 2 s, the engine-on rule of record of M546 option A; sensitivity: the 300 rpm onset of M701); controls are engine-off at-risk seconds with no onset within +-30 s; pre-set before any run: speed bins, SoC caliper (2 points), pack-temperature caliper (3 C), chronological nearest-neighbour matching without replacement, the unmatched share is reported; pre-event discharge is the trigger itself (reverse causality), so the primary outcome is the post-onset window [0, +N] s (N fixed in the quotation revision) minus matched controls, pre-event discharge is reported as context only; day-clustered bootstrap; the relation to `bufferImpulse`, `handoffSequence` and `startEventChain` is stated in the implementation spec (no duplicate finding).
6. C5 is dropped from this milestone: the sheet's values stay quoted facts as in M642 and M653; a comparison of the quoted 66 kW with `bmsPowerMap` is a future spec only (check the hp/kW header handling at compute_summary_arrays first).
7. C3 only reads the `engineStartHazard` speed term at pre-set covariate values; it adds no block and is pre-labelled "not established" while the pedal term is epoch-confounded.
8. Language rule `M703-oem-crosscheck-wording` (added with the first implementation) must forbid: validates / confirms Nissan, BSFC, sweet spot, optimal, efficiency inferred from occupancy, "rated" without "quoted", assist torque, "fixed-point operation shown".
9. Implementation order after the archive: C1, C2, C3 (wording only), C4. Follow-up recorded: check the circular "ours 5.84 Ah" row of the official-disclosure display.
Limits recorded: one vehicle in 12 months; C1 not blind; the generator state is an rpm proxy. The Director marks the download of the Technical Reviews as needing Andrii's decision.

## 9. Rev 2 (2026-11-16, after the two-pass transcription of the archived sources; proposal for Director review, supersedes sections 2 and 8 only where stated)
Sources: `analyses/M704_sources.json` (sha256, size, metadata, cited page-image hashes), two passes `analyses/M704_transcription_pass1.json` and `_pass2.json`, reconciliation `analyses/M704_reconciliation.json` (one disagreement resolved from the page text). What the documents actually give for the X-TRAIL (and what they do not):
1. C1 fixed-point occupancy: the document gives a POINT (2,000 rpm, minimum BSFC 217 g/kWh) and no window. The window is therefore OUR pre-registered design choice, not a source quantity: primary 1,850 to 2,150 rpm (2,000 plus or minus 150), sensitivities plus or minus 100 and plus or minus 250; the earlier 2,350 rpm (an older engine) and the 2,000 to 2,400 rpm of TR90 pdf 48 (a different engine) are not used. The 80 % statement stays "not comparable": WLTC plus Extra High procedure, the denominator is not stated and the vehicle is not named in that sentence. C1 is descriptive (occupancy with intervals); a TOST claim has no quoted target and is not made.
2. C1b (new, replaces nothing): the document states that engine speeds below 1,500 rpm are not used (a design statement). Pre-registered check: the share of engine-on time (rpm > 400, M546) below 1,500 rpm, excluding the 5 s ramp after an onset and the 5 s before a stop (ramps pass through low rpm by construction); a one-sided upper bound against a margin of 5 percentage points (margin to be confirmed by the Director); label "consistent with the design statement" only if the upper day-clustered bound is within the margin; relation to the existing `engineOnRule` band share (M522) stated in the implementation spec.
3. C2 high-rpm occupancy: the source quantity is the maximum-output point 106 kW at 4,400 rpm (domestic market); the window is ours: 4,300 to 4,500 rpm (plus or minus 100), sensitivities plus or minus 200; descriptive only. The 116 kW generator rating is not the engine output and is not used; the 4,800 rpm of TR90 pdf 48 belongs to another engine.
4. C3: the documents give only qualitative directions (fewer starts at low speed, more at high speed; fewer at 20-40 km/h and more at 40-60 km/h relative to a different vehicle, no counts) and no SOC or acceleration threshold for the X-TRAIL; the 0.15 G / 30 km/h / about 70 % figures are NOTE / SERENA / KICKS market data and are NOT compared. C3 is therefore only the within-vehicle ordering of the M701 speed term between the pre-set bands 20-40 and 40-60 km/h at fixed other covariates (a direction check), labelled "not established" while the pedal term is epoch-confounded; no between-vehicle claim.
5. C4 reframed: the document's assist is the battery-assist function with the turbo engine, coordinated with rising boost pressure to hide turbo lag, with a limited rate of engine rpm increase; no numbers. The pre-event matched design of Rev 1 is retired. Candidate checks (Director to choose): (a) within high-demand accelerations matched on speed, SoC and pack temperature, the engine rpm ramp rate distribution (the limited-rate statement) and (b) the battery discharge power during the engine ramp relative to matched windows without a ramp; both are descriptive (existing `rampLatency` / `bufferImpulse` blocks are the starting point; boost pressure is available only where logged).
6. Applicability rule added to section 0: every transcribed statement carries the vehicle and engine it applies to and its procedure; a statement about another vehicle or engine concept (NOTE, SERENA, KICKS, HR12DE, the dedicated 50 %-efficiency engine) is never used as an X-TRAIL target.
Questions for the Director: (1) is the plus or minus 150 rpm primary window for C1 acceptable as a pre-registered design choice (source gives a point); (2) the 5 percentage point margin and the 5 s ramp exclusion for C1b; (3) C3 as a within-vehicle direction check only; (4) which C4 check, if any; (5) is the milestone complete as 'specs plus archived sources' once Rev 2 is frozen, with implementation in separate milestones.
