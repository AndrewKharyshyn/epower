# M379b: written definitions of the cadence regime and the sampling emulator (for a blind re-derivation; values-free, code-free)

Purpose: an independent re-derivation of (i) the logger-cadence regime runs and counts, (ii) the emulator identity and interval match, and (iii) the emulated-to-original ratio of gross throughput on a fixed set of drives. It contains no result. The emulation is an emulated sampling sensitivity, not a causal cadence effect. You may call the repository pipeline as a black box (`compute_drive_summary_v6.analyze_bytes(csv_bytes, filename)`, one raw CSV in, one dict of per-drive keys out); do not read the emulator code (`tools/cadence_emulate.py`, `tools/cadence_sensitivity.py`) or any study result.

## 1. Raw files and regime
Raw logs: `raw/*.csv`, first column `time` (HH:MM:SS.mmm, no date; within a drive it is increasing), every other column a PID (text header) or `Latitude` / `Longtitude`. A row is one logger tick; a PID cell is empty unless the PID was updated at that tick. The HV-current PID column is the header `[BMS] HV Battery Current (A)`. Regime of a drive: take the timestamps of its non-empty HV-current cells, the consecutive differences in seconds, and their median `P`; the drive is "fast" if `P < 1.05 s`, "slow" if `P >= 1.05 s`, no regime if it has fewer than three current samples. The corpus listing of drives is `drive_master.csv` (column `file`, `date`, `time_start`); match raw files to master rows by the digits of the file name (YYYYMMDDHHMMSS). Runs: order drives by (date, time_start), skip drives without a regime, and report maximal runs of equal regime (regime, number of drives, number of calendar days, first and last start).

## 2. The emulator (thinning)
For one raw CSV and a target interval distribution: every data column except `time`, `Latitude`, `Longtitude` is thinned independently. Take the timestamps `ts` of the column's non-empty cells (at least 3). Keep the first sample. Then repeat: draw an interval `x` from the column's pool, multiplied by `factor` (1.0 = emulate the slow regime, 2.0 = twice the slow intervals); the target time is `t_last + x`, where `t_last` is the time of the last kept sample; find the two available samples that bracket the target time (the last sample before it and the first sample at or after it) and keep the later one with probability `(target - t_before) / (t_after - t_before)`, otherwise the earlier one (dithering; if the chosen sample is not later than the last kept one, take the next sample); stop when the target time passes the last sample. Cells of dropped samples are made empty; everything else is unchanged; the result is written back as CSV and passed to the pipeline. Pool of a column: the pooled consecutive-sample intervals (seconds; only intervals above 0.05 s and below 120 s) of that column over the pool drives listed in `audit/m379_pool_drives.txt`. A column with fewer than 200 pooled intervals uses the pool of the HV-current column scaled by (that column's native median interval in the drive being emulated / the native median HV-current interval of that drive). Randomness: any seeded generator; use at least 20 independent seeds per drive and report the spread.

## 3. What to deliver
(a) Regime: the counts (fast / slow / none) over the corpus and the runs of section 1; compare your counts with the `I_sample_period_s` column of `drive_master.csv` (that column is the pipeline's version of `P`; report any drive whose regime differs). (b) Identity: for the 10 drives in `audit/m379_audit_drives.txt`, the pipeline keys of the original file and of the file rewritten with NO thinning (all samples kept, written through the same CSV round trip) must be identical for `gross_throughput_kwh`, `gross_discharge_kwh`, `gross_charge_kwh`, `distance_km`, `n_I_samples`; report the maximum absolute difference. (c) Interval match: for the same 10 drives at `factor = 1.0`, the mean of the emulated HV-current intervals below 5 s, pooled over drives and seeds (weighted by the number of intervals), compared with the mean of the pool intervals of the HV-current column below 5 s; report both means, their relative difference, and the median and the 10th / 90th percentiles of both. (d) Gross throughput: for the same 10 drives at `factor = 1.0` and at `factor = 2.0`, the ratio of sums `sum over drives of the seed-mean emulated gross_throughput_kwh / sum over drives of the original gross_throughput_kwh`, with the seed spread of that ratio (standard deviation over the seeds of the per-seed ratio). (e) Classification: state whether each ratio of (d) lies inside [0.99, 1.01]. Report the choices this document leaves open (for example how ties and edge samples are handled) and their effect where you can state it.
