# M695 spec (Rev 1): inferred reverse-consistent episodes versus regeneration (descriptive payload; flagged sensitivities; no primary change)

Status: pre-registered before any result of this analysis exists. Origin: owner statement (Andrii, 2026-11-16, in chat; `verified:false`, an assumption, not a measurement): some negative motor torque in the logs is caused by REVERSE movement, not regeneration; reverse usually occurs at the start or end of a drive (parking manoeuvres) or sometimes mid-drive; it always follows zero speed and is a low-speed movement. Design drawn up by the research-director (Opus) 2026-11-16; this spec is its pre-registration, to be reviewed again before any code.

## 1. Question and non-claims
How large is the share of logged negative commanded-torque samples (and of the per-drive torque minimum) that lies inside reverse-consistent episodes, as opposed to forward regeneration, and how much does excluding them move the exposed outputs? Non-claims: direction is NEVER observed (the logger has no gear, range or direction PID: 141 distinct columns scanned in 57 sampled files; `[VCM] Vehicle Speed` is unsigned, 0 negative samples in 14,193 checked); an episode is an INFERRED reverse-consistent episode; the owner statement is a hypothesis this analysis tests, never imposed; torque is the commanded / logged (app-calculated) register, not shaft torque; no existing figure changes; no regeneration efficiency or energy figure is produced here.

## 2. Physics used
Forward: positive torque, speed rises; regeneration: negative torque, speed falls (battery charges). Reverse with unsigned speed: negative torque, |speed| RISES from zero; braking in reverse: positive torque, |speed| falls. The sign of torque times the sign of d|speed|/dt is opposite in reverse and equal in forward. The rejected `[VCM] Motor RPM` PID (M102) is a linear image of the torque register and cannot indicate direction.

## 3. Data
Master `drive_master.csv` (canonical clean, `ens_outlier_v2` False) and the raw drive CSVs (read-only, per-drive result cached by raw sha256 in `reverse_mask_cache/`; no `drive_master.csv` column). Series: unsigned speed, `[VCM] Target Motor Torque` (the actual-torque register is not used), HV battery current, engine rpm (for the engine-off subset), the cadence regime (`cadence_regime.csv`, fixed coding fast = 1, slow = 0).

## 4. Discriminator (all parameters frozen here; tau0 is fixed from the v = 0 torque distribution BEFORE any episode outcome is computed, and the SHA-256 of this spec is recorded in the CHANGELOG before the first run)
Per-sample labels: REV_CAND, REGEN_TO_STOP, NEG_AT_STANDSTILL, AMBIGUOUS_LOW, FORWARD; episodes per drive.
- Standstill onset: speed == 0 for at least 1 s (at least 1 sample at the slow cadence) immediately before the first sample with torque < -tau0.
- tau0: the 99th percentile of |torque| over samples with speed == 0 and the engine off (hold / creep noise floor), pooled over canonical-clean drives; computed once and written into the block.
- Dynamics rule: in the first k = 2 samples with speed > 0 the torque is < -tau0 and d|speed| > 0 (opposite signs). The episode then runs until speed returns to 0 and includes its positive-torque braking phase.
- Caps: max speed <= 15 km/h; duration <= 120 s; at least 2 samples with speed > 0; max speed >= 2 km/h (the speed resolution is 1 km/h).
- REGEN_TO_STOP: torque < 0 while speed falls and reaches 0 within 3 s: always forward regeneration, never reverse.
- NEG_AT_STANDSTILL: torque < -tau0 at speed 0 (hold / creep), a separate class.
- AMBIGUOUS_LOW: negative torque at low speed with flat speed, or a candidate that fails the dynamics rule: counted separately and NEVER moved into either class.
- Stratification: every rate is stratified by cadence regime (fast / slow); no pooled verdict across regimes.
Sensitivity grid (descriptive, never changes the primary): speed cap {10, 15, 20} km/h; standstill {0.5, 1, 2} s; k {1, 2, 3}; tau0 x {0.5, 2}.

## 5. Validation without ground truth (each can falsify the discriminator)
V1 current sign on the engine-off subset (minus the standstill auxiliary baseline): REV_CAND acceleration must discharge the battery and regeneration deceleration at matched speed must charge it. V2 sign coherence inside labels (tautological for onset; tested on the continuation phase). V3 negative control: the dynamics rule applied WITHOUT the caps to segments above 40 km/h (downhill coasting): its hit rate is the rule's false-positive bound. V4 position in the drive: share of episodes in the first and last 10 % of the drive time versus the middle, compared with the owner statement. V5 stability: the label count stays within a factor of 1.5 across the sensitivity grid. V6 overlap of REV_CAND with the events of `regenDisplayV2` and `regenCapture` must be 0: the count is reported, not assumed.

## 6. Falsifiers
The discriminator is falsified if V1 fails in more than 10 % of supported episodes, or V3 exceeds 1 %, or V5 is unstable. The owner statement is not supported if REV_CAND occurs in few drives relative to the owner's expected frequency, is not concentrated at the start and end, or V1 shows charging (which would indicate negative creep or hill-hold torque, not reverse). A failed or unsupported outcome is reported as it is.

## 7. Outputs and order of work
1 spec, Director review, blind audit of the spec (this document). 2 `tools/reverse_mask.py`: per-drive episodes keyed by raw sha256. 3 additive payload `reverseInference` (counts, drives, days, day-clustered percentile intervals, seed 42, 4000 draws; support ladder; all strata). 4 flagged sensitivities with inferred reverse excluded, one block per exposed output: `target_torque_min` / highest commanded torque, `torqueBySpeed`, `terrainTorqueDist` (+Low), `power_peak_temp`, `c1_joint_temp_crate`, and the regeneration overlap check (V6); each carries its own spec note, none overwrites a published figure. 5 a Director decision on any switch of a primary definition (method change: blind audit and Andrii's sign-off). 6 an ingestion stage only after step 5. No display in steps 1 to 4.

## 8. Language
"inferred reverse-consistent episode"; torque is "commanded / logged (app-calculated)"; "sensitivity with inferred reverse excluded". Forbidden: "reverse detected", "reverse measured", "reverse identified", "gear", "corrected regeneration", "regen corrected". The old wordings are added to `language_gate.py` RULES with the implementation.

## 9. Limits and risks
Direction is never observed; with the engine on, generator current masks V1; the 1 to 2 s cadence can miss short manoeuvres; forward downhill negative torque is bounded by V3 but not eliminated; the speed has a 1 km/h resolution. The best validation is prospective: about 10 drives with the owner's own notes of reverse times, pre-registered as held-out.

## 10. Questions to the owner (answers are inputs, not parameters set after a result)
(1) Typical number of reverse manoeuvres per drive and where (home parking)? (2) Does the car hold or creep with negative torque on slopes? (3) Is there a known reverse speed limit? (4) Willing to note reverse times on future drives (a held-out validation set)?

## 11. Director review (2026-11-16, verdict revise; no change of question) and how each is applied (Rev 1.1)
These override the matching text above.
1. tau0 population: samples with speed == 0, engine rpm < 300 (the project's engine-off proxy), excluding the 5 s before any speed > 0 transition; the number of samples, drives and days is recorded; tau0 = max(99th percentile of |torque|, the resolution of the torque register); tau0 is written and hashed in a SEPARATE run before any episode code runs.
2. Onset: the timestamp of the onset sample minus the timestamp of the last speed > 0 sample is at least 1 s (logger timestamps, speed sample-and-hold); the start of a drive counts as standstill.
3. Dynamics rule: torque < -tau0 on each of the k samples, AND speed(k) > speed(1) strictly (d|speed| > 0 is otherwise trivially true for the first sample, 0 to at least 1 km/h); k is counted in samples; its time equivalent is given per cadence stratum.
4. Label precedence (mutually exclusive, in this order): REGEN_TO_STOP, NEG_AT_STANDSTILL, REV_CAND, AMBIGUOUS_GAP, AMBIGUOUS_LOW, FORWARD; candidates that exceed the caps go to AMBIGUOUS_LOW and are reported.
5. Loader and alignment: `compute_drive_summary_v6._prep_bytes`; speed = the `[VCM] Vehicle Speed` column only (never GPS speed), with the despike / speed-priority version of the pipeline recorded in the block; torque, speed and current are aligned to the torque sample times by nearest-previous within a fixed tolerance of 1500 ms, no interpolation.
6. V1: the current sign convention is the project's (discharge positive in the Ah-throughput code); the baseline window is the last 5 s of standstill before the onset; "supported" = engine off (rpm < 300) throughout the episode and at least 2 current samples; speed-matching bins of 2 km/h; with fewer than 10 episodes on 5 days per stratum V1 is "not testable", never a pass.
7. V3: the denominator is the number of negative-torque onsets (torque < -tau0 at speed above 40 km/h following a speed decrease or coast) at speed > 40 km/h with the same sample rules; the minimum n is 50, otherwise "not testable".
8. V4 and the owner statement: the owner statement counts as supported only if the lower bound of the day-clustered interval of the share of episodes in the first or last 10 % of the drive time exceeds 0.20; the owner's answer to question (1) is frozen before the run, otherwise V4 is descriptive only.
9. V5: the one-at-a-time max / min ratio of the REV_CAND count per cadence stratum must be <= 1.5; V6 > 0 is not a falsifier: each overlapping case is listed.
10. A pass is worded "not falsified by V1 to V6", never "validated".
Missing items added: an episode across a logging gap longer than 3 x the median sample interval is AMBIGUOUS_GAP (listed in the precedence); drives with a mixed or unknown cadence regime are reported separately; the empirical speed quantisation and sample interval are reported per stratum; the corpus snapshot is frozen (master MD5 `77bbe275f8c22eaf62d8636867764af3`, 665 drives); the provenance of the data facts of section 1 is `analyses/M695_scan_provenance.json`; the support ladder is the project's (not shown below 10 drives or 5 days; exploratory when the effective number of days is below 10; supported otherwise); language rules additionally forbid "reverse share", "reversing" and "reverse torque", and require "share labelled REV_CAND under the frozen rule" to be shown next to the AMBIGUOUS share.

## 12. Owner answers to the section-10 questions (Andrii, in chat, 2026-11-16; inputs recorded BEFORE any run; verified:false; no parameter of section 4 or 11 changes)
1. Reverse manoeuvres: usually 1 to 2 at the start and at the end of a drive (not always, it depends on the parking position; in many drives there are zero reverse movements); rarely in the middle of a drive (1 to 2). A reverse movement usually starts from standstill at a speed of 0 to 1 km/h. Use: the expected pattern for V4 (frozen now: episodes concentrated in the first and last 10 % of the drive time; many drives with none; a mid-drive minority); it does not change the V4 threshold (lower bound of the start-or-end share > 0.20).
2. Hold on slopes: the car always holds at standstill on slopes. Use: NEG_AT_STANDSTILL (negative torque at speed 0) is expected to be rare; a large NEG_AT_STANDSTILL share would contradict this statement and is reported as such.
3. Speed: usually not more than 15 km/h and in most cases not more than 10 km/h; no physical upper limit known. Use: consistent with the frozen cap of 15 km/h; the sensitivity cap of 10 km/h is the "most cases" reading; no change.
4. Annotated held-out drives: technically possible. Protocol (prospective, pre-registered as held-out before any of those drives is analysed): for each annotated drive the owner notes the drive start time and the number of reverse movements near the start, in the middle and near the end (the clock time of each movement if easy); the notes are stored as `analyses/M695_owner_reverse_notes.csv` (template `analyses/M695_owner_notes_template.csv`); none of these drives is used to set or change any parameter; the discriminator is scored on them (episode recovered / missed / false) after the run on the rest of the corpus, with the count of drives fixed in advance (about 10). The notes are an owner statement (verified:false) of event counts, not a measurement of direction.

## 13. Deviation found at the first corpus pass (Rev 1.2; proposed, Director decision requested; recorded BEFORE any run with the changed speed source)
Observed at the first corpus pass of `tools/reverse_mask.py` (code committed at 049555b before the pass; VCM speed only, as section 11 item 5 says): 434 of the 663 canonical-clean drives have NO `[VCM] Vehicle Speed` PID (the later logger configuration logs only the standard OBD vehicle speed `Швидкість автомобіля (km/h)`, an ECU-reported integer km/h, not GPS); 229 drives were analysable. Outcome counts SEEN before this proposal (disclosed): 6 REV_CAND episodes in 6 drives (31 REV_CAND samples), V3 hit rate 0.0026 on 4,685 onsets (not falsified), V1 not testable (5 supported episodes), V4 share 5 of 6 with a wide interval, V5 episode counts over the grid 1 to 98 (tau0 x 0.5: 98; k = 1: 27; the primary: 6), which is a ratio far above 1.5 but rests on 6 primary episodes. tau0 (45.77 N.m) was computed earlier from the VCM-speed drives only (219 drives, 57 days).
Proposed Rev 1.2 (replaces section 11 item 5 only for the speed source): per drive, speed = `[VCM] Vehicle Speed` when present, else the standard OBD vehicle speed (the same ECU speed the pipeline already uses as its fallback, `compute_drive_summary_v6.py` speed_obd); never GPS; the speed source is recorded per drive; tau0 stays FROZEN at 45.77 N.m (not recomputed, because the first pass outcomes are known; a recomputation on the extended population is reported as a descriptive sensitivity); every rate is reported by cadence regime AND by speed source, with no pooled verdict across speed sources; the VCM-only result above is kept as its own stratum. The first-pass outcome counts above are part of the record and are not used to choose any other parameter. Question for the Director: how must V5 be applied when a stratum has few primary episodes (the pre-registered V5 has no minimum n); a minimum n introduced now would be post hoc and must be labelled so.

## 14. Director decision on the section-13 deviation (2026-11-16, verdict go with conditions; Rev 1.2 final)
Rev 1.2 is accepted as a DISCLOSED DEVIATION, not a new analysis: every parameter was frozen before the OBD-speed run and no OBD-speed outcome has been seen; coverage (434 of 663 drives without the VCM speed PID), not outcomes, prompted the extension. Conditions, which override section 13 where they differ:
1. The Rev 1.2 SHA-256 is recorded in the CHANGELOG (and in the commit) before the OBD run. The VCM stratum (first pass, as pre-registered) and the OBD stratum (Rev 1.2) are separate strata with NO pooled verdict. The snapshot difference is stated: 663 canonical-clean drives here against 665 master drives.
2. V5 is applied as pre-registered, with NO minimum n added as a gate, per cadence stratum within each speed source. In the VCM stratum the grid ratio (98 and 27 against 6) is far beyond Poisson noise at n = 6, so low n does not explain it: V5 is recorded as FAILED, i.e. "falsified by V5 (threshold-sensitive)" for the VCM stratum. An allowed supplement, labelled POST HOC: an exact Poisson ratio interval (Clopper-Pearson on a / (a + b)) for the max / min episode counts per stratum; if its lower bound is <= 1.5 the block adds "instability not distinguishable from small n"; the supplement never overrides the pre-registered verdict. The OBD extension cannot rescue the VCM stratum.
3. tau0 stays FROZEN at 45.77 N.m for the primary in both strata. In a SEPARATE run before any OBD episode code, the per-source descriptive tau0 is computed (OBD-speed drives, the same population rule; samples, drives and days recorded; `analyses/M698_tau0_obd.json`); if it falls outside [22.9, 91.5] (the tau0 x 0.5 and x 2 grid) it is flagged to the Director; it is never substituted.
4. A speed-source concordance check runs BEFORE the OBD episode run, on drives that log both PIDs: aligned per-sample difference; zero-state agreement; the sample lag at the maximum cross-correlation; the difference of the standstill-onset timestamps; the label concordance of the primary rule using VCM versus OBD speed. Thresholds frozen now: zero-state agreement >= 0.95 and median lag <= 1 sample; otherwise the OBD stratum is "descriptive only, speed source not concordant". If no drive logs both PIDs, the OBD stratum is "unvalidated against VCM" and is said so.
Limits and wording: direction is never observed; the VCM stratum is already falsified by V5 as pre-registered; V1 is not testable at the current support; the OBD speed has a different timing path and the onset rule is sensitive to it; "inferred reverse-consistent episode", "not falsified by V1 to V6" or "falsified by V5", "deviation (Rev 1.2), disclosed", the V5 supplement carries a post hoc label; never "reverse detected / identified / validated".
