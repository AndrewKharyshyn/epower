# M757 spec: DEM terrain grade (FABDEM v1.2) from cloud fixes, feasibility with a validation route independent of the 8 OBD-altitude days (report-only)

Rev 1, 2026-11-23. Committed BEFORE any tool is written or any DEM value is read. Author: main session (Sonnet). Number taken after checking the CHANGELOG head (M756), open PRs and the remote branch list. Owner: "FABDEM spec" (after the owner supplied FABDEM v1.2 tiles; project is non-commercial, owner-approved download of 17 tiles, 2026-11-23). Next step: Director review, then tool + known-answer tests, blind audit, Director decision, CHANGELOG, PR. No payload, dashboard or published figure changes in this milestone. Builds on `analyses/M737_spec.md` Rev 2 (stage G0 outcome: not testable, 8 of 30 days) and `analyses/M737_g0.json`.

## 0. Why, and what is already known
- M737 stage G0 (offline) found the position, geometry and reference-noise facts and stopped at the support gate: the validation set that the M737 design needs (OBD GPS altitude with a cloud track) spans 8 days / 20 drives / about 242 km (needs 30 days and 100 unique km). The OBD altitude channel exists only in the 42-column logging generation (from 2026-07-24), in 1 Hz bursts. The cloud `Geolocation` stream has latitude, longitude and heading at about 30 s cadence and NO altitude. Grade therefore has to come from a digital elevation model; the open question is how that can be validated when only 8 days carry a logged-altitude reference.
- External input now in hand (outside the repository, never committed): FABDEM v1.2 (University of Bristol; Copernicus GLO-30 with buildings and forests removed), 17 GeoTIFF tiles at 1 arc-second (about 30 m) covering the study area, sha256 per tile in `fabdem_v1-2/fabdem_tiles_manifest.json`; licence: non-commercial use. Heights are above the EGM2008 geoid, so only height DIFFERENCES are compared (a constant offset to a GPS altitude is irrelevant). Pillow reads the float32 deflate tiles (checked: mode F, 3600 x 3600, tile origin and 1/3600 degree spacing in the GeoTIFF tags); no new dependency is needed.
- Key design observation (new in this spec): the NET height change between two points is path independent. A cloud track of 30 s fixes cannot resolve the road at 400 to 900 m spacing (M737 P1: cloud-minus-OBD position difference is large at shifts of +-16 s and between fixes), so a route-based grade profile needs a road network (OSM, not approved, not requested). But the DEM height DIFFERENCE between consecutive cloud fixes needs only the two fix positions: grade over a cloud step is `(h_DEM(fix2) - h_DEM(fix1)) / d_step`, with `d_step` from the cloud odometer or the OBD distance counter. This removes map matching from the primary design. What it gives is a step-scale mean grade (400 to 900 m at highway speed), not a 500 m grade profile along the road; the study states that scale and does not claim more.
- Known limits stated before any result: DEM vertical error is a few metres (source-stated, not assumed adequate); over a 500 m step a 3 m height error is 0.6 pp of grade, over a 100 m step 3 pp; the cloud fix position error screened by the M737 rules is not zero; GPS-altitude references are themselves noisy (M737 reference floor at L = 500 m is about 0.39 pp on the 8 days).

## 3. Stages (each with a stop gate)
- **F0 tile integrity and coverage (offline).** Verify each tile sha256 against the manifest (mismatch: stop, exit 3). Share of screened cloud fixes inside a tile and with a valid DEM value (km-weighted: share of logged distance); share of drives with at least 3 fixes and with at least 1 km of track. The expected coverage from a bounding-box check is 636 drives with at least 3 precise fixes and 576 drives with a track of at least 1 km (84 % of logged km); F0 recomputes it from the files, it is not a result taken from this spec. Bilinear interpolation on the 1 arc-second grid; the reader is checked against a planted GeoTIFF (known-answer test).
- **F1 step-grade agreement with logged GPS altitude (the 8-day reference, descriptive only).** For cloud steps (consecutive screened fixes, step length at least 300 m, no export gap, no reverse movement as far as screenable) on OBD-altitude days: DEM step grade versus the grade from OBD GPS altitude over the same step (altitude smoothed at the OBD 1 Hz samples bracketing each fix time, shifts -16, 0, +16 s as sensitivity), difference in percentage points. Reported: bias (median), median absolute difference, P90, Pearson, Spearman, Lin concordance, each with a day-clustered interval, by speed class; plus the step-length distribution. Support floor (as M737): 30 days and 100 unique km; below it F1 is "not testable", the numbers are reported as descriptive with the intervals but carry no decision.
- **F2 independent validation: grade-term recovery in an energy balance (all drives with cloud fixes; no altitude channel needed).** For each cloud step the logged traction-side energy and the kinetic energy change over the step give a residual after the road-load terms; if the DEM step grade carries real grade information, the residual regresses on `m g dh` with a positive coefficient. Model per step: `E_traction_step = (F0 + F1 v + F2 v^2) * d + m (dv2/2) + b * m g dh + e`, with `F0, F1, F2` and `b` fitted jointly (road-load coefficients are NOT known: no CoC / WLTP data exist; they are nuisance parameters), `m` the assumed class mass (labelled), `dh` the DEM height difference between the two fixes, `E_traction_step` the model-derived traction energy of the step (reconstruction of the GTR recon engine; model-derived, not measured). Estimand: `b` with a day-clustered interval, n (steps, drives, days, km), the incremental variance explained by the `b` term (day-blocked cross-validation: fit on odd days, score on even days and vice versa), and `b` fitted separately on the OBD-altitude days using the logged-altitude `dh` (like-for-like reference `b_obd`). A noisy `dh` attenuates `b` toward zero (errors in variables): `b` below one is expected and is reported as an attenuation ratio, never tuned. Steps with reverse movement, standstill and a low-power band (traction power below a floor stated in the tool) are excluded; excluded shares are reported.
- **F3 report.** Grade-class shares (below -3 %, -3 to -1 %, -1 to 1 %, 1 to 3 %, above 3 %) of DEM step grade over the corpus and per cohort (descriptive, with the step-scale caveat); the share of logged regeneration kinetic energy on steps by grade class is computed ONLY if F2 reaches "informative" (R2 below). No change to any displayed regeneration figure: whatever the outcome this milestone only reports, and any later binding needs its own spec and Director ruling (M737 R3 stays the design for that).

## 4. Estimands (pre-registered)
- E-F0 coverage shares (km-weighted and per drive), reader check.
- E-F1 per-step difference DEM grade minus OBD-altitude grade (pp): bias, median absolute difference, P90, correlations, by speed class; descriptive when support is below the floor.
- E-F2 `b` and its interval; attenuation ratio `b / b_obd` on the OBD-altitude days (with interval); cross-validated incremental R2 with the day-blocked interval; the number of steps per grade class entering the fit.
- E-F3 grade-class shares (DEM step scale).

## 5. Decision rules (frozen before any result)
- R1 (F0 gate): tiles intact; DEM value at at least 90 % of screened fixes (km-weighted); otherwise stop, report coverage only.
- R2 (DEM step grade "informative", judged on F2): (i) `b` is positive and its day-clustered interval excludes zero; (ii) the odd-day and even-day fits both give a positive `b` and the cross-validated incremental R2 is positive with its day-blocked interval excluding zero; (iii) support at least 30 days and 100 unique km of steps entering the fit, and at least 1,000 steps with |DEM step grade| above 2 pp. "Informative at step scale" means the DEM step grade carries real grade signal; it does NOT mean accepted for the `m g dh` term. For the potential-energy term (R3) the stricter condition is that the `b` interval contains one OR `b / b_obd` has an interval containing one (the DEM is no more attenuated than the logged altitude on the same days); the sign and size of the shortfall is reported. Otherwise "not informative at this precision" if (iii) holds and (i) fails (a falsification of the claim "DEM step grade is usable here", with the minimum detectable `b` stated), or "not testable" if (iii) fails.
- R3 (potential-energy term vs grade-class stratification): grade-class stratification is the primary downstream use when R2 is "informative"; the `m g dh` term only if the stricter condition above holds (assumed mass labelled; DEM height error is a large share of the event kinetic-energy height).
- R4 (F1): "accepted" requires the M737 R2 primary criteria (|bias| at most 0.5 pp with the whole interval inside +-1.0 and median absolute difference at most 1.5 pp) at the support floor; below the floor F1 is "not testable" and cannot decide anything alone. Disagreement between F1 and F2 is reported, not tuned.
- Falsification and limits: the claim "FABDEM step grade carries real road grade at cloud-step scale" is falsified only by R2 "not informative at this precision". Limits: the DEM and the traction reconstruction are proxies and F2 conditions on the reconstruction's error structure; a traction-model bias correlated with terrain would inflate `b` (stated; sensitivity: refit with the energy reconstruction variants of the GTR sensitivity block, report the spread); the validation set of F1 is bursts, not the steepest driving; no claim about regeneration recovery is made here.

## 8. Questions for the Director

(b) Is the F2 energy-balance regression a defensible independent validation, or does the traction reconstruction (model-derived, road-load coefficients unknown) confound it too much; which reconstruction variants must the sensitivity cover, and should the road-load coefficients be fitted on flat-DEM steps only before the `b` term is added? (c) Are the R2 support floors (30 days, 100 unique km, 1,000 steps above 2 pp) and the stricter R3 condition sensible? (d) F1 below its floor is descriptive only: agreed? (e) Which exclusions for reverse movement are acceptable without a gear channel (negative torque with reverse is not regeneration; speed is unsigned)? (f) Should a Sonnet blind audit suffice because nothing displayed changes?

## 9. Rev 2: Director rulings on Rev 1 (override sections 1 to 8 where they differ; review record `analyses/M757_director_review_rev1.json`, Rev 1 sha256 03b5a972a003015f65f30141211c8741984bf27f64e88264dda3c9973f51b37a)
9.1 **Scale and time alignment.** The estimand is a STEP-MEAN grade over 300 to 900 m, not a road profile. Positions and the OBD energy window use the same alignment: the primary shift is **+16 s** (the best agreement of M737 G0 P1, chosen before any DEM value); 0 s and -16 s are sensitivities only; neither F1 nor F2 may select the shift. `d_step` is the OBD distance over the aligned window; the cloud odometer is used only if its resolution is 0.1 km or finer (F0 reports it).
9.2 **Bare-earth flag.** FABDEM is bare-earth: bridges and cuttings give spurious `dh`. Steps with |DEM step grade| above 8 pp are excluded in the primary (covariate-side flag, fixed now) and kept in a sensitivity.
9.3 **F2 is a validation CONDITIONAL on the traction reconstruction, not an independent validation.** `b` absorbs drivetrain and regeneration efficiency and scales with the assumed mass; the like-for-like quantity is `b / b_obd` (mass- and efficiency-free). Primary: the joint fit with an added duration term `c * duration` (auxiliary / HVAC load). Sensitivities (all reported, none selects): road-load coefficients fitted first on DEM-flat steps (|g| below 1 pp); separate `b+` and `b-` for `dh > 0` and `dh < 0` (regeneration asymmetry); by thermal cohort; every energy variant of the GTR sensitivity block, with the spread. No outcome-based exclusion in the primary: the low-traction-power exclusion of Rev 1 moves to a sensitivity.
9.4 **Negative control (frozen).** `dh` sampled at fixes displaced 2 km perpendicular to the heading; `b_placebo` must have an interval containing zero, otherwise R2 is downgraded by one level (informative becomes inconclusive).
9.5 **Rules R2 and R3 rewritten.** Support floors stay (30 days, 100 unique km, 1,000 steps above 2 pp) and add: at least 10 days with such steps of EACH sign of `dh`. R3 (potential-energy term): the "`b` interval contains one" branch is dropped (efficiency makes it uninterpretable); the criterion is `b / b_obd` with its interval inside [0.8, 1.25] AND `b_obd` days meeting the 30-day floor; with the current 8 OBD-altitude days R3 is "not testable". R2 "not informative at this precision" is a falsification ONLY if the upper bound of `b` is below 0.25 times the `b_obd` point estimate (below 0.2 if `b_obd` is not testable); otherwise the reading is "inconclusive", with the minimum detectable `b` stated.
9.6 **Reverse movement.** No gear channel exists, so the screen only bounds the problem: the primary excludes the first and last step of each drive and steps with a minimum OBD speed below 8 km/h (excluded km share reported); a sensitivity runs without exclusion. Negative torque in low-speed steps is never called regeneration.
9.7 **Tests and record.** Additional known-answer tests: the displaced-DEM placebo returns zero for a planted `b` of one; the sign split recovers asymmetric `b+ != b-`; the duration term recovers a planted `c`. The result JSON carries a `deviations_from_spec` section (Rev 1 to Rev 2 changes listed above are the registered ones).
9.8 **Audit.** Sonnet high blind audit suffices (report-only, nothing displayed). Before any binding (M737 R3), dashboard use or article use: Opus audit plus Andrii's sign-off. `needs_andrii_signoff`: false for this milestone.
