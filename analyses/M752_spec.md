## 0. Why, and what is already known
- The master's `odo_start` / `odo_end` are NOT the car's odometer: they are the logger's own distance counter (values 0 to 10,970 km at 0.1 km resolution), and `distance_km = odo_end - odo_start` in 99.9 % of drives (`distance_source`: 409 odo_endpoint, 1 trip_meter, 307 blank; 1 reset repaired). The absolute instrument-cluster ODO at a drive's start or end is therefore not tracked per drive anywhere in the corpus. The owner's dash ODO is given once per ingestion (`summary_config.json` vehicle.odometerKm 48,110 as of 2026-11-20, the end of the newest drive).
- The cloud Odometer stream (`StatusDistanceTotalizerComputed`, about every 30 s; first reading 34,819.77 km, last 48,104.81 km, net gain 13,285.04 km over the export span 2026-05-10 to 2026-11-19; registered M729) reports that vehicle ODO. Registered M729 `odometer_check`: cloud minus master per-drive distance, median +0.11 km, 90th percentile of the absolute difference 0.47 km, n = 431 drives with the nearest cloud row within 30 s of both ends; no day count, no interval, and the nearest-row rule extends the cloud window by the endpoint age, so a positive bias is expected from the method itself.
- Both counters derive from the vehicle's speed signals, so agreement is a cross-check of the LOGGER (dropouts at the start or end of a drive, integration artefacts, resolution), not an independent measurement of true distance. The cloud ODO is "cloud-reported", never "measured".

## 2. Data and eligibility
- ODO rows: Odometer stream rows with a numeric `StatusDistanceTotalizerComputed` and no entry in `conflicts.csv`. A row lower than the running maximum of accepted rows by more than 0.05 km is "regressive" and excluded (counted and reported).
- Drive window: master `date` + `time_start` as Europe/Kyiv local time, `duration_s`; t0 = start, t1 = start + duration (as M729).
- Primary endpoint estimator **E_int**: the ODO at time t is the linear interpolation between the last accepted row at or before t and the first accepted row at or after t, available only when both rows exist and are at most 35 s apart (the M729 step cap); otherwise the endpoint is "not available". No clock shift in the primary.
- Sensitivities: **E_near** (nearest accepted row within 30 s, the M729 rule), **E_br** (last row at or before t0, first row at or after t1), **E_int with the clock offset** (ODO at OBD time T read at cloud time T - 16 s, the registered M730 convention), and the 60 s age tier for E_near.
- Agreement set: canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2` False; missing is not clean) with both endpoints available under the estimator and `distance_km` not missing. Strata: logged distance below 2 km, 2 to 10 km, 10 km and above; month.

## 3. Estimands
- **P0 per-drive side table:** for every drive in the master (717) the cloud ODO at the start and at the end under E_int, the age (s) of the bracketing rows, availability flag and reason (outside the export span, no row within 35 s, hole, regressive). Coverage counts overall and by month.
- **P1 distance agreement:** for each agreement-set drive Delta_i = (ODO_end - ODO_start)_cloud - `distance_km`. Reported: n drives and n days; median signed Delta and median |Delta| and 90th percentile of |Delta|; aggregate relative bias sum(Delta) / sum(`distance_km`); each with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval (cluster: calendar day of the drive start, seed 42, 4000 draws) and the same for each stratum and for each sensitivity of section 2. Support floor: at least 20 drives and 10 days per stratum, otherwise counts only.
- **P2 resolution:** the smallest non-zero ODO increment in the cloud stream and the logged 0.1 km resolution, and what they imply for the smallest per-drive Delta that can be distinguished from rounding (a Delta below the combined resolution is not read as disagreement).
- **P3 anchor (known answer):** the owner's dash ODO (summary_config vehicle.odometerKm at vehicle.odometerAsOf, the end of the newest drive) minus the cloud ODO at the last accepted row of the export, compared with the logged distance of the drives that end after that last row; pre-registered tolerance 1.0 km (descriptive; the result is reported either way).
- **P4 integrity:** counts of regressive rows, conflict rows, duplicate timestamps, holes of 24 h or more; control V0: the cloud ODO gain between the first and last accepted row equals the registered M729 net gain 13,285.04 km (within 0.01 km), else STOP.

## 4. Decision rules (fixed now; judgement thresholds, Director to rule)
- "agrees at the pre-registered level": the day-clustered interval of the aggregate relative bias lies within +-3 % and the median |Delta| is at most 0.25 km; "small bias (direction stated)": the interval excludes zero but stays within +-3 %; "differs": the interval reaches beyond +-3 % or the median |Delta| exceeds 0.25 km. Stratum statements use the same wording with the support floor.
- A positive aggregate bias under E_br or E_near and none under E_int would confirm the expected window-extension artefact; the primary statement is made under E_int and the other estimators are shown beside it, not tuned.
- Whatever the outcome this milestone shows nothing on the dashboard. The note X1 can be reconsidered only through its own addendum.

## 5. Falsification and limits
- Falsified ("the logger's distance agrees with the vehicle odometer within 3 %"): the aggregate relative bias interval reaches beyond +-3 % under E_int.
- Limits: shared vehicle speed signals (see section 0); cloud rows every 30 s, so the interpolated endpoint carries an error of at most the movement within one row gap at the drive's edge; 184 of the 717 drives are shorter than 2 km, where 0.1 km resolution is a large relative error (reported by stratum, the aggregate is dominated by long drives); drives outside the export span or inside holes have no endpoints and are listed, not imputed; the drives without a clean flag are excluded from P1 but kept in P0.

## 9. Rev 2: Director rulings on Rev 1 (override sections 1 to 8 where they differ)
9.1 **Estimators.** E_int stays the primary but is NOT read as unbiased: the car is parked before t0 and after t1, so the interpolated ODO at the start comes out high and at the end low, which gives a NEGATIVE difference; E_br (last row at or before t0, first row at or after t1) extends the window and gives a POSITIVE one. E_int and E_br are reported as a lower and an upper bracket of the cloud-reported distance. The Rev 1 claim that E_near has an inherent positive bias is withdrawn (only E_br extends the window), and the Rev 1 test "a positive bias under E_br or E_near and none under E_int" is dropped. **Flat-bracket rule (pre-registered):** if the two bracketing accepted rows differ by at most 0.05 km the endpoint is exact whatever their time gap (the car was parked); without it most starts after parking would be "not available" and coverage would be selected. The +16 s clock shift stays a sensitivity only (M730 `adopt_global_offset` is false; it was fitted on displayed speed, not on the odometer stream); direction as in `tools/mynissan_clock_offset.py` (a cloud value at cloud time T is matched to OBD at T + offset).
9.2 **Control V0** uses the raw first and last accepted values (48,104.80859375 - 34,819.76953125 = 13,285.0390625 km), not the rounded 13,285.0; STOP if the difference exceeds 0.01 km.
9.3 **Thresholds.** The primary margin of the aggregate relative difference is +-1 %, with +-3 % shown beside it; the median |difference| limit 0.25 km applies to drives of 2 km or more; for drives under 2 km only counts and the P2 resolution comparison are given, with no agreement verdict. The floor of 20 drives and 10 days per stratum stays. Wording: "consistent within +-1 % (and within +-3 %)" / "difference of x % [interval]"; never "accurate".
9.4 **Drive classes.** Classified by the identity |distance_km - (odo_end - odo_start)| <= 0.05, not by `distance_source` (no Python file writes it; 307 blank values): a blank source that satisfies the identity is in the primary set; `trip_meter` drives and the repaired drive are a sensitivity; P1 is reported by class.
9.5 **P3 is a single-point plausibility check** (n = 1, no interval), not a known answer: the dash reading is an integer, probably truncated, and unlogged driving after the last cloud row (18:43 on 2026-11-19) cannot be ruled out, so the check is one-sided; only the part of a drive that lies after the last cloud row is added from the logged distance.
9.6 **Coverage.** Reported by reason (outside span, no row within the cap, hole, regressive) and available versus unavailable drives are compared by distance stratum and month (selection check).
9.7 **Wording and caveats.** The logger counter is "logged / app-calculated" (its PID is not shown to be an ECU value). Section 5 reads "consistent with the cloud-reported odometer", never "vehicle odometer accuracy". Caveat added: both counters rest on the same wheel-speed scale, so a common scale error (tyre circumference, calibration) is undetectable; the check covers logging completeness and edge losses, not distance accuracy; odometer-stream latency may differ from speed latency. The per-drive quantity is a "difference" (never error or correction); the cloud ODO at drive ends is "cloud-reported, interpolated" (never measured, true, reference, corrected or alternative distance) and is never substituted into `distance_km` or the logged-share KPI.
9.8 **Side table.** Stays a side table; master columns only through a separate ingestion-pipeline spec (the export does not cover future drives and a column would make the master depend on an auxiliary source).
