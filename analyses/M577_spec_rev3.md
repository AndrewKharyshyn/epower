## Question
(Q-A) Do the `step` and `slow` estimates of model A change when the two nuisance covariates `lrest` (rest time before the drive) and `first` (first drive of the day) are removed? This is an estimand-change question (omitted-covariate projection), not a measure of "collinearity harm": the VIF of `lrest` / `first` matters only through their partial correlation with `step` / `slow`, which is reported (R1). (Q-B) Does a wild cluster bootstrap on the calendar day agree with the CR2 t(G-1) and Satterthwaite intervals for the same two terms? The day-clustered wild bootstrap assumes independent days; it shares that assumption with CR2 and cannot repair undercoverage caused by autocorrelated days (R6 adds a week-clustered sensitivity).

## Change 1: nuisance-covariate sensitivity (Q-A)
- VIF of every covariate of model A on the common-support frame; the pairwise correlation and condition index of `lrest` and `first`; and, for `step` and `slow`, the VIF and the R2 of each on {`lrest`, `first`} given the remaining covariates (R1: how much of step / slow the two nuisance covariates carry).
- Variants (same drives, outcome, support and CR2 as Rev 2 model A): A (reference), A-noLrest, A-noFirst, A-noBoth. Named known-answer check (not a sensitivity, R1; Frisch-Waugh-Lovell): the `step` and `slow` coefficients of A equal those of the regression of y on step, slow and the residuals of `lrest` and `first` after partialling out {1, months, T, I, soc, socr} ONLY (not step / slow), to 1e-9; STOP otherwise. A-orth is not a variant.
- For `step` and `slow` in each variant: estimate, CR2 t(G-1) interval, Satterthwaite interval and df, and the PAIRED difference (variant minus A) with a day-clustered pairs-bootstrap percentile interval in which the SAME day draws refit both models (4000 draws; draws that cannot be fitted counted as degenerate, > 10 % = not evaluable). Margins are frozen as the named keys of `analyses/M577_levels_r2.json` (R2): the MDE80 of the term in A (`labels.<term>.mde80_A`). Readings use two paired intervals computed from the same draws: the 90 % percentile interval for "no change beyond X mV detected" (inside +/- margin: an equivalence-style reading, TOST-correspondence) and the 95 % percentile interval for "changes by about Y mV (interval ...)" (excludes zero); if both hold, the "changes by about" reading takes precedence and the margin statement is reported beside it; else "not resolved at this precision".
- Reading rule: the Rev 1 ladder label is attached to each variant for information only; the A label is not replaced; a variant label that differs from the A label is reported as "label depends on the nuisance-covariate set", never "robust" or "fragile".

## Change 2: wild-cluster-bootstrap intervals (Q-B)
- Cluster = calendar day (G = 120). For `step` and `slow`: wild cluster bootstrap-t with CR1 cluster-robust studentisation (the standard WCB choice, R4; CR2-studentised is an optional extra, reported if run), 4000 draws. Rademacher weights primary (G = 120 is large enough), Webb six-point weights as a sensitivity (R5).
- PRIMARY interval (R3): the restricted (null-imposed, WCR) bootstrap-t TEST INVERSION interval: the set of beta0 values not rejected at 5 % by the WCR p-value, found by a grid / root search with the SAME weight draws at every beta0 (boottest-style). The unrestricted (WCU) percentile-t interval is a sensitivity. The WCR p-value of H0: coefficient = 0 is reported with its Monte-Carlo standard error (about 0.003 at p = 0.05).
- Beside each interval: the CR2 t(G-1) and Satterthwaite intervals, and the one-way flag `interval-dependent` (the CR2 t(G-1) interval excludes zero and the test-inversion interval includes it, or the reverse). The flag is a disagreement to report, never reconciled and never a ladder label. The A label is not changed by Change 2.
- Per term (R5): the number of treated (step = 1 / slow = 1) and control days, the number of mixed days (days containing both arms), and the share of the term's variation that is between-day; WCB is unreliable with few or unbalanced treated days (step: 21 days with step = 1) and this is stated beside the result.
- R6 sensitivity (pre-registered, not a replacement): the same inversion interval with ISO weeks as the cluster (Webb weights primary there because the number of weeks is small), reported with the number of weeks; it relaxes the day-independence assumption partly, still not the autocorrelation structure.
- Seeds (R7): all streams from `numpy.random.SeedSequence(42)` spawned into separate generators (paired-difference bootstrap, WCB Rademacher, WCB Webb, week WCB, simulations), each stream's seed recorded in the output.

## Known-answer tests (planted state)
(a) simulated outcomes with an independent-day case and an AR(1) day effect (rho 0.5), a day-level regressor; the empirical coverage of the CR2 t(G-1) interval and of the WCR-inversion interval over 400 simulations is reported (no pre-judged winner; the test asserts only that the numbers are written and in [0.5, 1.0]); (b) a planted effect is recovered (inversion interval excludes zero, WCR p < 0.05); (c) the FWL check above on the real frame and on a synthetic one; (d) Rademacher draws are +/-1 with per-cluster mean about 0; Webb draws have the six-point support; (e) the WCR p-value of a zero-effect synthetic outcome is approximately uniform (MC check of rejection rate 0.05 +/- 3 MC SE at 400 simulations).

## Decision rules and wording
Report-only. Allowed: "sensitivity to the nuisance-covariate set", "interval from a wild cluster bootstrap on the calendar day (assumes independent days)", "agrees with / differs from the CR2 interval", "no change beyond X mV detected", "associated with". Forbidden: "robust", "fragile", "confirmed", "corrected", "proven", "coverage-corrected", "corrected interval", "valid interval", "exact interval", "stable", "insensitive", "collinearity-free", "degradation", "imbalance growth", "cadence effect". Multiplicity: 4 variants x 2 terms paired intervals plus 2 terms x 3 interval types, reported as such, not adjusted. Nothing here changes a label, the dashboard note or the confirmatory (M574 / M580) block.
