## 1. Problem
- UI-04. `thermalSupportLedger.timeToThreshold` (tools/m435_tte.py, M435-r2) is computed for cohorts all, warm and shoulder only (`COHORTS`); the Compare thermal view therefore has no Cold entry, and the chart that Compare shows is built from medians of the drives that reached the threshold ("reachers only"), which understate the time for all cold starts (the block's own label says so). The block already carries the right quantities per cell: E1 = observed share reaching the threshold within h before key-off, with a day-clustered bootstrap interval, plus E2 (Kaplan-Meier, hypothetical continued drive) and the Manski bounds.
- UI-06. The fill sensitivity (tools/m437_fill_sensitivity, spec M437 Rev 2) varies the forward-fill limit F in {0, 3*, 6} s. The external audit (backlog item UI-06 in analyses/self_audit_2026-11-11/backlog.json, source X) reports that a 15 s and a 30 s cap move the 10-minute coolant shares; its figures were not re-run by us and are not copied into this repo. The current limits text says the 0-6 s range is below the 95th percentile of the coolant sample interval, so the longer gaps are never tested.

## 2. Estimands and definitions (nothing new is invented; existing cell definitions are reused unchanged)
- Cell = cohort x fluid x threshold x horizon, exactly as M435-r2: `status == cold` drives (start below `_TWL_COLD_MAX`), at-risk at h, E1, E2, Manski bounds, tilt, RMST, support rule (>= 10 at-risk drives on >= 5 days), seed 42, 4000 draws, calendar day as the cluster.
- UI-04: the new cohort is Cold (assigned class `thermal_regime_raw == "cold"`, the label the Compare uses). Headline display cells stay as declared in M435/M438: coolant/80 and oil/70 at h = 600 s. Cold is exploratory when the cohort has fewer than 10 effective days (1 / sum of squared day shares of the cohort's analysed cold starts); then the point and the interval are shown with the word exploratory and no verdict wording.
- UI-06: fills F in {0, 3*, 6, 15, 30} s (3 stays the record S0; no setting is chosen), tolerance X fixed at 10 s for the added rows. Added settings are native recomputations (ffill with limit F on the 1 Hz grid built from the raw frames), not a post-hoc adjustment. The rows report E1 and E2 at the headline cells with their intervals, and the share of coolant sample intervals above F (computed from the data, not typed).

## 3. Display (Compare thermal view)
- Per cohort in the Compare selection: E1 at h = 600 s with its interval for the headline cells (coolant 80 C, oil 70 C), n at risk and days, effective days, exploratory flag. The reachers-only median is not drawn as a cohort summary; where it is kept (a tooltip or the existing All-data view) it carries the existing label "median of reachers only; understates the time for all cold starts".
- No difference between cohorts is drawn here (the M646 Cold-minus-Warm contrast covers other metrics; a time-to-threshold contrast is a different estimand and out of scope).
- Cold absent or below the support floor: the Compare says "Cold: below the support floor" with the counts, never an empty or zero-filled bar.
- UI-06 rows sit beside the chart as a small table: F, E1 [CI], E2 [CI], share of sample intervals above F; label "range over declared conventions, not a confidence interval; the record stays F = 3 s".
