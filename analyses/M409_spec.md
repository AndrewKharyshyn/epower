Origin: self-audit 2026-10-11 (lag-1 autocorrelation of the cell-spread residuals about 0.25, "CI optimistic"), deep audit F17, Director ruling M406 (deferred item "pre-register a serial-dependence sensitivity"), FEASIBILITY.md row F17/N06.

## Question
M406 showed that the cell-spread slope interval excluding zero depends on the procedure. M406 changed the resampling unit (day, ISO week) and the small-cluster correction; it did not model dependence between neighbouring days explicitly. Is there serial dependence in the residuals of the released design `y ~ months + T + I` at the day scale, how large is it, and how much does a time-distance-aware standard error move the slope interval?

## Data and design (fixed, not tuned)
Exactly the M406 designs and populations (`tools/dependence_sensitivity.py::_design`): `cellSpread` (`cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv`; covariates T and peak discharge current; 525 observations, 108 days at the M406 build) and `resistanceVreg` (`vreg_R_pack_mohm`; covariates T and `vreg_I_p95_A`; 242 observations, 89 days); canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` not True). The point estimate of the slope is the released OLS slope in every procedure below except G (GLS), which is reported as a separate estimator. Time axis: calendar day number (days since the first drive date). The corpus is irregularly spaced (logging gaps); all statistics use calendar-day distance, never the observation index.

## Quantities (all fixed in advance)
A. **Day-mean residuals.** OLS residuals of the released design averaged within calendar day (the cluster mean), one value per logged day.
B. **Lag-1 autocorrelation over gaps.** `rho_g` = Pearson correlation of day-mean residuals for all day pairs whose calendar distance is exactly g days, for g = 1, 2, 3 (reported with n pairs); and `rho_{1-3}` pooled over distance 1-3 days. CI: percentile bootstrap resampling ISO weeks (blocks), seed 42, 4000 draws (the week block keeps within-week pairs intact). Pairs with distance > 3 days are not used for the headline autocorrelation.
C. **Semivariogram by distance bin.** Half the mean squared difference of day-mean residuals for distance bins {1, 2, 3-4, 5-7, 8-14, 15+} days, with n pairs per bin; the sill is the sample variance of the day-mean residuals. A monotone rise to a sill, versus a flat profile, is read descriptively.
D. **Time-distance HAC (Conley-type) standard error of the slope.** Bartlett kernel on calendar-day distance with truncation L = 7 days (the pre-registered primary-by-design truncation for the HAC row, chosen as the weekly scale because the known driving rhythm is weekly), with L = 3 and L = 14 as the sensitivity rows. Computed on the observation level with within-day dependence retained (observations on the same day get kernel weight 1). Small-sample factor G/(G-1) x (n-1)/(n-k) as the released CR1. Interval: normal and t(G-1), both reported.
E. **Time-distance-aware pairs bootstrap.** Moving-block bootstrap over the calendar-day axis with block length 7 days (and 14 days as sensitivity), non-overlapping, seed 42, 4000 draws, full refit of the design in each draw. It is not a replacement of the M406 week-block row: the ISO week is calendar-aligned, the moving block is not.
F. **Residual-based AR(1) sandwich check.** Estimate `phi` from B (rho_1), form the AR(1)-implied variance inflation factor of the slope for the realised day-spacing `VIF = 1 + 2 sum_{d>=1} phi^d c_d` where `c_d` is the observed autocorrelation of the slope regressor (months) residualised on the other covariates at distance d, truncated at 14 days. Reported as a diagnostic ratio only (no interval).
G. **Gap-aware AR(1) GLS on day means (separate estimator).** Day-mean design, Gaussian AR(1) with correlation `rho^|t_i - t_j|` (distance in days), `rho` by profile maximum likelihood on [0, 0.95], slope and SE from the GLS fit; the interval is normal. This is a different estimator (it also re-weights the point estimate) and is shown as such, never as a replacement of the released slope.
H. **Trend-misspecification control.** The slope on `months` is partially collinear with a smooth seasonal profile; so rho_1 is also computed after removing a smooth function of calendar day (cubic regression spline on day number with 4 interior knots at day-number quantiles, fitted jointly with the design). If rho falls to 0 only after the spline, the dependence is read as slow-varying mean structure (season / drift), not as short-memory noise. Reported beside A-G; the spline model is NOT used for any slope interval (it would absorb the trend being tested).

## Reporting rules (pre-registered)
1. Fixed order and identical format for the new rows, appended after the M406 rows: HAC L=3, HAC L=7, HAC L=14, moving block 7 d, moving block 14 d, GLS. No primary interval and no envelope (as the M406 ruling); the L=7 label is "weekly-scale truncation", not "preferred".
2. Disagreement is reported, not tuned: a row is never dropped or its parameters changed after the result is seen. Truncations and block lengths above are the full set.
3. Interpretation rules, fixed now (Rev 1 replaces the Rev 0 text, see below):
   - "Serial dependence at the day scale is supported" iff the lower bound of the 95 % pairs-resampling CI of `rho_{1-3}` is above 0. Otherwise: "not supported by these data at this precision" (never "absent", never "CR1 adequate"). The HAC(L=7)/CR1 standard-error ratio is reported as a point value and never promotes or demotes any row.
   - Classification of an interval that excludes zero: "procedure-dependent" if ANY row (the five M406 rows or the new OLS-based rows HAC L=3/7/14, moving block 7/14 d) includes zero. There is no "excluded under every procedure" class. GLS (G) is a different estimator, is shown beside and takes no part in the rule. For the resistance proxy, any row excluding zero is reported as "procedure-dependent", never as a trend.
   - H (Rev 1): see below; applied only if the first rule is "supported".
4. Wording: sensitivities of conditional intervals; they do not remove season, logging-regime or calendar-drift confounding and do not change the released estimators or the TOST decision. "Estimated"; never "measured" or "significant/confirmed/robust/stable/detected"; W3-mde and W3-power80 unchanged. Standing statements ("trend not resolved; equivalence not established; no stability or SOH claim") unchanged.
5. Also reported, flagged: n pairs per distance, the number of days without a neighbour within 3 days, and the share of observations on days with a single drive.

## Known-answer harness (to be built with the implementation; in CI)
- White noise on the real day grid (the actual gap pattern, 2000 replications): rho_{1-3} centred on 0, HAC(L=7)/CR1 ratio near 1, nominal coverage within Monte Carlo error for all rows.
- AR(1) noise with a known phi (0.3 and 0.6 per day, gap-aware correlation phi^distance) on the real day grid: the pooled rho estimate recovers phi within Monte Carlo error at distance 1; HAC L=7 coverage closer to nominal than CR1; GLS rho recovers phi; the VIF diagnostic F agrees with the simulated variance ratio within 15 %.
- A smooth seasonal mean plus white noise: rho_{1-3} > 0 before and about 0 after the spline (control H).
- Moving-block bootstrap on iid data: width ratio to the day-cluster pairs bootstrap near 1.
- Hand-computed small cases for the Bartlett-kernel HAC (3 observations, 2 days) and the semivariogram bins.
- Singular draws counted and skipped, as in M406.

## Limits (disclosed in advance)
One vehicle, May-October; irregular logging means most day pairs at distance 1-3 days come from the dense weeks and few from gaps, so rho at larger distances is imprecise; a smooth drift and positive residual autocorrelation are not separable without a model of the mean (H addresses one direction only); HAC and block procedures are asymptotic and the effective number of blocks is about 22 weeks; GLS assumes a stationary Gaussian AR(1); none of these procedures removes confounding by season, logging regime or calendar. Multiple rows are shown precisely so that no single one is selected by outcome.

## Rev 1 (Director review of the pre-registration, 2026-10-12; applied before any code or result)
Director verdict: "revise" (methodology only; no Andrii sign-off needed for the spec). The following replace the Rev 0 text where they conflict; all are fixed now.
1. **Disclosure.** The spec was written knowing the self-audit lag-1 value (about 0.25) and the M406 interval bounds; the rules below were therefore fixed to be one-directional and exhaustive, not tuned to a known outcome.
2. **B (autocorrelation).** Each day pair is assigned to the ISO week of its FIRST day; the bootstrap resamples whole weeks' pair-sets (so cross-week pairs are kept) and the residuals are refitted from the design in every draw. 95 % percentile CI; `rho_{1-3}` is also reported with the null mean from the harness (residual rho is not exactly 0 under the null).
3. **D (HAC).** Weights `w(d) = 1 - d/(L+1)` for calendar-day distance `d <= L` (Bartlett), same-day observations weight 1, small-sample factor as CR1; intervals normal and `t(W-1)` with W the number of ISO weeks with data (the t row is the conservative one; the normal row is shown beside it).
4. **E (moving block).** Overlapping blocks: block start uniform on `0..D-L` (D = number of calendar days spanned), `ceil(D/L)` blocks per draw, observations in a block are those whose day falls inside; the range of the resulting draw sizes is reported. L = 7 and 14 only. (The Rev 0 words "non-overlapping" are withdrawn.)
5. **F (VIF diagnostic).** `VIF = x' Omega x / x' x`, `Omega_ij = phi^{|t_i - t_j|}` at the day level on the realised calendar days, `x` = months residualised on the other covariates; no truncation of c_d. Diagnostic ratio only.
6. **G (GLS).** REML for `rho`, interval `t(W-1)`; shown as a separate estimator, outside every rule; wording never "corrected".
7. **H (misspecification control).** Fit `y ~ T + I + s(day)` WITHOUT the months column (cubic regression spline, 4 interior knots at day-number quantiles; no linear term, so no collinearity with months). Applied only if the B rule is "supported". Read as: "slow-varying" iff the paired bootstrap CI of `rho_pre - rho_post` excludes 0 AND `rho_post < 0.5 rho_pre`; "short-memory" iff the CI of `rho_post` is above 0; otherwise "not separable at this precision". The sentence never says "the dependence is season".
8. **Harness.** Uses the real regressor matrix X; reports the null mean of rho and requires |bias| <= 0.05; publishes the coverage of every row and flags (never tunes) failures; the rules R3 and H must misclassify at most 10 % of replications under AR(1) and under season-plus-noise, otherwise the spec is revised before use. Nominal coverage is reported, not required row by row.
9. **Wording risks recorded:** "not supported" is not "no dependence"; no "excluded under every procedure"; H is not "the dependence is season"; the GLS slope is not "corrected".
