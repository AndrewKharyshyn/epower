# M380 spec (Rev 1): regime-stratified view and regime-covariate re-run of the gross-throughput decision figures

Status: work in progress, Director review pending. Nothing implemented. Facts read on 2026-10-05 (master MD5 7c3bc9883f223e8a488b84ad053e13b5, 524 drives). Origin: Director follow-up of M379b: "a regime-stratified report and a regime covariate for every time-trend, seasonal or TOST figure that uses gross throughput (GTC, FCE, rainflow EFC, kWh/100 km); escalate if any decision flips".

## 1. Facts
- Inventory (read-only, from the code; not run): no trend of any quantity against cumulative GTC exists, and the cell-spread and resistance trends do not use gross-throughput columns (the resistance trend already carries its own `cadenceControlled` variant, M170). The decision-bearing gross-throughput figures are: (a) `seasonalCharts.charts.EfficChart.adjusted.warm_vs_shoulder` and `.warm_vs_cold` (covariate-adjusted day-clustered bootstrap contrasts of gross throughput per km, `cohort_arrays.py`, `seasonal_adjust.adjusted_contrast`, covariates speed, stationary %, % highway, distance, categorical drive type); (b) `EnergyIntensity.data.{all, warm, shoulder, cold}` (`value` ratio of sums kWh/100 km, day-clustered CI; `refresh_seasonal_kpis.py`); (c) `riskColdEngineBattery.cmp.gtcPer100km` (Mann-Whitney, coolant split, not season; `compute_summary_arrays.py`); (d) projections that multiply gtc per km by annual km (`cycleLife`, `seasonalLife`, `fadeModes`, scenario / projection, not tests); (e) corpus totals (`meta.gtcSum`, `fceSum`, `rfEfcSum`, `grossThroughputKwh`, `metricConvention`, `energyPath`).
- Confounding structure (script output, `seasonal_drive_master.csv` with `cadence_regime.csv`, drives with an energy block): warm cohort 109 fast / 289 slow drives, fast share of gross throughput 19.7 %; shoulder 20 fast / 85 slow, 12.4 %; the 7 cold-insufficient drives (2 fast / 5 slow) are suppressed as a cohort. Both cohorts contain both regimes, so a regime indicator is identifiable inside each cohort. The fast runs are in time order (2026-08-14 to 09-06 warm-season; 2026-10-01 onward shoulder / cold), so regime and season are time-confounded.
- M379b (emulated sampling sensitivity, not a causal cadence effect): gross throughput emulated / original 0.974 (95 % CI 0.973-0.976) at the slow-regime interval distribution, 0.908 at twice the intervals.

## 2. Plan
P1 Confounding table (script): cohort x regime counts, days, share of gross throughput and of km, from the same drive set as each figure.
P2 Regime-stratified view: for `EnergyIntensity` (all, warm, shoulder) the value per regime within each cohort (ratio of sums kWh/100 km) with the day-clustered percentile bootstrap CI (seed 42, 4000 draws) and n (drives, days); labelled observational (confounded by season, ambient and drive mix).
P3 Regime-covariate re-run (read-only, scratch): `adjusted_contrast` for warm vs shoulder (and warm vs cold if available) with the logger-cadence regime added as an additional categorical covariate next to drive type; report original vs regime-adjusted adjusted difference, CI, common-support n and decision (CI excludes 0 or not), unchanged estimator code and seed.
P4 Slow-equivalent sensitivity (descriptive, not a correction): scale the gross throughput of fast-regime drives by r (central 0.974 = the M379b slow-level ratio; bounding 0.908 = the factor-2 ratio) and recompute `EnergyIntensity` (value, CI) and the P3 contrasts with the original estimator and seed; report each shift in units of the original CI half-width.
P5 Totals and projections (arithmetic only, nothing recomputed): relative change of the corpus gross-throughput sum and of the per-class gtc per km implied by r and the fast share (corpus 19.0 %), and the proportional consequence for the gtc-per-km-driven projections (`cycleLife`, `seasonalLife`, `fadeModes` crossing years scale inversely with the rate), stated as arithmetic under the emulation.
P6 `riskColdEngineBattery.cmp.gtcPer100km` (Mann-Whitney): the test re-run on the same inputs with the fast drives scaled by r (central and bounding) and stratified by regime; report p and decision before / after.
P7 Decision rule (frozen before any run): a DECISION FLIP is (a) a contrast CI or a Mann-Whitney p that changes side of 0 / 0.05 between original and any P3 / P4 / P6 variant, or (b) a change of `status` of an adjusted contrast; any flip is a STOP for the Director. A shift above one quarter of the original CI half-width is flagged "material to report"; smaller shifts are reported as "below the quarter-half-width rule" (necessary but not sufficient evidence of robustness, to be worded so).
P8 Outputs: `analyses/M380_regime_stratified.json` (script-written; provenance: master MD5, spec sha256, script sha256), an additive payload block `loggerCadence.stratified` (frozen summary with its basis, rebuilt by the `cadence_regime` stage from the committed file, as for M379b), a dashboard sentence in the cadence paragraph and a line next to the EnergyIntensity and EfficChart contrasts, bound to the payload. Blind audit (analytical-auditor, definitions only): the confounding table, the regime-stratified EI of one cohort, and the sign / side of one P3 contrast.

## 3. Out of scope
Changing any published figure or the estimators; the generator / traction family (f_gen, eta_bus; separate follow-up with `recon_engine` emulation); the article.
