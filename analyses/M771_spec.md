# M771 spec: weather proxies from the cloud export (wipers, lighting) and the drive-level energy figures: support gate and association look (report-only)

Rev 1, 2026-11-24. Committed BEFORE any tool is written or run. Author: main session (Sonnet). Owner request (Andrii, chat, 2026-11-24, item 4): "Wipers and lighting: A proxy analysis has to be performed to detect the weather influence on car's performance." Parents: `analyses/M729_spec.md` (cloud channels: wipers = precipitation, spray or washing proxy; lighting = visibility and darkness proxy), `analyses/M729_cloud_coverage.csv` (per-drive shares of moving cloud rows: `wiper_nonzero_share`, `lowbeam_share`, `highbeam_share`, `drl_share`, `position_lamp_share`, `front_fog_share`, `rear_fog_share`), M769 and M770 (design pattern: pre-registered expectation, outcome-blind sizing, lower-end reading rule, report-only, null results as legitimate). Next step: Director review, then tool and known-answer tests, blind audit, Director decision, CHANGELOG, PR. No payload, dashboard or published-figure change.

## 0. What the data hold (counted before this spec; counts only, no outcome read)
Of 717 drives, 662 have moving cloud rows. Any wiper activity: 49 drives; wiper share of moving rows at least 0.2: 8 drives; of those only 6 on the fuel basis (489 drives). Low beam on at least 0.2 of moving rows: 234 drives (darkness proxy; daytime running lights are on in 66 % of drives and carry no information). The wet proxy is therefore very thin: a fuel-basis confirmatory estimate is not supportable; the battery-side outcomes of the master exist for all drives. The study is designed as a SUPPORT GATE first and an association look second.

## 1. Constraints
- Report-only. `drive_master.csv` MD5 d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98 asserted before and after (a different master stops, exit 3, nothing written); `analyses/M729_cloud_coverage.csv` and `fuel_recon_master.csv` pinned by sha256; no winter-frozen file (M726); no absolute cloud odometer values, no positions or tracks used or written. Outputs `analyses/M771_*`: aggregates only, no per-drive rows, dates or file names.
- Language: wipers are "a precipitation, spray or washing proxy (the channel does not distinguish them)"; lighting a "darkness and visibility proxy"; never "rain" or "wet road" as a measured state; fuel "logged / app-calculated"; battery-side energy "estimated, integrated from logged current"; "associated with", never "effect of weather". No equivalence test; a null is "not informative at this support".
- Statistics: calendar-day cluster bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws); every estimate with n (drives, days) and an MDE (2.8 x bootstrap SD); ONE confirmatory estimand (A1); `design_mde` written before any estimate; the expectation and reading rule fixed before any result.

## 2. Definitions
- **Wet-proxy drive:** `wiper_nonzero_share` at least 0.05 (primary) of the moving cloud rows; sensitivity thresholds 0.02 and 0.20; dry reference: share exactly 0 on a drive with at least 200 moving rows. Drives with a share between 0 and the threshold are excluded from the contrast (reported as counts).
- **Dark-proxy drive:** `lowbeam_share` at least 0.5 of the moving cloud rows; reference: share below 0.05.
- **Eligible:** canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` false), distance at least 5 km (2 and 10 km sensitivities as point estimates), cloud moving rows at least 200, covariates complete.
- **Covariates:** drive class (4), thermal class, ambient (time-mean), log distance, mean moving speed and its square, month (to separate season from the proxy), first-drive-of-day. Engine-on share is not a covariate (mediator).

## 3. Estimands
- **G (support gate, written first):** counts of wet-proxy and dark-proxy drives by class, thermal class and month, the number of days, and the matched-pair support (a wet-proxy drive is matchable when a dry drive of the same class, thermal class and month with distance within 25 % exists on another day); the design MDE of A1 from the outcome SD, the proxy prevalence and the effective days; the verdict "supported" only if A1's design MDE is at most half of the lower end of the expectation, else "not supported at this support: A1 is written as counts only".
- **A1 (confirmatory, battery-side):** the adjusted difference (wet-proxy minus dry) of the master's net battery draw per 100 km, corrected (`net_draw_per100km_corr2p`), in kWh per 100 km and as a percentage of the dry mean, on all eligible drives (not only the fuel basis), day-clustered interval, MDE. Pre-registered expectation (stated before any result, assumed): precipitation raises the road load by wet-road rolling resistance and spray (about +3 to +10 % of the traction energy), the wipers and defogging add about 0.1 to 0.3 kW of auxiliary power; the net battery draw per 100 km is therefore expected to rise by about +2 % to +8 %. Reading rule: informative only if the MDE is at most half of the LOWER end (2 %). Wrong-sign: "inconsistent with the expected sign, confounding suspected".
- **A2 (exploratory):** the same difference for the regeneration share of the charge, gross discharge per 100 km, and, where the fuel basis exists (6 wet-proxy drives at the 0.2 threshold), fuel L/100 km as COUNTS ONLY (no estimate below 20 wet-proxy drives and 10 days).
- **A3 (exploratory, darkness proxy):** the same differences for the dark proxy; the physical expectation is small (lights are a few tens of watts) and the proxy mainly marks evening and winter driving; labelled so.
- **P (placebo):** displaced-day placebo as in M770 (the proxy label of another day within the same class, thermal class and month, distance within 25 %); no ratio rule; the placebo interval is shown.
- **C (integrity):** channel coverage by month, the share of drives with no cloud rows, wiper activity by month (does it cluster in specific months), the comparison of drives with and without cloud rows on class, ambient and distance.

## 4. Falsification and acceptance
Known-answer tests before the first run (one CI-list line): a planted wet-proxy difference is recovered inside its interval through a planted season confounder; the proxy definitions classify planted shares exactly (thresholds on the boundary); the matched-pair support counter is exact; the design MDE and reading rule apply the lower end; the displaced-day placebo uses only other days of the same class, thermal class and month; a foreign master stops; no per-drive rows, dates or names in a result. 'Not supported at this support' is a legitimate outcome and likely.

## 5. Not in scope
No dashboard binding or article use. No weather-service data (no external data are imported). No per-drive rain label. No change of any reconstruction constant. Positions, tracks and the cloud odometer are not used.

## 6. Questions for the Director
(a) Is A1 on the battery-side net draw (all drives) the right single confirmatory estimand given that the fuel basis holds only 6 wet-proxy drives? (b) Is the expectation bracket (+2 % to +8 %) and the lower-end rule defensible, or should it be derived differently? (c) Are the thresholds (wet: share at least 0.05 versus exactly 0; dark: at least 0.5 versus below 0.05) and the matched-pair support rule sound, and is the support gate in front of A1 the right design? (d) Does the wiper channel's inability to distinguish rain, spray and washing allow any wording beyond "proxy"? (e) Should month enter as a covariate (it absorbs the seasonality but also most of the wet-proxy variation)?
