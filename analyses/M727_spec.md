# M727 spec Rev 2 (pre-registered before any winter scoring result and before the frozen-basis consistency check is run): extractors of the observed quantities of the M717 winter predictions P2, P3, P5, P7

Status: Rev 2 after the Director review of Rev 1 (decision "revise", six changes, all adopted; record `analyses/M727_director_review_rev1.json`). No observation after the freeze is used to build or tune anything in this milestone. Author: main session. Origin: M726 (Director decision: the extractors are owed before the first scoring look and before any cell reaches support; build and test on synthetic or frozen-basis data only, never on post-freeze observations).

## 1. Scope and split
M726 left every cell of P1-P7 'not evaluable (extractor not frozen)'. This milestone writes, specifies and freezes the extractors of P2 (Ah share below 10 C), P3 (resistance-proxy ratio at 0 C), P5 (Ah per km in the cold, against autumn) and P7 (engine-on share against ambient). P1 (state-model chain), P4 (BMS charge-limit register) and P6 (peak charge C-rate within the limit map) are a separate milestone (M728), together with a realised-ambient P2 band from a frozen scenario-engine copy as a secondary. `winter_score.py` v2 scores only the predictions listed in the registry; the others stay 'not evaluable (extractor not frozen)'. No claim that the cold outputs are supported is made until P1 is scored.

## 2. Common rules (inherited from M726, frozen)
Scoring set = drives that start after F + 1 h (F = 2026-11-17T19:01:29+02:00) from `analyses/M713/exposure_drives.csv` joined to the master and `seasonal_drive_master.csv` on the drive key; clean = `ens_outlier_v2` not True (a missing flag is kept, M707 B1). Interval: day-clustered percentile bootstrap (calendar day = cluster), seed 42, 4000 draws, 95 %. Support: at least 10 drives on at least 5 days per cell, else 'not evaluable' and no number. Region label: 'interpolation check' inside the temperature or ambient range of the frozen basis (pack probe minimum 1.0 C; drive-mean ambient -1.0 to 37.0 C), else 'extrapolation test'. The frozen predictions, bands and parameters are read ONLY from the freeze file and the frozen copies (M726 loader); no live file. The unit of analysis is the drive.

## 3. Extractors (all numbers, windows, classes, weights and thresholds are frozen here; alternatives are labelled sensitivities and never change a status)
P2. Observed: pooled Ah share below 10 C of the scoring set = sum over drives of Ah (charge plus discharge) with pack temperature below 10 C / sum of total Ah, ratio of sums, from the frame columns `ahc_T_<0, ahc_T_0-5, ahc_T_5-10` and the discharge counterparts `ahd_*` over `ah_chg + ah_dis`: the same Ah definition and the same pack-temperature source (the pack-probe mean of the exposure frame) as the scenario quantity `E_real.ahShare_T_lt10` that defines the band. Rule (pre-registered): the day-cluster 95 % interval lies inside the frozen band [0.0316, 0.1476] -> 'within the band'; wholly outside -> 'outside the band'; otherwise 'inconclusive'. Disclosed deviation from the freeze text (not a change of the prediction): the freeze says the band is "computed for the REALISED ambient when it is known"; that needs the live scenario engine, which the scoring code must not read, so P2 is "scored against the frozen band, not the realised-ambient band". The frozen band is the union of the scenario bands S2 (balanced year), S3a (mild winter) and S3b (cold winter) over annual scenario years, wide and not a winter window: the comparison is lenient and is labelled optimistic-leaning. The scoring window is the scoring set whatever its calendar months.
P3. Observed: the pulse-resistance proxy ratio at 0 C against its 25 C value. Pulses: `R_pulse_mohm` and `T_pulse` of the scoring-set drives, pulse temperature in the MAIN window [-2, +2] C (temperatures in kelvin in the formulae). Each pulse is brought to 0 C with the frozen activation energy and reference: ratio_i = R_pulse_i / R25_frozen x exp(-EaR / Rgas x (1/T_i - 1/273.15)), a correction below 2 K; estimate = unweighted mean of ratio_i over drives (each drive one pulse), day-clustered interval. Reported beside, never the status: the sensitivity window [-5, +3] C, the UNCORRECTED ratio R_pulse_i / R25_frozen, and the ratio by temperature bin. Pulses with T_pulse below 1.0 C carry the label 'extrapolation test'. Rule (pre-registered): primary = the POINT estimate lies inside the frozen interval [1.833, 1.992] (the freeze's own wording: "lies inside the interval"); reported beside: whether the observed day-cluster interval lies inside it. Disclosed limits: the test is conditional on the frozen single-activation-energy form in the window; it also tests the frozen R25, and ageing is confounded with temperature (a drift of the 25 C level is not separated).
P5. Observed: the cooling slope of ln(Ah per km), winter against autumn. Sets: winter = scoring-set drives, autumn = drives of `month` 9 to 11 (Sep-Nov) that start before F - 1 h; both sets with the prototype filters (`km > 0.5`, `ah > 0`, `spd_s > 60`, ambient present) and the prototype ambient `A = (ambient_start_c + ambient_end_c) / 2`; winter drives need A below 20 C. Classes: distance below 3 km, 3 to 10 km, 10 km and above, crossed with mean speed vmean = km / dur_h below 30, 30 to 60, 60 km/h and above; only cells with at least 3 drives in BOTH sets are used and the share of winter drives dropped by that rule is reported; autumn cells are weighted to the winter cell counts. Statistic: dln = weighted mean ln(Ah / km) of winter minus the same of autumn; dT = mean autumn A minus mean winter A; the rule statistic is s = dln - ln(1.06) / 10 x dT (the +6 % per 10 C lower end of the alternative of the freeze, ln(1.06) / 10 = 0.005827 per C). Day-clustered bootstrap with the days of the two sets resampled INDEPENDENTLY; pre-registered bound: the 2.5th percentile of s (conservative, the lower end of the two-sided 95 % interval). The prediction ("winter Ah per km is NOT higher than autumn") is 'rejected' iff that 2.5th percentile is above zero, else 'not rejected'. 'Not rejected' is not 'no increase'. dT below 3 C -> 'not evaluable'. An association (season, route and period move together), not a temperature effect.
P7. Observed: the slope of the engine-on share on cooling over the prototype set: filters `km > 0.5`, `ah > 0`, `spd_s > 60`, ambient present, A = (ambient_start_c + ambient_end_c) / 2 below 20 C, y = 100 x eng_on_s / max(rpm_s, 1) (points), OLS on [1, A, ln km, vmean = km / dur_h], day-clustered interval; the cooling slope is minus the coefficient of A. Not `engine_on_pct`, not `speed_mean_moving`, not `ambient_time_mean_c`. Minimum winter ambient spread: interquartile range of A at least 3 C, else 'not evaluable'. Rule (pre-registered): primary = the POINT estimate lies inside the frozen interval [0.566, 1.061] points per C of cooling; reported beside: whether the observed interval lies inside it. An association, not a temperature effect.

## 4. Check, build and freeze (no post-freeze observation)
Built in `tools/winter_extractors.py`, tested on synthetic frames with planted truth and on the FROZEN BASIS only (drives through 2026-11-09; the build masks any row after F). Pre-registered acceptance criteria of the consistency check on the frozen basis, written BEFORE it is run (every output of every run is stored): C-P7: the extractor reproduces the prototype slope -0.00812 per C and its day-cluster interval [-0.01061, -0.00566] on the prototype set within 1e-5 absolute; C-P3: on pulses with T_pulse in [23, 27] C the mean of R_pulse / R25_frozen lies in [0.95, 1.05]; C-P2: the extractor's Ah share below 10 C on the frozen basis equals the sum of the three lowest bins of the prototype's observed free-run share (`ahShareByTbin_obs`) within 0.005 absolute; C-P5: the estimator is new and has no prototype value, so it is run once as a placebo (autumn months September against October-November, same filters and classes) and reported with NO tolerance. One revision is allowed after the first run, limited to an implementation mismatch with the documented prototype (a wrong column, a filter), recorded as a diff and a new spec Rev; windows, classes, weights and thresholds must NOT change after the check. A criterion that fails after the one revision is reported and the extractor of that prediction is NOT frozen (its cell stays 'not evaluable').
Freeze: `analyses/M726_extractor_registry.json`, written by a script, with per prediction id: function name, version, column list, the freeze file sha256, this spec's sha256 and the `winter_score.py` v2 sha256; `modules` (path to sha256); its own sha256 is recorded in the CHANGELOG. `winter_score.extractors_frozen(pid)` (v2) requires pid in the registry and every module sha256 equal to the registry. The registry and `tools/winter_extractors.py` join the read-only set of `tools/guard_paths.py`. After the freeze no extractor is edited; a change is a new version with a new registry.

## 5. Acceptance
A1 known-answer tests (CI list): each extractor on synthetic data with planted truth within 1e-9 (the ratio of sums, ratio_i, the matched-cell statistic, the OLS slope); the cell rules ('not evaluable' below support, a winter-autumn ambient difference below 3 C, a winter ambient IQR below 3 C, cells with fewer than 3 drives in a set dropped and their share reported); refusal of live files; a drive inside the F +- 1 h window is excluded; v2 behaviour: P1, P4, P6 still 'not evaluable', a module sha256 mismatch is refused.
A2 the consistency report (C-P7, C-P3, C-P2, C-P5 placebo) is script-written and stored; no post-F observation is read by the build or the tests (the scoring set may be empty and the extractors then answer 'not evaluable').
A3 blind reproduction by `analytical-auditor` of the four extractors on the frozen basis and on a planted synthetic frame (definitions in words only): each output within 1e-6 of the claim.
A4 language: model-derived; logged / app-calculated, never 'measured'; associations, not temperature effects (P5, P7); 'not evaluable' is not 'no effect'; 'not rejected' is not 'no increase'; P2 is 'scored against the frozen band, not the realised-ambient band'; never 'validated', 'confirmed', 'robust', 'proven'.
A5 `release_check.py` and the CI python / js lists green; the registry sha256 and the frozen sha256 values are in the CHANGELOG entry; the P2 deviation from the freeze text is recorded in it. Andrii is informed (tooling, no dashboard or article change); no sign-off is needed.
