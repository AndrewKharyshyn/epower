# M339 Fuel analytics v2 (FUEL-03 stationary vs moving fuel, FUEL-02 warm-up trajectory): pre-registered spec rev 2 (Director changes applied; work in progress)

Origin: audit v3 FUEL-02 and FUEL-03; agreed order (FUEL-04 is separate: it depends on the unverified CAP 2.1 kWh). Predecessor: M337 (`fuelAnalytics`: eligibility ELIG-A, rate integral on the rate series' own timestamps, day-clustered bootstrap, support rules, F03 labels). No new estimand beyond logged-fuel litres by state and by position. Existing views (ThermalFuelPenalty, SocBalancedFuel, GTR) are NOT changed; see decision D1.

## 0. Language and basis (binding)
Logged/app-calculated fuel (Car Scanner ELM OBD2). States are temporal states of the logged rate, never exclusive fuel-source allocations, never "waste", never "idle penalty": use "stationary fuel" and "fuel consumed during net pack charging". Curves and bands are associations unless a causal design is stated (none is). Basis raw/ (F03 provenance-sensitive), not M299-reproducible. No energy, cost, BSFC or generator quantity.

## 1. Eligibility and clock (shared with M337)
Trip eligibility = M337 analysis set (ELIG-A, canonical-clean, no counter reset; expected 242 trips). Common clock = the rate series' own valid sample times inside the trip's counter window, per-sample dt capped at 5 s (sensitivity 2 s and 10 s). Speed is taken per rate sample from the pipeline speed channel (VCM speed, else the OBD speed fallback, as recon_engine/_hf_perdrive use) with the pipeline's nearest-sample tolerance of 1.5 s; a rate sample without a speed sample inside the tolerance has state "unknown speed". The speed source per trip (VCM or OBD) is recorded. Distance along the trip = time-integral of the same speed series (km), reported next to the master `distance_km` for each trip (the difference is flagged, not corrected).

## 2. FUEL-03: fuel by speed state
- States per rate sample: stationary (speed <= 1.0 km/h), moving (> 1.0 km/h), unknown. Sensitivity thresholds 0.5, 2 and 3 km/h; dwell sensitivity: stationary only if the speed stays <= threshold for >= 5 s continuously (shorter pauses reclassified as moving).
- Per trip: litres in each state (rate integral on the common clock), total litres (the same integral); acceptance: sum of state litres = total integral within a relative tolerance of 1e-9 for every trip (script-asserted); the counter-based V_cnt (M337) is shown next to it, not mixed.
- Optional sub-classification (only where valid HV current and voltage exist and the battery offset is available, i.e. `I_offset_A_applied` not NaN; the excluded trips are counted): within each speed state, litres while net pack power is charging (battery power < -0.1 kW, discharge-positive frame as recon_engine) vs not charging; labelled "fuel consumed during net pack charging", a temporal state.
- Corpus statistics (ratio-of-sums only): stationary share of litres = sum(L_stationary)/sum(L_total), moving share, unknown share, with day-clustered percentile bootstrap CIs (seed 42, 4000 draws, day = trip start date); by trip-length band (the M337 bands; >= 7 days for an interval, 5-6 low-cluster, < 5 points only) and by drive type; litres per band and shares in paired stacked bars (state colours fixed across modes); n (trips, days) on every estimate; the unknown share is always visible and its trips counted.
- Compare mode: cohort bars beside each other (warm, shoulder; cold n = 0 when unavailable), same state colours, cohort labels/borders.

## 3. FUEL-02: warm-up fuel trajectory
- Alignment event t0: the first rate sample with rpm > 400 AND rate > 0 in the trip ("first observed engine start of the trip"); trips with no such sample are excluded and counted. Initial temperature = first valid engine-coolant sample at or before t0 within the trip (oil as fallback; source flagged); missing initial temperature -> excluded from banded curves with a count. Bands: < 40 C, 40-60 C, >= 60 C (descriptive, not the model cold gate); the >= 60 C band is labelled "warm restart-like start" and the others "first starts from cooler conditions" (a label of the starting temperature, not of the soak history, which is not observed).
- Time axis: cumulative logged litres versus seconds since t0 on a 30 s grid to 600 s. Distance axis: cumulative litres versus km since t0 (speed integral) on a 0.25 km grid to 5 km. Interval consumption: litres per 100 km in successive 0.5 km moving intervals (moving samples only; stationary fuel tracked separately as its own cumulative curve).
- Censoring: a trip contributes to a grid position only if it reaches it (trip end or an unsupported gap > 5 s ends its contribution); no zero-filled continuation. Each curve reports the number of contributing trips AND distinct days at every position; positions with fewer than 10 trips or fewer than 7 days are not drawn as a CI curve (points only, labelled). Composition control: a second set of curves restricted to trips that reach the last displayed position ("constant population") is drawn beside the all-contributing curves, so longer trips cannot silently replace short trips along the curve.
- Statistic at a position: the mean cumulative litres per contributing trip (sum of litres / number of trips, day-clustered bootstrap); interval consumption = pooled ratio of summed litres to summed km in the interval (day-clustered CI). No fitted curve, no intercept, no "startup fuel", no penalty claim; the existing cold-start fuel-rate association (ThermalFuelPenalty) stays unchanged and is cross-referenced.
- Compare: same alignment, bands, grid and support rules per cohort (warm, shoulder), common axes; matched-condition differences are NOT in v2.
- Show both initial temperature and the thermal cohort (they describe different conditions).

## 4. Provenance (F03) and sensitivity
Hash-passing vs hash-failing trips side by side with n (trips, days), "not a paired test". The read-only raw-vs-originals check of M337 is extended to the speed and SoC/HV columns for the same files (read-only, nothing copied into raw/); the M337 permission covered the fuel columns only, so the extension runs only if Andrii allows it, otherwise the speed columns are reported as unchecked. Integration variants: dt cap 2/5/10 s, trapezoid; speed thresholds 0.5/1/2/3 km/h.

## 5. Implementation contract
`tools/fuel_analytics2.py` (new; imports helpers from `tools/fuel_analytics.py`), script-written additive payload keys `fuelStates` and `fuelWarmup` (artifact stamps as for `fuelAnalytics`), ingest stage after `fuel_analytics`; per-trip rows stored; known-answer synthetic tests (constant-rate trip with a scripted stop: stationary litres = rate x stop time; state sums = total; unknown state when speed is missing; censoring: a short trip ends its contribution and is not zero-filled; constant-population subset; threshold and dwell reclassification); dashboard: new components in the Fuel tab after the M337 sections, all figures bound to the payload; language-gate rules (forbidden: "idle waste", "wasted fuel", "idle penalty", "fuel wasted", "startup fuel", "warm-up penalty", "measured stationary fuel"; required: logged/app-calculated, temporal states, n (trips, days), support, F03 label) and semantic-gate REQUIRED checks; jsdom coverage for All/Warm/Shoulder/Compare/Cold; blind audit (claim + data path only) and Director decision before the dashboard build.

## 6. Decisions requested of the Director
D1 Extend the existing ThermalFuelPenalty builder (audit text) or keep v2 as separate keys with a cross-reference and decide the unification later? (Proposed: separate keys, because the existing block is an adjusted association with its own audit and moving it changes stored figures.)
D2 Is the 1.0 km/h stationary threshold and the first-rpm>400 alignment acceptable as primary, with the stated sensitivities?
D3 Is the "warm restart-like start" label for the >= 60 C band acceptable given that soak history is not observed?
D4 Stop conditions: stop for Director review if the stationary share of litres changes by more than 5 percentage points between thresholds 0.5 and 3 km/h, if the unknown share exceeds 10% of litres, or if state sums miss the 1e-9 tolerance on any trip.

## Rev 2 (Director verdict "revise": changes applied; these override the text above where they conflict)
- D1 accepted with a stated deviation: v2 uses SEPARATE keys (`fuelStates`, `fuelWarmup`) and does not extend the ThermalFuelPenalty builder, which departs from the audit's first-release scope; the deviation is recorded here and in the CHANGELOG, both blocks cross-reference each other, any disagreement with the ThermalFuelPenalty scalar is reported, and unification is logged as a follow-up.
- D2 accepted: 1.0 km/h primary only with the speed resolution per source (VCM, OBD) written by the script; if a source logs integer km/h, the 0.5 km/h threshold equals speed == 0 and the 0.5 versus 1.0 sensitivity is reported as the same bin. Alignment event (first rpm > 400 and rate > 0) accepted with left-censoring handling: trips whose first sample already has rpm > 400 are flagged, reported separately (not mixed into the first-start curves) or excluded with a count; pre-t0 time and the electric-only distance before t0 are recorded per trip.
- D3 rejected as worded: bands are labelled by initial coolant only ("initial coolant < 40 C", "40-60 C", ">= 60 C"); the words "restart" and "first start" are dropped. An optional soak descriptor from the previous master drive's end is allowed only if flagged "unlogged drives possible".
- D4 accepted plus four stops: (a) excluded trips (no t0 or no initial temperature) above 20% of the analysis set; (b) capped-integral litres differing by more than 2% between the dt caps 2 s and 10 s; (c) |speed-integral km - master distance_km| above 5% on more than 10% of trips; (d) a VCM-only sensitivity moving the stationary share by more than 2 percentage points versus the mixed-source primary.
- Clock: each rate sample's dt is the time since the PREVIOUS valid rate sample (backward interval, capped), the state is taken at the interval's end sample; litres and seconds removed by the cap are reported as coverage per trip and in total.
- The state-sum check (1e-9) is a partition identity and is kept only as a sanity assertion; the real acceptance tests are the agreement of the total state litres with the counter delta V_cnt (M337, reported as an aggregate with CI) and the coverage figures.
- Charging sub-state: the recon_engine battery-current sign convention (open item) is asserted by a known-answer test (a synthetic trip with a known charge-positive raw current must give a charging state); the applied `I_offset_A_applied` is estimated, and this is stated in the text.
- FUEL-02: initial-temperature staleness limit (maximum seconds before t0 that the coolant sample may lie) is script-written and recorded; censoring reasons are counted separately (trip end versus gap above 5 s); an intermediate fixed horizon (300 s and 2.5 km) is added to the constant-population curves besides 600 s and 5 km; CIs are labelled "pointwise"; the distance-axis cumulative curve is stated as total litres (including stationary litres) with the stationary and moving cumulative curves shown separately; the interval L/100 km is labelled "moving-sample".
- The expected trip count (242) is script-written and compared, not typed.
- Risks recorded: weekly composition and date confounding in the F03 split (labelled); the speed-column F03 extension needs Andrii's permission and is otherwise reported as unchecked.
