## 3. Definitions (frozen by this spec)
- Sustained movement start M0 of a drive: the first cloud speed sample inside the padded drive window that begins a run of at least 3 consecutive samples with speed above 0 km/h (gap between samples at most 35 s). Drives without it have no screened ambient. Pre-registered sensitivity: speed above 5 km/h instead of above 0.
- Screened in-drive ambient (primary): duration-weighted trapezoidal mean of cloud ambient samples at t >= M0 + 180 s up to the drive end (padded window), using only intervals whose adjacent samples are at most 35 s apart; requires at least 5 valid samples and at least 70 % of the post-cutoff time represented. Drift flag (flag-only): the last 60 s of samples span more than 1 C AND more than one display quantum; flagged drives are kept, the flag is reported, and they are excluded only in the "drift-free" variant. Sensitivity cutoffs: 120 s and 300 s. Early samples are retained and flagged, never deleted.
- Baseline comparison ambient: the same mean without the settling cutoff (first valid sample to end), as in the handoff baseline.
- Thermal class of a drive uses the published thresholds (the half-open bands [lo,hi) of `n02_seasonal_fits.BANDS`: below 5 C, 5 to 15 C, 15 C or warmer) on the manual (published) ambient; a "cohort switch" is a drive whose class changes if the screened ambient replaced the manual one. Physical-bin overrides in the master are reported, never changed.
- Parked gap: consecutive canonical-clean drives, gap 0.02 to 36 h (the Q3 definition of `n02_seasonal_fits.q3`), with the M729 gap class `stationary_consistent` for the cloud-screened subset; gaps containing cloud driving bursts (`driving_observed`, `odometer_increase_only`) are excluded from the screened subset and listed.

## 4. Estimands (pre-registered)
T1 Ambient reconciliation (descriptive). For drives with a screened ambient: signed and absolute difference screened minus manual (median, P90, share above 3 C), by thermal class and by month; cohort switches (count, list); coverage (drives with screened ambient, share of drive time represented, drives lost to the cutoff) at 120/180/300 s; share of drives flagged by the drift check. Day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws) intervals for the median difference; n drives and n days.

T2 Q1 and Q2 sensitivity. The shift is decomposed in three steps on the same resampled days: (i) published estimate (all drives, manual ambient); (ii) matched control = published estimator on the drives that have a screened ambient, manual ambient (subset effect); (iii) screened = the same drives with the screened ambient (ambient effect). The estimator is `n02_seasonal_fits.q1_q2` imported unchanged. Arms (ii) and (iii) are refit on the SAME bootstrap day draws (joint day-clustered percentile bootstrap, seed 42, 4000 draws); the ambient effect is (iii) minus (ii) with its paired interval. The drive-time ambient of Q1 and Q2 is replaced; `ambient_start_c` / `ambient_end_c` (the Q2 start-on-start design and Q3) are KEPT at their published values in T2 (cloud counterparts enter only in T3), and the output states this. Variants: 180 s primary; 120 s; 300 s; speed above 5 km/h start; drift-free only. Escalation to the Director applies only to the 180 s primary and only when the ambient effect (iii) minus (ii) has an interval excluding zero or the screened estimate lies outside the matched-control interval; all other variants are descriptive with no verdict. A Q2 slope change is read as an errors-in-variables sensitivity (two noisy ambient readings of one sensor), never as a correction. The output also reports how the screened subset differs from the published set (distance, duration, class mix, month mix). Nothing is called a temperature effect.

T3 Parked-pack temperature forcing (model comparison, no adoption). Screened cloud endpoint ambient: previous-drive end = screened mean over the last 10 minutes of samples taken after M0+180 s (at least 5 samples, else the gap has no cloud endpoint and is excluded from F2-F4); next-drive start = screened mean over the first 10 minutes after M0+180 s of the next drive (samples before the cutoff are never used, which handles heat-soak of the sensor after parking). The next-start ambient is observed together with the pack outcome, so this is a consistency comparison, not a forecast; this holds for every variant including F0 and is stated, not corrected. On the Q3 gap set (restricted to the `stationary_consistent` subset with cloud-supported start and end ambient). Reference model F0 = the first-order model with constant forcing = mean of the previous end and next start ambient and tau REFIT PER FOLD (primary); the published `seasonalFits.Q3` tau is in-sample and is shown only as a labelled in-sample reference. Variants:
- F1 manual endpoints, constant forcing (control, must reproduce F0);
- F2 screened cloud endpoints, constant forcing;
- F3 screened cloud endpoints, linear-in-time forcing A(t) = A0 + b t with b = (A1 - A0)/g (A0 end ambient, A1 next-start ambient, g gap in hours); exact solution of dT/dt = -k (T - A(t)), k = 1/tau: T(g) = A0 + b g - b/k + (T0 - A0 + b/k) exp(-k g); the known-answer test checks it against numerical integration;
- F4 screened cloud endpoints with the M709 diurnal-sinusoid boundary (the `tools/park_model.py` M2 form, parameters refit per training fold; fresh call of its public functions, no edit).
Comparators: persistence (M1) and full relaxation to the mean ambient. Outcome: next-drive-start pack-probe mean (4 probes, logged), absolute error. Validation: the published fold construction of `n02_seasonal_fits.q3` (contiguous arrival-day blocks, same k) is primary; the month-blocked May-August fit / September-November test is secondary, a transfer check that mixes season with the hold-out and is never a clean hold-out. Report per ambient band (cold <= 5, shoulder, warm >= 15 on the mean gap ambient): median absolute error, P90, mean signed error, empirical coverage of 80 % and 90 % absolute-error intervals by split-conformal quantiles (index ceil((n+1)(1-a)) of training-fold absolute residuals, pooled across bands), coverage per band with day-clustered intervals and by gap-length tercile, n gaps, n days. Undercoverage in the cold band is reported, never recalibrated. Only the primary contrast (F2 minus F0 on the primary folds) carries a verdict; F3, F4 and the secondary split are descriptive. Paired day-clustered difference of absolute error (F2 minus F0, F3 minus F0, F4 minus F0) with intervals. ERA5-Land hourly forcing is NOT used here (needs an external download and an agreed parking location; deferred to its own spec).
Second thermal state: not fitted. Pre-registered trigger only: the eight warm-rise gaps (next-start pack mean more than 1 C above the previous end; list regenerated on the live corpus) are audited against a CLOSED list of alternative explanations, each with a script-written criterion: A1 screened ambient rise of at least 2 C between the end and the next start; A2 first valid probe reading of the next drive later than 60 s after its log start; A3 probe spread above 2 C at either boundary; A4 cloud speed above 0 inside the gap (activity between logs). A case with none of A1-A4 is "unexplained". A second-state fit becomes a later milestone only if at least 5 cases on at least 3 days are unexplained; the blind auditor adjudicates the list. An existing P-R2 result is not reinterpreted.

T4 Coolant anchor feasibility (counts and residuals only). Cloud coolant snapshot within 30 s before to 60 s after each drive end; paired residual (cloud minus OBD coolant within 15 s) by phase (inside the log, after the log end) and age; counts of cloud-only bursts with an end snapshot (from M729). No coolant model is fitted here.

## 6. Language

## 9. Rev 2.1 clarifications (written AFTER the first run and the blind reproduction; no threshold, estimand or decision rule changed)
The blind reproduction (analytical-auditor) reproduced C1 and C2 and found C3 sensitive to two rules that the text of Rev 2 left open. The rules below are exactly what `tools/mynissan_thermal.py` implemented in the first run; they are recorded, not chosen after seeing a preferred result. The audit disagreement on the C3 gap set (349 gaps / 115 days under the auditor's reading, against 321 / 113 here; difference of mean absolute error -0.054 against -0.060, same sign, intervals overlapping) is reported in the CHANGELOG.
- Screened in-drive ambient (T1, T2): samples are taken from M0 + cutoff up to the PADDED end (drive end + 60 s); the 70 % represented-time fraction uses the UNPADDED drive end as its denominator (drive end minus (M0 + cutoff)); at least 5 samples counted over the padded range.
- Gap endpoint ambient (T3): previous-drive end = samples in [max(M0 + 180 s, padded end - 600 s), padded end]; next-drive start = samples in [M0 + 180 s, min(M0 + 780 s, padded end)]. Each window needs at least 5 samples and at least 70 % of the SPAN of the selected samples (first to last) represented by adjacent samples at most 35 s apart. A drive without a sustained-movement start M0 has no cloud endpoint.
- Gap inclusion for F0 to F4: both drives in the primary set; clean; gap 0.02 to 36 h; probe endpoints (previous end, next start) and both manual ambients present; M729 gap class `stationary_consistent`; both cloud endpoints present. Folds: 10 contiguous blocks of arrival days of THIS gap set.
