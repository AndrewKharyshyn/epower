## 3. Definitions (frozen by this spec)
- Sustained movement start M0 of a drive: the first cloud speed sample inside the padded drive window that begins a run of at least 3 consecutive samples with speed above 0 km/h (gap between samples at most 35 s). Drives without it have no screened ambient.
- Screened in-drive ambient (primary): duration-weighted trapezoidal mean of cloud ambient samples at t >= M0 + 180 s up to the drive end (padded window), using only intervals whose adjacent samples are at most 35 s apart; requires at least 5 valid samples and at least 70 % of the post-cutoff time represented. Drift check: the last 60 s of samples span at most 1 C, otherwise the drive is flagged (kept, flag reported, excluded in the "drift-free" variant). Sensitivity cutoffs: 120 s and 300 s. Early samples are retained and flagged, never deleted.
- Baseline comparison ambient: the same mean without the settling cutoff (first valid sample to end), as in the handoff baseline.
- Thermal class of a drive uses the published thresholds (cold <= 5 C, shoulder 5 to 15 C, warm >= 15 C) on the manual (published) ambient; a "cohort switch" is a drive whose class changes if the screened ambient replaced the manual one. Physical-bin overrides in the master are reported, never changed.
- Parked gap: consecutive canonical-clean drives, gap 0.02 to 36 h (the Q3 definition of `n02_seasonal_fits.q3`), with the M729 gap class `stationary_consistent` for the cloud-screened subset; gaps containing cloud driving bursts (`driving_observed`, `odometer_increase_only`) are excluded from the screened subset and listed.

## 4. Estimands (pre-registered)
T1 Ambient reconciliation (descriptive). For drives with a screened ambient: signed and absolute difference screened minus manual (median, P90, share above 3 C), by thermal class and by month; cohort switches (count, list); coverage (drives with screened ambient, share of drive time represented, drives lost to the cutoff) at 120/180/300 s; share of drives flagged by the drift check. Day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws) intervals for the median difference; n drives and n days.

T2 Q1 and Q2 sensitivity. Re-run the published estimators `n02_seasonal_fits.q1_q2` (imported unchanged, `nb = 4000`) on the same drives with the manual ambient column replaced by the screened ambient (variants: 180 s primary; 120 s; 300 s; drift-free only; manual-ambient-with-screened-subset as the matched control). Report for each: estimate, interval, n drives, n days, and the difference to the published estimate with a day-clustered paired-difference interval on the common drives. Reading rule: the shift is "inside" if the screened point estimate lies inside the published interval AND the paired-difference interval contains zero; otherwise "outside" and the case is escalated to the Director (CLAUDE.md escalation trigger: KPI outside its previous interval). Nothing is called a temperature effect (Q1/Q2 are associations by thermal class).

T3 Parked-pack temperature forcing (model comparison, no adoption). On the Q3 gap set (restricted to the `stationary_consistent` subset with cloud-supported start and end ambient). Reference model F0 = the published fixed first-order model with constant forcing = mean of the previous end and next start ambient, tau as published by `seasonalFits.Q3` (no refit) and the same refit-per-fold variant. Variants:
- F1 manual endpoints, constant forcing (control, must reproduce F0);
- F2 screened cloud endpoints, constant forcing;
- F3 screened cloud endpoints, linear-in-time forcing between start and end ambient;
- F4 screened cloud endpoints with the M709 diurnal-sinusoid boundary (the `tools/park_model.py` M2 form, parameters refit per training fold; fresh call of its public functions, no edit).
Comparators: persistence (M1) and full relaxation to the mean ambient. Outcome: next-drive-start pack-probe mean (4 probes, logged), absolute error. Validation: day-blocked 10-fold cross-validation (primary); month-blocked May-August fit / September-November test (secondary, with the M709 caveat that it is not a clean hold-out). Report per ambient band (cold <= 5, shoulder, warm >= 15 on the mean gap ambient): median absolute error, P90, mean signed error, empirical coverage of a 80 % and a 90 % absolute-error interval taken from training-fold residual quantiles, n gaps, n days. Paired day-clustered difference of absolute error (F2 minus F0, F3 minus F0, F4 minus F0) with intervals. ERA5-Land hourly forcing is NOT used here (needs an external download and an agreed parking location; deferred to its own spec).
Second thermal state: not fitted. Pre-registered trigger only: the eight warm-rise gaps (next-start pack mean more than 1 C above the previous end; list regenerated on the live corpus) are audited and listed with cloud ambient change, late OBD sampling, probe agreement and cloud activity between logs; a second-state fit becomes a later milestone only if at least 5 of the audited cases (or at least 5 on at least 3 days if the live list is larger) have no alternative explanation. An existing P-R2 result is not reinterpreted.

T4 Coolant anchor feasibility (counts and residuals only). Cloud coolant snapshot within 30 s before to 60 s after each drive end; paired residual (cloud minus OBD coolant within 15 s) by phase (inside the log, after the log end) and age; counts of cloud-only bursts with an end snapshot (from M729). No coolant model is fitted here.

## 5. Decision rules and gates
R1 (reproduction, before any new number is read): T1 baseline comparison on the drives meeting the handoff's baseline rule reproduces the handoff medians (absolute difference median 0.28 C, P90 2.23 C) within 0.05 C on the handoff's 565-log subset where identifiable; T3 F1 on the handoff's 223-gap rule reproduces the fixed-model median absolute error 1.30 C within 0.05 C; counts are reported on the live 717-drive corpus beside the handoff counts, differences explained (new drives), not tuned. R2: with the manual ambient the T2 estimator reproduces the published `seasonalFits` Q1 and Q2 primary estimates to 1e-5 (the import is the published code). R3: `drive_master.csv` MD5 unchanged; no frozen file hash changes; no payload file changes (deep diff). A failed gate stops the milestone; thresholds are not changed to pass.
- No outcome here replaces a published figure. Using the screened ambient in any published output, or changing the park model, needs a separate spec, a blind audit and a Director decision. T3 results are labelled "model-derived, held-out" and reported as method disagreement where F0 and F2-F4 disagree; nothing is tuned to agree.
- Support: every cell n >= 10 drives on >= 5 days else "not evaluable"; cold cells with fewer than 10 effective days are exploratory without verdict wording; cold results do not enter any winter evaluation.

## 6. Language
Cloud values are "displayed/logged by the vehicle cloud"; the ambient sensor reads engine, road and airflow heat (owner's manual), so screened ambient is "less settling-affected", never "true" or "accurate"; the clock offset (+16 s) is "estimated"; Q1/Q2 are associations by thermal class; pack and coolant model outputs are "estimated / model-derived"; "not established" is not "no effect"; never "validated", "corrected", "confirmed", "robust". Language gate rules apply to every string written to a tracked file.
