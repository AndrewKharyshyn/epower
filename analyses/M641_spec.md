## 1. Problem
The released gross throughput integrates current x voltage with the interval between aligned samples capped at `DT_CAP_S` = 5 s (`compute_drive_summary_v6.py`, crossing-aware trapezoid). An external audit reported 1,836 kWh when intervals longer than 2.5 s are dropped (1.65 % lower): the difference is a support policy on slow-polled cadence, not an error. The dashboard should show how the total depends on the policy, how many drives carry no integral, and which drives rest on thin support. The Monte Carlo block covers alignment tolerance, ADC quantisation and a gap-truncation bound conditional on the 5 s capped rule; it is not a coverage interval for the true throughput and must be labelled so.

## 2. Estimands and policies (pre-registered, no tuning)
For each drive with a published `gross_throughput_kwh` (651 drives), re-integrate with the pipeline's own integrator (same I and V series, same `_asof` alignment at 1500 ms, same crossing-aware directional split, same sign convention) under these gap policies applied to the interval between consecutive aligned samples:
- cap-at-c: interval duration min(dt, c), c in {5 (published rule), 10, no cap};
- drop-above-g: intervals longer than g contribute no energy, g in {1.6, 2.5, 3.5, 5, 10, 30}.
Reported per policy: gross throughput (discharge + charge), discharge, charge, integrated hours, share of the published total. The published rule (cap 5) is the headline and is reproduced per drive to the stored 4-decimal value before anything else is reported (known-answer, section 4).

## 3. Per-drive support tag and coverage statement
- Support share of a drive = integrated time in intervals of at most 2.5 s / integrated time under the published rule. Reported distribution over the 651 drives (counts below 0.9, 0.8, 0.7) and the ten drives with the largest absolute difference between the published value and the drop-above-2.5 s value (file, published kWh, drop-2.5 kWh, support share). The per-drive median absolute difference, p95 and p99 (Wh) are reported.
- Coverage statement (script-derived from the master): number of drives and km with no integral (null `gross_throughput_kwh`; at the time of writing 14 drives, 207 km; counts are computed, not typed), the reason codes present (`energy_null_reason`), and the share of corpus km they represent. The 665-drive scale (headline energy per km applied to the unintegrated drives) is stated as arithmetic only, labelled "not an estimate of their energy".
