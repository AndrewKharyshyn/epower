# M555 spec Rev 1 (2026-11-01, before any code): cell-channel observability against the pack-voltage namespace (audit 2026-10-29 N12, second half)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT for Director review. Nothing implemented. Tier C (new analysis, descriptive data-quality). Branch base: `main` at M554 (`224b19b`). Ledger id `AUD29-N12`. Split out of M554 by the Director (M554 spec Rev 2 R7); the requirements recorded there are binding here: no sum-of-cells versus pack probe (the logged cells are not the pack, and any scaling would be a calibration); consistency by Max >= each logged cell >= Min within resolution and by pack / cell timestamp alignment; age = time since the last SAMPLE (not since the value last changed) with the censored share; the "sparse in a complete subset" rule is replaced by a rule on the share of drives with a complete set. The block-secant half of N12 already exists in `capacityBasis` (M554 section 0) and is untouched.

## 0. What the raw files contain (surveyed on disk before this spec; read-only header survey of `raw/`, 617 files)
- Cell-voltage channel sets by file header: 231 files carry `[BMS] Max Cell Voltage (V)` and `[BMS] Min Cell Voltage (V)` only; 369 carry those two plus `G01 Cell Voltage  (V)` (a single selected cell channel); 3 early files carry per-cell channels (`[BMS] Battery Cell #01..#14`, `#70..#80` and variants: 25, 82 and 62 cell-related columns); 14 files (e4ORCE exports) carry none and no `[BMS] HV Battery voltage` either. The per-cell sets are therefore a selected-channel example from three early drives, not the corpus.
- The logger polls channels round-robin: in the two drives probed, pack voltage and Max Cell Voltage share timestamps while Min Cell Voltage and G01 sit about 0.06 - 0.1 s later (non-co-timed), so exact-timestamp joins are empty and a tolerance is required. Probe values are orientation only and are never typed into the repo; every figure is produced by the tool.
- Max / Min Cell Voltage feed the published cell-spread analyses (`degradationTrends.cellSpread`); this milestone changes none of them and computes no spread statistic.

## 1. Question and non-question
Which cell-voltage channels exist per drive, how complete and how stale are they relative to the pack-voltage samples, how far apart in time are they from the pack samples, and are the logged values internally consistent (Max >= Min; Min <= G01 <= Max; Max >= each logged cell >= Min)? Non-question: no spread, balance, health, ageing or capacity statement; no sum-of-cells probe; no repair or imputation; missing channels stay missing.

## 2. Definitions
- Population: raw files in the master with a pack-voltage channel (`[BMS] HV Battery voltage (V)`); canonical-clean drives are the headline population, the others counted. Strata: channel-set era (max-min / max-min-G01 / per-cell / none) and, within the two large eras, logger cadence regime (`cadence_regime.csv`); eras are not pooled and no contrast between strata is drawn.
- Channel samples: non-missing numeric values of the channel; the pack-voltage sample times are the namespace (filter 200..450 V as in the pairing). Resolution of a channel = the smallest positive difference between its distinct logged values in the drive (stored per drive; consistency tolerances use the larger of the two channels' resolutions).
- Completeness: n_samples(channel) / n_samples(pack) per drive; class complete >= 0.95, partial 0.50 - 0.95, sparse < 0.50 (display / analysis classes, not identified thresholds).
- Alignment: for each channel sample the offset to the nearest pack sample (absolute, seconds); reported: exact co-timed share, median and 95th percentile per drive, share within 0.25 s and within 1.5 s.
- Age: for each pack sample, the time since the last sample of the channel at or before it (not since its value last changed); samples with no earlier channel sample are censored; reported per drive: censored share, median, 95th percentile, and the share of pack samples with age > 5 s.
- Pairing for consistency: each Max sample is paired with the nearest Min sample within a tolerance tau (primary 0.5 s; sensitivity 0.25 s and 1.5 s); a pair is consistent if Max >= Min - resolution. For the G01 era each G01 sample is paired with the nearest Max / Min pair within tau and is consistent if Min - resolution <= G01 <= Max + resolution. For the per-cell files each logged cell sample is paired the same way (Max >= cell >= Min within resolution).
- Per-drive values go into the per-stratum distribution; intervals by the day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws) only where the stratum has >= 5 days (p99 >= 20 days); the per-cell example (3 files) gets counts, no interval.

## 3. Known answers (pre-registered)
- KA1 channels exactly co-timed with the pack channel: alignment offset 0, age 0 at every pack sample, completeness 1.
- KA2 pack sampled every 1 s, channel every 2 s at a 0.5 s phase offset: age alternates 0.5 s and 1.5 s (median 1.0 s), nearest offset 0.5 s, completeness 0.5 (class partial).
- KA3 injected inversions (Max < Min by 2 resolution steps on a known 10 % of pairs): the inconsistency share is exactly 10 % at tau covering the pairing and 0 % when the inversions are within one resolution step.
- KA4 (hard gate, real drives): per drive the channel sample count equals the non-missing count obtained by a second independent route (a plain `read_csv` + `notna` count in the test) for every channel; a miss stops.
- KA5 monotone in tau: the share of paired samples is non-decreasing in tau and the paired-sample set at 0.25 s is a subset of that at 0.5 s and 1.5 s.
- KA6 censoring: a channel whose first sample is later than the first pack sample has censored share equal to the share of pack samples before it.
- Expectations (not gates, reported either way): Max / Min are near-complete in their eras; Min is non-co-timed with Max; G01 is sampled about twice as often as the pack channel; inversions are rare. Disagreement is reported, not tuned.

## 7. Rev 2 (2026-11-01): Director review (verdict revise); incorporated. Rev 1 SHA-256 before this edit: 107756f3d89dfe26bb4d76fe8a5b19318a0d2c8cc1d8a746ce4c675f396581e1
Where this section and sections 1-6 differ, this section governs.
- R1 (Q1; population). An era = the header set AND at least one non-missing value of the channel (header-only-empty channels are counted per era, not credited). The "none" era (no pack voltage) is outside the population: inventory only. The 617 header-surveyed files are reconciled against the master rows and the `comparison_only` files and the counts are stored; a mismatch escalates. Cadence strata only inside the two large eras; the three per-cell files are a counted selected-channel example with no interval.
- R2 (Q2; measures). Completeness is replaced by COVERAGE = the share of pack-voltage samples that have a channel sample within +-tau (classes on coverage: complete >= 0.95, partial 0.50 - 0.95, sparse < 0.50; analysis classes, not identified thresholds); the sample-rate ratio n_channel / n_pack is reported separately and may exceed 1. Age (time since the last SAMPLE, censored share) and nearest offset stay as defined.
- R3 (Q3; pairing and resolution). PRIMARY pairing = the logger's polling cycle: each Max sample is paired with the first Min sample at or after it and before the next Max sample, capped at tau = 1.5 s, one-to-one (an unmatched Max or Min is counted, not dropped silently; equal timestamps pair first). SENSITIVITY = nearest within tau in {0.25, 0.5, 1.5} s with reuse allowed (reuse count stored). Resolution per channel per drive = `grid_step` from `signal_representation.json` (the GateA2 per-channel quantisation) when the file is present, else 10^-d with d the largest number of decimals among the logged values; the two are cross-checked and a disagreement escalates. Timestamps are parsed as `integration_scenarios.load_iv` does (`fast_time`, mixed formats); duplicate timestamps within a channel keep the last sample; the 200..450 V pack filter is applied and the number of pack samples it removes is stored.
- R4 (G01 scale pre-check). The logged `G01 Cell Voltage` channel is checked against the Min..Max scale BEFORE any band test: the ratio of G01 to the mid-range (Max + Min) / 2 at paired samples is reported (median, 5th and 95th percentile). A band test (Min <= G01 <= Max within resolution) is run only if the median ratio lies in [0.9, 1.1]; otherwise G01 is reported as "a logged channel whose values are about N times the Max / Min scale" (N script-written) with no band verdict and no cell wording, and this is not counted as an inconsistency. The same pre-check applies to each per-cell channel of the three early files.
- R5 (Q4; thresholds). Max >= Min inconsistency: the statistic is the day-clustered pooled pair share per stratum; escalate if the point is > 1 % OR the upper interval bound is > 1 %. Tolerance sensitivity: escalate if the verdict against 1 % differs across the three tolerances or the polling-cycle and nearest pairings differ by more than 0.5 percentage points. Complete set = Max AND Min with coverage >= 0.95 (plus G01 in the G01 era); escalate if the share of drives with a complete set is below 90 % in an era with >= 20 days. Bootstrapped statistics are named per measure: pooled pair share (inconsistency), median of per-drive values (coverage, offset, age).
- R6 (Q5, Q6; display and hand-off). Payload, one compact era table and one sentence, after the audit, placed with the cell-spread / data-quality content, not as a Conclusions finding. The published cell-spread relaxation resamples Max / Min to a 1 s mean with a forward fill (limit 3 samples), pairing non-co-timed and up to 3 s stale values; M555 reports the share of 1 s grid points that would be carried forward and the age at 3 s, with no change to any cell-spread value (the hand-off is a follow-up). Extra triggers: age p95 > 3 s in more than 10 % of a stratum's drives; header-only-empty channels in more than 5 % of an era; the pack filter drops more than 1 % of pack samples; survey / master count mismatch.
- R7 (tests and language). Added known answers: an oversampled channel (coverage <= 1 by construction while the rate ratio exceeds 1), the polling-cycle pairing (Max, Min offset by 0.06 s), resolution on decimal-rounded data, duplicate timestamps. Language: "BMS-reported, logged"; never "synchronous" or "simultaneous", "sensor fault" or "sensor error", "G01 is cell 1" or any cell wording for G01 unless the R4 pre-check passes; N is always script-written; co-timed means exactly equal timestamps only.
