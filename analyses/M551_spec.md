## 1. Question and non-question
N01: how large are the observed same-sign electrical exchange runs of the HV battery, and what share of the energy do tiny runs carry (the audit: counts of tiny reversals must not be read as a proportional share of stress)? Neither is a cycle count, an ageing or damage measure, a rating or an operating-point percentile; the existing reversal / rainflow counts answer another question (the continuous energy ledger stays unchanged, and so do the legacy 1 / 5 / 10 / 30 s sustained-current records, which are a different quantity: current, with unrestricted interpolation, and are not reused).

## 2. Substrate (read from the code at M550)
- Per-drive supported stream = the master's own current / voltage pairing (`tools/ah_throughput.py` and `power_peak_temp.py` precedent): `D._prep_bytes`, `D._series` (I with |I| < 900, V 200..450), `D._asof` (voltage nearest to each current sample within `V_ALIGN_TOL` 1.5 s, unpaired rows dropped), Id = -I, P = Id * V / 1000 kW (discharge positive). Power is piecewise linear between pair samples (the ledger's convention, F01 / M229); an interval is SUPPORTED when its raw time step is <= `DT_CAP_S` = 5 s (the audit uses the same 5 s support rule). An interval longer than 5 s is a GAP: the continuous ledger still adds energy for it with the step capped at 5 s; here the gap interval breaks the segment and its capped energy is carried separately (`gapBridgedKwh`).
- Segment = maximal chain of consecutive supported intervals. File start and end are segment boundaries.
- Decision for the Director (Q1): this substrate keeps the packets reconciled to the released ledger (`gross_discharge_kwh + gross_charge_kwh`) and the project's pairing, instead of the audit's analytic integration on the union of current and voltage knots; the audit's own difference between the two is a method delta of -0.106 % on 592 drives.

## 3. N01 energy packets
- Per interval the ledger's directional split: crossing interval (p0 * p1 < 0): the positive and negative portions d (p+^2 | p-^2) / (2 (|p0| + |p1|)); otherwise the signed trapezoid goes to discharge or charge by its sign; both endpoints zero gives no energy and breaks runs ("idle" interval).
- A RUN = maximal set of consecutive intervals whose directional portions have the same sign within one segment; a crossing interval ends the previous run (its same-sign portion belongs to it) and starts a run of the other sign. Run energy (Wh) = the sum of its portions, duration (s) = the sum of the (split) interval durations, sign, `censored` = the first or last run of a segment (file start / end, adjacent to a gap, or adjacent to an idle interval at a segment edge), n intervals.
- Reconciliation (STOP on a miss): per drive, sum of all run energies (both signs, censored included) + `gapBridgedKwh` + idle (zero) = the master gross throughput `gross_throughput_kwh` to the 4-decimal rounding of the master column (tolerance 5e-5 kWh), and discharge and charge separately against `gross_discharge_kwh`, `gross_charge_kwh`.
- Estimands (complete, i.e. uncensored, runs only; censored runs reported as counts and energy): count, median / p90 / p99 of run energy and duration, the share of complete runs below 1 Wh (count share) and the share of complete-run energy they carry, by sign and overall, and the censored share of runs and energy. Sensitivities: threshold 0.5 / 2 Wh; a current deadband (|I| <= 1 / 2 / 5 A set to zero for RUN DEFINITION only, the energy in the band reported as an explicit residual so the ledger still reconciles).
- STRATA. The run count depends on the logger cadence (more samples means more, shorter runs). The B6 cadence-invariance check still FAILS (`analyses/PCL1_sizing.json`), so every N01 and N02 result is reported per cadence stratum (`cadence_regime.csv`: fast / slow, unknown separate) and NO pooled headline, no contrast between strata is drawn and none is described as "comparable" (the M446 pattern).
- Intervals: day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws) for the count and energy shares (ratio of sums over days) and for the pooled-run median / p90 / p99 (per-day fine log-histograms resampled by day); fewer than 5 days: exploratory, no interval.

## 4. N02 power-duration profile
- For each window W in {5, 10, 30, 60, 120} s: windows start on a 1 s grid anchored at the start of each supported segment and must lie completely inside one segment (no window spans a gap, so no unsupported window is counted); the window value = the exact integral of the piecewise-linear power over the window / W (net mean kW, evaluated analytically with the piecewise-quadratic cumulative energy, not interpolated samples). Per drive and W: the maximum (discharge) and the minimum (charge, reported as a magnitude) over its windows; the number of supported windows; drives with no supported window for a W are absent for that W (support counted).
- Optional variant (uninterrupted direction): windows in which the sign of P does not change (all pair samples inside have the same sign); the main profile is the net mean (longer windows can include reversals), as the audit states.
- Pooled across drives: percentiles of the per-drive extremes (p50 / p90 / p95 / p99 / max), day-clustered bootstrap for p50 / p90 / p95 (seed 42, 4000 draws; the drive is the unit inside the day cluster); never a time-weighted operating percentile; the maxima are "observed, start-grid-dependent lower approximations to continuous-window maxima", not ratings. Sensitivity: the 1 s grid shifted by 0.5 s (start-grid dependence reported as the change of the per-drive maxima).
- Per cadence stratum, with the support (drives, days) beside every row.

## 5. Known answers (pre-registered)
- KA1 (N01 reconciliation, real): per drive, run energies + gap bridge reconcile to the master ledger columns within 5e-5 kWh on every drive; any miss STOPS.
- KA2 (N01 synthetic): alternating +10 / -10 kW 1 s segments give runs of known energy and the expected censored first / last runs; a crossing interval splits at the analytic portions (10 kW to -10 kW over 2 s: 0.5 Wh... values stated in the test); a 6 s gap breaks a run and censors both neighbours; an idle interval breaks a run.
- KA3 (N02 synthetic): constant power P0: every window value = P0 for all W; triangular ramp: the window mean equals the analytic value; a gapped segment: windows spanning the gap are excluded, a window ending exactly at the gap edge is counted, a segment shorter than W gives no window; the grid shift of 0.5 s changes the maximum of a ramp by the analytic amount.
- KA4 (cross-check, real): the 5 s window maximum of |net mean kW| is at least the 1 s-sample-based kW implied by the legacy sustained records is NOT used (different quantity); instead per drive the W = 5 s maximum is <= the drive's `peak_discharge_kw` and the 5 s charge magnitude <= |`peak_charge_kw`| (a window mean cannot exceed the sample extreme): any violation STOPS.
- Expectations (not gates): per stratum the p95 of the per-drive maximum decreases with W in both directions; charge extremes exceed discharge extremes at 5 s. The audit shows the same ordering; disagreement is reported, not tuned.
