## 1. Question and the three estimands (B1)

The published coolant and oil "median time to 60 / 70 / 80 C" are medians of the drives that reached the threshold (reachers only; 389 cold starts; 332 / 243 / 172 reached coolant 60 / 70 / 80 C on the live corpus). A drive that ended (key-off) before the threshold is right-censored: its warm-up was not finished, not failed.

Three quantities are kept apart (per fluid f in {coolant, oil}, threshold c in {60, 70, 80} C, cohort in {all, warm, shoulder}; T = seconds from the first second of the 1 Hz grid until the fluid first reaches c; oil start at or above c: T = 0, the estimator's own rule):
- **E1 (identified without assumption):** the observed share of cold-start trips that reach c within h seconds before key-off, E1(h) = P(T <= h and T <= trip end). With trip-end censoring only, E1 equals the lower Manski bound.
- **E2 (hypothetical, needs an assumption):** 1 - S_KM(h), the Kaplan-Meier (product-limit) estimate of the time to c under independent censoring; the time to c had the drive continued. It is a description of a hypothetical continued drive on this vehicle (route and load of the continuation are not defined), not a probability that the car warms up, and it is never called "cumulative incidence".
- **E3 (comparison only):** the published reachers-only median.

Headline cells (declared before any result): cohort "all", coolant 80 C and oil 70 C, at h = 600 s. Every other cell is descriptive. No contrasts between cohorts (warm versus shoulder) are tested or claimed. "All" includes the 7 cold-regime drives (all rows of the M418 population); the cold cohort is not computed separately (suppressed below the minimum support of 10 drives on 5 days). Horizons h in {300, 600, 900, 1200, 1800} s, shown only where the support rule (section 2) holds.

Not an estimand: physical time constants of the cooling or oil system, winter warm-up, any fuel or efficiency quantity, the pack warm-up tau (`thermalStepResponse`: 9 accepted fits of 138 candidates; only re-stated with its candidate/fit counts and labelled a conditional long-drive transient statistic, not re-estimated). F mixes drives with different start temperatures; strata by start coolant are a follow-up.

## 2. Population, times and censoring (B2)

Per-drive records come from the M418 code path (`tools/thermal_support_ledger.lag_record`, the same `_twl_grid` 1 Hz grid with forward fill of at most 3 s, `_TWL_*` constants, slim frames), so the cold-start flag and the event time are the estimator's own. Cohort = `thermal_regime` of `seasonal_drive_master.csv`. Day = calendar day of the drive (the resampling cluster).
- **Event:** the first grid second at which the fluid value is >= c (a crossing inside a gap is therefore observed at the first valid second after the gap). The number of events that follow a gap longer than 10 s of invalid values is reported.
- **Censoring time:** the last grid second at which THAT FLUID's channel has a valid value (not the end of the grid), so channel dropout is not counted as time at risk.
- **Censoring reason (declared rule):** `tripEnd` if the censoring time is within 10 s of the last grid second of the drive, otherwise `channelLoss` (the fluid channel went invalid more than 10 s before the trip ended). E1 equals the Manski lower bound only for the tripEnd drives; the table reports both reasons.
- **Ties:** an event and a censoring in the same second: the event comes first.
- Reported per cohort: the quartiles of the start coolant temperature, the lag from the time origin to the first valid coolant second (quartiles), and for oil the share with time 0 (start at or above c), separately from the events after the start.

## 3. Support rule

A horizon h is reported for a cohort/fluid/threshold only if at least 10 cold-start drives on at least 5 calendar days are still at risk at h (observation time >= h and not reached before h); otherwise "not supported (n at risk = k, d days)". The RMST (restricted mean time, seconds within the horizon) uses ONE horizon tau per fluid/threshold: the smallest, across the three cohorts, of each cohort's longest supported declared horizon (smaller than 300 s: RMST not reported).

## 4. Median (B6)

Median = inf{t : S_KM(t) <= 0.5}. It is displayed only if it is identified in the point estimate, its day-clustered interval is identified and it is identified for every theta in [0.5, 2] (D3). In the bootstrap, unidentified draws count as +infinity and the percentiles are taken over all 4000 draws; if more than 2.5 % of the draws are unidentified the upper limit is printed as "beyond supported range". Otherwise the text reads "cohort median not identified without an assumption on censoring" together with E1.

## 5. Informative key-off: sensitivity (B3, B4)

1. **Worst-case (Manski) identification bounds** at each supported horizon: F_low(h) = (reached by h) / n and F_high(h) = (reached by h + censored before h) / n, where "censored before h" means censoring time c_i < h strictly and the drive has not reached. They are identification bounds, never confidence intervals; each endpoint gets its own day-clustered percentile interval and the table prints the outer interval [lower limit of F_low, upper limit of F_high].
2. **Hazard-tilt sensitivity** (delta-adjusted hazard-multiplier pattern-mixture model): a drive censored at c_i contributes S_theta,i(t) = 1 for t <= c_i and (S_KM(t) / S_KM(c_i))^theta for t > c_i, which multiplies the cumulative hazard -log S_KM of the remaining time by theta (on the Kaplan-Meier step function; not the discrete hazard); an event drive contributes 1{T_i > t}; S_theta(t) is the mean over drives. theta = 1 reproduces the Kaplan-Meier estimate exactly (Efron's redistribution to the right). theta < 1: drives that stop are less likely to warm up than the ones who stay; theta > 1 the opposite. Display rows theta in {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4}; the pre-declared plausible band is theta in [0.5, 2] (used in D1). S_KM is re-estimated inside every bootstrap draw (the tilted estimator is a function of the draw). The tipping point is found by bisection on log theta over [0.01, 100] (the smallest/largest theta at which a headline reading changes class; "none in [0.01, 100]" if it does not).
3. Reading classes (D1), one per reading, assigned by the script from the numbers: "holds at both worst-case bounds"; "holds for every theta from 0.5 to 2"; "depends on the censoring assumption" (this class goes in the table only, never in a sentence or a KPI). A reading is a stated comparison of F with a fixed value (for the headline cells: F(600 s) below 0.5, i.e. fewer than half have reached by 10 min).

## 6. Uncertainty

Day-clustered percentile bootstrap (cluster = calendar day, resampling whole days with replacement among the cohort's cold-start days, B = 4000, seed 42): E1, E2, F_low, F_high, every F_theta, the median and the RMST. Report n (cold-start drives), days, reached, censored by reason, and the Kish effective days of the cold-start drives per cohort. The Kaplan-Meier interval is conditional on independent censoring; the tilted rows on the stated theta.

## 7. Distance-grid support (B8)

For the distance-domain `warmupCurve` (classes urban, mixed, mixed_highway, highway; published values unchanged) the new key carries, for every class and every grid point: the number of drives and the number of calendar days still contributing (drive span km >= grid km), `supportEndsKm` (first grid point with fewer than 10 drives or fewer than 5 days; `null` if the support holds to the last point) and the sentence "beyond this point the curve averages only the drives still running". The dashboard draws the curve de-emphasised beyond `supportEndsKm`. Per-cohort variants as for the time-to-threshold block (all, warm, shoulder).

## 8. Gates and tests

- G1 reconciliation: for every cohort, fluid and threshold: n cold starts, reached and censored equal the M418 ledger counts (and the published estimator n); censored = tripEnd + channelLoss; days equal the ledger's; the curve grid drive counts equal the ledger's `gridAttrition`. G1 or a KAT failure: no display (D4).
- G2 isolation: every new leaf sits under ONE additive key `thermalSupportLedger.timeToThreshold`, produced by the ledger stage (so it is regenerated at every ingestion and cannot go stale); deep diff: nothing else changes; the display relabel of the reachers-only median is text only.
- G3 identity: a second run gives a byte-identical block.
- Known-answer tests (criteria declared here): (i) no censoring: Kaplan-Meier equals the empirical distribution function; (ii) a hand-computed 8-drive example with ties, three censored times and censoring after the last event equals the product-limit hand values; (iii) theta = 1 equals Kaplan-Meier on random data, and the EXACT limits: theta -> infinity gives 1{T_i > t} for events, 1 for c_i >= t, and for c_i < t gives 1 if S_KM(t) = S_KM(c_i) (no Kaplan-Meier step in (c_i, t]) else 0; theta -> 0+ gives 1 for c_i < t if S_KM(t) > 0 and 0 if S_KM(t) = 0 (theta = 1e-9 and 1e9 are compared with these closed forms within 1e-6); (iv) the Manski bounds bracket Kaplan-Meier and every tilt row; (v) the median is "not identified" when S stays above 0.5, and an unidentified bootstrap draw counts as +infinity; (vi) the support rule suppresses a horizon with 9 at risk or 4 days and prints the counts; (vii) time rules: censoring at the last valid fluid sample, the 10 s tripEnd/channelLoss rule, event before censoring in the same second, event after a gap counted, strict c_i < h; (viii) coverage simulation matching the live structure: 113 days, Poisson 3.4 drives per day, a shared day frailty (gamma, variance 0.5), event times Weibull and exponential, independent censoring giving 30 to 60 % censoring, times discretised to 1 Hz, 400 simulations, B = 400: the day-clustered 95 % interval for F(600 s) covers the truth in [0.92, 0.98] (GATE); coverage at a horizon with 15 to 30 drives at risk is reported, not gated; an informative-censoring scenario (censoring hazard tied to the event hazard): Kaplan-Meier is biased, and the tilted row at the true theta recovers the truth within Monte Carlo error (two Monte Carlo standard errors); (ix) distance grid: hand-computed drives/days/supportEndsKm on a synthetic span set. Coverage outside the band means the bootstrap is not used and the case returns to the Director (D4).
- Blind audit (analytical-auditor, claim + data path only) reproduces E1, E2, F_low, F_high and the 600 s tilt rows for coolant 80 C and oil 70 C in the cohort "all" from the raw files and the declared rules; Director ruling before any dashboard text.

## 12. Rev 2.1 (2026-10-14, Director confirmation of Rev 2: approve with recorded clarifications C1-C8, before any run)

- **C1 Cohorts.** The computed cohorts are all / warm / shoulder only, whatever `byCohort` holds; the cold regime is not estimated (`byCohort.cold` has 11 cold starts on 4 days, below the 5-day support, so the "7" of section 1 is replaced by this count from the payload). G1 covers the three cohorts.
- **C2 Grid facts.** The forward fill copies an earlier value only, so the first grid second with value >= c is always a sampled second; "valid" means non-NaN after the fill (limit 3), so a censoring time can lie up to 3 s after the last raw sample. Gap event: the event second minus the previous valid second is longer than 10 s. The reported E1 = reached / n is exact if no drive was censored for channel loss and otherwise a lower bound. The M418 `riskSet` rows use the whole observation span and differ from this study's, so G1 does not compare them.
- **C3 KAT (iii).** The limits are as stated; "no Kaplan-Meier step in (c_i, t]" is tested by step index, not by float equality; beyond the largest time S_KM = S_KM(t_max).
- **C4 Readings and classes.** Both readings are scored per cell and horizon: R+ F(h) < 0.5 and R- F(h) >= 0.5. Class "holds at both worst-case bounds": R+ if the upper 95 % limit of F_high is below 0.5; R- if the lower 95 % limit of F_low is at least 0.5. Class "holds for every theta from 0.5 to 2": the same test with the tilted share at theta = 2 (R+) or theta = 0.5 (R-); F_theta is monotone in theta (a KAT is added). Classes are assigned first-match; otherwise "depends on the censoring assumption". Tipping point: the theta at which the point F_theta(h) crosses 0.5, and separately the theta at which its 95 % limit on the side of the Kaplan-Meier reading crosses 0.5; bisection on log theta until the interval is shorter than 0.01. One set of 4000 day-resamples per cohort (the days of the cohort's cold starts), shared by all cells and thetas.
- **C5 Median.** The Kaplan-Meier median is shown if it is identified at theta = 0.5 AND the support rule holds at the median time. "Upper limit beyond supported range" appears in table rows only (more than 2.5 % unidentified draws); it does not suppress a median that satisfies the first rule.
- **C6 Simulation.** Days with no drive are dropped; the censoring times are resampled from the live cold-start observation spans (committed per-drive table `analyses/M435_result_per_drive.csv.gz`, column tripEndS) scaled by their own day frailty (gamma, variance 0.5); the truth is the analytic gamma-frailty F at 1 Hz; the 0.92-0.98 gate applies per scenario (Weibull shape 1.5 and exponential events); the informative scenario has, after the censoring time, a remaining-time hazard theta times the at-risk hazard, theta in {0.5, 2}: Kaplan-Meier must be biased by more than 2 Monte Carlo standard errors and the tilted row at the true theta must recover the truth within two (both gating, D4).
- **C7 G2 extras.** Besides the new key, the following change under `thermalSupportLedger`: the reworded `censoringReading` entry (B11), `_provenance` and the module docstring ("no time-to-event model" removed), `methodVersion` 2, and the artifact stamp. The simulation runs in the tests, not in the stage.
- **C8 Required sentences.** R2 verbatim: "describes the time to c C had the drive continued (route and load undefined); the observed share before key-off is shown beside it". The A2 gate checks that the counts of R1, R2, R4 and R6 equal the number of Kaplan-Meier tables, and that the count of R5 equals the number of cells with an unidentified median. "KM" is never used as an abbreviation (it collides with km): Kaplan-Meier is always written out.
