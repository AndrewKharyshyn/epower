## 0. What is known from the registered files (before any run)
- Tool (M752): 717 drives; unavailable for E_int: no row within the 35 s cap 100, hole 12, odometer jump 17, outside span 4: available 584; identity class, 2 km or more: 418 drives, 130 days.
- Audit (blind, own rule): no row within cap or hole 120, odometer jump 7, outside span 4: available 586; the same class: 420 drives, 130 days.
- Arithmetic: tool 112 (cap or hole) + 17 (jump) = 129; audit 120 + 7 = 127. The two differ by 2 unavailable drives (584 versus 586, 418 versus 420). The jump-labelled counts differ by 10, of which 8 reappear in the audit's cap-or-hole count (120 versus 112). **Hypothesis H1 (not yet checked):** the 17 versus 7 is mostly an ORDER-OF-REASONS effect (the tool tests the jump first and labels the drive `odometer_jump`; the audit tests availability first and labels the same drive cap or hole), leaving about 2 drives whose availability genuinely differs between the two rules. Two rule details also differ and may explain those 2: (a) the tool's check window reaches one row beyond each end of [t0 - 35 s, t1 + 35 s] (`jump_in_window` takes the row before the first and after the last row inside the window, so a step that straddles the window edge counts), the audit's rows are those inside the window; (b) the tool counts a positive step as a jump only with dt > 0 and a step larger than dt x 180 km/h + 0.05 km, the audit counts a positive step with dt <= 0 as a jump. H1 and the two details are hypotheses until J1.

## 3. Decision rules (frozen before any run)
- **Physical rule P.** A drive is jump-affected iff a negative step (more than 0.05 km below the previous row) or a positive step implying more than 180 km/h (dt > 0; a positive step at dt <= 0 also counts) occurs between the last row at or before t0 and the first row at or after t1 (the stretch of rows that the endpoint interpolation and the bracketing rows actually use, together with the drive's own span). This is the minimal rule under which a jump can change the drive's odometer difference; it is stated here, not tuned.
- **R1 (explanation).** H1 is "confirmed" if the order-of-reasons effect accounts for all the jump-labelled drives that are also unavailable by A; the remaining drives (available by A, excluded by some jump variant) are listed by count and edge class. The explanation is reported as stated by J1, not by the author's expectation.
- **R2 (which rule governs).** P governs the registered comparison. If the tool's rule B excludes drives that P keeps (only the extra edge rows, or a step outside the stretch above), B is "over-exclusive"; if the audit's rule excludes drives that P keeps, it is "over-exclusive" in the other direction; if P excludes drives that both keep, both are "under-exclusive". The result states which.
- **R3 (replacement trigger).** The X1 block is replaced (own milestone and Director ruling, `tools/cloud_payload.py` plus the dashboard note) only if under P any displayed X1 number changes at its displayed precision (two decimals for percent and km, integer counts) or a verdict word changes. Otherwise the X1 disclosure "documented, not resolved" is rewritten, by a script-written sentence in the same later milestone, to the resolved explanation, and the numbers stay.
- **R4 (falsification).** H1 is falsified if the jump-labelled drives that are unavailable by A number fewer than 8 of the 10; the explanation then rests on the rule details (a) and (b) and J1 says so. A result that moves the verdict ("consistent within +-3 %") or the sign of the difference goes to the Director immediately.

## 7. Rev 2: Director rulings on Rev 1 (override sections 0 to 6 where they differ; review record `analyses/M765_director_review_rev1.json`, Rev 1 sha256 recorded there; decision: revise (minor), every ruling adopted; committed before any run)

Only the TOTALS are known: "8 reappear in the audit's cap-or-hole count" is an inference from totals and reads "a net difference of 8".
7.2 **R1 replaced by frozen predictions (ruling 2).** H1 is confirmed only if J1 reproduces ALL of: drives unavailable by A (cap, hole or outside span) = 124 (120 + 4); drives flagged by B that are also unavailable by A = 8; drives flagged by B that are available by A = 9; drives flagged by B3 (audit-like) that are available by A = 7. The J1 report lists the drives each rule flags that the other does not (counts per cell), not only the net difference. 7.3 **R4 restated (ruling 3).** H1 is falsified if the per-drive cross-tabulation (B and unavailable by A) is below 8; the same J1 check confirms that the 4 outside-span drives are the same drives in the tool's and the audit-like sets.
7.4 **Naming (ruling 4).** The rule reconstructed from the audit's `rules_inferred` text is called B3 (audit-like); it is not the auditor's code. R2 uses that term.
7.5 **P per estimator (ruling 5, Q(a)).** P is defined per estimator by the endpoint rows that estimator actually uses (E_int and E_br: the rows from the last row at or before t0 to the first row at or after t1; the +16 s sensitivity: the shifted endpoint rows). The primary is P on E_int.
7.6 **Excursion diagnostic (ruling 6).** Because P can miss a level excursion that starts before the first endpoint row (only its slow step back up falls in the stretch), J1 adds a DIAGNOSTIC (not a rule): the number of drives with an endpoint inside one of the 17 recorded level excursions.
7.7 **Verdict scope (ruling 7).** No M752 threshold is touched. R4 and R3 cover the whole verdict wording: the +-1 % criterion (primary), the +-3 % criterion and the 0.25 km median criterion. The lower interval bound 1.04 % (audit 1.03 %) is close to 1 %: a P estimate whose lower bound falls below 1.00 % changes the +-1 % word and goes to the Director immediately. The result states whether each P estimate lies inside the M752 interval.
7.8 **Disclosure and side-by-side (ruling 8, Q(b)).** H1 was derived from the registered totals and the rule texts and P was frozen before any per-drive look; the result states this, reports B, B1, B3 and P side by side, and never selects P because of its result.
7.9 **Replacement trigger (ruling 9, Q(c)).** Accepted. Any change of n, days or the available count is an integer change and triggers a replacement; interval bounds count as displayed numbers.
