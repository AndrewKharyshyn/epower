Origin: self-audit backlog N-02 (depends on D-05, owner decision 2026-11-12: the forecast basis for the pack cool-down constant is the temperature-scale definition, about 8 h; the project value 10.78 h and the previous-drive-max proxy 6.9 h stay as sensitivities). Already seen before pre-registration (disclosed): the self-audit scripts `analyses/self_audit_2026-11-11/B/b2_scenario_sensitivity.py` (part A: throughput cold slope, pack-ambient coupling, urban month check) and `G/g4_more.py` (cool-down definitions: 7.88 h temperature-scale L1 on 632 pairs, 10.97 h project-like, 7.1 h previous-drive max) were run in the self-audit; their values are NOT targets and are not typed into this repository. The existing M28 block `seasonalLife` is unchanged and stays the labelled assumption-driven extrapolation; N-02 does not retire it.

## 1. Question and non-claims

Which of the M28 seasonal priors can be replaced or bracketed by quantities fitted to the logged drives, and how much support do those fits have in the cold range? Four quantities (Q1 to Q4) are fitted, each with a day-clustered interval, support counts and an explicit support status, and are stored beside the M28 priors (not instead of them).

Non-claims: no battery life, no years of life (every year output stays retracted, M114), no degradation or ageing statement, no causal temperature effect (season, route, load and period move together), no extrapolation below the lowest observed drive-time ambient (about -1 C at the time of writing; 45 drives below 5 C): the winter range is "not observed at this support" until it is. Ambient is the driver-recorded dashboard reading entered by hand (uncertainty class vehicle_uncalibrated), not the parked environment. The Ea = 0.5 eV Arrhenius factor, the 25 C reference and the 20,000 GTC threshold stay labelled multipliers (`verified:false`).

## 2. Data

Canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2` / `ens_invalid` excluded, explicit-True reading as in M646) from `drive_master.csv` (665 drives at the time of writing, MD5 anchor `77bbe275f8c22eaf62d8636867764af3`); columns as in the self-audit loader (`ah_throughput`, `distance_km`, `duration_s`, drive class, `ambient_time_mean_c`, `pack_temp_time_mean_c`, `pack_temp_max_c`, first and last pack temperature, ambient start and end, `gap_h`). The new script `tools/n02_seasonal_fits.py` reads the master and the existing per-drive columns only (no raw reread), is deterministic (seed 42), and is a stage `seasonal_fits` after `cohort_contrast`. Recomputed at every ingestion over all drives; no snapshot constants.

## 3. Quantities

Q1 throughput cold slope gamma: d ln(Ah per km) per C below 15 C drive-time ambient, OLS with drive-class dummies and log distance (the classPlusLogDistance specification of the self-audit; the class-only specification is the sensitivity). Compared with the M28 prior slopes (lo, mid, hi). Cold defined as max(0, 15 - ambient).

Q2 pack-ambient coupling beta: d(pack time-mean temperature) / d(ambient), OLS with drive-class dummies and log duration. Sensitivities: the rise (pack minus ambient) regressed on cold degrees below 15 C; the start-temperature slope (pack start on ambient start).

Q3 cool-down constant tau (the D-05 decision): temperature-scale model for consecutive drive pairs with gap between 0.02 h and 36 h: excess at the end of the previous drive e0 = (previous drive's last pack temperature) - ambient_ref, excess at the next key-on e1 = (next drive's first pack temperature) - ambient_ref, ambient_ref = mean of the previous drive's end ambient and the next drive's start ambient; model e1 = e0 exp(-gap/tau); tau by minimising the sum of absolute errors (L1) on the temperature scale with no outcome-based filter (the primary); no pair is dropped for its outcome. Sensitivities, always printed beside the primary: the project definition (log-scale through-origin Sen slope after the residual filter; the outcome-based filter makes it selection on the outcome and its held-out skill against persistence was negative in the self-audit), the previous-drive-maximum proxy, the L2 loss, gap strata (below 1 h, 1 h or longer, 4 h or longer). Held-out score: day-blocked 10-fold cross-validation (contiguous calendar blocks, as M430), MAE of the predicted next-start temperature against persistence (no decay), reported with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap interval of the skill. Skill is scored against BOTH references: persistence (no decay) and full relaxation to ambient_ref (e1 = 0); tau is useful only if its skill beats the better of the two (R2). The skill interval is a day-clustered percentile bootstrap over the held-out per-pair errors (folds fixed once, held-out errors resampled by arrival day, seed 42, 4000 draws). Fallback if the gate fails (R3): the block prints the fit as a summary only, Q4 is shown as a 3-tau grid (primary L1 temperature-scale, project 10.78 h, previous-drive-max 6.9 h) with NO primary value, the block carries a flag `d05Support: "not supported"`, and the final report tells Andrii that D-05 lacks support so he can re-decide it; no silent switch to another tau.

Q4 calendar factor with drive heat: the Arrhenius time-average (Ea 0.5 eV, reference 25 C) of the pack temperature over a year, built from the observed drive-time pack temperatures, the Q3 cool-down (temperature scale) and the parked ambient climatology of `summary_config.json` `seasonalAssumptions.monthlyTempC` (an external, user-supplied assumption, labelled), expressed as a fraction of a 25 C year; printed for tau at the primary value and for the sensitivity values (project 10.78 h, 6.9 h) so the dependence on tau is visible. This is an exposure index, not a life. The self-audit scenario code (day resampling by temperature analogue) is NOT ported as a year simulation: the block stores only the index and its tau dependence. Parked-temperature treatment (offset 0 and +/- 3 C) is a stated sensitivity, and so is Ea 0.3 and 0.7 eV. Months whose climatology lies below the lowest observed drive-time ambient (January, February and December at the time of writing) have no observed drive or heat-soak pack temperature: they are assigned via Q2 (beta applied to the climatology) and labelled "extrapolated", or marked "not observed" when Q2 is not supported; the block reports the share of the index from parked hours, drive hours and cool-down (soak) hours and the share that is extrapolated (R4). The index is called an "Arrhenius-weighted temperature exposure index relative to constant 25 C (assumed Ea, verified:false)".

## 4. Estimation, support and ladder

Every interval: day-clustered percentile bootstrap, calendar day as the cluster, seed 42, 4000 draws, resampling whole days with all their drives (pairs for Q3 are clustered on the arrival day); a draw that leaves a quantity undefined is dropped and the drop rate is reported (more than 1 % makes the quantity "not estimable"). Every quantity carries n (drives or pairs), days, effective days (inverse of the sum of squared day shares, as in M648) and the largest-day share. Support ladder per quantity and per ambient band (below 5 C, 5 to 15 C, 15 C or warmer): not shown below 10 drives on 5 days; exploratory when the floors are met and the effective days are below 10; supported at 10 or more effective days. Q1 and Q2 are fitted over all drives; the block also prints the lowest observed ambient and the drives and days below 5 C and below 0 C; the slope below 5 C is printed only where the band is at least exploratory and is otherwise "not observed at this support". The whole block is labelled exploratory until supported days exist in the cold band; a winter statement is allowed only for supported bands.

## 5. Method disagreement and abort / validity rules

Reported, not tuned. Spread = max minus min of the point estimates across the primary and its sensitivities (R6); a spread wider than the larger of the primary interval half-widths flags the quantity "definition-dependent", for Q1, Q2 and Q3 alike. The project (outcome-filtered) Q3 variant is known to be biased, is shown but kept out of the spread flag. Outcome rules: a Q1 interval that includes 0 reads "not resolved at this support", never "no effect"; beta outside [0, 1.5] is flagged "outside the physical range" (an attenuated or implausible estimate). The analysis aborts (block shows "not estimable") if: fewer than 100 clean drives with the required columns; the known-answer test fails; a quantity's drop rate exceeds 1 %; the master MD5 stamp does not match. The block compares each fitted value with the M28 prior and prints the comparison as a difference with its interval; it never writes into `seasonalLife` and never changes any displayed year output (those stay null).

## 7. Risks recorded

Ambient is hand-entered at driving time, so the cool-down reference and the pack-ambient coupling carry its error (attenuation toward zero for beta); era and season are confounded; the cold band has few days, so Q1 and Q2 below 5 C are exploratory at best; the cool-down fit rests on pairs whose gaps are dominated by short parks; the external monthly climatology is an assumption; outcome-selected variants (project definition) are kept only as labelled sensitivities.

## 9. Implementation record and deviations (M667, 2026-11-15; written before the blind audit)
Implemented by `tools/n02_seasonal_fits.py` (stage `seasonal_fits`, payload block `seasonalFits`). Deviations from Rev 1.1, all stated before any audit result: (D1) section 2 said no raw reread; the master has no end-of-drive pack temperature, which Q3 needs, so the script reads the four pack probes T1..T4 from raw for two numbers per drive: the pack temperature at the start and at the end of the drive, defined literally as the mean over the probes of each probe's first (last) valid reading (-40 to 90 C) provided that reading lies within 60 s of the drive's first (last) timestamp, with at least 3 of 4 probes; cached by raw sha256 (`seasonal_fits_cache/`, git-ignored). Q3's start temperature is this raw first reading; the master `pack_temp_start_c` is used only in the previous-drive-maximum proxy. (D2) The unlogged-driving flag (R8) is the next drive's `unlogged_distance_est_km` above 1.0 km (whole-km odometer resolution); the primary keeps all pairs, a sensitivity excludes the flagged ones. (D3) The held-out gate (R2, R3) is defined as: the 95 % interval of the skill against the better reference lies above zero. (D4) The spread flag includes the ambient-reference shifts of Q3 (R5) and excludes the outcome-filtered project variant (R6). (D5) Q4 carries no interval: it is an index with a sensitivity grid (tau label x Ea x parked offset); propagating Q1 to Q3 uncertainty into it is not done, and the block says so by listing the grid as the bracket. (D6) Q4 drive-time pack temperature = the time-weighted corpus mean point moved along the Q2 line to the climatology; cool-down uses the corpus median initial excess and the empirical gap distribution (20 quantiles, capped at 18 h); diurnal amplitude 4 C is an assumption carried from the self-audit scenario code. (D7) The estimators of Q1 to Q3 were run on this corpus in the self-audit and their numbers were seen; no value is typed into the repository and none is a target.

## 10. Blind-audit reconciliation (M667, 2026-11-15)
The blind audit reproduced Q3 exactly (tau, pair and day counts, gap strata, held-out MAEs and skill, 9 of 11 sensitivities) and the Q1 band counts; the Q1/Q2 point estimates differed by about 1e-4 and 1e-3 because the spec text "drive-class dummies" was ambiguous: the script had merged `mixed_highway` into `highway`, the audit used the four master drive types. Resolved literally (Rev 1.2, written after the audit): the drive class is the master `drive_type` (urban, mixed, mixed_highway, highway; one unknown drive keeps its own dummy), the merged coding is kept as a labelled sensitivity (`sensitivityMergedClasses`, included in the spread flag). Rules the audit found undisclosed, now stated: Q1 and Q2 use drives with distance above 0.5 km, positive Ah throughput and non-missing ambient and pack temperature; the Q2 rise sensitivity includes log duration and the class dummies; effective days are distance-weighted for Q1 and Q2 and pair-count-weighted for Q3 (stated in the block as `essBasis`; the ladder status of a band can differ between the two weightings); Q3 pairs are consecutive drives in the full chronological order with both drives canonical-clean (so the gap is exact; pairs that skip an excluded drive are not formed); the project-definition variant keeps pairs with e0 above 0 and e1 above 0.5 C (the outcome filter) and takes the through-origin median log slope; Q4 uses clean drive hours and clean drives divided by the calendar span between the first and last clean date, and a month is "extrapolated" when its climatology is below the lowest observed drive-time ambient. Q4 was reproduced only partly (index 0.545 against 0.532, shares within 0.01) because these two inputs were not fixed by the spec text; the block's Q4 stays a sensitivity grid, not a headline. Added to the block text: the held-out skill is not a validation of a battery property and its interval excludes the variance of the fitted tau; no multiplicity control across the sensitivities.
