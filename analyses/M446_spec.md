## 2. Why the display is cadence-stratified (record of the route)

- The pooled native v2 level is cadence-mixed: the fast-polled share of the kinetic energy runs from about half in the 10-20 C bin to a few percent in the 40-50 C bin (`regenCaptureV2.cohorts.all.cadence.mixByTemperatureBin`). A pooled bar therefore mixes a temperature gradient with a logging-resolution gradient.
- M441 B6 (decimation of fast-polled drives) failed in a majority of cells: v2 is cadence-sensitive at the order of the assumed-mass half-width. M442 harmonization reduces but does not remove this, its level is resolution-conditional, and its gate G2 (fast versus slow drives, paired by day) is NOT TESTABLE on the existing data (no cell supported in both arms on the paired even days). PCL-1, the protocol that would make G2 testable, was not run (decision in CLAUDE.md).
- Route allowed by the Director (M442 Rev 1.3 E4): display native v2 levels PER POLLING STRATUM with the M442 resolution bracket `b` attached, no pooled bar as the primary figure, no fast-versus-slow contrast.

**Consequence for the M441 Rev 2.2 ME conditions (declared now, not assumed):** (1) a Rev 3 plateau rule is NOT adopted: the plateau convention of Rev 2.2 stays (section 5) and its alternatives are shown as a convention range. (2) B6 is NOT re-run (threshold not relaxed); the display is cadence-matched, but the literal condition "passes within each regime" cannot be evaluated: B6 compares fast drives with their own thinned version, it has no counterpart for the slow stratum, and G2 is untestable. This is put to the Director as open question O1; the spec does not claim the condition is met. (3) A blind audit with one bootstrap-CI reproduction is part of this stage (section 11). (4) The Director rules before any display.

## 3. Estimands (all: apparent battery-side KE recovery, assumed class masses)

Ratio of sums over the intervals of the v2 layer (M441 Rev 2.2: engine-off proxy RPM <= 300, assumed masses, kept plateaus). For a polling stratum `p` in {fast-polled, slow-polled}, a view group `g` (pack-temperature bin, speed bin or speed zone) and a SOC selection:

- **E1 Observed, All SOC:** `R[p,g] = 100 * sum(charge) / sum(KE)` over intervals of drives in stratum `p`.
- **E2 Observed, one SOC band `s`:** `R[p,g,s]`, the conditional observed estimate within band `s` (edges 40/50/55/60/63/66/69/72/100 and the explicit "below 40 %" row, decision (b); display bands, not identified BMS thresholds). It is not a standardization.
- **E3 SOC-standardized (All SOC only):** `R_std[p,g] = sum over s in S*[p] of w~[s] R[p,g,s]` with the ONE fixed all-cohort KE-weighted reference mix `w` of M444 section 2 (decision (c): never per cohort, per stratum or per bar), common support `S*[p]` and the availability rule of M444 section 3 (coverage >= 0.80, >= 2 groups, drop order) applied WITHIN stratum `p` and view; `w~` renormalized once over `S*[p]` for that stratum and view. The standardization adjusts for SOC composition only.
- Strata: fast = master `ev_rpm_dt_med_s` < 1.0 s; slow = otherwise defined; a drive with an undefined statistic belongs to neither (M444 S15); drives on or after a PCL-1 START DATE (none today) are held out of the strata (holding rule in `tools/regen_v2_agg.py`).
- Cohort selection (`all`, `warm`, `shoulder`, `cold`, plus the existing compare mode) is applied to the drive set before stratification, by the same rules as the other additive blocks. No stratum x cohort cell is displayed as supported unless it meets section 6.

Pooled (cadence-mixed) values are not drawn as bars. They remain in the tooltip/table as a labelled reference line ("cadence-mixed pooled, reference only") so a reader can tie the new display to `regenCaptureV2` (open question O2: whether to show them at all).

## 4. Resolution bracket `b` (separate from every other interval)

Per temperature bin and speed bin, `b` = the M442 quantity (`analyses/M442_result_rerun.json`, `bracket`): max over thinning factors f in {2,3} of |harmonized(native) - harmonized(decimated f)| in percentage points, measured on fast-polled days only; cells without support borrow the largest `b` of their view and carry `borrowed: true`. It is displayed as a resolution bracket beside each bar of BOTH strata, with the statement that it was measured on fast-polled drives thinned by 2 and 3 and is a sensitivity bound, not a correction. Applying it to the slow stratum is an extrapolation, declared as such (open question O3). For SOC-band and zone cells that have no tabulated `b`, the view's largest `b` is used and flagged `borrowed`. `b` is read from the stored result file by the script and never typed; if the file changes (a later M442 rerun) the stamp records its SHA-256.

The display carries three separate quantities, never combined into a "total accuracy" interval: (i) the day-clustered 95 % interval (statistical), (ii) the assumed-mass range (closed form, M441), (iii) the resolution bracket `b`; the plateau convention range (section 5) is a fourth, also separate.

## 5. Plateau convention range

Primary = kept plateaus (M441 Rev 2.2). Reported beside each displayed cell, not drawn as an interval: the strict-drop-only value and the kept-plus-trimmed value (both already computed by the v2 layer / M444 study block). A cell where these differ from the primary by more than the cell's day interval half-width is flagged "convention-sensitive".

## 6. Support policy (one global rule, declared before results; owner decision (d))

Cell states: `empty` (no data), `failed` (cannot form an interval), `thin` (exploratory), `supported`. Supported = >= 200 strict-drop intervals on >= 10 drives and >= 5 days (legacy floors, applied within stratum x cohort x SOC selection) AND an interval precision field (half-width) is stored and shown. `thin` cells are drawn hatched and labelled; `empty` and `failed` are drawn as absent with the reason, never as 0 %. A genuine observed 0 % is distinct from "no qualifying data". These are operational reporting rules, not universal scientific guarantees. The full observed temperature-bin range is always drawn (decision (e)); the cold view is built and shows cold winter data wherever collected, with its status (the cold cohort is thin by construction).

## 7. Uncertainty

Day-clustered percentile bootstrap, calendar day as the cluster, B = 4000, seed 42, numerator and denominator of every cell resampled together, ratios and standardized sums recomputed inside each draw with `w~` fixed (conditional inference), one resample matrix per (cohort, stratum) shared by all cells of that population (the day set is the days WITH DATA of that population; M444 S14). Failed draws counted and dropped; more than 2.5 % failed = interval `failed`. Week-block sensitivity (ISO week cluster) reported in the payload for supported cells, where >= 5 weeks of support exist; adjacent samples are never treated as independent. No fast-versus-slow contrast is computed or displayed (G2 untestable). Adjacent-bin contrasts, if drawn, are within a stratum, labelled unadjusted and descriptive, and use the M444 rules (failed draw if either side fails; gap flag).

## 8. Interactive controls (compact, scoped)

1. **Polling stratum** (open question O4, recommended default "both"): "Fast-polled", "Slow-polled", "Both" (two labelled series per bin, fixed colours; counts per series). Always visible; the stratum labels explain the logger-resolution meaning ("drives logged at < 1 s median RPM sampling interval").
2. **SOC selection:** "All SOC" (default), the 8 bands, "Below 40 %".
3. **Basis:** "Observed" (default) / "SOC-standardized". The standardized basis is enabled only for "All SOC"; for a single band the control is disabled with the reason. When the standardized comparison is unavailable for a stratum or a view the chart falls back to Observed with a visible note, never silently.

Controls change the temperature, KE-speed and speed-zone views together and nothing else on the page; the charging-power and total-energy charts carry a "not affected by these controls" note. Captions, tooltips, counts and support states are all derived from the same selected rows. The three states are held in React state and survive cohort changes where valid.

## 9. Display payload and binding

Every displayed figure binds to ONE new additive script-written key `regenDisplayV2` (no hardcoded literals, no JSX arithmetic beyond layout): per cohort x stratum x SOC selection x basis, the rows of the three views with the estimate, day interval, week-block interval (where available), mass range, `b` and `borrowed`, convention range, status, n (intervals, drives, days), KE share, precision, the reference-mix coverage, dropped groups and the reasons; plus the y-axis extent inputs and the narrative fields now produced by `regenCaptureMeta` (extrema, plateau bounds, thin bins) computed from the SAME selected rows (so global extrema never describe another cohort or SOC selection). Built by a new stage function in `tools/regen_v2_stage.py` from the content-hash cache and `regen_v2_agg` (decision (f)); no raw re-read per control change and no new full-corpus scan; refreshed at every ingestion by the `regen_v2` stage; a payload stamp records spec SHA-256, master MD5, extractor version, policy hash, the SHA-256 of `M442_result_rerun.json`, the M444 reference-mix hash and the polling-stratum definition. The y-axis extent is derived from displayed estimates plus their intervals with readable ticks (no fixed 70/80 % ceiling).
