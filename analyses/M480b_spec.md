## Question
The released outcome is the per-drive p95 of the max-minus-min cell-voltage spread over all pairs with |I| > 50 A. It mixes charge and discharge, current magnitude, SoC and the timing quality of each pair, and the number of pairs varies by drive (the sidecar shows `n_loaded` below 20 on 138 drives and below 30 on 228). M480 could only adjust for per-drive summaries of these. M480b asks: **does the positive conditional slope remain when the outcome itself is restricted to pairs measured under similar conditions (current band, SoC band, small max/min report offset), with the dependence of the outcome on the number of pairs removed by design?** Answers are conditional on the restricted outcomes; none is a physical-mechanism finding, a degradation rate, or a statement about the BMS internal acquisition time.

(Rev 2) Three things are stated explicitly: (i) the one-half fraction is inherited from M480, not derived; (ii) the 50 ms threshold and the 50-80 A / 50-70 % band are the audit's choices, adopted here so that the test is comparable, not because they are known to be optimal; (iii) the foreseeable path is that O1m is underpowered (the audit's O1 interval was wide), which would leave the verdict resting on O2m alone, and that a cut at 50 ms largely selects one logging regime: the signed max/min report offset has a median of about 60 ms in the fast regime (q05 60, q95 64 ms in `timing_support_summary.json`), so cuts at 50 ms and 25 ms drop most fast-regime drives, and the regime changes with calendar time (fast stratum: 36 days, about 2 months; slow stratum +0.469 in M480). Consequences: the restrictions, the estimator, the support rule, the power gate and the decision rule are frozen here before any M480b estimate is computed; the audit's numbers are not targets; the only known-answer targets are internal (below); every reported number comes from the M480b script.

## Population, clustering, model
Drives, months, `day`, the covariates `T` (`T_pack_mean_avg`) and `I` (`peak_I_discharge`), the canonical exclusion and the day clustering are exactly those of the released model and of M480 (`degradation_trends._prep`, expected 576 drives, 117 days before outcome-specific support rules). Model for every outcome: OLS of the outcome on months, T, I; day-clustered CR1 with the t(G-1) interval (primary), plus the wild cluster bootstrap-t and CR2 (`tools/dependence_sensitivity.py`) for every primary outcome, plus the 7- and 14-day moving-block intervals (`tools/serial_dependence.py`) for the reference and the primary outcomes. Seed 42, 4,000 draws. (Rev 2) For O2m the model also contains the regime indicator (fast = 1; `I_sample_period_s` below 1.05 s), in the restricted fit and in its same-row reference fit alike; drives with an unknown regime are dropped from O2m; the regime mix (drives and days per regime) and the span in calendar months are reported for every outcome.

## Pair-level cache (design, frozen)
One compact `.npz` per drive under `pair_cache/` (git-ignored; a few MB in total), keyed by the raw-file SHA-256 (`raw_manifest.json`, else hashed bytes), the extractor source hash (`tools/pair_cache.py`), and the production constants it uses (`SPREAD_TOL` = 300 ms, `V_ALIGN_TOL` = 1500 ms, the SoC spike-mask constants of `soc_spike.py`, the |I| < 900 A artefact filter). Contents: one row for every max/min pair that production forms (`merge_asof` nearest within 300 ms of the cleaned `Vcell_max` series onto `Vcell_min`, then `dropna`), with the pair time (seconds from the first sample), the signed spread in mV, the signed offset (Rev 2: time of the paired min-cell report minus time of the max-cell report, ms; the sign is tested), the matched current (`_asof` nearest within 1,500 ms, as production), the absolute time between pair and matched current (ms), and the SoC (Rev 2: `_series(df, t, 'soc')`, which applies the M388c spike mask, matched by `_asof` nearest within 1,500 ms; NaN if none). Pairs without a matched current are excluded from O3m and from every current-defined set. Aggregation never rereads raw CSVs. A changed raw hash or extractor hash invalidates the file.
Internal known-answer checks, run on the full corpus before any outcome is computed (a failure stops the run): for every drive, the number of pairs with |I| > 50 A equals `n_loaded_spread_samples` of the master and `n_loaded` of the M479 sidecar; the pair count equals the sidecar `n_pairs`; the p95 of the spread over the loaded pairs (pandas linear interpolation, as production) equals the master `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv` within 0.05 + 1e-9 mV (the master rounds to 0.1 mV, `compute_drive_summary_v6.py` lines 963-964).

## Outcomes (frozen)
All outcomes are per-drive and use the same fixed-n estimator so that the number of pairs does not drive differences between them.
- **Fixed-n upper L-statistic (Rev 2, E1; "about q0.90", not "p95")**: for a drive with N eligible pairs sorted ascending, x(1) <= ... <= x(N), and N >= m, the outcome is the exact expectation of the p95 (numpy linear interpolation) of a random m-subset drawn without replacement: with m = 20 the p95 of a subset is 0.95 times its 19th plus 0.05 times its 20th order statistic, and E[X(j:m)] = sum_i x(i) C(i-1, j-1) C(N-i, m-j) / C(N, m). It is computed in closed form (exact hypergeometric weights, log-gamma arithmetic), so there is no random subset, no seed and no Monte Carlo noise. m = 20 is primary; m = 10 and m = 30 are sensitivities. A drive with N < m contributes no row (support floor = m). The estimand is the expected upper-tail order statistic of m exchangeable pairs; within a load episode pairs are autocorrelated, so the expected value still depends on the number of episodes; two sensitivities address that (below).
- **O0m (reference)**: eligible pairs = the released loaded set (|I| > 50 A). Its slope on the rows of a restricted outcome ("S0|rows", the same rows) is the comparator of that outcome. O0m is not the released p95 (a variable-n statistic); the released-statistic fit on the same rows is reported beside it.
- **O1m (current and SoC band)**: 50 < |I| <= 80 A and 50 <= SoC <= 70 %; pairs with no matched SoC are excluded.
- **O2m (small max/min offset)**: loaded pairs with |offset| <= 50 ms (primary); sensitivities at 25 ms and 100 ms (three thresholds, no other). Primary-adjacent, never in the verdict (Rev 2, E5): O2m within the slow regime alone.
- **O3m (negative control, low current)**: pairs with a matched current and |I| < 5 A. Interpretation fixed now: a slope here does not indicate loaded resistance; if it is positive and comparable to O0m it points to a load-independent imbalance or a logging effect; O3m never enters the verdict.
Sensitivities and descriptive companions (reported, never in the verdict; one that reverses the sign of an informative primary outcome is stated beside the verdict): m = 10 and m = 30; the fixed-n median (average of the 10th and 11th order statistics for m = 20); an episode-balanced variant (Rev 2: at most k = 5 pairs per load episode, kept at evenly spaced ranks within the episode, then the same closed form; episodes are runs of loaded pairs separated by gaps over 10 s, as in M479); a WLS fit weighted by min(N, 200) beside the OLS fit; the released-statistic per-drive p95 (variable n) of each restricted pair set with the support floor N >= m; the slow-regime stratum of O0m, O1m and O2m; the cutoff series (2026-07-31, 2026-08-31, 2026-09-30, last date) and leave-last-8-days-out for O0m, O1m, O2m; drive and day counts, the span in months, the fraction of the reference drives lost per outcome, the regime mix and the distribution of N.

## Comparison with the reference (Rev 2, E2)
For each restricted outcome the difference Delta = b_restricted - b_O0m|rows is estimated on the rows of the restricted outcome, with a day-cluster bootstrap (4,000 draws, seed 42, whole days resampled with replacement, both fits refitted on every draw) giving a percentile interval for Delta. A point-estimate ratio alone is not a comparison.

## Power gate and operating characteristic (frozen, run before the real outcomes are interpreted) (Rev 2, E4)
For each primary outcome (O1m, O2m, m = 20) on its actual rows: remove the fitted months component from the outcome, add a known slope theta, resample whole days with replacement (500 draws, seed 42; every duplicated day counts as a distinct cluster), refit with the same model, and record the detection probability (share of draws whose CR1 lower bound is above zero; at theta = 0 the nominal level is 0.025), the median CR1 interval half-width and the share of draws returning each verdict of the decision rule below. theta is run at 0, 0.5 * b_ref and b_ref, where b_ref is the O0m|rows estimate of that outcome. The minimum effect with 80 % power is (t_0.975 + t_0.80) * SE with df = G - 1, G the day-cluster count of that outcome. An outcome is **underpowered** if its detection probability at theta = b_ref is below 0.8 or its CR1 half-width on the real data exceeds b_ref; an underpowered outcome can neither support nor refute the claim ("underpowered / inconclusive", never "explained").

## Decision rule (frozen; each outcome can occur) (Rev 2, E2 and E3)
Informative = a primary outcome (O1m, O2m at their primary settings) that has at least 100 drives on at least 40 days and is not underpowered.
- **Not supported (explained at least in part)** iff both primary outcomes are informative and, for each, the point estimate is below half of its O0m|rows estimate and the upper bound of the Delta interval is below zero; or any informative outcome has a negative point estimate with the upper bound of its Delta interval below zero.
- **Supported (provisional)** iff at least one primary outcome is informative and, for every informative primary outcome, the lower bound of the Delta interval is above -0.5 times its O0m|rows estimate and its CR1 lower bound is above zero.
- **Consistent, imprecise** iff at least one primary outcome is informative and neither rule above applies.
- **Underpowered / inconclusive** iff no primary outcome is informative.
The one-half fraction, the 100-drive and 40-day minimum and the 0.8 detection probability are fixed conventions chosen before any estimate; they are not tuned. The verdict is reported with the full table whichever it is, and it names the informative outcome(s): when only one primary outcome is informative the verdict and every sentence derived from it name that outcome (never "restricted pair sets" in the plural for one outcome).
Combined reading with M480 (fixed now) (Rev 2, E8): if M480 returns `supported_provisional` and M480b returns "supported (provisional)", the only allowed sentence is "the positive conditional slope is not explained by the tested drive-level proxies, and has a point estimate of at least half of the same-row reference (Delta interval stated) in [the named informative restricted outcome(s)]; logger-timing and loading-mix confounding at the pair level are not excluded for untested restrictions". If M480b returns "not supported" the M480 sentence must not be used; if "consistent, imprecise" or "underpowered / inconclusive" the M480 verdict remains provisional and unreportable.

## Language (frozen; enforced by the M480 language rule and an added rule at implementation)
Allowed: "restricted-condition slope estimate (fixed-n upper L-statistic, about q0.90, m = 20)", "has a point estimate of at least half of the same-row reference (Delta interval stated)", "not explained by the tested restrictions (provisional)", "consistent, imprecise", "underpowered / inconclusive", "procedure-dependent lower bound", "logged timestamp difference between the max-cell and min-cell reports". Forbidden: synchronized-pair spread, true or simultaneous spread, load-independent, cadence-corrected / -invariant, not a logging artefact, robust, confirmed, validated, persists / persistent, established, degradation, SOH, capacity loss, rules out, acquisition latency, measurement error of the BMS, and "p95" for the fixed-n statistic.
