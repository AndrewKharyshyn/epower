## 0. Why, and what is already known
- The published panel "Between-drive logged share by month" (M683, `S.betweenDriveLogging`) uses the OBD app-distance counter only. It says 100 % for October, which a reader reads as "nothing was missed". The cloud export (MyNISSAN, auxiliary since M729) shows movement outside the OBD windows in every month. A reader needs the two distances side by side by month.
- Owner decisions (Andrii, chat, 2026-11-25): cloud stays an auxiliary source but is complementary to OBD (same car, same drives); distance is counted once; 17/18 October and the following unlogged weeks are real drives that the cloud tracked. The KPI "Logged share of lifetime odometer" (total logged km / dash ODO, owner 2026-10-03) is NOT touched.
- Already established and not re-opened here: M739 (audit and Director decision on file) found the cloud step rule reconstructs a logged drive's distance with a median absolute relative error of about 2 % but an aggregate signed bias of about -6 % (interval -10 % to -3 %), verdict "not met" against 5 %: cloud kilometres are NOT carried as a reconstructed distance. This milestone therefore reports them as "at least" values (a lower bound under a stated rule), never as a total or a share.

## 1. Constraints
- OBD stays primary; the cloud never replaces or rescales an OBD value. Distance is counted once: cloud movement inside a padded OBD window is not added.
- Language (rev 2): the words "missed", "missing", "lost km" and "cloud-confirmed" are not used (M749 / M760). "At least E2 km of speed-supported cloud movement outside the OBD logs" counts as cloud movement outside the logs and M749 restricts cloud kilometres outside the logs, so this milestone adds a `language_gate.py` rule with a ledger id that NARROWS M749 for this block only (the only permitted phrase is the one above, always with the "auxiliary; not an article figure" note), and the CHANGELOG entry quotes the owner verbatim: "OBD + cloud are mutual-complementary since they cover same car and same drives." and "Oct 18 and next 2 weeks were unlogged (no OBD), but with real drives that cloud data was still tracking." (Andrii, chat, 2026-11-25). Wording otherwise: "reconstructed / estimated"; "speed-supported cloud movement outside the OBD logs"; never "measured", never "the missed distance is X" (it is "at least X"), never a logged share for a month that includes cloud kilometres (the denominator is not a census, section 4 D3).
- No published figure changes: `drive_master.csv` (MD5 `d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98`) unchanged; the M683 panel and its numbers stay, moved behind a disclosure titled "Continuity of the app-distance counter". New payload block `cloudMonthlyCoverage` is additive (new key, nothing else in `summary_arrays.json` changes; isolation diff).
- Privacy: aggregates only: no coordinates, no per-drive tracks, no VIN in the payload.
- Time: every timestamp is parsed to UTC from the full local timestamp before interval arithmetic; the Kyiv calendar month and date are derived from UTC afterwards (zoneinfo Europe/Kyiv). Both DST folds and midnight are tested.

## 2. Data (read-only)
`drive_master.csv` (windows: Kyiv start time + `duration_s`), the cloud store (`XT_CLOUD_STORE`; Odometer, Trip_Information speed streams), `analyses/M739_unlogged_exposure.json` (cloud missingness, quoted by reference), `tools/mynissan_unlogged.accepted_steps` as the step builder (reused code, not re-derived). Export span: first to last odometer sample; drives outside it are excluded from the OBD column and listed.

## 3. Definitions (frozen)
- OBD window: the [start, start + duration_s] of EVERY raw OBD drive file (canonical, excluded, comparison_only and drives outside the master), padded by P seconds at each end (primary P = 45 s). Files not in the master are listed by name in the output, so logged driving cannot inflate E2 or G1.
- Accepted cloud step (primary rule): two successive odometer samples at most 35 s apart, positive increment, step-implied speed at most 180 km/h, a speed sample within 25 s of the step midpoint with speed above 2 km/h. A step is "outside" when it does not overlap any padded OBD window at all. Month = Kyiv calendar month of BOTH endpoints; a step whose endpoints are in different months is counted in the category "month unknown" (kept visible, not assigned).
- Gap increment: a positive odometer increment across more than 35 s (any length) that the step rule does not accept. Class G1: its interval overlaps no padded OBD window (movement of unresolved timing and route, outside the logs). Class G2: its interval overlaps an OBD window (it may include logged driving; never counted as missed). Month rule as above.
- Rejected steps are counted, not dropped silently: negative increment, non-monotonic or conflicting timestamp, implied speed above 180 km/h, no speed support.
- OBD distance by month: sum of master `distance_km` of drives whose start (Kyiv) is in the month and inside the export span. Partial months are flagged (first and last month of the span).
- Cloud reporting gaps: dates inside the span with no odometer and no speed row, and holes of at least 24 h, listed by date (from the stream, rechecked here).

## 4. Estimands and rules (pre-registered)
- E1 per month: OBD km, drives, days. E1 and E2 are censuses under a stated rule: they carry n (drives, steps, days) and a definition range, not a sampling interval (the day-clustered bootstrap of CLAUDE.md applies to samples, which these are not).
- E2 per month: speed-supported cloud km outside the OBD logs under the primary rule, with the number of steps and the number of days.
- E3 per month: G1 km (unresolved timing) and G2 km, each listed separately and NOT stacked on E1 or E2.
- E4 range per month: lower value = E2; upper value = E2 + G1. It is a definition range, never a bracket, bound or confidence interval: driving inside cloud holes (21 dates without rows) is unobservable here, so E2 + G1 is not an upper bound on the true movement; it carries a different mark.
- Sensitivity (reported as the min-max of E2 per month): P in {30, 45, 60} s; cloud-to-OBD alignment shift in {-16, 0, +16} s applied to the cloud (shifting moves the cloud, not the windows); speed-support window in {20, 25} s; speed floor in {0, 2} km/h; step-gap threshold in {25, 35, 45} s (it moves km between E2 and G1). The grid is FULL FACTORIAL (108 combinations); the min-max of E2 per month over all combinations is reported, and each factor one-at-a-time is also listed. The primary value stays the primary rule's.
- D1 (display): the chart is displayed iff (a) the audit-independent reproduction (section 6) matches each month's E1 within 1 km and E2 within 1 km or 1 %, whichever is larger, and (b) the E2 sensitivity range (full factorial) for every month with E2 above 50 km is within +-10 % of the primary value (months at or below 50 km are not gated and are labelled "small"); otherwise only the table with the range is shown.
- D2 (reading): E2 is called "at least"; the M739 result (cloud step distance of logged drives below their OBD distance: aggregate signed difference and the shortest-tercile difference, both BOUND from `analyses/M739_unlogged_exposure.json`, never typed; the shortest tercile is much larger than the aggregate) is stated beside it as the reason the figure is a lower value. It is described as the cloud reconstruction being lower than the OBD distance, never as a "bias of the logged distance". The lower value is not strict: misalignment can add movement.
- D3 (no percentage): no monthly or overall percentage that mixes OBD and cloud km is published. The M683 percentages stay on their own basis and are labelled "counter continuity".
- D4 (day check): for every calendar day, E2 + G1 is listed in the tool output, and the audit cases are tested: a day with no OBD log but cloud movement (the 18 October case) must appear as a day of speed-supported cloud movement outside the logs; a day with a very long gap increment (the 17 October case) must put it in G2, not in E2.
- Falsification: the claim "the OBD logs miss measurable movement in a month" is falsified for a month when E2 and E4's upper value are both below the stated resolution (5 km: about two accepted steps of a short trip and about the median logged unit scale of M739, so a month below it holds at most one short trip). The claim "OBD plus cloud is complementary" is not tested by this milestone beyond D1 to D4.

## 5. Validation (reported, not gating)
- V3 capture check: the same step rule applied INSIDE the OBD windows: cloud km inside windows divided by the OBD km of those windows, per month (a transport check of the cloud rule; cross-check with the M739 aggregate, bias about -6 %). Reported beside the chart in the method note.
- Cross-reference: the total E2 against the M739 strict-unit total (about 1,918 km over the full span); a difference above 5 % is explained in the tool output (different padding and joining rules) or reported as a deviation.

## 6. Gates
1. `drive_master.csv` MD5 unchanged before and after; isolation diff: only the new `cloudMonthlyCoverage` key (and its stamp) added to the payload.
2. Known-answer tests: constructed odometer and speed series on a synthetic master: a step fully outside a window is counted; a step overlapping a padded window is not; a step across a month boundary goes to "month unknown"; a negative step is rejected and counted; a G1 and a G2 gap increment are separated; a DST-fold timestamp and midnight crossing land on the right local date; planted month totals are recovered exactly; the tool skips cleanly without a store.
3. Every number in the payload is written by the script from the live corpus; the dashboard binds to `S.cloudMonthlyCoverage` only (no typed number).
4. Blind audit (claim and data paths only, no code): reproduces E1, E2 and the G1/G2 split per month and the two audit days, from the raw OBD drive files and the raw cloud store, with its own implementation.
5. Director decision; CHANGELOG entry with this spec's sha256 and the master MD5.

## 10. Rev 2.1 clarifications (written AFTER the first tool run and the blind audit; no threshold, estimand or decision rule changed)
Rev 2 file sha256 before this section: f98f404213641cfc5f2b8a47a24417757d07922bdb7a3427ca69894503dc67c5. The first tool run implemented "a speed sample within 25 s of the step midpoint with speed above 2 km/h" as the NEAREST sample; the blind audit (analyses/M776_audit/) implemented it as ANY such sample, which is the literal spec text, and differed by 0.3 to 13.6 km per month. The tool was corrected to the literal reading before any payload was written; after the correction tool and audit agree on every month (OBD km, drives, days; cloud km outside the logs and its steps; G1, G2). Pinned readings:
- Speed support: any speed sample with |t - midpoint| at most the support window and speed above the floor.
- Overlap with a padded window is inclusive (a step touching the padded edge overlaps).
- Study span starts at the first OBD window (11 May); cloud steps before it are not counted (including them would add 92.9 km to May, audit note). The export span ends at the last odometer sample; drives starting inside it enter E1.
- A day is the Kyiv date of the END of the step or gap increment (t2); an increment crossing local midnight (2.5 km on 17/18 October) therefore falls on the later date, the audit assigned it to the earlier date; month totals are unaffected.
- e4ORCE_*.csv files in raw/ belong to another vehicle and are not OBD windows of this car (listed in the output); the two comparison-only files (2026-07-30 22-06-51, 2026-08-01 20-53-20) ARE windows (the Director's ruling).
- Odometer rows listed in conflicts.csv are dropped; zero-increment pairs are ignored.
- Grid: the step-gap value 25 s is below the nominal 30 s cloud cadence and reclassifies almost every step as a gap increment, so the pre-registered D1(b) check fails on the full grid by construction. The tool reports both the full factorial and the same grid without the 25 s point (fields ...Gap35Plus); the verdict on D1(b) is the Director's, and the deviation is recorded here.
