## 0. What the stream holds (counts only, before this spec was written)
`Vehicle_Information` rows are logged at a median interval of 30 s (97 % of consecutive rows within 30 s; larger holes exist). `StatusWipingFront` takes the values 0 (38,025 rows), 6 (335 rows), 7 (15 rows) and is missing on 1,167 rows. The M771 shares counted a drive's wiper share as the fraction of its moving rows with a non-zero code; a single on-row (one 30 s sample) is enough for the 0.05 threshold at about 12 rows per drive, and a washer burst or a one-off sweep can set that row. The M729 definition is "consecutive ON samples spanning more than 30 s".

## 1. Constraints
- Report-only. `drive_master.csv` MD5 d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98 asserted before and after (a different master stops, exit 3, nothing written); the cloud store manifest sha256 pinned; no winter-frozen file (M726); no positions or absolute cloud odometer values read or written. ## 2. Definitions fixed here
- **Wiper span rule (primary):** within a drive's moving window (speed at least 10 km/h, as M729), a wiper EPISODE is a run of at least two consecutive `Vehicle_Information` rows with a non-zero `StatusWipingFront` whose timestamps span MORE than 30 s (that is, at least two rows, the first and last more than 30 s apart) with no hole of more than 90 s inside the run. A drive is a **wet-proxy drive (span rule)** if it has at least one episode and at least 10 moving rows (as M771); the dry reference is a drive with no non-zero row at all and at least 10 moving rows; drives with only single isolated on-rows are a third group, "isolated wiper rows", reported as counts and excluded from the contrast. The per-drive episode share = on-rows within episodes over moving rows.
- **Source codes:** the primary pools codes 6 and 7; a sensitivity restricts episodes to code 6 (code 7 has 15 rows in total and is reported as counts).
- **Cold-start flag (the single fixed definition, used from M772 on):** a drive is a cold start when the gap between the end of the previous drive of the master and its start is at least 3 hours (or it is the first drive of the master). The previous drive is the one before it in the master ordered by start time; the gap uses `time_end` and `time_start`. The former calendar first-drive-of-day flag (M769 to M771) is kept as a sensitivity. The 3-hour value is an assumed engine-cooling time (stated, with sensitivities at 2 and 6 hours). M769 to M771 are NOT recomputed (their registered results stand); the new definition applies to every later milestone and to the M772 retest.

## 3. Estimands
- **G' (support gate, retest of M771 G):** counts of wet-proxy drives under the span rule (primary), under code 6 only, and under the M771 share definition for comparison, by class, thermal class and month, with the fuel-basis counts and the floor of 20 drives on 10 days on the fuel basis; the verdict. The gate result of M771 (2 fuel-basis wiper-proxy drives on 2 days) is the comparison.
- **A1' (confirmatory, only if G' passes):** as M771 A1 (fuel L/100 km, logged / app-calculated, wet-proxy minus dry, month fixed effects), with the fixed cold-start flag, the same expectation (+2 % lower end) and reading rule, MDE, rank-deficient resamples counted. Below the floor: counts only.
- **A3' (exploratory):** the dark-proxy fuel contrast of M771 recomputed with the fixed cold-start flag and with the 2 and 6 hour sensitivities and the calendar flag, showing how the estimate moves with the definition; no expectation; unresolved readings stay unresolved.
- **C:** how many drives change cold-start status between the calendar and the gap definition; the distribution of the gaps; the share of cold starts by class and month.

## 4. Falsification and acceptance
Known-answer tests before the first run (one CI-list line): planted wiper streams (an isolated on-row, two consecutive rows exactly 30 s apart (not a span: it needs MORE than 30 s), two rows 31 s apart (a span), a run with a 100 s hole (split), codes 6 and 7 mixed, missing values) are classified exactly; the moving-window restriction applies; the cold-start gap rule is exact on the boundaries (2 h 59 min versus 3 h) and for the first drive; a foreign master stops; no per-drive rows, dates or file names in a result. The tool reads the compressed stream directly from the pinned store and never writes into the store.
