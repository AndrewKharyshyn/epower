## 0. What the stream holds (counts only, before this spec was written)
`Vehicle_Information` rows are logged at a median interval of 30 s (97 % of consecutive rows within 30 s; larger holes exist). `StatusWipingFront` takes the values 0 (38,025 rows), 6 (335 rows), 7 (15 rows) and is missing on 1,167 rows. The M771 shares counted a drive's wiper share as the fraction of its moving rows with a non-zero code; a single on-row (one 30 s sample) is enough for the 0.05 threshold at about 12 rows per drive, and a washer burst or a one-off sweep can set that row. The M729 definition is "consecutive ON samples spanning more than 30 s".

## 1. Constraints
- Report-only. `drive_master.csv` MD5 d514d6009d6d9a0a168ad4fb09b9bb98 asserted before and after (a different master stops, exit 3, nothing written); the cloud store manifest sha256 pinned; no winter-frozen file (M726); no positions or absolute cloud odometer values read or written. Outputs `analyses/M772_*`: aggregates only, no per-drive rows, dates or names.

## 2. Definitions fixed here
- **Wiper span rule (primary):** within a drive's moving window (speed at least 10 km/h, as M729), a wiper EPISODE is a run of at least two consecutive `Vehicle_Information` rows with a non-zero `StatusWipingFront` whose timestamps span MORE than 30 s (that is, at least two rows, the first and last more than 30 s apart) with no hole of more than 90 s inside the run. A drive is a **wet-proxy drive (span rule)** if it has at least one episode and at least 10 moving rows (as M771); the dry reference is a drive with no non-zero row at all and at least 10 moving rows; drives with only single isolated on-rows are a third group, "isolated wiper rows", reported as counts and excluded from the contrast. The per-drive episode share = on-rows within episodes over moving rows.
- **Source codes:** the primary pools codes 6 and 7; a sensitivity restricts episodes to code 6 (code 7 has 15 rows in total and is reported as counts).
- **Cold-start flag (the single fixed definition, used from M772 on):** a drive is a cold start when the gap between the end of the previous drive of the master and its start is at least 3 hours (or it is the first drive of the master). The previous drive is the one before it in the master ordered by start time; the gap uses `time_end` and `time_start`. The former calendar first-drive-of-day flag (M769 to M771) is kept as a sensitivity. The 3-hour value is an assumed engine-cooling time (stated, with sensitivities at 2 and 6 hours). M769 to M771 are NOT recomputed (their registered results stand); the new definition applies to every later milestone and to the M772 retest.

## 3. Estimands
- **G' (support gate, retest of M771 G):** counts of wet-proxy drives under the span rule (primary), under code 6 only, and under the M771 share definition for comparison, by class, thermal class and month, with the fuel-basis counts and the floor of 20 drives on 10 days on the fuel basis; the verdict. The gate result of M771 (2 fuel-basis wiper-proxy drives on 2 days) is the comparison.
- **A1' (confirmatory, only if G' passes):** as M771 A1 (fuel L/100 km, logged / app-calculated, wet-proxy minus dry, month fixed effects), with the fixed cold-start flag, the same expectation (+2 % lower end) and reading rule, MDE, rank-deficient resamples counted. Below the floor: counts only.
- **A3' (exploratory):** the dark-proxy fuel contrast of M771 recomputed with the fixed cold-start flag and with the 2 and 6 hour sensitivities and the calendar flag, showing how the estimate moves with the definition; no expectation; unresolved readings stay unresolved.
- **C:** how many drives change cold-start status between the calendar and the gap definition; the distribution of the gaps; the share of cold starts by class and month.

## 7. Rev 2 (2026-11-25; adopts the Director review of Rev 1, `analyses/M772_director_review_rev1.json`; overrides sections 0 to 6 where they differ; before any code)
7.1 **Episode rule (primary).** The cloud `Vehicle_Information` stream is logged about every 30 s. A wiper EPISODE is a run of at least TWO adjacent rows with a non-missing, non-zero `StatusWipingFront`; two rows are adjacent when their step is at most 35 s (as the M729 rear-fog rule); a step over 35 s, a row with a zero code, a row with a missing code, or a moving-window exit (a stop: the displayed speed below 10 km/h) ends the run. The sample-hold duration (rows x step) of an episode therefore exceeds 30 s. Sensitivities: the strict timestamp span (first to last row more than 30 s), the hole limit of 90 s, and episodes restricted to code 6 (codes 6 and 7 are pooled and unnamed in the primary; code 7 has 15 rows and is counts only). The speed source for the moving window is the cloud displayed speed (`Trip_Information`, nearest row within 20 s, at least 10 km/h) exactly as M729.
7.2 **Population and the dropped threshold (a second definition change).** A drive is a wet-proxy drive (span rule) if it has at least one episode and at least 10 moving rows with a non-missing wiper code; the dry reference has at least 10 such rows and no non-zero code at all; drives with only isolated on-rows are EXCLUDED from the contrast (counts only; they enter only through the nested counts); drives whose moving rows all have a missing code are excluded. The 0.05 share threshold of M771 is DROPPED: a long drive with two on-rows out of 40 or more is a wet-proxy drive, so the set is bounded by the any-activity set (about 49 drives), not by the 8 drives of M771. The M771 definition (share at least 0.05) is reproduced inside the tool as the nested control.
7.3 **Nested control (hard gate).** From the streams the tool reproduces the M771 gate exactly (241 eligible drives on 102 days, 8 wet-proxy drives on 7 days at 0.05, 2 on 2 days on the fuel basis, 212 dry) and the dark-proxy fuel difference -0.114 with the flag over the whole master and -0.373 with the flag within the sample (tolerance 1e-9 on counts, 1e-6 on the estimates; the M771 numbers are read from `analyses/M771_weather_proxy.json`). Any mismatch stops (exit 3) and writes nothing.
7.4 **Long-soak flag (the single fixed definition from M772 on).** "Long-soak start": 3 hours or more between the end of the previous logged drive of the FULL master (ordered by start time; `time_end` and `time_start`, duration `duration_s`) and the start of this drive; computed over the full master, never within a sample; the first drive of the master is flag "unknown" and is excluded from any soak contrast (not called a long-soak start). Sensitivities: 2 hours, 6 hours, and the calendar first-drive-of-day flag of M769 to M771. Wording: "long-soak start (3 h or more after the previous logged drive; assumed cooling time)", never "cold start". Unlogged driving shortens real gaps, so the flag over-labels long soaks (stated). M769 to M771 are NOT recomputed; they used the calendar flag (disclosed).
7.5 **G' and A1'.** G' counts the wet-proxy drives under the span rule (primary), the strict-span and 90 s sensitivities and code 6 only, by class, thermal class and month, with the fuel-basis counts and the floor of 20 drives on 10 days on the fuel basis; the gate is outcome-blind (no fuel value is read below the floor; the design MDE check applies). A1' (fuel L/100 km, wet-proxy minus dry, month fixed effects, the long-soak flag as the covariate, the +2 % lower-end rule, rank-deficient resamples counted) is written only if G' passes, else counts only.
7.6 **A3'.** The dark-proxy fuel contrast with the primary definition = the M771 wet-exclusion set (so only the flag varies) with the 3 hour long-soak flag; the span-rule wet exclusion and the 2 hour, 6 hour and calendar flags as sensitivities; nothing is picked; the spread of the estimates across the definitions is the finding.
7.7 **C.** Gaps between consecutive logged drives as binned aggregates only (bins of 1, 3, 6, 12 and 24 hours); the number of drives whose flag differs between the calendar and the 3 hour definition; the share of long-soak starts by class and month; no per-drive rows.
7.8 **Known-answer tests (before the first run, one CI-list line).** Planted streams: an isolated on-row; two on-rows 30 s apart (an episode); a 36 s step (a split); a missing code inside a run (a split); a stop inside a run (a split); codes 6 and 7 mixed; a run of 3 rows; the strict-span sensitivity on a 30 s pair (not a span) and a 31 s pair; the moving-window restriction; the soak flag on the boundaries (2 h 59 min versus 3 h), for the first drive (unknown) and over the full master; the nested control fails on a planted difference; a foreign master stops; no per-drive rows, dates or file names in a result.
7.9 **Order of work.** (i) tool `tools/wiper_episodes.py` and tests with a CI-list line, before the first run; (ii) first run; (iii) blind audit; (iv) Director decision; (v) CHANGELOG (closing the coast-down item by the owner's decision and recording the long-soak definition), open_items, release_identity, release_check, PR.
