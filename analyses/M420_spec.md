## What exists and what is missing (facts on disk, to be reproduced by the tool, not typed)
- `energyUncertaintyMC` (Tier 1 precompute + MC, recomputed every ingestion): conditional interval over I/V alignment tolerance (500-2000 ms), ADC quantisation, gap truncation and the current offset; `sourceBudget` (alignment-only 0.0736 %, quantisation-only SD 9e-05 %, gap-truncation-only 0.4672 %), `offsetGrossSensitivity` (literal pass at the released offset: +0.346 %), `independentIntegrationCheck` (true trapezoid / midpoint / 1 Hz down-sampling / coulomb check; a code-correctness check, not a scenario table).
- `loggerCadence.sensitivity` (M379b): emulated fast-to-slow thinning, gross-throughput ratio 0.9741 (CI 0.9728-0.9755; 130 drives, 26 days); not a causal cadence effect.
- `capacityBasis` / `exposureBasis`: capacity references (legacy 2.1 kWh, rated 5.0 Ah documentary, window capacity ~1.80 kWh); GTC / FCE scale inversely with the chosen reference.
- NOT present: the estimator-choice scenarios the audit quotes (left-hold instead of linear power about +4.49 %, no bridging of intervals over 5 s about -0.0956 %, backward-only voltage matching about -0.0032 %: "not re-run"), a gap-cap sweep (the self-audit: "3 s vs 10 s moved net by 1 %"), and any single place that lists the components with their basis, support and chronology, and says which are omitted.

## Deliverable
An additive block `integrationScenarios` (+ stamp) written by `tools/integration_scenarios.py` (modelled on `tools/serial_dependence.py` / `tools/thermal_support_ledger.py`: idempotent, allow-list `/integrationScenarios`, `/_artifactStamps/integrationScenarios`, `--dry-run`), a stage after `thermal_support_ledger`, and a Health-tab panel beside the MC. Table of scenario rows; each row: `id`, `family`, `kind` (estimator choice / data-process / reference choice), `definition`, quantity (gross throughput kWh, net draw kWh; GTC / FCE are the throughput divided by the reference), `scenarioKwh` and `shiftKwh`, `ratioToReleased` (+ day-clustered percentile bootstrap CI, seed 42, 4000 draws, resampling unit = calendar day, ratio of sums), `nDrives`, `nDays`, `support` (first / last date), `status` (computed in this run / carried from a named milestone with its own n), `inMonteCarlo` (true / false: which MC component, if any, already carries it). Rows are NEVER summed, unioned or turned into one interval (no total / combined field exists in the block; the semantic gate checks the absence).

## Scenario set (fixed here, not tuned; re-integration from the raw I / V samples of every drive with a finite released `gross_throughput_kwh`)
Baseline pass: tolerance 1500 ms nearest, 5 s step cap, piecewise-linear power with the zero-crossing-aware directional split, offset 0 - the production algorithm of `compute_drive_summary_v6.py` (and `energy_mc_precompute._integrate`). It must reproduce the master `gross_discharge_kwh`, `gross_charge_kwh`, `gross_throughput_kwh` per drive (gate: maximum absolute deviation <= 5e-4 kWh; deviating drives listed).
1. Alignment (family `alignment`): A1 tolerance 1000 ms, A2 tolerance 2000 ms (nearest), A3 backward-only voltage match (the last voltage at or before the current sample, tolerance 1500 ms).
2. Interpolation (family `interpolation`): B1 left-hold (interval power = the power at the interval start), B2 right-hold (power at the interval end); the production trapezoid is the baseline. For a constant-power interval the sign split is trivial; no crossing term.
3. Gaps (family `gaps`): C1 no bridging (intervals longer than 5 s contribute zero instead of the 5 s cap), C2 cap 2 s, C3 cap 10 s, C4 cap 30 s.
4. Offset model (family `offset`, `inMonteCarlo: true`): D1 sample-level offset at the released point estimate, D2 / D3 at the two ends of its day-cluster 95 % interval (read from `offsetUncertainty`); net draw is shown beside (the offset does not cancel in the gross integral, it does in the net up to the V*h term).
5. Cadence (family `cadence`, `status: carried`): the M379b emulation rows copied from `loggerCadence.sensitivity` with their own n (130 drives / 26 days) and the not-a-causal-effect label; not recomputed.
6. Quantisation (family `quantisation`, `carried`): the MC component (SD of the percent shift) copied from `energyUncertaintyMC.sourceBudget`.
7. Capacity reference (family `capacity`, `kind: reference choice`, applies to GTC / FCE only, throughput unchanged): the ratio of the rated-5-Ah to the legacy-2.1-kWh reference (`exposureBasis`) and of the window-capacity point estimate (`capacityBasis.lag["0"].E_kWh.est`) to 2.1 kWh, read from the payload; described as a reference scaling, not an uncertainty of the measured throughput.
Omitted components, stated in the block and on the dashboard: lag beyond the tolerance sweep (not identifiable from round-robin logs; Ready-mode experiment pending), I-V co-timing and PID polling order, voltage-sag / internal resistance effects on power, the current-sensor gain, unlogged drives and unlogged intervals, the fuel-side quantities.

## Reporting rules (pre-registered)
1. Named estimand: the corpus gross throughput of the drives with a finite released `gross_throughput_kwh` (kWh); every row is a change of ONE choice; no interaction between choices is evaluated or implied.
2. Each row carries its chronology and common support (first / last date, nDrives, nDays); all computed rows share the same drive set (excluded drives listed with a reason).
3. The MC interval stays as released and is described as conditional on the included components; the sentence names the included and the omitted ones. A scenario spread is written as "scenario range of single-choice changes", never as an interval, a CI, an error bar or a total.
4. Language: forbidden "total uncertainty", "combined uncertainty", "overall uncertainty", "full uncertainty", "error budget" (display text), "true throughput", "ground truth", "accuracy of" for the MC; required for each row group the `kind` label; the left-hold / no-bridging / backward-only rows are "estimator sensitivity, not an identified ground truth".
5. A scenario whose CI excludes 1 is "differs from the released value under this choice", never "significant".

## Known-answer tests (`tests/synthetic/test_integration_scenarios.py`, in CI)
Synthetic I / V series with hand-computed energies: constant power (every interpolation equal), a linear ramp (left-hold, right-hold and trapezoid differ by the closed form), a discharge to charge crossing (production gross = closed form; hold variants have no crossing term), a gap of 8 s (cap 5 s gives 5 s, no-bridging 0 s, cap 10 s the full 8 s), a V series that lags I by 1.2 s (nearest vs backward-only), an offset on a symmetric signal (gross shifts, net does not). Baseline reproduces `energy_mc_precompute._integrate` on the same frames to 1e-9. Mutation: changing a scenario definition changes the row; the block contains no `total`, `combined` or `pooled` key (recursive check).

## Limits (disclosed in advance)
Single vehicle, one driver, May-October; the scenarios change one choice at a time and say nothing about their joint effect; the production trapezoid is a released convention, not an identified ground truth (the audit says the same); cadence and quantisation rows are carried from their own studies and are not on this run's drive set; `lag` cannot be bounded from the round-robin logs (omitted, stated); percent shifts are corpus ratios of sums and are dominated by long drives (the per-drive spread is reported beside).

## Rev 1 (2026-10-12, after the Director's review "revise"; implemented before the tool was run on the corpus)
- **Estimands.** Primary: gross throughput (kWh) integrated from logged HV current and voltage over the drives with a finite released `gross_discharge_kwh` (this is the filter of `energy_mc_precompute.build`, i.e. the Monte Carlo drive set; the gate compares the baseline total with the Monte Carlo nominal). Secondary: net draw, reported ONLY as a shift in kWh (a percentage of a net of about 50 kWh would inflate it). Gross discharge and gross charge are reported per row. "measured throughput" is not used in any text.
- **Scenario set changes.** Added A0 (tolerance 500 ms, so the sweep matches the Monte Carlo range 500-2000 ms) and a 3 s cap (C3; C2 = 2 s, C4 = 10 s, C5 = 30 s); hold variants use the same alignment and cap as the baseline; the midpoint rule is cited from `independentIntegrationCheck`, not duplicated. A gap inventory of the baseline (count and duration of intervals over 5 / 10 / 30 s, time beyond the cap) is part of the block. D1 must reproduce `offsetGrossSensitivity.throughputShiftKwh` (gate, 0.05 kWh); D2 / D3 are deterministic rows at the ends of the offset interval, carry NO variability interval and are not called bounds of the throughput.
- **`inMonteCarlo` pre-registered.** A0, A1, A2: true (alignment tolerance sweep); C2-C5: "partial" (gap truncation is in the Monte Carlo as time beyond the 5 s cap); A3, B1, B2, C1: false; D1-D3: true (offset); cadence rows false; quantisation true.
- **Reporting.** The computed shift is exact on this corpus. The day-cluster percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws, calendar day) of the ratio is allowed and is labelled "variability of the ratio across days" (key `dayVariabilityOfRatio95`), never an uncertainty of the total and never with test language. The per-drive spread is required: min, P10, median, P90, max over the drives with a baseline throughput >= 0.1 kWh, with the count of drives for which it is undefined. Subtables: computed rows; carried studies (cadence with its own n, quantisation, midpoint) which are never applied to the computed drive set; capacity references (G1 rated / legacy factor, G2 window-capacity factor with the `capacityBasis` interval carried; reference scaling of GTC / FCE only; no ratio interval; the window estimate is a descriptive `capacityBasis` result, not an accepted capacity). The Monte Carlo may share the panel as a separate reference row with its conditional sentence and the statement that its centre is not the nominal (the offset shifts it); it is never on one axis with the scenarios, never drawn as whiskers, never unioned.
- **Language rules (gate).** Forbidden in the displayed text: "measured throughput", "bias" (for a scenario), "corrected", "error" (for a scenario), "worst case", "bound"/"bounds" (of throughput), "total uncertainty", "combined uncertainty", "overall uncertainty", "full uncertainty", "error budget", "true throughput", "ground truth" (except in the negated reading sentence), "EFC" for FCE. Required: "throughput integrated from logged HV current and voltage", "raw/ = sha256-verified originals (M366)", "not a causal cadence effect", "reference scaling; CAP_KWH=2.1 verified:false", "FCE". Audit numbers stay out of code and tests (they are cited as known-answer targets in the CHANGELOG only; disagreement is reported, not tuned).
- **Audit.** Blind (analytical-auditor, definitions + data path only, no code): baseline, A3, B1, B2, C1, the 3 s and 10 s caps, D1, the per-drive spread and the bootstrap unit. The Director's wording ruling on the dashboard text precedes the binding.
- **Side by side.** The "union of the bootstrap CI and the method range" is withdrawn (`analyses/M403_spec.md`: reported side by side); this block follows that rule.
- **Risks named by the Director.** The hold-variant and cadence shifts are about ten times the Monte Carlo width, so the Monte Carlo must not be readable as accuracy (hence the omitted-components sentence in the same panel); ratios are dominated by long drives; the shift may vary with season and cadence (follow-up split by month and regime, not in this block); drives that fail the baseline gate on the M366-swapped files are listed, not tuned; any use in the article needs an Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off.
