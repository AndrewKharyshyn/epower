## 1. Support collapse (reporting only)
Per stratum and direction the payload gets `supportCollapse`: for each of the three definitions (primary interpolated, `noInterp`, `deadband`) the eligible share at W = 5, 10, 30, 60, 120 (already stored per cell as `eligibleShareByBracket`; M566 collects them in one place) and the first W whose share is below 50 % (`firstWBelow50`; null if none). Script-written, computed from the cached per-drive records. Display: the existing table already shows the share per row; a one-line script-written sentence names `firstWBelow50` per stratum and direction.

## 2. Upper bracket for the run end
The sign between two samples is not observed; the primary run end is the linear zero crossing, the lower bracket (`noInterp`) ends the run at the last same-sign sample. UPPER bracket: the run is extended on each side to the adjacent opposite-sign sample time, with the power ramping linearly from the last same-sign value to 0 at that time (a zero knot placed at the opposite-sign sample time; if the adjacent step has zero duration nothing is added; nothing crosses a gap or a segment end). Pointwise the upper power is not below the primary power, and the upper domain contains the primary one.
Reported per stratum, W and direction (payload `runEndBracket`): the median and p95 of the per-drive upper value, of the primary value on the same drives, the median and p95 of (upper - primary) and of (primary - noInterp) per drive, and the eligible share under the upper bracket. Not part of the escalation flags; shown as a sentence beside the existing run-end note: "the run end is bracketed by the last same-sign sample (lower) and the adjacent opposite-sign sample (upper); the primary is the interpolated zero".

## 6. Rev 2 (2026-11-02): Director review (verdict revise); incorporated. Rev 1 SHA-256 before this edit: d3f7b8359e9d8383184505c547a2f533265bd9b3d4f41017dd7c268ab04af7f5
Where this section and sections 1-5 differ, this section governs.
- Meaning (Director). The upper bracket is a true outer bound on the run EXTENT and so on the eligible share; it is NOT an outer bound on the window VALUE (the ramp is the pointwise supremum only among linear-to-zero paths with the zero between the last same-sign sample and the opposite-sign sample; a constant hold gives a higher value over the same extent, and unobserved excursions mean the data alone bound no value). The value is reported as "upper bracket under an assumed linear-to-zero path". The discharge and charge extensions overlap on the same step and cannot both hold at once. A single-sample run becomes a triangle of up to 10 s (this inflates eligibility at W = 5 / 10; stated beside the figures). Added after the M561 results were seen: a sensitivity, not a gate on M561.
- R1 (known answers re-pinned). KU1 (P = +10 at t = 0..10 s, 1 s samples, then -10 at t = 15): W = 12: noInterp ineligible (run 10 s), primary 28/3 (window [0, 12], run [0, 12.5]), upper 29/3 (run [0, 15], ramp 10 -> 0 over 5 s); W = 14: only upper eligible, value 62/7, constant hold 10, net (M552) 54/7. KU2 is replaced by this W = 12 / 14 pin (the W = 10 case is degenerate: all three equal 10).
- R2 (KU8). An opposite-sign sample is a hard boundary: P = +10, -10, +10 at 5 s steps gives two upper runs [0, 5] and [5, 10] that touch at the zero knot but are never merged (zero-touch rule); each run is separate.
- R3 (KU9). An observed zero sample at a run end (10, 0, -10) is not extended: the run ends at the zero sample.
- R4 (KU5 numbers; threshold variants). Gap: +10 at t = 0..10, next sample at t = 16 (step 6 s > 5): upper = primary = noInterp (no extension over a gap) with explicit numbers; KU1 / KU3 sit exactly on the 5 s step limit, so a 4 s variant (-10 at t = 14) is added; segment ends (first / last knot) are never extended.
- R5 (population). The (upper - primary) comparisons are paired on drives eligible under both definitions; drives eligible only under the upper bracket are counted separately (`upperOnlyDrives`); the percentile base is conditional on an eligible run, as in M561 R4.
- R6 (Q1). The linear ramp stays the upper bracket; a constant-hold extension (the last same-sign power held to the opposite-sign sample time) is added as a payload-only sensitivity `hold`, with the exact gate ramp <= hold (same extents).
- R7 (escalation, replaces section 4 A3 numbers). Director trigger (written to `analyses/M566_check.json` and `runEndBracket.triggers`, not a display flag): (upper - primary) at the p95 exceeds 5 % of the primary p95 or the interval half-width at a displayed W; the upper eligible share exceeds the primary share by more than 10 percentage points at a displayed W; `firstWBelow50` differs between brackets. Any trigger escalates to the Director.
- R8. `firstWBelow50` = the smallest W in {5, 10, 30, 60, 120} whose share is below 50 %; the share need not be monotone in W (the denominator, drives with a segment of length W, shrinks); the payload reports `monotoneInW` and names the denominator in the sentence.
- R9 (audit). The blind audit reproduces the upper exact value, the upper run lengths (hence the upper eligible share) and the KU8 merge case on the 22-drive sample; gates are exact-value orderings only (no grid ordering: the run-start grid is not nested).
- R10 (Q3). No W = 60 special case (post hoc). One W-independent rule: any displayed row whose eligible share is under 50 % is marked low support (as already done in M561).
- Language (ledger AUD29-N02): "upper bracket under an assumed linear-to-zero path", "run end bracketed by the last same-sign sample and the adjacent opposite-sign sample"; never "true / actual run end", "maximum possible", "outer bound on power", "measured"; the upper value is never shown as an ordered pair beside the M552 net value (it can exceed it: KU1 W = 14: 62/7 against net 54/7). Rule with a ledger id in `language_gate.py`.
