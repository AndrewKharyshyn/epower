# M700 spec (Rev 0, pre-registered): selection of the number of breaks under day-level dependence (follow-up F1 of M693)

Status: DRAFT for Director review; written 2026-11-16 before any estimator exists or runs. Payload only (`changePoints.kSelectionStudy`), no display, no article figure. Not a re-gate of M693 (its verdict stands): M693 found that no location-set construction is admissible because the BIC choice of K is unreliable (planted K recovered in 0.68 / 0.56 / 0.23 of replicates in AR(1) one-step, AR(1) three-step and day-random-effect scenarios; false-break rate 0.72-0.77 in the day-effect scenario). This spec asks whether any rule for K is reliable at the real drives-per-day structure.

## 1. Question and non-claims
Is there a rule that selects the number of mean-shift breaks K of a per-drive series (X2 structure: n = 633 clean drives, 126 days) with a planted-K recovery rate of at least 0.80 and a false-break rate at most 0.10 in the core scenarios, including serial dependence and a day random effect? Non-claims: a selected K is a statistical statement about a logged series, not a cause, not the date of the software update, not degradation or capacity. The reference date 2026-08-25 is not used anywhere (it enters only `compare_reference` after the primary hash, as in M676/M693).

## 2. Candidate rules (all use the exact segment-neighbourhood DP of `tools/changepoints.py` for the candidate segmentations K = 0..Kmax, Kmax = 6, minimum segment 8 drives and 3 days as M675)
- K0 reference (not admissible by construction): fixed BIC (2K+1) ln n, the M676 rule.
- K1 design-effect BIC: the BIC penalty multiplied by lambda_hat, the day-cluster variance inflation (Rev 1.1 formula of M693 section 8.1, computed on the K = 0 residuals; floored at 1).
- K2 day-level BIC: segment the day-mean series (weights = drives per day) with the same DP and a BIC at the day level (n = 126); breaks map to the first drive of the day.
- K3 sequential day-clustered bootstrap test: K -> K+1 accepted while the supF-type statistic (largest RSS reduction from one more break) exceeds the 95th percentile of its distribution under a day-clustered wild Rademacher residual bootstrap of the K-break fit (199 draws per step in the study, 4000 in the payload); stop at the first non-rejection.
- K4 day-blocked cross-validation: 5 folds of whole days (stratified by month), prediction error of the fitted segment means on the held-out days, K = smallest K within one standard error of the minimum (one-SE rule).
No candidate is added after the result; a candidate that cannot run is reported as such.

## 3. Study (offline, `python tools/k_selection.py --study OUT.json`; seed 42; 200 replicates per scenario)
Planted structures at the real drives-per-day layout and the M693 generator (`tools/location_sets.scenario`): core S1 (one step 1.5 sd, iid), S2 (AR(1) 0.3), S3 (three steps), S4 (three steps, AR(1)); day-effect scenarios D5a (one step 1.5 sd with intraclass correlation 0.3) and D5b (three steps with ICC 0.3); null scenarios N0 (iid, no step) and N5 (day random effect, no step). Descriptive (100 replicates): D1 (0.75 sd), D2 (3 sd), D6 (t3 noise), S5 (semi-synthetic: real residuals rotated by whole days).
Reported per rule and scenario: distribution of selected K, recovery rate (selected K equals planted K), over-segmentation rate (K above planted), under-detection rate, false-break rate (K >= 1 in N0 and N5), Wilson 95% bounds, median runtime.

## 4. Gate (pre-registered; no tuning)
A rule is ADMISSIBLE if (a) recovery >= 0.80 with lower Wilson bound >= 0.70 in S1-S4 and D5a-D5b; (b) false-break rate <= 0.10 in N0 and in N5; (c) over-segmentation rate <= 0.10 in every core and day-effect scenario. Among admissible rules the selected one has the smallest median runtime (ties: the order K1..K4). If none is admissible: the block records the study, states "no admissible rule for the number of breaks at this series structure", and F1 is closed for this series length by the Director's decision (the question is whether more data, not a different method, is needed); K0 stays the reference and the existing 'exploratory, number of breaks not established' labels stay. A subset-admissible rule needs a new spec revision and is labelled post hoc.

## 5. Application to the real series (only after the gate, only if a rule is admissible)
Apply the selected rule to X1-X3 at the primary hash; report K (and the BIC K for comparison) per series and the stability of K under a day-block bootstrap (share of 4000 resamples with the same K). If K changes against the primary, the primary block is NOT replaced: the result is an additive sensitivity `kSelectionStudy.application` and the Director decides on a later spec. Location sets (M693) are not revisited here.

## 6. Outputs and tests
Additive key `changePoints.kSelectionStudy` (study file `analyses/M700_study.json` with the design fingerprint of the real structure, staleness detected as in M680; admissibility table; verdict; application if any); isolation guard (only the new key); no ingestion stage until a rule is selected. Known-answer test `tests/synthetic/test_m700_k_selection.py`: K0 reproduces BIC on a hand-built series; K1 equals K0 when lambda_hat = 1; K2 on a pure day-effect series selects K = 0 where K0 does not; K3 returns K = 0 on iid noise at the nominal rate over a small number of replicates and the planted K on a strong step; K4 deterministic folds; the study routine runs on a 3-replicate toy. Language: 'selected number of breaks under a pre-registered rule, simulated performance in stated scenarios'; never 'the true number of breaks', 'detected the update', 'validated'.

## 7. Risks and open points for the Director
R1 compute: K3 at 200 replicates x 199 draws x 8 scenarios is long; the study runs in the background. R2 the planted noise models are not the real noise (drifts, heteroscedasticity); a pass is necessary not sufficient. R3 ICC 0.3 and phi 0.3 are inherited from M693, not fitted. R4 K4's fold layout is a design choice. R5 whether an admissible rule can exist at n = 633, 126 days at all (power under ICC 0.3 may be too low for any rule to meet (a) and (b)); a negative result is an acceptable outcome. Questions: (1) are the thresholds (0.80 recovery, 0.10 false-break and over-segmentation) appropriate; (2) is Kmax = 6 and the 8-drive/3-day minimum segment right; (3) should the day-effect null N5 also bound K3's size more strictly (0.05).

## 8. Rev 1 (2026-11-16, Director review "revise"; overrides conflicting text above)
1. K1: lambda_hat is computed on the residuals of the BIC (K0) selected fit, once, and floored at 1 (not on K = 0 residuals, which absorb a planted step). Single pre-registered choice; no iteration.
2. K2: day-level weights are 1 / (s_b^2 + s_w^2 / m_d), with s_b^2, s_w^2 the between-day and within-day variance components estimated from the BIC-fit residuals (method of moments, floored at 0); the 8-drive / 3-day minimum segment is applied at the day level (3 days and 8 drives).
3. K3: every bootstrap draw refits both the K-break and the K+1-break segmentations with the exact DP; the study and the payload use the same B per step (199, stated; the application stability uses the same inner B). Sequential Monte Carlo stopping with a pre-registered runtime cap per replicate (cap and stopping rule written to the study file before the full run); a 5-replicate pilot runs first and its runtime is recorded.
4. K4: the segmentation is refitted on the training days of each fold only; fold seed fixed (42); folds stratified by month.
5. Recovery = the correct K AND a one-to-one order-preserving match of every estimated break inside its window (M693 8.3); "correct K, misplaced" is reported separately and does not count as recovery.
6. Scenarios added: S5 semi-synthetic returns to the core set (M693 8.9) with a semi-synthetic null (residuals rotated by whole days, no planted step); the circular day shift may add a wrap-point step if residuals drift: the study reports the wrap-point step size per replicate and the share of replicates where it exceeds 0.5 sd; an AR(1) + ICC combined scenario (phi 0.3, ICC 0.3, one step 1.5 sd). The share of fits at Kmax is reported per rule and scenario and flagged above 5 %.
7. Gate (b) becomes: false-break rate point <= 0.10 AND upper Wilson 95 % bound <= 0.15 in N0, N5 and the semi-synthetic null. No 0.05 point bound; K3's size against the nominal 0.05 is a diagnostic only.
8. Selection among admissible rules: the highest minimum core recovery; runtime only breaks ties.
9. Application (section 5): X2 only (the study covers X2's structure); X1 and X3 carry "no study". Not applied if the real series' lambda_hat or ICC exceeds the studied values (0.3 ICC). Bootstrap stability states the inner B.
10. A blind audit reproduces the admissibility table (from the study JSON seed and the generator definition, not my code) BEFORE any payload key is written.
11. A negative outcome records, per rule, the failed criteria and the closest rule (reference only), and a re-run trigger fixed in advance with this frozen spec: re-run when the clean-drive series reaches >= 180 days (currently 126). K0 output carries M693's BIC false-break rate under the day-effect null (0.72 to 0.77).
12. Answers to the Director questions: thresholds 0.80 / 0.10 / 0.10 kept (the 0.70 lower Wilson bound will not bind at 200 replicates and is retained as written); Kmax = 6 and the 8-drive / 3-day minimum kept (K0 stays equal to M676), Kmax reported when it binds.
Language: "in the stated scenarios" on every verdict; never "no break exists", "true K", "detected the update", "validated".
