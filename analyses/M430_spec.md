# M430 spec - fit quality of the parked-soak time-constant fits, held out by day (audit F05)

Status: PRE-REGISTERED, written before any result of this analysis exists. Rev 2, 2026-10-14 (Rev 1 reviewed by the research-director: verdict "revise", six blocking items B1-B6, all adopted; changes in section 7). Owner: Andrii. Origin: external audit 2026-10-13, finding F05 ("Cooling time constants have log-space predictive R2 of -1.158 (pack), -16.577 (coolant), -17.421 (oil); UI dismisses and omits it. Retain robust-slope association but demote physical tau; report prediction errors, residuals, gap sensitivity and changing-ambient model validation"), triaged in M427; verified on disk 2026-10-14 on the 578-drive corpus.

## 1. What is wrong (verified)

- Payload: `heatSoakCarryover.tauFitR2` = -1.051 (350 pairs), `interDriveCarryover.thermalDecay.coolant.r2` = -16.124 (458), `.oil.r2` = -16.775 (447). These are in-sample R2 values of the through-origin line `slope * gap` against a free-level mean of y = ln(resid excess / init excess); negative does not mean "no decay", it means the fitted line is worse than a constant mean predictor in squared error on the log scale.
- Dashboard (Thermal tab, JSX near 11716): "Sen's slope is not variance-minimizing, so R2 is not a meaningful diagnostic for this fit and is omitted here"; the coolant and oil R2 appear nowhere; no residual, prediction-error or held-out result is shown for any of the three fits. M429 added the gap split (pack pairs parked under one hour show no resolvable decay; coolant and oil decay faster over short gaps), which makes "does the single exponential predict held-out pairs?" the natural next question.

## 2. Estimand and what does not change

Estimand (per fit: pack, coolant, oil; one fit = the pairs the existing producers already select): the out-of-sample prediction error of the existing single-exponential through-origin model (`y_hat = slope * gap`, `slope` = Sen's slope refit on the training days) for the log excess ratio y, with calendar-day blocks held out, compared with fixed loss-consistent alternatives, pooled and inside two gap strata. Every existing leaf of the three blocks (tau, slope, nfit, R2, intercepts, M429 intervals) must reproduce exactly (gate G1); the new leaves are diagnostics only, tau stays a model-derived, gap-mix-dependent summary and no physical time constant is claimed. Pairs are selected on the outcome (`ie > 3 C` and `re > 0.5 C`): long-gap pairs are floored at `y >= ln(0.5/ie)` while the through-origin line keeps falling; this is stated wherever the metrics are shown and the number of pairs removed by the residual-excess filter is reported per fit.

## 3. Method

1. Pairs and clusters: the exact fitted pairs (gap x, log ratio y) and the arrival-day key of M429 (the helper `tools/soak_tau.py` already receives them).
2. Held-out predictions: sort the distinct arrival days, split them into k = 10 contiguous blocks (as the M119-v2 blocked-day scheme: whole days never split; each fold trains on all other blocks, including later ones, so this is blocked k-fold cross-validation, not a forward-only scheme; at most one drive is shared across a block boundary). For each fold refit Sen's slope on the training pairs and predict the held-out pairs. Folds with fewer than 8 training pairs or fewer than 3 distinct training gaps are skipped and counted.
3. Models, each scored under its own loss (loss-consistent references):
   - M: single exponential through the origin, `y_hat = slope_train * x` (`exp(y_hat)` on the retained-fraction scale).
   - B0: persistence, `y_hat = 0` (no decay, retained fraction 1).
   - B1: one constant level from the training pairs: the training MEDIAN of y for the MAE and MedAE metrics, the training MEAN of y for the log-scale MSE metric.
   - B2: the training median (MAE and MedAE metrics) or mean (log-scale MSE metric) of y inside the existing pack `gapBins` edges (0-1, 1-3, 3-6, 6-12, 12-24, 24+ h; coolant and oil have no bins of their own and use these edges); a bin with fewer than 5 training pairs falls back to B1.
   - B3 (attribution diagnostic only, never used in a statement): Theil-Sen with a free intercept (median residual), which separates the through-origin constraint from the curve shape.
4. Metrics (held-out, pooled over folds). PRIMARY (declared before any result): `skill_MAE = 1 - MAE_M / MAE_B` on the retained fraction `exp(y)` versus `exp(y_hat)` (bounded, physically read, not dominated by the floored long-gap pairs), for B in {B0, B1, B2}. SECONDARY, always printed: log-scale MSE skill `1 - MSE_M / MSE_B` (the held-out analogue of the printed R2), log MedAE of each model, median signed residual of M. If the sign of the relevant interval bound differs between the primary and a secondary metric for the same statement, the dashboard says "metric disagreement" beside the statement (it is never tuned to agree). A point skill whose denominator is zero (MAE_B = 0 or MedAE_B = 0, likely for the pack's B0 below one hour) is null with the reason; bootstrap draws with a zero denominator are counted and excluded, and when their share is above zero no statement is made for that cell.
5. Uncertainty: calendar day (arrival day) is the resampling cluster; paired percentile bootstrap over held-out days (the same day draws score M, B0, B1, B2 and B3), B = 4000, one fresh `default_rng(42)` per (fit, cell); every estimate carries n pairs, n days. Intervals are conditional on the fold assignment and on the training fits (training variability is not propagated). Intervals are per comparison and are not adjusted for multiplicity (27 intervals per metric: 3 fits x 3 cells x 3 references); this is stated.
6. Statements (declared rules, not scientific thresholds): only for the pooled set and the two strata gap < 1 h and gap >= 1 h, each only if it has at least 20 held-out pairs and at least 10 held-out days (a percentile bootstrap over fewer days is degenerate). On the PRIMARY skill: S1 "improvement over persistence established" iff the lower bound of skill vs B0 is > 0, otherwise "improvement over persistence not established"; S2 "gain over a constant level established" iff the lower bound of skill vs B1 is > 0, otherwise "not established"; S3 "worse than the empirical gap bins" iff the upper bound of skill vs B2 is < 0, otherwise "not shown to be worse". Gap bins are DESCRIPTIVE only (n, median observed and predicted retained fraction, median residual) and carry no statement.
7. Sensitivity: leave-one-arrival-day-out cross-validation; any statement that differs from the k = 10 result is flagged. In-sample R2 is kept and PRINTED for the three fits with a plain explanation of what it compares; it is no longer called not meaningful.

## 4. Gates and tests

- G1 reproduction: every existing leaf of the three blocks equals the current payload exactly (the diagnostics are additive). G2 isolation: the deep diff may touch only the new leaves `heatSoakCarryover.soakFitQuality`, `interDriveCarryover.thermalDecay.coolant.fitQuality`, `.oil.fitQuality`, `.battery.fitQuality` (a reference copy of the pack leaf) and the two method strings (`heatSoakCarryover.method`, `interDriveCarryover.methodology`). G3: a single-key recompute from the producers is byte-identical to the spliced payload.
- Known-answer tests, criteria declared here before any result. The planted designs REUSE the real per-day gap sets and arrival days of the stored pair tables (`analyses/M429_pairs.json`), a day-shared offset, integer-degree temperatures and the real selection: initial excess ie drawn as an integer in 4..25 C, true log ratio `eta`, observed residual excess `re = round(ie * exp(eta + day offset + noise))`, then the filter `ie > 3` and `re > 0.5` (so `re >= 1`) and `y = ln(re/ie)`. Declared parameters: total residual standard deviation 0.35 on the log scale with intra-day correlation (ICC) 0.25 (day-offset standard deviation 0.175, pair noise 0.303). (i) single exponential, tau = 12 h (`eta = -x/12`): in at least 85 % of R = 100 replications (B = 300) the lower bound of the primary skill vs B0 is > 0 AND S3 does not fire (upper bound of skill vs B2 >= 0); (ii) two timescales, `eta = -0.6 x` for `x < 1` and `-0.6 - 0.05 (x - 1)` for `x >= 1`: S3 fires (upper bound of skill vs B2 < 0) in at least 60 % of replications; (iii) no decay (`eta = 0`): the lower bound of skill vs B0 is <= 0 in at least 85 % of replications; (iv) a hand-computed 12-pair example for MAE, MedAE, MSE, the skills and the zero-denominator rule. If a criterion fails, the finding is reported as a statement about the test's power or the method, and the criteria are not changed.

## 5. Out of scope (listed, not changed)

The ambient-offset sensitivity of these diagnostics (the existing +-3 C tau sensitivity stays as is), the warmup tau (`thermalStepResponse`, audit F17), a gap-stratified or two-timescale soak model and interval censoring for the y = 0 mass (own spec), the standby-draw fit, any change to the point estimator or the pair selection, and per-bin accounting of the pairs removed by the filter (only the per-fit count is reported).

Wording: "held-out, blocked-day cross-validated (k = 10 arrival-day blocks)", "model-derived", "conditional on the pairing, the fold assignment and the manual driving-time ambient field", "pairs with residual excess <= 0.5 C are excluded (selection on the outcome)", "gap-mix-dependent summary"; never "measured", "validated", "fits well", "physically consistent", "intrinsic time constant".
