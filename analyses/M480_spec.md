Pre-registered; the Director re-reads it before any run (skill new-analysis, step 1). The SHA-256 of this file is recorded in the CHANGELOG entry before the first run. Origin: Director review (Opus, "revise") of the external cell-spread / cadence recheck of 2026-10-22, follow-up M478 -> M480 ("spec + blind audit + Director; the spec must record that the audit's results were seen beforehand"). Consumes the M479 sidecar `timing_support.csv`.
No change to the released estimator, `drive_master.csv` (MD5 `5556a0c6c8bcde0f6c64b8b9655208ed`), `summary_arrays.json`, the dashboard or any published value by this spec. Whether any M480 result enters the payload or the dashboard is decided after the Director's ruling on the results, as a separate step.

## Question and what would count as an answer
The released model (`degradation_trends.py`, `_analyse(dm, 'cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv', ..., covars=('T','I'), icol='peak_I_discharge')`) is an OLS of the per-drive loaded p95 cell-voltage spread on calendar months, mean pack temperature (`T_pack_mean_avg`) and peak discharge current, day-clustered (CR1, interval t(G-1)); it gives a positive conditional slope with a lower bound above zero under that procedure. The loaded p95 is a percentile of a changing set of loaded observations whose count, timing quality and loading mix can themselves depend on calendar time (the logger polling configuration changed mid-corpus, M126/M379). The question of M480 is **not** whether the pack is degrading. It is: **is the positive conditional slope explained by changes in logger timing, max/min synchronization, loaded support or loading mix that the released model does not control?** A "not explained by the tested covariates" answer is conditional on the tested proxies; it is not a physical-mechanism finding.

## Population and eligibility
Canonical-clean drives of the released model: `ens_outlier_v2` false, non-null `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv`, `T_pack_mean_avg` and `peak_I_discharge` (released `_prep`; expected n = 576 drives on 117 calendar days at the 606-drive master). Months are measured from the first eligible date with the released constant MONTH_DAYS = 30.4375. Resampling and clustering unit: the calendar day. Sidecar covariates are joined by `file`; a drive missing a covariate of a specification is dropped from that specification only, and every comparison with S0 is made on the identical rows (S0 is refitted on the rows of the specification it is compared with and reported as `S0|rows`).

## Specifications (frozen)
All are OLS with the same outcome, `months`, `T`, `I` (peak discharge current) and the covariate blocks below added; day-clustered CR1 with the t(G-1) interval (the released reference). Covariates are used as columns of the sidecar, centred and scaled for numerical stability only. (Rev 2) Transforms are fixed here: `log1p(n_loaded)` (not log, which is undefined at zero); `log1p(i_gap20_s)`; the zero-inflated `i_frac_dt_gt3s` enters as the indicator `i_frac_dt_gt3s > 0`; all other columns enter untransformed; no winsorizing. Dropped-row rule: a specification uses the complete cases of its own columns (for example `cur_age_p95_ms` exists for 577 drives); the number of rows dropped relative to S0 is reported for every specification.
- **S0** the released model (known-answer: slope reproduces the released `degradationTrends.cellSpread.clusterRobustOLS.slope` to 0.002 mV/month and n = 576, days = 117; the run stops otherwise).
- **S1 production cadence proxies**: + `i_dt_q50`, `i_row_cov` (the two quantities the released power-fade control uses, from the sidecar's untruncated interval median and row coverage).
- **S2 timing block**: + `log(n_loaded)`, `i_dt_iqr_ratio`, `i_frac_dt_gt3s`, `i_gap20_s`, `vmax_dt_q50`, `off_abs_p95_ms`, `cur_age_p95_ms`.
- **S3 loading-mix block**: + `i_abs_med`, `i_abs_p95`, `soc_med`, and the charge share `n_loaded_chg / n_loaded`.
- **S4** S2 and S3 together.
- **S5 regime-stratified** (Rev 2: fixed at two strata): S0 within each of the two drive-level regimes (fast / slow, `I_sample_period_s` below / at-or-above 1.05 s); the drives with unknown regime belong to neither stratum; each stratum is reported with its span in calendar months, its drives and days, and the precision gate below; no pooled contrast is drawn (the strata are not comparable as in M446).
- **S6 support subset**: S0 on the drives with `n_loaded >= 30` and `n_episodes >= 3` (exploratory thresholds, not validated production thresholds), reported next to S0 on the same rows. This is a subset analysis of the unchanged outcome, not a restricted-condition outcome. (Rev 2) The number of rows dropped relative to S0 is reported; the precision gate below applies to S6 as to S1-S5.
Not in M480: any outcome that is re-extracted at pair level (|I| 50-80 A with SoC 50-70 %, skew-restricted p95, low-current p95). Those need a pair-level extraction that the sidecar does not hold and are a separate milestone (M480b) with their own spec.

## Inference and uncertainty
Primary interval per specification: CR1 with t(G-1). Reported beside it (Rev 2: for S0 and for every specification S1-S4, not S4 alone): the wild cluster bootstrap-t with Webb weights and the null imposed on the tested coefficient, and the CR2 interval with Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, both from `tools/dependence_sensitivity.py` (`wild_cluster`, `cr2`; same code path, not reimplemented; 4,000 draws, seed 42); for S0 and S4 also the day-cluster pairs bootstrap (percentile) and the 7- and 14-day moving-block intervals of `tools/serial_dependence.py`, so the procedure dependence is shown for the adjusted models too. CR1 with up to ten regressors on 117 clusters can be anti-conservative when the leverage on `months` sits in a few days, hence the additional procedures. Every estimate carries n (drives, days). No interval is selected to force an answer; no multiplicity adjustment is applied because no single specification yields a claim: the verdict below is the pre-registered conjunction.
Precision gate (applies to S1-S6): a specification whose cluster SE is more than twice the S0|rows SE, or in which the VIF of `months` exceeds 10, is labelled **imprecise** (time is not separable from the added covariates) and can neither support nor refute the claim; it is reported with that label. (Rev 2) Reported for every specification beside the gate: the partial R-squared of `months` on the added covariate block, and the number of days that carry half of the leverage on `months`.

## Mechanical dependence between covariates and outcome (Rev 2)
`i_abs_med`, `i_abs_p95`, `soc_med`, `n_loaded_chg` and `cur_age_p95_ms` are computed on the same loaded pairs that define the outcome (`tools/timing_support.py`). The loaded subset is selected on the current, not on the spread, so the outcome is not leaked into the covariates; but the p95 of a small sample depends on the sample size, and `i_abs_p95` overlaps the released covariate `peak_I_discharge`. The results report the correlation of `i_abs_p95` with `peak_I_discharge`, and a synthetic known-answer check shows the sample-size dependence of the p95 at small `n_loaded` (below). The covariate blocks are drive-level proxies; a confound acting within drives (pair skew, transients) is not tested here, and `flag_skew_gt50ms` is true on every drive with cell data so skew does not vary across drives at that threshold.

## Descriptive companions (no inference on persistence)
- **Covariate drift**: for each added covariate, its slope on calendar months with a day-clustered interval (does the logging regime or loading mix move with time? this is the confound path itself).
- **Cutoff series**: S0 refitted on the drives dated up to each cutoff in {2026-07-31, 2026-08-31, 2026-09-30, last logged date}, with the number of drives and days; plus leave-last-8-days-out. Cumulative refits reuse most observations, so persistence across cutoffs is not independent replication, and the series makes no claim about any earlier dashboard release.
- **Influence**: leave-one-day-out slope range and the slope without the three most influential days; descriptive, not a rule.

## Decision rule (frozen; each outcome can occur) (Rev 2)
Let S_adj = {S1, S2, S3, S4} that are not labelled imprecise. Claim C1 is "the positive conditional slope is not explained by the tested drive-level timing and loading-mix proxies." For each specification the half-of-S0 comparison is made against the S0|rows estimate computed on that specification's own rows. S1-S4 are nested and correlated, so "three of four" is a count of reported specifications, not independent evidence.
- **Supported (provisional)** iff at least three specifications of S_adj exist and, among them, every point estimate is above zero and at least half of its S0|rows estimate, and the CR1 lower bound is above zero for at least three of them.
- **Not supported (explained at least in part)** iff at least two specifications of S_adj have a point estimate below half of their S0|rows estimate AND a CR1 upper bound below their S0|rows estimate (so the attenuation is distinguishable from sampling noise), or any specification of S_adj has a point estimate below zero with a CR1 upper bound below its S0|rows estimate.
- **Attenuated / inconclusive** otherwise (including a point estimate at or below zero with a wide interval, and fewer than three non-imprecise specifications).
The one-half fraction and the count of three are fixed conventions, chosen before any estimate and justified only as a magnitude comparable to the S0 standard-error scale; they are not tuned. The verdict is reported together with the full table, whichever it is, and "supported" always carries "provisional; pair-level test M480b pending". S5 and S6 are reported and never enter the verdict (the strata and the subset change the population, not only the adjustment); but a stratum slope at or below zero that passes the precision gate is stated beside the verdict, because a within-regime slope is the most direct test of the mid-corpus cadence change.

## Language (frozen; enforced by a language-gate rule added at implementation)
Allowed: "positive conditional slope", "not explained by the tested drive-level timing and loading-mix proxies" (always with "provisional; pair-level test M480b pending"), "cluster-robust SE" and the existing key names (`degradationTrends`, `clusterRobustOLS`), "attenuated", "imprecise (time not separable from the added covariates)", "procedure-dependent lower bound". Forbidden: cadence-invariant, not a logging artefact, cadence-corrected / cadence-adjusted slope, robust, confirmed, validated, persistent, established, degradation, SOH, capacity loss, "rules out" a mechanism, any statement that a covariate set "removes" or "controls for" the logging effect completely. Offsets and gaps are logged timestamp differences, not acquisition latency.

## Out of scope
