## 1. Question and non-question
Part A (N06 + F17): when two integration methods have different support, what is the difference on the intersection, what was added or lost, how much energy sits in the added / lost support, and how much does the largest-gap drive set influence the result? Instance: published method P (nearest-pair, 1.5 s tolerance, 5 s step cap, piecewise-linear power, zero-crossing-aware split) versus the union-clock method U (current and voltage each linearly interpolated onto the union of their sample times; 5 s step cap; support = union steps whose both channels are inside the interval spanned by their own valid samples). Part B (N12): which cell-voltage channels are complete and how stale are they relative to the pack-voltage namespace? Non-question: no correction of the published energy, no replacement of P by U, no new headline total; U is a named sensitivity only. Cell-array observability gives no capacity, no balancing or ageing statement.

## 2. Part A contract (reusable for any pair of methods)
For methods M1, M2 on drives i with per-drive value x_i (gross throughput kWh, net draw kWh) and support s_i (seconds with a finite integrand):
- Support sets: S1, S2 (drives with finite x); intersection I = S1 ∩ S2, added A = S2 \ S1, lost L = S1 \ S2. Report counts, days, seconds and the energy x of A and L (under the method that defines them), never summed into a delta.
- Intersection difference: ratio of sums over I, sum x2 / sum x1 - 1 (kWh and %), with the day-clustered percentile bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws), per-drive differences listed by percentile, and the share of drives whose |difference| exceeds 1 %.
- Invariance contract (hard known answer): the intersection estimate is bit-identical when unrelated drives are added to or removed from either method's support (tested on random subsets).
- Gap influence: the intersection difference recomputed after removing the k drives with the largest P-method gap seconds (k = 1, 5, 10 and the top decile), and the Spearman correlation of per-drive difference with gap seconds.
- Gained support is reported next to, not inside, the delta; the "pooled with added support" figure appears only as a labelled comparison row, with the sentence that it mixes support.
Instance outputs: gross and net energy for P versus U; the two F17 drives with the recoverable flag (`recoverableUnderAlternativeAlignment`), their U support seconds and energies, and the P null reason, as a named sensitivity block.

## 3. Estimands and known answers (Part A)
- KA1 identical methods (M2 = M1): intersection difference exactly 0, A and L empty.
- KA2 constant power on a regular grid with offset clocks: U equals the analytic P0 T for any phase offset between the channels when both are constant; P (nearest) equals it within the tolerance rule.
- KA3 a 6 s gap in both channels: neither method integrates across it (5 s cap), identical supports.
- KA4 (hard gate, real drives): on the intersection of P and U, the per-drive P energy equals the master `gross_*` columns (released ledger, 1e-4 kWh); a miss stops. U per drive must satisfy |net| <= gross.
- KA5 invariance contract above.
- KA6 sign: U support is a superset of P support on the two F17 drives and U gross > 0 there.
- Expectations (not gates): the intersection difference is small (the audit reports a small pooled difference); added support is the two drives, a small energy. Disagreement is reported, not tuned.
- Strata: logger cadence regime for the intersection difference; no pooled headline, no contrast between strata.

## 4. Part B (N12): cell-channel observability
Channels: `[BMS] Battery Cell #01..#14`, `#70..#80` (25 channels), `Max Cell Voltage`, `Min Cell Voltage`, against `[BMS] HV Battery voltage` as the pack namespace. Per drive and channel: non-missing share of the drive duration, share of pack-voltage samples with a valid value of the channel within 1.5 s, median age of the latest value (time since the channel last changed, capped at 5 s), and a completeness class (complete >= 95 %, partial 50-95 %, sparse < 50 %). Per channel and cadence stratum: the distribution of those classes over drives. A pack-consistency probe on drives where all 25 cell channels are complete: the sum of the cell voltages versus the pack voltage (relative difference, p50 / p95), reported as a plausibility statement about the namespace, never as a calibration. Output: the count of drives with a complete 25-channel set and the list of channel names that are incomplete in more than 10 % of drives ("selected-channel example" wording). No cell statistic elsewhere in the dashboard is changed.

- R1 (Q1; method U). U is the named second method (a sensitivity on already-seen data, not a validation). Definition: the valid samples of each channel (current |I| < 900, voltage 200..450, exactly as `integration_scenarios.load_iv`); each channel is linearly interpolated between consecutive valid samples only if their gap is <= 5 s (the per-channel maximum interpolation gap; sensitivity rows 1.5, 3 and 10 s), never across its own gap; the union grid is the sorted unique times of both channels; a union step is supported iff it is <= 5 s long and both channels are defined at both ends (no bridging). Inside a supported step both channels are linear, so the power is an exact quadratic: the energy is d (I0 V0 + I1 V1) / 3 + d (I0 V1 + I1 V0) / 6 (per 1000 for kW and 3600 for h) and, because V > 0, the discharge / charge split is at the single root of the linear current inside the step (exact). The published method P is IMPORTED (`integration_scenarios.integrate`, not recoded); a tolerance-ladder row of P (nearest-match tolerance 1.5 / 3 / 5 s) separates the tolerance from the alignment change.

Invariance applies to the point estimate (bit-identical when unrelated drives are added or removed); the interval is identical only with the day set fixed.
