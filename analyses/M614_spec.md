## 1. Problem (what is known, from source and the CHANGELOG; nothing here is a new result)
`degradation_trends._analyse` selects the HEADLINE estimator of each trend by a stateless per-run rule (M155): the mixed model (random intercept per calendar day, REML) is the primary iff it fitted, converged, returned a finite positive fixed-effect SE and that SE is not more than 25 times the cluster-robust OLS SE (`me_ok`); otherwise the day-clustered cluster-robust OLS is primary. The equivalence test (TOST) ALWAYS uses the cluster-robust OLS slope, SE and t(G-1) reference (P0-05), whatever the headline is.
Because `me_ok` depends on whether the estimated day variance sits on the singular boundary, the headline can flip between ingestions when nothing about the vehicle changed: `degradation_trends.py` documents `resistanceVreg` as mixed-primary at 289 drives; the M608 ingestion found it OLS at 632 drives (mixed fit singular) and mixed at 665 (non-singular, group variance 8.48 against residual variance 150.2, ratio 0.056, blind audit M608 verdict accept). Each flip needs a `--ack-estimator-change` (`ingest_core.estimator_gate`), moves the displayed slope (1.249 versus 1.354 mOhm/month at 665 drives, both intervals marginal) and costs an ingestion stop. The flip carries no scientific information: it is a property of how close one nuisance variance estimate is to zero.
Standing conventions this spec must respect: the day is the resampling cluster; a reported estimate carries a CI and n (drives, days); method disagreement is reported, not tuned away; `resistanceVreg` is `sensitivity_cadence_uncontrolled` (the primary estimand of the family is `powerFade`); "not established" is not "no degradation"; the quantity is a proxy resistance trend, never measured resistance; no article figure without the Opus audit and Andrii's sign-off.

## 2. Estimand and population (unchanged)
Slope of `vreg_R_pack_mohm` on months (days since the first eligible drive / 30.4375) adjusted for `T_pack_mean_avg` and `vreg_I_p95_A`, canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2` false) with a non-null outcome, day-clustered. Unit mOhm/month. The estimand, the covariates and the TOST (basis, bounds, released bound) do not change in any option below.

## 3. Options (Rev 0 recommends A; the Director decides)
**A. Fixed headline.** The headline is always the cluster-robust OLS (slope, SE, t(G-1) interval), the same model and reference as the TOST, so slope, interval and equivalence test come from one inferential model (the P0-05 argument extended to the headline). The mixed model is always computed and shown as a sensitivity with explicit validity fields; its status can no longer change the headline. No threshold is introduced. Flips: none by construction.
**B. Hysteresis.** Keep "prefer mixed when valid" but make the switch sticky: switch to mixed only after `me_ok` held at K consecutive evaluation points, and back to OLS only on a hard failure (non-convergence, SE blow-up, non-finite) at J consecutive points. Needs stored state (the previous primary and counters, in the payload) or a deterministic replay over corpus prefixes, and three new thresholds (K, J, an evaluation grid) that cannot be derived from first principles; any choice can be accused of being tuned to the observed 632 / 665 behaviour. Flips: reduced, not removed.
**C. Diagnostics only.** Leave the rule; report `reCovarianceSingular` and the variance ratio at every ingestion and add a flip-risk note. Flips continue and keep costing stops.
Selection criteria, in this order and fixed now: P1 no headline flip attributable to the singular boundary; P2 slope, SE and reference distribution of the headline from one model, consistent with the TOST; P3 no threshold chosen after seeing the replay (section 5); P4 smallest change to published figures; P5 method disagreement stays visible. A meets P1, P2, P3, P5; it changes the published `resistanceVreg` headline once (mixed 1.354 -> cluster OLS 1.249 at the current corpus; cluster OLS was also the headline estimator at 632 drives), which is P4's cost. B meets P1 only partly and fails P3 unless the Director fixes K, J and the grid on principle. C meets none of P1-P3.

## 5. Replay (descriptive, declared now, never used to choose or tune)
Grid: chronological prefixes of the eligible `resistanceVreg` drives at counts 100, 110, ..., up to the current eligible count (294) in steps of 10, plus the current corpus. For each prefix, with the CURRENT rule: primary estimator, `me_ok` components, group variance, variance ratio, SE ratio to OLS, mixed and OLS slopes; report the number of grid points at which the primary differs from its predecessor (flips), under the current rule and under A (zero by construction) and, if the Director chooses B, under B with the Director's K and J. Prefixes are cumulative, not independent: counts are descriptive, no interval is attached, no threshold is read off them. Also reported: the largest absolute difference of the two slopes in OLS standard errors over the grid (P5), so a reader sees whether hiding the mixed model would ever have changed a conclusion.

## 6. Pre-registered known-answer tests (written before the code)
T1 synthetic data with a negligible day variance: mixed fit singular or blown up -> `mixedEffects.valid` false with its reason, headline OLS (under A and under the current rule alike). T2 synthetic data with a real day variance (ratio about 0.05): `valid` true; under A the headline stays OLS and the mixed slope is within tolerance of the planted slope; the current rule picks mixed (the contrast). T3 a boundary series (days appended one at a time to a data set whose day variance estimate crosses zero): the current rule yields at least one flip, A yields none. T4 TOST fields (`tostSlopeUsed`, `tostSeUsed`, `tostCandidates`, `releasedEquivalence`, `equivalenceBoundStatus`) byte-identical before and after on the real corpus. T5 isolation diff on the real payload: only the keys named in section 4 change, every other leaf identical (deep diff, allow-list). T6 `cellSpread` headline, intervals and TOST unchanged. T7 `reCovarianceSingular`, `varianceRatio` and `seRatioToOLS` equal an independent recomputation from the model fit. T8 the displayed sentence and every displayed slope follow the headline in all cohort modes (dump_tabs).
