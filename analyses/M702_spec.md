# M702 spec (Rev 0, pre-registered): current-step response resistance and a Thevenin 1-RC fit per drive (backlog N-09, part 1)

Status: DRAFT for Director review; written 2026-11-16 before any estimator exists or runs. Payload only (additive key, no display, no article figure). Road-load / CoC / mass items stay closed. Depends on N-01 (M622 winter protocol, M634 temperature controls): temperature enters only through those pre-registered forms.

## 1. Question and non-claims
Can an event-based "apparent 1 s resistance" and a 1-RC time constant be estimated from the logged HV current and voltage channels at the logger's cadence, with a defensible uncertainty, separately for the fast-polled and slow-polled strata, and does a day-clustered trend of that resistance (with the M634 temperature control) differ from the existing regression proxy `vreg_R_pack_mohm`? Non-claims: an apparent resistance of the logged channels is not a cell or pack internal resistance, not state of health, not a degradation estimate; the cadence limits what a "1 s" response can mean; the Bayesian state-space SoH tracker and the semi-empirical aging model of N-09 are NOT part of this milestone (part 2, own spec). Calendar and cycle ageing terms cannot be separated on one vehicle (Ah and time near-collinear): stated as a design limit, no such model is fitted here.

## 2. Data, events and eligibility (fixed before any result)
- Canonical-clean drives (`ens_outlier_v2 == False`), HV current `I` (BMS, A, discharge positive per the repo convention) and pack voltage `V` on the logged native timestamps (no resampling, no interpolation across a gap longer than 2 x the drive's median interval).
- Step event: a native-timestamp window in which `|I(t0 + 0.0..) - I(t0 - 1.0..0)|` is at least `STEP_A` (pre-set at implementation from `signal_representation.json`: at least 20 current grid steps and not below 15 A), the pre-step current is within 5 A for the 1 s before, and the post-step current stays within 20 % of its post-step value for the response window (1.0 to 3.0 s). Events are non-overlapping (first step in a 10 s neighbourhood), within 20 s of a gap or a drive boundary excluded.
- Apparent 1 s resistance of an event: `R1 = (V_pre - V(t0 + 1 s)) / (I(t0 + 1 s) - I_pre)`, with V and I at t0 + 1 s taken as the nearest native samples within 0.5 x the median interval (otherwise the event is unusable, with the exclusion counted by reason). Instantaneous jump `R0` is NOT defined at the logger cadence (stated limit) and is not reported.
- Per drive: the median of its event `R1` (>= 3 events) with the event count; a drive below 3 events has no value (not imputed).
- Cadence strata: `cadence_regime.csv` regimes (fast / slow); never pooled across strata; the B6 cadence-invariance check stays failed and shown; no fast-versus-slow contrast.
- Feasibility table written BEFORE any estimate (eligibility counts only: events, drives, days per stratum, by month, by thermal class, with the exclusion reasons), recorded in the CHANGELOG; the support ladder (10 drives on 5 days) applies per stratum; below it the stratum is "not estimable" with its counts.

## 3. Methods
- E1 (event library): per drive and per stratum, event counts, median R1 and the within-drive dispersion (MAD).
- E2 (1-RC time constant): for events with at least 4 native samples in 0 to 3 s after the step, fit `V(t) = V_pre - I_step * (R0 + R1c (1 - exp(-(t - t0)/tau)))` by non-linear least squares with `tau` bounded to [0.5, 30] s and R0, R1c >= 0; report the event-level fit residual and whether the fit is identifiable (a parameter at its bound or a correlation of R0 and R1c above 0.99 means "not identifiable"); a per-drive `tau` is shown only for strata where at least 50 % of the events are identifiable and at least 10 drives on 5 days; otherwise "not identifiable at this cadence".
- E3 (trend): per stratum, `R1_drive ~ 1 + months + temperature control + I_step` with the M634 pre-registered temperature forms (linear primary, quadratic, Arrhenius, support-flagged spline as sensitivities) and a calendar-day random effect (or the day-clustered percentile bootstrap, seed 42, 4000 draws, as M393); the slope is a descriptive trend of an apparent resistance over the observation window, with MDE and the TOST margin of the winter protocol (M622), never a degradation rate; no extrapolation.
- Comparison with `vreg_R_pack_mohm`: Spearman correlation per stratum on shared drives and the Bland-Altman difference with day-clustered intervals; method disagreement is reported, not tuned.
- Known-answer simulation (before any real run): synthetic current steps through a known 1-RC circuit, sampled at the two real cadence distributions (fast and slow) with PID jitter and quantisation from `signal_representation.json`; the estimator must recover R1 within 10 % (bias) and report `tau` as not identifiable when the sampling is too coarse; a day random effect and a drift planted in R must be recovered by the trend model within its interval at coverage >= 0.90 over >= 500 replicates; a null (no drift) must hold false-positive <= 0.075. A failure blocks any result wording.

## 4. Primary vs sensitivity
Primary: E1 and E3 with the linear temperature control, per stratum. Sensitivities: step threshold (15, 20, 30 A), response window (1.0 vs 0.5 or 2.0 s), the nearest-sample tolerance, the minimum events per drive (3 vs 5), temperature forms, SoC band stratification (low / mid / high), thermal class stratification, exclusion of drive-start events (first 60 s).

## 5. Accept / reject / falsify
- Supported (wording ladder): the stratum has support, the known-answer simulation passes, the trend interval excludes zero with the same sign in the sandwich and the day-bootstrap intervals AND under the Arrhenius and spline controls; otherwise "trend not established" (TOST "not established" is not "no degradation"; the window is too short).
- Falsifiers: R1 depends on the step size or SoC in a way that dominates the trend (an effect of the covariate mix, not of time); the R1 trend equals the vreg proxy trend but both vanish under the Arrhenius control (a temperature artefact); a cadence-regime change (2026-08-14, 2026-10-01) moves R1 by more than the slope window.
- No claim about the pack health from any outcome; no article figure.

## 6. Outputs, tests, language

Language rule `M702-thevenin-wording`: 'apparent 1 s resistance of the logged current-step response (estimated)'; never 'internal resistance', 'measured resistance', 'state of health', 'degradation rate', 'cell resistance'; a time constant is 'apparent 1-RC time constant at the logged cadence', never 'the electrochemical time constant'.

## 7. Risks and open points for the Director
R1 cadence: at the slow stratum's median interval (about 0.77 s) a 1 s response has one or two samples; E2 may be not identifiable there, and R1 itself rests on one nearest-sample pair (noise, quantisation of 0.05 A and 0.16 V). R2 step events are load-excited by the driver and the controller (the e-POWER battery current is set by the generator and motor control): events are not independent experiments. R3 temperature, SoC and current covary with events; the M634 controls are pre-registered but the confounding with the period remains. R4 the current sign convention and the BMS-versus-VCM current channel difference (M258+ notes). R5 the response window excludes the slow diffusion tail. R6 the first 60 s of a drive carry the key-on load transient. Questions: (1) is `R1` at t0 + 1 s the right estimand or should the estimand be a window mean 0.5 to 1.5 s; (2) is the identifiability rule for tau adequate, or should E2 be dropped for the slow stratum in advance; (3) is the simulation gating adequate (bias 10 %, coverage 0.90, false-positive 0.075); (4) should E3 add the day random effect model or keep the bootstrap only, given the existing M393 convention.

## 8. Rev 1 (2026-11-16, Director review "revise"; overrides conflicting text above)
Design facts from `cadence_regime.csv` (eligibility information, no estimate): median HV-current sample period: fast regime 0.84 s (range 0.73 to 1.06, 228 drives), slow regime 1.41 s (interquartile 1.26 to 1.48, range 1.14 to 3.89, 425 drives). The "about 0.77 s" quoted for the slow stratum in section 7 R1 is withdrawn: that figure belongs to the fast stratum; in the slow stratum a 1 s response holds at most one sample.
1. Estimand renamed "apparent short-horizon step resistance" (R_sh): dV/dI between co-timed pre- and post-step sample pairs (not "R at 1 s"). Per event the gap from the last pre-step to the first post-step sample and the V-I timestamp skew are recorded; the skew is capped at 0.25 s (pre-set; events above are excluded and counted); the gap distribution is reported per stratum.
2. Pre-step steadiness is counted in samples (at least 2 samples within the 5 A band), not in seconds; the post-step stability rule is applied to the first post-step samples available.
3. Current channel: `[BMS] HV Battery Current (A)` (the `(Low)` channel carries a -1600 sentinel and is not used). The logged channel is charge-positive and the repo code uses discharge-positive `Id = -v`; the sign conversion is stated in the block and tested by a known-answer case.
4. Primary = discharge-direction steps (a rise of discharge current). Charge-direction steps are a sensitivity only (as the vreg proxy excludes charge).
5. E2 (the 1-RC time-constant fit) is DROPPED from part 1: with one to three samples in the window it is not identifiable at either stratum. The 1-RC model is used only inside the simulation; a real-data `tau` needs a later spec that cites the simulation.
6. E3: one primary, the CR1 day-clustered OLS on the M622 basis; the day-bootstrap (seed 42, 4000 draws) must agree with it (disagreement is reported); the random-effect model is a sensitivity only. The "or" in section 3 is removed.
7. No TOST in part 1: the M622 equivalence margin is locked for the vreg proxy and is not transferred to R_sh after the vreg data have been seen. The block reports the slope, its interval and the MDE only, follows the M622 look schedule, and is marked provisional.
8. Simulation (replaces section 3 simulation): semi-synthetic, real current traces and real step events from drives are fed through a known OCV(SoC) plus 1-RC model, with real onset censoring, V-I skew, the 0.05 A / 0.16 V quantisation and a day intraclass correlation; a drift planted in R sized to the MDE; the gate is bias stability (|bias| <= 10 % overall and no systematic trend of bias across step size, sampling gap, SoC band and the cadence breaks 2026-08-14 and 2026-10-01), coverage >= 0.90 of the planted drift, false-positive <= 0.075 under the null, over >= 500 replicates; the Monte Carlo SE is reported. Failure blocks any result wording.
9. Structural-break sensitivities: the two cadence breaks and the battery software update date (owner: intake-air outage) are reported as date-split sensitivities; an R_sh shift across a break larger than the slope window is a falsifier of the trend wording.
10. Per-drive weighting fixed before any run: each drive contributes its median event value; the trend regression weights drives equally (day-clustered); an event-weighted version is a sensitivity.
11. The feasibility table (events after every exclusion, drives, days per stratum, by month and thermal class) is produced before any estimate; if fewer than 10 drives on 5 days remain in a stratum it is "not estimable". Expectation recorded: largely a cross-check of the vreg proxy with far fewer events; no new trend endpoint is claimed.
Answers to the Director questions: (1) the sample-pair estimand, not a window mean, with the gap as a recorded covariate and a gap-stratified sensitivity; (2) E2 dropped in advance for both strata; (3) thresholds kept (Monte Carlo SE about 1 % at 500 replicates), design replaced by item 8; (4) CR1 primary plus the bootstrap check, random effect as a sensitivity.
