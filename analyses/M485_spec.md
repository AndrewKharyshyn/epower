## Question
The released cell-spread slope (cluster-robust OLS of the per-drive loaded p95 on months, mean pack temperature and peak discharge current; day clusters; CR1 interval with the t(G-1) reference) is estimated on a short window (about 5.1 months, 159 calendar days). A reader of the headline cannot see how the estimate was reached as data accumulated. The question this block answers descriptively is: **over what window is the released cell-spread slope estimated, and how did the same released estimator behave on the data available at earlier calendar cutoffs?** It does not test persistence, replicate anything (cumulative refits are nested and reuse almost all observations, so the counts below are not independent evidence), or say anything about degradation.

## Population and estimator (identical to the released model)
`degradation_trends._prep` / `_cluster_ols` for `cell_spread_loaded_p95_mv`, covariates `T_pack_mean_avg` and `peak_I_discharge`, canonical-clean (`ens_outlier_v2` false, read through the module's own `_as_bool`, so string-valued flags are handled as released), cluster = calendar day, CR1 with the small-sample factor, t(G-1) reference. The tool imports and calls those functions; the estimator is not reimplemented. (Rev 2, R1) The formula is pinned to the released `y ~ months + T + I`: `_covars_present` keeps a covariate only when more than half of the rows carry it, so on a thin early subset a covariate could silently drop out and change the estimator. A cutoff whose fitted formula differs from the pinned one is listed as not evaluable ("covariate support"), never fitted with a different formula. (Rev 2, R2) "Eligible" means the complete-case rows (non-null outcome, `T` and `I`, flag false), and the window facts below come from these rows; the months origin is the earliest date of the subset, and the slope does not depend on the origin because months enter linearly with a constant. Cutoffs are compared on `day` (the calendar date, as a date, even if the master carries a timestamp), not on timestamps.

## Frozen rules
- **Window facts** (complete-case rows of the released fit): first and last eligible date, calendar span in days and in months (days / 30.4375, one decimal), number of eligible drives and days (equal to `degradationTrends.cellSpread.clusterRobustOLS` nObs / nDays). (Rev 2, R8) The resistance window length is NOT carried here; at most the block holds a key-path pointer to the existing M482 binding (`powerFade.windowMo`).
- **Cutoffs**: the last calendar day of every calendar month from the month of the first eligible date up to, and excluding, the month of the last eligible date, plus the last eligible date itself (a last date that falls on a month end appears once). For each cutoff the released estimator is refitted on the eligible rows with `day` on or before the cutoff. (Rev 2, R3) A month-end cutoff with no new eligible drive since the previous cutoff repeats the previous row: it is listed as `noNewData` and is left out of m, k and s below (a month with no eligible drives is therefore not a row of its own in the counts). The final row moves at every ingestion by design.
- **Support to report a cutoff row**: at least 20 eligible drives on at least 5 calendar days, and the pinned formula; otherwise the row is listed as not evaluable with its counts and the reason.
- **Row content**: cutoff date, drives, days, slope (mV/month), CR1 standard error, 95 % interval (t(G-1)), whether the interval excludes zero, the sign of the point estimate (Rev 2, R4: an exactly-zero estimate has sign 0 and counts as no change).
- **Leave-last-8-days-out** (Rev 2, R5): a separately labelled row, outside m, k and s: the released estimator on the eligible rows after dropping the 8 most recent distinct eligible days. The 8 is the number used by `tools/record_spread_fit.py` (its stability flag); that rule refits the first stage on the keep set, a different object from this block, and the block says so.
- **Derived summary (all computed from the rows)**: number of evaluable cutoffs m (`noNewData` and not evaluable rows excluded); number k whose interval excludes zero; number s of sign changes of the point estimate across consecutive evaluable cutoffs (Rev 2, R4: over evaluable rows only); the first cutoff at which the interval excludes zero (or none), kept in the key and the table only; whether that first cutoff is the last eligible date; the span in months. No classification such as stable or unstable is stored.
- **Reconciliation (hard stop)**: the final cutoff row must equal the released `degradationTrends.cellSpread.clusterRobustOLS` slope, interval, nObs and nDays (payload rounding); otherwise the stage fails.

## Dashboard binding (reporting only)
- (Rev 2, R6) One sentence after the cell-spread headline sentences that already carry the procedure-dependence note (M477), triggered on ANY cell-spread headline (positive or not): "Cell-spread observation window W months (N drives on D days). Cumulative refits of the released estimator at month-end cutoffs: the interval excluded zero at k of m cutoffs and the point estimate changed sign s times; the refits are nested, reuse almost all observations and are not independent replication; no claim beyond the released interval." The sentence contains none of the forbidden words (it says "no claim beyond the released interval", not "no persistence/stability/degradation claim"), does not carry the first-exclusion date (that stays in the key and the table), names the quantity (cell-spread), and is generated from the key only.
- A compact table of the cutoff rows (including `noNewData`, not-evaluable and the labelled leave-last-8 row) in the Health tab next to the serial-dependence panel: cutoff, drives, days, slope with interval.
- The sentence and table render only when the key is present; counts and dates come from the key; nothing is typed. The semantic gate recomputes the sentence from the payload and fails on a mismatch, and fails if a cell-spread headline lacks the window sentence while the key is present.

## Language (frozen; enforced by a language-gate rule added at implementation)
Allowed: "observation window", "cumulative refit of the released estimator at the cutoff", "the interval excluded zero at k of m cutoffs", "the point estimate changed sign s times", "nested, reuse almost all observations and are not independent replication", "no claim beyond the released interval". Forbidden: persistent / persists, stable / unstable / stability, robust, confirmed, validated, trend over time, replicated, degradation, SOH, capacity loss, "earlier dashboard release" (the refits are current-code refits of the current corpus, not reconstructions of past releases), "onset", "first became significant". The gate rule has an explicit ledger id; the sentence above is checked against it.
